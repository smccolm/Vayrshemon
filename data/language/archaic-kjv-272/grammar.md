# KJV historical register 0.10-kjv.272 — throne, song and seals

## The seer, the returning voice and the shared singers
The seer's ordinary I remains personal. In4:1 the returning trumpet-like voice has quoted plural-I, continuing the already marked voice from1:10–19 without inserting a new speaker name. In5:9–10 the redeemed singers have singular-we and our-singular, while kings and priests stay plural. Shared identity does not erase the individual offices. In6:16 the varied earthly groups share a fearful singular-we address. The martyrs' our-blood in6:10 is one communal claim. None of these forms supplies a count of divine persons, and the one seated in4:2 is still explicitly one.

## Enumeration, products, fractions and ordinal seals
Twenty-four is two-twelves, retaining the Unta counting basis. The repeated sevens, four beasts, six wings each, numbered beast descriptions and successive seals remain independently visible. Ten-thousand times ten-thousand is the explicit product100000000 in5:11. The following thousands of thousands uses plural thousand-groups; its two group labels must not be silently turned into a second exact million or a grand total. In6:6 each source penny and the wheat/barley quantities remain unconverted. In6:8 fourth part is exactly1/4. Pair of balances in6:5 is a weighing apparatus, not two counted instruments; it therefore receives no physical dual. Fifth and sixth use inherited ordinal morphology on five and six.

## Stones, vessels and premature fruit
Sardine is an explicit stone label beside jasper, without a modern mineral equation. Rainbow uses rain plus the inherited bow extension; it is not a bowing gesture. The golden vials use the bowl form as fragrance vessels, not medical tubes. Untimely figs are before-time fruit, without an invented cultivar or calendar. Death the rider and Hell the follower receive separate contextual labels sharing their existing roots. Fellowservant is share-servant, keeping the fellow relation distinct from the additional brethren. Eight common entries are added; there are no new proper names or inflectional paradigms.

## Supplied readings and unresolved relationships
Keep are-and-were-created and thy-pleasure in4:11; read-the-book in5:4; us in5:9; kings,us,our and we in5:10; and him-that-liveth-forever in5:14. All four Come and see refrains in6:1,3,5,7 remain complete. The throne-sitter and Lamb retain separate grammatical relations and both receive the final praise; the translator does not collapse them into an explanatory formula. In6:17 the supplied his-wrath remains his, not their. Fulfilled in6:11 receives no invented exact martyr quota. Death among the four means in6:8 is not diagnosed as an unstated epidemic. Revelation's source descriptions and violent imagery remain represented as source narrative and speech.
