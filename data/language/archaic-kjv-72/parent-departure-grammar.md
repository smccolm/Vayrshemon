## One returned priest and the plural command

The source orders one of the deported priests returned, then says let them go and dwell, then let him teach(17:27). The target retains cardinal one, plural they and singular he. It neither invents an entourage nor silently repairs the number shift. The next verse's one priest is again cardinal one_num, not the inherited generic-human one. This unresolved command is documented so a future editor does not mistake its awkwardness for an accidental pronoun conversion. By contrast, each nation in17:29distributes actions among separate groups rather than turning them into one collective speaker.
