# KJV historical continuation254 · 2 Thessalonians1–3

## Shared ministry and personal autograph

The ministry acts as singular-we while the three named senders remain three named people. The possessive our-one marks their common relation or undertaking, not arithmetic identity. Personal-I remains in the recollection2:5 and autograph3:17; no plural-I or physical dual is inserted. Lord and Father remain coordinated in2:16, with the petition continuing into2:17. The false claimant's self-exaltation2:4 is third-person attributed speech, not the narrator calling him God.

## Claimed letter, restraint and deferred revelation

The supplied day of Christ2:2 is retained. A letter as-from-us is a claimed provenance, while3:17 gives the source's autograph-sign claim; neither becomes modern forensic authentication. Withholdeth2:6 and letteth2:7 mean restraint. Preserve what then he, without identifying the restrainer, scheduling an end date or replacing the printed passage with another tradition. First2:3 marks precedence; then2:8 continues sequence. Apostasy is falling-away from allegiance, not a body falling down. The man of sin and son of perdition remain descriptive labels, not an invented named contemporary person.

## Destruction, delusion and attributed agency

Everlasting destruction from presence/glory1:9 retains the difficult from relation rather than deciding separation versus source. Lord mouth-spirit and coming brightness2:8 are source images, not unprinted named agents. The hostile coming after Satan's working2:9 is distinct from the Lord's coming. God explicitly sends the delusion in2:11; it means deceptive thought, never a psychiatric diagnosis. Condemnation2:12 is kept without adding punishment mechanics. From the beginning2:13 is not replaced by firstfruits.

## Work, willingness and brotherly discipline

Would not work3:10 is unwillingness, not inability. Working not but meddling3:11 motivates the busybody entry; it does not condemn everyone who is busy. Night/day travail3:8 is toil, not childbirth. The ministry's right to support3:9 is distinct from its voluntary example. Not all have faith3:2 retains scoped negation. Social withdrawal3:14 is bounded by3:15: do not regard the person as an enemy; warn as a brother. Doing good3:13 remains enjoined. Walk is conduct, not a gait diagnosis, and unreasonable means perverse rather than inability to reason.
