# Historical continuation59: broken allegiance and contradictory messengers

## 1. Petitioning singular-we and the royal plural self

In1Kings12:4 the labouring people speaks as wesg, a singular collective whole, with ourone/yoke. Its many members remain plural people outside the speech. The king uses ipl for his governing answer and punitive policy in12:6,11,14, while the bodily audience instructions and fear of death use personal i. These distinctions follow the source speech act; they are not a blanket replacement of every first-person word. The rejection in12:16 is another singular polity speaking, not a claim all its members have become one physical organism.

## 2. A quotation changes the centre of we

In12:9 outer wesg is the king's governing circle as one answering body. Inside the reported petition, wesg resets to the people bearing the yoke. In12:10 ourone and wesg again belong to quoted petitioners, while my/little finger and my/father belong to the prescribed royal bodily boast. The conlang keeps these awkward shifts rather than replacing all pronouns with explanatory names. The verse back-reading records the centres. Ordinary plural advisers remain you; they are not silently recast as one self.

## 3. Divine agency and personal vulnerability

The LORD's from-me in12:24 is ipl. The speaker both claims the split and forbids fighting brothers. In14:7–10 divine ipl/mypl marks the judgment speech, while the messenger's i in14:6 is personal. Jeroboam's14:2 request to his wife is personal i, but the reported royal role uses ipl. His13:6 injured hand uses personal i/my;13:7 contrasts come with me with a plural royal reward-giver. Do not infer three persons or insert a Trinity count into these passages. No modern mental-health diagnosis is a grammatical category.

## 4. Reported deception and later attributed revelation

In13:18 the older prophet claims an angel authorized the meal, but the narrator explicitly says he spoke falsely. kjv_lie_false is false-speak; the inherited lie is positional and cannot serve here. In13:20 the narrator then attributes a LORD-word to that same host. This uncomfortable reversal remains. Dual they:du tracks the two principal diners without asserting there were no other people anywhere in the house. The target does not silently endorse the first false message, excuse the victim, or invent a pardon for the deceiver.

## 5. Bodies, commands and exclamatory address

The addressed altar in13:2 receives :excl; the lament my/brother in13:30 does too. This follows the existing grammatical exclamation system, not an unsupported ! glyph. The face petition, dried hand, disobeyed mouth, unburied carcass, lion and ass remain concrete. Both negatives in13:28 survive: the lion neither ate the carcass nor tore the ass. Personal burial instructions use i/my. Grammatical object marking in14:11 makes dead people the eaten objects and dogs/birds the agents; unusual word order must not reverse that relation.

## 6. Source-sense vocabulary rather than spelling substitution

Burden relief uses kjv_lighten, not lighter derived from illumination. Whips use the existing scourge/lash noun rather than pluralizing the whipping verb. Scorpions preserve the threatening image without an asserted exact weapon design. kjv_consult is ask-counsel, kjv_chastise punish-teach, kjv_ordain authority-set. The turn of events uses turn:ev rather than the verb cause as an unexplained noun. Stoning uses the inherited stone-attack verb. Guard-custody of shields uses entrust rather than commit an act. Uproot is root-out, not cause roots to grow. Left in the destruction threat means remaining, not the left-hand direction. Unnamed objects are not silently people: one_num refers to a calf, whereas one of the priests is a legitimate human anaphor.

## 7. Unresolved source tensions stay visible

Only Judah in12:20 remains beside Judah-and-Benjamin in12:21. Samaria in13:32 is retained despite the later founding narrative. The commanded withdrawal in12 remains beside continuing war in14:30. City-entry death in14:12 remains beside the narrated door threshold in14:17. The abrupt what, even now in14:14 is not given an invented timetable. The sick child is singled out for burial because of the stated good thing, without an invented explanation that makes the death comforting. Repeated and conflicting royal claims are not modernized into one clean chronicle.

## 8. Names, quantities and charged labels

Seven proper-name records distinguish local Shemaiah, future Josiah, Samaria, Abijah son of Jeroboam, Nadab his successor, Naamah the Ammonitess and Abijam son of Rehoboam. Ammonitess retains the source feminine designation. Ten loaves,180000warriors,2calves,half a house,third-day return,eighth-month fifteenth-day feast,22year reign,41year age,17year reign and fifth-year invasion are checked independently as values, not inferred calendars. Cracknel remains a source food label; cruse a vessel without invented capacity. The source sodomite label is retained as a charged historical borrowing; it neither automatically means a modern sexual-orientation identity nor asserts an unprinted act or institution. The source's accusations remain attributed to their narrative speakers.
