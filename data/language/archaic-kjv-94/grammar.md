## Crown, wounded body and shared liability

Josiah's official inquiry in 34:21 uses plural-I `ipl` for the crown,followed by singular-we `wesg` and `ourone` for the common body under wrath. The inquiry explicitly includes both king and survivors;the common pronoun does not erase that distinction. His wounded cry in 35:23 uses personal `i`: the injured body is not a palace or army. Necho's campaigning self in 35:21 and Cyrus's commissioned self in 36:23 use `ipl` as foreign crowns. Corporate identity is not restricted to approved speakers, nor substituted into every human first-person clause.

## Foreign divine commission and unsoftened promises

Necho says God commanded haste in35:21. In35:22 the narrator calls his words words from God's mouth. Cyrus credits the God of heaven for all kingdoms and a building charge;the plural-self remains the recipient,not the deity. The archaic translation keeps this awkward allocation of authority.
