# KJV historical register 0.10-kjv.1 — frozen, deliberately awkward

This is an authored historical register for the user's complete KJV project. Its first complete book is Jonah. It begins at the immutable consolidated 0.10 parent; it is **not** the replacement for current dramatic Vayrshemon. Historical fiction and actual evidence must remain distinct: tren is a real earlier project form, while assigning it to an imagined ancient translation tradition is an editorial construction. None of the new grammar is claimed to be an excavated or community-attested ancient language.

The inherited grammar is copied in inherited-grammar.md. This document supplies the historical register's choices. The phonographic slots, Maxima map, three-font treatment, Unta arithmetic and existing case/morphology identities are unchanged. Subsequent books may extend the archive in new pinned register versions; they must not regenerate an already frozen book with the newer forms.

## 1. A translation straining against its concepts

The target voice is a foreign translation tradition, chunky and over-explicit. It uses the grammar it has to carry concepts it does not comfortably lexicalize. It does not randomly omit words, confuse participants, turn positive statements into negatives, invent comic content or cancel the source narrator's assertions. The reader should feel the conceptual seams, not have to repair accidental mistranslations.

An English back-reading accompanies every verse in the editorial ledger. It is an editorial explanation of the intended reading, not an automatically verified reverse translation. The unchanged supplied KJV occupies the fourth Workbench column. The third is a source-order construction gloss, not a polished substitute for the KJV.

## 2. Heavy reference and serial finite clauses

Prefer complete finite clauses linked by and/after, and repeat lexical subjects at agency changes. The sailors, Jonah, the fish, the plant and the deity cannot inherit one another's actions through convenient ellipsis. Full nominative -läy on lexical subjects is common even when modern dramatic prose would omit it. Transitive objects remain fronted where practical. This makes the historical prose ponderous on purpose.

Do not add a temporal and-after to every lexical and: a conjunction within a list or description remains coordination. Direct speech keeps its own person and time. Third-person LORD in a prayer can turn into second-person thou without a translator replacing either. A reported future remains future relative to the quoted or reported intention, not a completed event.

The retained same-subject and different-subject converbs distinguish an arrived word saying from a ship going while Jonah finds it. There is no new converb ending. Present, past, perfect and prospective complements remain semantically distinct. A ship near being broken is not a ship already broken.

## 3. Carried words, carried prayers, resumptive objects

The word comes, saying; the prayer comes into the reserved house. Do not smooth these nominal events into ordinary he spoke or he listened. Event nominalization and genitive possession make speech feel like a carried object. The form is old-translator style, not a claim that Vayrshemon literally believes words are physical parcels.

Heavy constituents can be introduced, explained, and resumed by thatone before the governing verb. In 3:2 the object is the proclaiming which I command to you, that thing proclaim. In 2:8 the subject is they, lying emptiness they attend to, their mercy they forsake. The repeated pronoun is a discourse resumption, not a second independent participant. Record the referent in the verse note whenever it could be ambiguous.

Possessors bind to the head. Thus fierce his/anger and evil their/way; his/fierce anger would put possession on the modifier. When a possessive constituent is itself plural, the compiler's nested stem preserves that number. No competing outer cases are licensed.

## 4. The religious calques

The established modern dictionary already has religious vocabulary. The historical translator deliberately reaches for cumbersome older material and social images instead of consistently using those lexicalized modern terms:

| KJV expression | Historical accommodation | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| God / god | above-owner | Applies to an individual mariner's deity too; not exclusively Jonah's god. |
| LORD | name-lord | Proper divine title, distinct from lord as human rank. LORD God is one appositional subject. |
| pray | above-ask | Address toward a deity, without asserting literal height. |
| sacrifice | cut-gift | Keeps the material/transactional seam; adds no species or ceremonial details. |
| gracious | gift-good | Unspecified bestowed favor, without importing a developed doctrinal system. |
| merciful / mercy | harm-back | Compassionate withholding; their own mercy in 2:8 retains its difficult possessive. |
| soul | life-self | Inner embodied life; neither a new claim of immortality nor a silent substitution of throat. |
| hell in Jonah's poem | burial-inside | Deathward enclosed region; no added fire or moral punishment. |
| holy temple | apart-house | A building reserved for sacred use, not merely a random empty house. |
| God repented of evil | intention-back-turn concerning bad-event | Withdrawal of an intended calamity; not confession of divine sin. |

These are sense-scoped entries. Do not automatically apply burial-inside to every future occurrence of hell, or intention-back-turn to every human repent. Later books must confront their own meanings. The dissonance comes from the calques and constructions, not from silently rewriting theology to suit the translator.

## 5. Old counting words, exact quantities

Retain tren for three from 0.8/0.9. Retain tatus as the old stem in tatus’t, second, with an explicit ordinal marker. These are named historical alternatives, not changes to the modern three=venik or second=thātus’t entries. Numeral phrases use a bare count noun, giving three day and three night in the literal reading.

Jonah's forty days remains decimal 40. A source score is explicitly 20; six score thousand is 6 × 20 × 1000. More than sixscore thousand means **more than 120000**, not exactly that population and not a base-twelve reinterpretation. Every explicit numerical clause has an exact-value annotation. Unta document folios are a separate system and retain their approved chart values and rendering.

## 6. Commands, uncertainty and voice

Retain commands as commands, pleas as pleas, predictions as predictions, and the question who can tell as uncertainty. The king causes publication of a decree; the translator does not make the king personally perform each public announcement. Its third-person injunctions use the inherited optative command-reading in an explicit decree frame. Human and animal participation is retained, however strange.

The sailors could not bring the ship to land uses final negation of ability. May we not perish negates perishing within the desired outcome. Should I not spare Nineveh is a negative rhetorical question over the expectation to spare, not a positive obligation to destroy it. Mechanical negation substitution is forbidden.

The already established expressive enclitic ’hā remains available, rather than an unsupported exclamation glyph. Jonah's even unto death in 4:9 receives it for obstinate insistence. Most commands and prayers are unmarked: emotional subject matter is not a reason to append the same ending everywhere.

## 7. Concrete senses and deliberate unresolved readings

Fish as an animal is an explicit noun sense of the inherited fishing stem; the source does not say whale. Rowing is oar propulsion, distinct from row as a line. A fast is not-eating, distinct from speed. Cattle in 4:11 denotes domestic animals broadly; it is not narrowed to modern bovines. Right-hand direction receives luren rather than misusing lamet, moral/legal right. The gourd is shade-plant, with the source's plant identity retained without claiming a modern botanical species.

The supplied 1:15 says look up Jonah. The target uses lift/take up; this is an explicitly recorded contextual editorial reading, not a change to the English source. Three days' journey does not resolve the city's circumference versus traversal versus itinerary. In 2:8 lying vanities and their own mercy remain difficult. In 4:11 the people are not called children by the supplied wording. Keep these issues visible for literary review; compilation cannot settle them.

## 8. Freeze policy

Every completed book has its own language-versioned folder, source ledger, exact original-file hash, verse coverage, construction notes, compiler identity, Forge parent/head, page checks and FROZEN.json file hashes. A frozen book is excluded from comparison/consolidation migrations. Do not change its folder version to a later standard, regenerate its text, or promote this register to current.json. Future archive work copies and extends the register under a new version if necessary, keeping completed books attached to the version they actually use.

The hashes detect changes; they are not an operating-system prohibition on the owner's manual edits. Read a book in the Workbench normally. For experimentation, use Save As into a different, explicitly non-frozen working copy. Never silently replace the frozen original. A genuine transcription defect may be recorded as an erratum without rewriting the book. Any future corrected historical edition would require the user's explicit instruction and would retain the original.

The register and selected tests are versioned in Git. The supplied source, complete books, private Forge history and large editorial ledgers remain in the ignored Inputs/Saves directories, in accordance with the user's installation and privacy choices.
