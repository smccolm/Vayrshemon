# Historical KJV 0.10-kjv.38 · Collective passage, contested inheritance and a fatal word

## Israel's me, us and my need not collapse into one number

In11:17 the explicitly personified Israel asks permission for me to pass. Plural-I marks the many-member self. In11:19 the same named Israel asks let us pass into my place: singular-we marks the common action, while my-plural marks the destination claimed by the collective self. That difference follows the source's us/my alternation. Making every form either ordinary plural or undifferentiated singular would conceal its strange but useful construction.

Jephthah and the opposing king retain personal-I when asserting diplomatic claims over my land. They are named human speakers representing political interests; representation alone does not make all their first-person references plural-I. Their competing claims remain attributed to the speakers, without a translator's ruling on rightful ownership.

## Community commitments and individual witnesses

Israel's confessions and pleas in10:10,15 use singular-we and our-one deity. Gilead's elders offer a captaincy and community leadership using singular-we in11:6–9. The us in the witness-between-us formula11:10 comprises Jephthah and multiple elders, so it is plural-we; the following if-we-do-not clause is again the community's singular-we commitment. A legal relation between parties does not establish that there are only two individual speakers.

The brothers in11:2 speak of the father shared by multiple sons with of-plural-we. Their exclusion of Jephthah remains a contested kinship claim, not a deletion of the preceding statement that Gilead begat him. In12:5 the narration moves from plural fugitives to a representative singular applicant; the singular request and interrogation remain singular. No guessed number of brothers or guards is introduced.

## Refusal followed by grief is left unresolved

The deity's deliverance recollection and no-more-deliverance refusal use plural-I. His order to seek help from the chosen gods retains the taunt's actual grammatical agency. Israel then removes strange gods, serves the LORD, and his soul grieves over its misery. The explicit soul, distress and change in the narrative remain beside the refusal. The translation supplies neither a denial of the earlier statement nor a philosophical reconciliation.

The source's that-year and eighteen-years juxtaposition remains awkward. Named gods of Syria, Zidon, Moab, Ammon and the Philistines remain separately listed beside Baalim and Ashtaroth. No list is collapsed into a generic religious label. Chemosh giving land in11:24 is Jephthah's diplomatic argument and remains phrased as such, without adding a disclaimer inside the translated speech.

## Spirit, vow and outcome retain their sequence

The Spirit comes upon Jephthah before the vow. The translation does not say the Spirit commanded that vow, and victory does not acquire an extra clause certifying divine approval of its terms. The vow retains whatever comes from the doors to meet the returning speaker, shall be the LORD's, AND I will offer it as a burnt offering. It does not substitute or, animals only, a monetary payment or a temple-service exception.

The daughter is his only child, and the repeated neither-son-nor-daughter clause preserves that exclusivity. Her timbrels and dances remain beside the father's rending clothes and blaming speech. His cannot-go-back statement is his own claim; the narrator has not supplied a universal rule that promises can never be repudiated.

## The daughter's voice remains a voice

She says do to me according to what came from thy mouth, asks two months to wander the mountains with her companions, and laments her virginity. The translation retains these words and their conditional structure without adding a modern refusal, romanticizing consent or replacing virginity with a more comfortable general sorrow. Her individual-I remains distinct from the companions explicitly joined to her.

The father does according to the vow; the source does not narrate a method, and the translation adds none. Nor does it rewrite the fulfillment as lifelong non-sacrificial service. She knew no man remains a separate clause. The yearly four-day lament follows the supplied KJV wording rather than an unannounced alternative translation. The daughter remains unnamed.

## A sound contrast can be the content

Judges12:6 quotes Shibboleth and Sibboleth as different word-forms. The new lexical entries retain the contrasting initial sh/s using `ÛibboleÕ` and `sibboleÕ`; both Thimfora and Transliteration7 keep them distinct. They are quoted foreign forms, not newly invented Vayrshemon regional pronunciations. Replacing each with the same translated meaning would erase the test and break the narrative.

Pronounce is formed as mouth-speak, while correctly is true-way: matching the demanded articulation rather than moral righteousness, a right-side direction or a legal entitlement. The narration's inability claim is retained, not generalized into a real-world judgment of intelligence or linguistic worth. The killings and the number42000 remain the source account, without additional operational detail.

## Inheritance, names and old kinship senses

Tola, Jair, Elon and Abdon as judges have scoped identities distinct from earlier ancestors or cities of the same names. Puah the father is distinct from the Exodus midwife. Shamir in Ephraim and Aijalon in Zebulun are recorded without asserting that they are identical with earlier places. Gilead the named father differs from the country and ancestor, while local spelling Mizpeh remains beside Mizpah. Names do not silently force identities across the genealogy.

Nephews in12:14 is rendered with the historical lineal-descendant sense, child-child beside sons, rather than automatically importing modern sibling-sons. This sense is independently attested in [Dictionary.com, nephew, obsolete sense4](https://www.dictionary.com/browse/nephew). That lexical evidence supports the available older sense; the selection here is a contextual translation decision. Source English remains exact. Forty sons and thirty later-generation descendants do not become seventy biological sons.

## Precise quantities and concrete senses

Three separate thirty counts in10:4 remain sons, ass colts and cities. The three-hundred-year territorial claim remains Jephthah's claim without recalculation from an external chronology. Two months recur in the request, departure and return; four days yearly is a different interval. Ibzan's thirty daughters going out and thirty coming in retain their opposing directions. Ass-colt uses the inherited ass-offspring entry, not the horse-colt noun.

Defend Israel is rendered save/preserve in context; no new military apparatus is implied. Passing through the country uses boundaries, not assumed seacoasts, and Jordan passages use the existing ford/crossing-place term rather than a textual passage. Driving out expresses expulsion. Troublesome action uses vex, avoiding the noun that only names the resulting distress. Tribute, offices and kinship retain their historical referents without modern administrative substitutions.
