# Luke17–18:shared obligation and uneven first-person voices

Eighty new verses continue the frozen KJV register. Earlier targets,paradigms and seals remain unchanged. This is translator self-review,not independent literary certification;the complete Bible deliverable is still pending.

## Plural-self does not replace every first person

The quoted repentant brother,the imagined servant's owner,the unjust judge,the praying Pharisee,the publican,the ruler and the blind petitioner use personal-I. The teacher's framing I-trow-not in17:9 returns to plural-I after the owner's quotation. Direct Jesus speech uses plural-I where an explicit I/me occurs,including the object in18:19:why call I-many good? The reply that none is good except one,God,remains beside it. The translation adds no numerical identity equation and does not soften the question.

The apostles' our-faith and the servants' our-duty use the singular-we possessive. The ten lepers' shared appeal,the followers represented by Peter,and the travelling company including Jesus use singular-we. Ten lepers and twelve companions remain numerically explicit;grammatical singular-we does not erase them. In18:41 the alternating speaker boundary is visible:what shall I-many do for you;that I-personal receive sight. Neither shared identity nor prayer licenses changing the petitioner's pronoun.

## Repeated obligations and persistent petition

Seven offences and seven returns within a day remain two repeated quantities,not forty-nine. The servant comparison retains its uncomfortable demand and unprofitable self-description. It is represented with existing negative-plus-profitable vocabulary,not an invented modern employment principle. The judge does not become compassionate:his private calculation is that the widow troubles him and may wear him out. God bearing long with the elect remains a separate comparison,not an assertion that God shares the judge's injustice. The concluding question about faith on earth stays interrogative.

The two prayers preserve their contrasting personal-I. The Pharisee's twice-weekly fast and all-possession tithes are attributed claims. The publican stands afar,does not raise eyes,and strikes his breast. Neither has an invented subsequent biography. Justified-rather-than and exalted/humbled reversals remain.

## Source boundaries and difficult images

Sycamine is a new source-label loan distinct from the inherited sycomore. No modern species identification is imposed. The kingdom-within-you wording follows the supplied KJV while its inward-versus-among interpretation is left open in the note. Body and eagles remain the supplied final image,without silently substituting corpse and vultures. The comparisons with Noe and Lot keep ordinary activities and destruction without inventing dates or additional sins.

The source's two men in one bed,two women grinding,and two men in a field all remain,including17:36. One taken and the other left does not acquire an added declaration about which outcome is salvation. The wife-of-Lot reminder remains short;the salt event is not imported into its text. Infants and children are retained as given. The ruler's wealth demand is all,not a partial donation. Luke's manyfold stays unnumbered rather than becoming another Gospel's hundredfold.

The approach to Jericho stays near-to,not leaving. The blind man stays unnamed;Bartimaeus is not imported. The twelve's non-understanding is repeated in all three clauses. Saved,whole and cleansed remain distinct wording in their respective verses. Exclamatory morphology carries emphatic warnings and cries without introducing an unsupported exclamation glyph.

## Delta and checks

One common noun added:sycamine. No proper names,inflectional endings or possessive aliases added. Existing biblical Peter,Noe,Lot,Samaritan,leprosy,flood-ark and livestock senses are reused rather than conflating them with play characters or unrelated objects. All80verses have source-critical anchors;number sequences,personal/plural-self/collective references and Maxima compile separately. Approved four-column dimensions,font sizes,38ptleading,paragraph alignment and Unta numbering are preserved.
