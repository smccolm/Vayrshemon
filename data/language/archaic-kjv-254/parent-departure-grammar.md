# Historical KJV register 0.10-kjv.253 — 1Thessalonians1–5

## A ministry speaking as one without deleting its people

The three named senders speak as `wesg`,one acting ministry whole. The text still explicitly calls them plural men1:5,has plural hearts2:4,and plural own life-selves2:8. Our-one is therefore not a claim that the participants have one biological heart or one undifferentiated interior life. It is the alien language's shared-agent grammatical stance. The bodily/personal activities are not mechanically singularized in number.

Personal-I remains explicit in2:18's even-I-Paul,3:5's sending after he can no longer forbear,4:9 and4:13's writing/explanation,5:1,5:23's prayer,and5:27's charge. We-alone-at-Athens3:1 is kept beside personal-I3:5 rather than harmonized through a reconstructed itinerary. No physical dual is inserted for a three-sender group,and no plural-I is manufactured where the source contains no explicit shared-self claim. Communal we-live3:8 and live-together-with-him5:10 remain `wesg`,not personal plural-self.

## Maternal and paternal care, toil and emotional force

Gentle2:7 follows the supplied reading. The nurse cherishes her own children,and the next paternal comparison has a father addressing his children. These are separate maternal and paternal analogies rather than a literal merged parent biography. Affectionate desire to share not only good news but own plural life-selves remains intimate and awkward. The same ministry can be one acting whole and contain multiple embodied lives.

Travail2:9 is burdensome toil;travail5:3 is childbirth labour. Allowed2:4 means approved for trust,not mere permission. Exhortation,comfort,warning and command retain different functions. Bodily separation but not heart separation2:17 and conditional we-live-if-you-stand3:8 retain the source's emotional intensity without medical or clinical claims. All reported claims of holy/right/blameless conduct remain the author's testimony rather than translator certification.

## Historical accusation and moral instruction remain attributed

The hostile collective accusations in2:14–16 are preserved as the source voice,including own countrymen,Jews,prophets,persecution,opposition and wrath. They are neither sanitized nor expanded into present-day allegations or universal claims about later people. No invented historical catastrophe is inserted as the wrath's referent. Named Satan in2:18 and the tempter role in3:5 are kept distinct in form without creating a new biography.

Sanctification,fornication and moral uncleanness retain their historical categories. His-vessel4:4 remains the difficult container image rather than being silently decided as body or wife. Defraud in any affair4:6 keeps the unspecified affair;matter is not physical substance. Quiet-study4:11 means earnestly aim at quiet conduct,not academic study. Honest-walking4:12 is honourable conduct. The source's given-to-us Spirit4:8 remains communal us,not another witness's you.

## Arrival, sleepers and differentiated pastoral care

Prevent4:15 means go before:the living do not precede the sleeping dead. First4:16 and then4:17 preserve the explicit order;no later chronological chart,date or named doctrine is supplied. The Lord descends with shout,archangel voice and divine trumpet. Archangel remains an unnamed chief-angel designation,not an inserted personal name or equation with the Lord. Together with them retains the rejoined community. Sleep shifts from the death image4:13–17 to inattentiveness5:6 and ordinary sleep5:7;wake-or-sleep5:10 keeps its layered wording.

Unruly persons receive warning;fainthearted persons receive comfort;weak persons receive support;all receive patience. Feebleminded5:14 is fainthearted,not a claim of intellectual disability. Soberness includes composed self-command beside the explicit drinking/night contrast. Prove5:21 is test,fast is firm,and quench5:19 preserves fire imagery applied to Spirit. All appearance of evil5:22 retains the source ambiguity of manifestation/form without narrowing it to reputation alone. Whole spirit/life-self/body5:23 retains the three nouns without inserting a numeral or imposing an exhaustive metaphysical scheme.

## Lexicon, coverage and frozen continuity

Three common additions: archangel,unruly,and fainthearted. Existing Silvanus,Timotheus,Thessalonian,Macedonia,Achaia,Philippi,Judaea and Athens forms are reused;no new names or inflectional endings. Once-and-again2:18 remains repetition without an exact total of two;first4:16 is the only other explicit counted/ordinal expression. Reciprocal one-another and every-one-of-you remain relational/distributive rather than new cardinals.
