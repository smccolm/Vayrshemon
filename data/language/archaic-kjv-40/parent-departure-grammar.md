## A real pair gets dual-we

Manoah and his wife provide an explicit two-person context. The plea for instruction, the hospitality request, their prospective honour, Manoah's fear of dying and his wife's counterargument use dual-we. Their hands are expressed through of-dual-we, without adding a new possessive paradigm. Number belongs to the participating people, not merely to the fact that two grammatical roles exist.

The wife first receives the announcement and receives the second visit while her husband is absent. Her personal-I report is not replaced by a collective voice that makes him a witness before he arrives. The following named pair is dual; her individual experience remains singular. The three later travellers, Samson and both parents, are not casually assigned the same dual form.

## Messenger-I is not automatically divine plural-I

In13:11 the visitor answers I am to a question asking whether he is the man who spoke to the woman. This is personal identification. He says what I told her, refuses to eat, and directs a burnt offering to the LORD in the third person. Those clauses use the messenger's personal-I. Unlike the earlier visitor claiming the covenant-maker's deeds, this speech does not explicitly claim the divine collective self.

Manoah later says the pair saw God; the narrator continues to call the visitor an angel. Both remain. The translation does not force every angel into a different ontology, nor collapse every I-am phrase into one divine-name assertion. This makes the eventual divine identity passages more meaningful than a blanket plural-I replacement. No clinical description or unprinted Trinity count is introduced.

## The woman's argument has a counterfactual structure

Manoah predicts certain death, while his wife argues that a LORD pleased to kill them would not have accepted offerings, shown the signs and told them these things. Conditional past-perfect forms preserve the rejected consequences. All three pieces of evidence remain. The pleasure verb expresses disposition or satisfaction; it is not the unrelated courtesy particle used in a request.

Secret remains the supplied description of the name; no other edition's wonderful is silently substituted there. The narrator's wondrous action remains in its own clause. The child will begin deliverance, not necessarily complete it. The reported lifetime Nazarite endpoint and the later intermittent movement of the Spirit are kept where the source puts them.

## Human desire and hidden agency coexist

Samson's personal-I asks for a woman who pleases him. His parents' objection and their unawareness of the claimed divine purpose remain separate. The he who seeks an occasion in14:4 stays a source pronoun rather than receiving an added disambiguating proper name. The Spirit accompanies the lion episode and the later killings; the translation does not remove that uncomfortable causal framing.

The lion carcass, bees, honey, shared food and withheld origin remain literal. No zoological repair or religious-exemption explanation is inserted. The father's later explanation for giving the wife to the companion is his claim, and the proposed younger sister is his offer, not an invented agreement by either woman. Samson's more-blameless claim stays self-justification rather than narrator verdict.

## Riddle, coercion and repeated time

The riddle keeps eater versus food and strong versus sweetness. Its repeated came-forth wording remains. No fabricated native pun is presented as an exact equivalent of a source-language sound play. Thirty linen sheets and thirty changes of garments are separate stakes on each side. Sheet is a historical linen cloth or wrapping sense without a confidently reconstructed garment pattern or modern bedlinen assumption.

Three days without a solution, the seventh-day threat, seven days of weeping and seventh-day disclosure all remain. The threat to burn the woman and her father's house is explicit, so the disclosure is not recast as a harmless party trick. The later actual burning names her and her father. The heifer-ploughing accusation remains Samson's degrading metaphor, not the translator's neutral description of the woman.

## The polity, the delegation and the spectators

Judah asks why the Philistines came against singular-we, and the Philistines answer as a different corporate singular-we seeking retaliation. Judah's subjected community remains singular-we in15:11. The actual men sent to bind and hand over Samson use plural-we for their own undertaking in15:12–13, including the promise not to kill him themselves.

The lords' promise in16:5 is explicitly each one of plural-we paying1100 pieces. It is not a single collective1100 or an invented5500total. Dagon's worshippers claim our-one deity delivered our-one enemy and destroyer of our-one country, but many of us counts individual victims and uses plural-we. The people asking for sport are plural spectators; the translation does not invent what precise performance they demand.

## Three deceptions, one disclosure and a failed expectation

Green undried withs, unused new ropes and seven hairlocks woven into a web remain three distinct episodes. The third response ends at the source's if-clause, without importing a missing consequent from another edition. Men lying in wait are distinguished from the falsehoods Delilah accuses Samson of telling. Her quotation I love thee reports his claimed profession; the surrounding first-person complaints are hers.

The final disclosure links his Nazarite status and uncut hair to his claimed strength. A called man performs the shaving at her direction; the translation does not silently make her the shaver. Samson expects to shake himself free but does not know the LORD has departed. That expectation is not translated as a successful escape. Hair regrowth is reported without adding immediate full restoration of strength.

## Historical concrete senses and restrained literalness

New entries distinguish hairlocks from door locks, harvest shocks from impacts, tow fibre from towing, firmly from quickly or fasting, and physical leaning from trust or thinness. Withs are flexible binding material without an unsupported exact botanical or sinew identification. Firebrand, jawbone and sheaf-bundle are transparent compounds. Ass jaw, the hollow place in the jaw, Lehi and Enhakkore remain in their stated relationships without a modern geological reconstruction.

The terse blinding, confinement, grinding and final collapse retain the source actions without added methods or construction details. About3000 on the roof is not declared the entire building population. The two eyes, two pillars and right/left hand allocation remain exact. The final comparative death claim is not converted into an invented numerical total, and the repeated twenty-year judgeship is not summed into forty.
