A third-person royal reference does not automatically identify every earlier I as a king or license a plural-I replacement. Psalm71's life-course testimony likewise remains individual: mother, womb, youth, failing strength, grey head and future-generation teaching belong to one aging speaker. ## Numerical fidelity and difficult rhetorical forms

Psalm62:11has one speaking and two hearings. Their inequality is preserved without invented explanation. Psalm68:17has twenty-thousand chariots and plural thousands of angels; the second phrase has an explicit thousand-unit but no exact total. The numeric audit records that unit without claiming precisely1000angels. Handful in72:16is an unfixed bodily measure, while more-than-hairs in69:4and unknowable numbers in71:15remain uncounted. Generic one and the Holy One designation do not add a headcount. Conditional hopes and hostile optatives remain grammatically marked:69:22uses a perfect infinitive with should for intended welfare, and69:27's request to add iniquity is not rewritten as forgiveness or mere accounting. The terrible divine acts, bloody images, swallowed soul and pit-mouth retain their source discomfort without editorial endorsement, modern diagnosis or an invented procedure.
