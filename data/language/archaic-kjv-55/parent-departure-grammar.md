# KJV historical register 0.10-kjv.54 — royal song, roster and responsibility

This continuation adds115 individually authored verses,2Samuel22–24, completing all695 verses of second Samuel. The Bible now has8,766 of31,102 authored verses;22,336 remain. Completing this section is not completion of the requested whole Bible in one `.vay`. Parent0.10-kjv.53, all previous corrected targets and frozen Jonah remain unchanged. This historical edition is permanently excluded from modernization. Reviews are translator self-review, not independent certification.

Sixty-nine lexical records are added: sixty proper-name/identity records and nine common senses. There are no new inflectional endings or possessive aliases. The deliberately archaic register develops through contextual voice, metaphor, heavy relations and explicit senses rather than arbitrary replacement of pronouns.

## 1. Personal rescue and the royal body in the song

The song begins with personal `i` in fear, trust, petition, rescue and moral claims. Bodily running and leaping in22:30 likewise remain personal feats. At22:38–45 and48, `ipl` and `mypl` represent the royal speaker through his fighting and ruling body: pursuing enemies, receiving their necks, subduing people and receiving strangers' submission. This is commanding agency, not an excuse that somebody else did the acts.

The rescued personal self returns in22:49 and performs thanksgiving in22:50. Within22:44 the unknown people are personally unknown to David but will serve his royal plural-self. The shift is motivated by the relation expressed, not by a cycle that alternates forms to avoid repetition. Repeated images and claims in poetry are retained when the source makes them structural.

## 2. The singular whole and divine speech have local boundaries

**Our God** in22:32 uses `ourone`, the worshipping body's shared relationship. It is not a count of gods. In23:2 the LORD's Spirit speaks through David and the word is in his tongue. Those source expressions are preserved without inserting a named Trinity or deciding a number of divine persons. The parallel divine titles in the song do not manufacture additional speakers.

The direct oracle in24:12 uses divine `ipl` for offering and executing one of three alternatives. Three counts alternatives, not divine identities. Gad's following **what answer I shall return** is personal `i`, with the quotation boundary restoring the messenger's referent. David's response in24:14 moves from personal anguish to `wesg` falling into the LORD's hand and back to personal fear of man's hand. The choice bears a community while the distress remains his own.

## 3. Moral claims, incitement and confession are not harmonized

The song's claims of righteousness, clean hands, uprightness and fidelity remain the singer's personal claims beside the narrative of wrongdoing. My iniquity remains in22:24. No added confession, legal justification or doctrine makes the tension disappear. The reciprocal address to the merciful, upright, pure and froward retains its difficult unsavoury response.

The LORD moves David to number Israel in the supplied24:1. Another book's Satan wording does not replace this agent. David then says **I have sinned** personally in24:10 and17. His responsibility is not diluted by a plural-self confession. The LORD's repentance of the evil in24:16 is retained as written. David's sheep question protests the people's suffering and offers himself and his father's house; the victims are not made to confess or assigned an unstated guilt.

## 4. Images remain strange without becoming accidental word-sense errors

The song keeps rock, fortress, horn, tower, refuge, waves, snares, temple, nostrils, mouth, fire, dark pavilions, wings of wind, exposed foundations, deer feet, steel bow, gentleness, necks, dust and mire. It neither reduces them to generic protection nor turns them into an anatomical, meteorological or military reconstruction. The darkness and brightness both remain. The supplied **was seen** upon the wind's wings, **steel** bow **broken**, and **tower** of salvation are not replaced by alternative-witness wording.

Prevented is rendered as coming before/confronting, not a modern stopping action. Stay in22:19 is support, while the command to the angel in24:16 is stop the hand. Discovered foundations are uncovered. Hinds are female deer; close places are enclosed, not nearby. Shield uses the explicit defensive noun, while shielding the plot and being fenced with iron use the protective verb. The older burial-inside `kjv_hell` in22:6 avoids silently importing the inherited fiery-punishment sense into the deathward song image.

## 5. Nine common lexical additions and cross-checks

`kjv_sea_channel` is water-way; `kjv_cleanness` is pure-state; `kjv_froward` is contrary-heart; `kjv_unsavoury` is taste-bad in figurative dealing; `kjv_ungodly` is god-disdain as the source's polemical label. The last does not endorse a judgment of actual nonbelievers. `kjv_pavilion` records a shelter sense sharing tent. `kjv_psalm` is sacred-song. `kjv_temple_sanctuary` explicitly distinguishes the inherited temple form's sanctuary sense from the side of a head and does not assert that Solomon's building already exists. `kjv_goodly` is see-great, notable appearance without an invented measurement or moral endorsement.

Historical English cross-checks support opposed/disagreeable **froward** ([Merriam-Webster](https://scrabble.m-w.com/grammar/words-in-disguise-do-these-seem-familiar)), figuratively unpleasing **unsavory** ([Webster1828 transcription](https://webstersdictionary1828.com/Dictionary/unsavory)), and appearance/size rather than moral goodness for **goodly** ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/goodly), [Webster1828 transcription](https://webstersdictionary1828.com/Dictionary/goodly)). These are lexical checks, not replacements for the supplied KJV. The exact contextual calques remain authored translation choices.

## 6. Roster identity is evidence-based and sometimes unresolved

Forty-two newly written source forms cover the roster's designations and the census/altar places. Eighteen further identity records share earlier surface forms. Eleazar son of Dodo is not the priest. Shammah's Harodite and Hararite designations remain distinguishable, and the bare Hararite entry neither proves nor disproves identity with Shammah son of Agee. Ira the Tekoite and Ira the Ithrite remain distinguishable from the Jairite official. Benaiah the Pirathonite and Ittai son of Ribai are not silently merged with the earlier commanders.

The sons-of-Jashen/Jonathan fragment and the nested Eliphelet/Ahasbai/Maachathite relation remain awkward as supplied. Eliam son of Ahithophel is recorded without asserting or denying identity with Bathsheba's father. Nathan of Zobah is not automatically the prophet or David's son. Elhanan's Dodo parentage remains distinguishable from Jaareoregim in21:19. Shared form does not prove shared identity; a distinct record is not by itself proof of distinct people.

The source's Tachmonite/Adino construction, Hezrai, Mebunnai, Anethothite and other spellings remain unnormalized. The difficult Tahtimhodshi and Danjaan remain without speculative geographic emendation. Uriah closes the roster without erasing his earlier death.

## 7. Water, blood, royal giving and costly offering

David's wish for Bethlehem water is personal and optative, with grammatical exclamation. It is not silently converted into an order to risk lives. The three bring water, David refuses and pours it to the LORD, calling it the blood of men who endangered their lives. The metaphor does not mean that they actually died or bled into it.

Araunah's gift is described **as a king** to the king, without inventing a second contemporary monarchy or deleting the phrase. David personally insists on paying for what he offers. The purchase's stated fifty silver shekels covers the floor and oxen as supplied, without importing another account's amount, metal or extent. The final altar, offerings and cessation remain in sequence without an added causal theory.

## 8. Exact counts and deliberately unresolved ranks

The roster preserves eight hundred killed at one time, three from thirty, Abishai's three hundred, two lionlike men versus a separate actual lion, and thirty-seven in all. The several threes and the first three remain distinguished as the text distinguishes them; no invented roster equation repairs the ranks. Historical `kjv_three` is used consistently within compounds as well as alone.

The hundredfold increase is Joab's wish, not a census result. The journey lasts nine months and twenty days. Israel's800000 and Judah's500000 remain separate. The offered penalties remain seven years of famine, three months of pursuit and three days of pestilence. Seven is not replaced by another witness's three. Seventy thousand die; the end time remains the appointed time without an invented exact hour or assurance that all three offered days elapsed.
