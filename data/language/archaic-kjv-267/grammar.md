# Historical KJV 0.10-kjv.267 — 1 John

All 105 verses are authored here. Sequential publication and native verification would bring coverage to 30,646/31,102,leaving 456. The complete deliverable remains one Bible in one .vay;drafts and source indexing do not count as verified translation. Earlier historical targets and seals remain unchanged and permanently excluded from modernization. Translator self-review and structural validation are not independent literary certification.

## A shared witnessing self

The opening witnesses speak with singular-we while their eyes and hands remain plural. Hearing,seeing,looking and touching stay separately expressed. This communal self receives life,keeps fellowship,confesses sins,knows,asks and loves. The source deliberately shifts between our hearts in3:19 and our heart in3:20–21;both grammatical numbers remain under the communal possessive. Our lives in3:16 stays plural. Reflexive selves and each-other reciprocity do not become physical duals.

The writer's I in the repeated writing formulas is personal. The ordinary claimants saying I know him in2:4 and I love God in4:20 likewise use personal-I. No first-person divine quotation occurs in this letter,so no automatic plural-I is injected into its third-person claims. The language's plural-I remains available from earlier divine voices. Father,Son,Word,Spirit,witnessing community and recipients retain their source discourse roles.

## Three are one;three agree in one

The supplied KJV 5:7 explicitly has three witnesses in heaven,the Father,the Word and Holy Ghost,and says these three are one. All three names and all numerical statements are translated. Holy Ghost uses the established holy-Spirit wording,not a haunting ghost. Verse5:8 has the earthly Spirit,water and blood and says these three agree in one. Its agreeing-in-one is not flattened into the preceding being-one. Both verses retain three twice and one once,expressed in the existing numerical system.

These are source assertions,not newly added trinitarian arithmetic. Singular-we is a shared speaking identity,not a substitute for translating an explicit third-person number. No medical diagnosis is attached to divine or communal plurality. The awkward coexistence of unity,different roles and indwelling is deliberate. The demonstrative this in5:20 remains without a translator-inserted resolution of its antecedent.

## Fellowship,confession and inability

Fellowship uses the established reciprocal-companionship term,with Father and with Son both retained. Abiding and dwelling preserve repeated in-relations,including he in him and God in him. These do not become a polished modern spiritual paraphrase. God's love keeps its broad genitive relation where the source leaves direction open.

The conditions concerning saying we have no sin in1:8 and saying we have not sinned in1:10 remain grammatically distinct. Confession,forgiveness and cleansing stay separate. The advocate-with-Father in2:1 follows an explicit if-anyone-sin,despite the stated aim of not sinning. Later3:6 and3:9 retain the unqualified does-not-sin and cannot-sin wording. No habitually,truly-except or other unprinted qualifier is added to harmonize the tension. Seed in3:9 remains generative imagery without a modern genetic explanation.

The new advocate term is behalf-speak-person,with Jesus Christ named in that role. Propitiation reuses the earlier mercy-cover term,without importing a later atonement system. Whole-world sins in2:2 remains broad. God-is-love is not reversed into every-love-is-God. No-person-ever-saw-God in4:12 is not rewritten to reconcile another book's seeing account.

## Old and new command;repeated address

The old command and new command in2:7–8 stand together. Printed Brethren and both from-beginning phrases in2:7 remain. The repeated present I-write and perfect I-have-written to fathers,young men and little children in2:12–14 remain;source rhetoric is not randomly varied away. The wicked one is a descriptive personal designation,not a cardinal quantity. Holy One likewise remains a title.

Antichrist gains against-Christ as a contextual noun,used singular and plural as supplied. No modern figure or institution is assigned to it. Unction/anointing receives a contextual noun on the inherited anoint form:received,abiding and teaching,not medicine or an invented physical substance. The printed all-things in2:20,shall-abide in2:27 and both halves of2:23 remain. Seducing in2:26 means deceiving,not an automatic sexual sense. World keeps contextual moral scope,without equating every occurrence with physical matter.

## Embodied affection and practical love

The goods of3:17 are resources,and bowels of compassion reuses the Philemon inward-affection sense. Bodily contact in1:1 uses touch rather than the inherited handle's unrelated sense. Deceptive lying uses kjv_lie_false;5:19 alone uses situated lie,in wickedness. First love in4:19 keeps the priority and printed him. Hatred/murder and child-of-devil claims remain the letter's rhetoric,not assigned to unprinted present groups or clinical categories.

The love-of-God wording in3:16,our sins in3:5,the full Jesus-Christ-come-in-flesh denial in4:3 and only-begotten in4:9 remain. In3:18 speech,word and tongue contrast with deed and truth;the instruction retains its communal hortative. Confidence in3:21 and5:14 differs from boldness in4:17. Torment in4:18 stays source wording without a claim about a reader's mental health.

## Requests,death-sin and final assurance

Petitions in5:15 are requests,not necessarily written petitions. The by-his-will condition in5:14 remains. Verse5:16 leaves the he-giver pronoun unexpanded;not saying to pray is not rewritten as forbidding prayer. No list of death-sins is invented. Verse5:18 retains keeps-himself,not a different he-keeps-him reading. Verse5:19 retains lies-in-wickedness rather than another wording about the evil one's power. The second belief-purpose in5:13 and final Amen remain.

Three common lexical additions:advocate,antichrist,and contextual unction/anointing. No proper names or inflectional paradigms added.
