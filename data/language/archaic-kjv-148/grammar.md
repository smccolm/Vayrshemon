## Claimed divine selfhood does not certify a claim

Divine speech uses plural-I and my-plural,including the contested speech attributed to Hananiah in28:2–4and28:11. The grammar signals the identity he claims to voice,not the narrator's certification that God sent him.28:14and28:16then carry the responding divine plural-I;28:15explicitly denies the rival commission. The same grammatical person can occur in rival claims without making the claims equivalent or blending their speakers.

## Individual messengers and a corporate judging voice

25:3,15,17and26:12,14,15retain the individual prophet's personal-I.27:2,12,16and28:1,7,8also locate an individual bodily recipient or speaker.26:16and26:19use singular-we for the assembled civic judgment,with our-one God and our-one souls. The plural possessed souls do not become one body;communal unity belongs to the grammatical possessor. No human dual or numerically specified divine group is introduced in these chapters.

## Optative assent can precede a challenge

28:6keeps Amen and the wishes that the LORD do so and perform Hananiah's words. Optative clauses distinguish the desired result from a certified prediction.28:7's nevertheless then opens the public test.28:9does not ban peaceful prophecy;it makes fulfilled speech the condition of recognition. The translation does not infer sarcasm,private anger or assent from Jeremiah's departure28:11.

## Material restraints and an exploited servant

Binding-restraint is bind-thing,a physical object beside the yoke27:2,rather than the inherited relational bond. Wood broken by Hananiah28:10contrasts with iron28:13/14. The supplied thou-shalt-make28:13stays second person,not an editorial I-will-make.27:6retains the foreign king as my-plural servant and the gift of field beasts.25:14and27:7use causative serve for serve-themselves-of-them/him:the later kings compel service rather than becoming the defeated ruler's servants.

## Forensic and agricultural senses

Litigate is judgment-contend,used as both the controversy noun and the pleading verb25:31. It is separate from the existing plea supporting a request;royal court is likewise distinguished from the temple's enclosed court26:2. Till-land is land-work27:11,without narrowing cultivation to a single tool or crop.27:19uses the established sea-basin sense for the temple furnishing. Light25:10is genuinely candle illumination;principal25:34–36is chief rank,not money.

## Names do not repair chronology

Tyrus is preserved beside Tyre. Dedan,Tema,Buz and Zimri receive contextual group/place identifiers rather than being merged with earlier genealogical people. Sheshach remains the printed name,not an inserted cipher solution. Micah the Morasthite,Urijah son of Shemaiah,Elnathan son of Achbor and Hananiah son of Azur have local identities.27:1's Jehoiakim heading remains beside27:3's Zedekiah and28:1's Zedekiah chronology.
