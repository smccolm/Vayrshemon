# Romans 1–3: a shared argument and an adversarial I

These 92 drafted verses begin Romans. Projected authored coverage is 28,023 of 31,102, leaving 3,079. Draft coverage is not sealed native coverage or a completed combined Bible. Earlier targets and seals remain unchanged and permanently excluded from modernization. Structural validation and translator self-review are not independent literary certification.

## Personal author, imagined objector and communal we

Paul's I in the opening thanks, prayer, intended visit, obligation and readiness remains personal. My God, my spirit and my prayers belong to that authorial voice. Through Jesus Christ is a mediating relation, not a reason to turn the author's I into divine plural-self. Our Lord and our Father are possessions of a community represented as singular-whole. The we receiving grace and apostleship in 1:5 presents a shared commission without supplying its membership count.

The argument-we in 2:2 and 3:5/9/19/28/31 speaks as a deliberating whole. The our unrighteousness in 3:5 belongs to that rhetorical community. In 3:8, we are slanderously reported and we allegedly say frame the quoted let-us-do-evil proposal. The singular-we inside that proposal is reported speech: the translation does not turn it into the author's ethical instruction.

The personal I-speak-as-a-man in 3:5 marks an aside. The I and my falsehood in 3:7 voice an objection within the argument; they are not a confession of some invented event in Paul's biography. I-say in 3:26 remains the supplied explanatory aside. No divine first-person pronoun occurs in these chapters, so no plural-I is inserted merely because God, Son and spirit are mentioned. The divine addressee in 3:4 is second person. This restraint preserves the force of plural-self when actual divine I-speech returns elsewhere.

## Number and identity are different grammatical jobs

One God in 3:30 is an explicit numerical assertion. It is retained beside the distinct first-person number classes elsewhere in this historical language; neither is silently converted into the other. The two no-not-one refrains in 3:10/12 also retain cardinal one. Every one in 1:16 is distributive person, not a head count. Both in 1:12/14 and 3:9 joins categories or parties, not a concrete two-person referent that would trigger physical dual.

Four-footed beasts in 1:23 assigns four to the feet of the animal class, not to a group of four animals. First in the opening discourse and Jew-first ordering retains its local function. There are no invented population counts or numerical arguments about the Trinity.

## A foreign theology should remain foreign

The introduction keeps his Son, our Lord, made of David's seed according to flesh, and declared Son of God with power according to spirit of holiness. Declared is not changed to became; spirit of holiness is not automatically replaced by a proper Holy Spirit title. The established divine-condition word renders Godhead in 1:20 without adding a count of persons.

From faith to faith remains the repeated relation in 1:17. Faith of God in 3:3 and faith of Jesus Christ in 3:22 keep their supplied genitives. The translation does not decide these relations by silently substituting faithfulness or faith in. Both to all and upon all remain in 3:22. Without the deeds of the law in 3:28 does not acquire an unprinted alone.

Propitiation receives the deliberately alien mercy-cover construction. It names the provision set forth in relation to faith and blood without resolving a later atonement theory, claiming a literal textile, or substituting the ark cover for the person. Remission is sin-release, with past sins and divine forbearance separately stated. Redemption retains the established release vocabulary without inventing a payee or transaction price. Freely-as-gift distinguishes gratuitousness from merely being physically unconstrained.

## Leave the argument's difficult joints visible

The doers shall be justified in 2:13 remains beside no flesh justified by law-deeds in 3:20. Without law in 3:21 remains beside witness by law and prophets. The final we-establish-law in 3:31 remains beside the rejection of law-deeds as the basis named in 3:28. These are the source's argumentative joints; the translator does not repair them by inserting a harmonizing commentary into the text.

The second-person turn in 2:1 challenges the judging person doing the same things. Its questions about theft, adultery and sacrilege remain questions, not newly certified incidents. Jewish identity, circumcision and inward/outward contrasts stay within that argument. They are not expanded into a modern census rule, an instruction against a present-day community or a replacement of all Jewish traditions by a single slogan.

Letter in 2:27/29 is written-law, not correspondence or an alphabetic glyph. Heart circumcision retains its awkward bodily metaphor. The spirit contrast is left as supplied rather than automatically assigned to a named divine person. Respect of persons means partiality in judgment, not absence of ordinary respectful regard. Counted circumcision is reckoned standing, not numerical counting.

## Preserve source judgments without supplying new ones

Romans 1's moral catalogue and sexual descriptions remain the historical author's claims. The translation preserves the source categories, the women/men distinction, passions, natural-use relation, exchanged relation, shame and recompense. It adds no modern orientation category, clinical diagnosis, named disease or unprinted act description. The newly recorded sexual-use sense retains the euphemism without making a person a tool in an unrelated ordinary-use construction.

Reprobate is disapproved or rejected in the mind-description, not a clinical category or an inserted doctrine of predestination. Despiteful is insolently injurious, not the concessive word despite. Implacable remains in the supplied list; fornication also remains where printed. Debate is strife here. Whisperers, backbiters, haters, boasters and inventors remain separate list members. Familial natural affection in 1:31 differs from the passions in 1:26.

Falsehood uses the explicit false-speaking root, never the bodily lie root. Incorruptible/corruptible uses the decay contrast rather than moral corruption of an animal. Bodily uncleanness here is the source moral impurity claim, not a rewritten material ritual condition. The letter's condemnation is retained without adding a punishment procedure or treating the translator as a present-day accuser.

## Emphatic refusal without an exclamation glyph

God forbid in 3:4/6/31 uses the existing hortative and exclamatory morphology. This preserves a forceful refusal without asking Thimfora to render an unsupported exclamation mark. It does not add a new inflectional ending. The abrupt questions and brief answers in 3:27/29 remain abrupt; their discomfort belongs to the archaic register rather than being smoothed into explanatory prose.

Several explicitly share an existing surface form while distinguishing contextual senses; they are not falsely counted as ten unrelated phonological roots. No new proper names are needed. Existing names retain their established forms and Maxima.
