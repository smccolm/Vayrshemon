# Historical KJV register0.10-kjv.180 · Nahum1–3

All47Nahum verses are newly authored after179. Projected coverage after verification is22,732of31,102;8,370remain. The deliverable remains ONE complete Bible in ONE`.vay`. Four contextual names are added;common vocabulary,inflectional paradigms and earlier targets remain unchanged. Frozen Jonah remains unchanged despite the new Nineveh oracle. This is translator self-review,not independent literary certification.

## Divine plural-I and the observing personal-I

The divine threats and release promises1:12–14,2:13and3:5–6use plural-I. The oracle's third-person introductions are retained rather than made first person everywhere. The question3:7about seeking comforters uses personal-I as an explicit interpretive choice for the observing prophetic voice;the source gives no named speaker transition there. No communal singular-we,dual speaker or invented number of divine persons is introduced. This morphology marks discourse role,not a clinical category.

## Repeated vengeance and unsoftened destruction

The two revenge assertions1:2remain repeated,and reserving wrath means keeping wrath. Judicial acquittal1:3is distinct from physical cleanness. Clouds as feet-dust,earth burned1:5and firelike poured fury1:6retain their source images. The stronghold1:7does not become an added guarantee of every individual's safety. Second time1:9is ordinal under negation. The thorn-folding1:10means entanglement,not multiplication;one1:11means a person,not a cardinal.

## Quiet multitudes,changing possessors and announced peace

Quiet and many1:12remain beside the unnamed he who passes through. Divine plural-I admits having afflicted the addressee before promising no more affliction. His yoke and thy bonds1:13remain distinct possessors. Sowing thy name1:14is retained;gods remains plural. Peace-announcement1:15uses the established exclamatory morphology because the font has no exclamation glyph. Feasts,vows and the absolute cutting-off promise remain. The wicked is not renamed Satan.

## Siege language and printed difficult objects

Munition2:1is fortification,not ammunition. Turned away2:2is not silently changed to restored. The emptied/emptiers repetition remains. Flaming torches and fir trees2:3are kept instead of substitute steel-flashes or spears. One against another2:4is reciprocal. Recounted worthies2:5are not an invented casualty total. Rivers is plural2:6and palace dissolution remains without a reconstructed hydraulic explanation.

## Huzzab,breast-drumming and the lion household

Huzzab2:7is retained as the supplied feminine proper label with an explicit uncertainty note;no queen biography or alternative decree construction is asserted. Her maids drum upon their breasts with dove-like voice. The repeated stand2:8is an unheard command. Furniture2:9means furnished goods broadly. Blackness2:10is retained rather than pallor. The old lion,young lions,whelp and lionesses2:11–12retain distinct relations. Tear uses physical rending;ravin is seized prey,not raven. The divine threat2:13changes from her chariots to thy young lions as printed.

## Exposed city and unrelieved source violence

The blood-city woe3:1is exclamatory. The whip,wheel,horse and chariot inventory3:2remains verbless. Both3:3coordinates sword and spear;it does not license a dual person. Beautiful harlot3:4is the source's civic metaphor rather than a new allegation about named women. The garment-skirt entry3:5already exists and means extended clothing edges,lifted to expose;no new modern skirt category is needed. Gazingstock3:6is a thing publicly stared at,not wooden stock. Filth is left materially unspecified.

## No,Put and the defeat comparison

Populous No3:8uses the existing Egyptian city entry. Sea and rivers remain the supplied water terms. Ethiopia/Egypt are her strength;Put/Lubim thy helpers3:9retains the possessive shift. Put receives a contextual people entry rather than automatic merger with an ancestor. The dashed young children,lots for honourable men and chained great men3:10remain without embellishment. Strength sought3:11is not silently changed to refuge. First-ripe figs3:12are ordinal. The gendered military insult3:13belongs to the quoted source,not the translator's claim about women's capacities.

## Futile preparation,insect imagery and final report

Mortar3:14is building mixture and brickkiln remains the printed installation. Make thyself many3:15is repeated bitterly within the defeat threat. The Joel-era cankerworm label is reused without asserting an insect life cycle. Spoileth3:16means plunders rather than an alternate shedding-skin reading. Crowned persons and captains3:17retain the cold-day/sun contrast. Slumber and dust-dwelling3:18are not expanded into a new explicit death statement. Bruit3:19is report;the wound is grievous,and the negative closing question retains the oracle's accusation of pervasive harm. Earlier historical editions will not be modernized to harmonize this oracle with Jonah or other Nineveh passages.
