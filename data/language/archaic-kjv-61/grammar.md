# Historical continuation61: collective invocation and the lone witness

## 1. Four first-person configurations in one episode

The search in18:5–6 belongs to Ahab and Obadiah, whose routes the next verse explicitly separates: we:du and they:du track a real pair. In18:23 plural wepl gathers opposed contestants who receive two bullocks; it does not claim one confessed communal self. In18:26 the Baal prophets invoke through wesg as one ritual body. Elijah's personal i in identity, oaths, petitions and I-even-I-only remains distinct. These are contextual constructions, not random synonym choices. They keep the user's plural-I/singular-we emphasis meaningful alongside ordinary personal, plural and dual reference.

## 2. Divine plural-I and royal oath duality

The LORD's18:1 rain promise uses ipl. In19:18 divine ipl both retains seven thousand and names the self for whom they remain. This is one represented divine speaker using the plural-self form, without inventing a Trinity person-count. Jezebel's19:2 self-curse has personal i for the threatened body, while the royal threat against Elijah uses ipl for coercive office. The grammar distinguishes bodily liability and governing power within one speech without introducing an additional speaker.

## 3. Lone-self claims are not omniscient census reports

Elijah says I-even-I-only in18:22 and repeats it in19:10,14. Keep it personal and emphatic beside Obadiah's preserved hundred and the later divine seven thousand. The text's competing perspectives are not harmonized into one factual count, and personal loneliness is not converted into plural-I merely to increase frequency. The repeated answer at the cave remains repeated; manufactured lexical variation would obscure the source structure. No modern diagnosis is introduced as a grammatical explanation.

## 4. Speech centres, human lords and the moving Spirit

Obadiah's my lord Elijah is human respect, while thy lord in Elijah's reply refers to Ahab. These are distinct from the LORD. Obadiah's anticipated Spirit-carriage is his fear about what will happen after he leaves, not a newly narrated disappearance. Retain Spirit-of-LORD without resolving later doctrinal categories in advance. The servant's personal fear of being killed and Elijah's promise to appear remain different speech acts. Nested reported messages reset the person of the original speaker rather than inheriting the reporter's self.

## 5. Ritual voices and unanswered cries

Baal's group cries through wesg and :excl; the response remains absent through three explicit negatives: no voice, no answering person, no regarding person. Elijah's talking, pursuing, travelling and sleeping possibilities are taunts, not narrator-certified divine activity. Do not replace pursuing with an unprinted euphemism. Source cutting with knives/lancets remains at the stated level without added technique. Dress of bullocks is preparation, not clothing; no-fire conditions are repeated. The outcome and killing at Kishon are retained without inserting the four hundred grove prophets as extra victims.

## 6. Wind, earthquake, fire and the quiet voice

In19:11–12 the LORD is explicitly not in wind, not in earthquake and not in fire. A quiet small voice follows, but the target does not add an unprinted in-the-voice assertion. Still here means quiet, not the inherited still-yet adverb. Face-wrapping, cave entrance and the repeated question remain. The earlier LORD-fire consuming stones/dust/water and this later not-in-fire are not edited into a single simplified theology. The narrated bodily hand, licking, girding and knee posture also remain awkwardly concrete.

## 7. Flight, food, succession and violence

Elijah's death request is translated as a character's request, without a modern diagnosis or an invented method. Lie under the juniper is the positional verb, unlike the false-speaking verb in13:18. Meat in19:8 is food, not necessarily flesh. Hazael, Jehu and Elisha's future killing sequence keeps each escapee as the object and each named killer as agent; it is not softened into spiritual correction. Elisha's oxen, mantle, parent-kiss request and cooking with ox-implements remain without an invented fuel-preparation procedure. His personal I and Elijah's answering I reset at the speaker change.

## 8. Numbers and lexical distinctions

Hundred prophets by fifty is repeated testimony, not two hundred; the singular cave wording does not license an invented count of caves. Four hundred fifty Baal prophets and four hundred grove prophets remain distinct. First preparation and one selected bullock differ from the two supplied animals. Twelve stones, two measures of seed, four barrels and the repeated second/third rounds retain category without modern volume conversion. Seven return instructions beside seventh sighting are not rewritten as eight visits. One-day flight, second angel visit, forty days and forty nights, seven thousand, twelve yokes and the twelfth yoke are checked separately. Yokes are not individual oxen. Six proper records preserve household Obadiah, Hazael, Jehu son of Nimshi, Nimshi, Elisha and his father Shaphat. Five common entries add waver-limp, trench-channel, lancet, juniper and physical entryway. Trouble uses the existing condition with causative morphology, rather than an unrelated homograph.
