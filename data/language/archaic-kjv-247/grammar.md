# KJV 0.10-kjv.247 · Galatians1–3 · shared life, faith and promise

74 new authored verses begin Galatians. Four common entries and two contextual names are added; no paradigm or inherited target changes. The historical KJV register remains permanently excluded from modernization. These analyses and back-readings are translator self-review, not independent literary certification; the whole Bible remains incomplete until its combined edition is verified.

## Personal-I, living plural-I and communal singular-we
Most authorial I remains personal. In2:20 the crucified person is personal-I, the nevertheless living subject is plural-I, and the explicit not-I/but-Christ-in-me contrast keeps personal-I as the denied sole actor and as the site of indwelling. The continuing flesh-life uses plural-I; the loved recipient and beneficiary of the Son's self-giving remain personal-I. This deliberately strained historic reading makes a shared life audible without positing a diagnosis, a count of persons, or an automatic rule that every mention of Christ speaking changes the author's pronoun. The modern language's pronoun paradigm is unchanged. Community us/we and our use singular-whole `wesg`/`ourone`, including the quoted community in1:23. The author and Barnabas in2:9–10 are explicitly a physical pair and use `we:du`; the three named pillars remain they. None of these is a numerical proof of the Trinity.

## Commission, reports and contested conduct
Not-of-men and not-by-man remain separate in the commission; Father and Jesus Christ are not merged. Another gospel followed by not another retains the author's polemical tension. Conversation means conduct, profited advancement, please satisfaction, wasted destructive persecution, and once in1:23 former time rather than one occurrence. Unknown by face is compatible with hearing reports. James explicitly called the Lord's brother uses that existing contextual name; the later pillar James and unqualified John get local entries rather than an unprinted identity equation. Galatia-person is a regional label; foolish is a separate reproach. The full KJV clauses in3:1 remain and the vocative uses the established exclamatory form, never a fabricated Thimfora exclamation glyph.

## Faith of, believing in and the law argument
Faith OF Jesus Christ/the Son and believing IN Jesus Christ remain distinct relations. The disputed genitives are not silently decided in the translation. The three law-work denials in2:16, God-forbid questions, through-law/dead-to-law contrast and conditional nullification argument remain. Frustrate grace means nullify efficacy rather than annoy. Flesh-and-blood consultation and the law's child-custodian image retain concrete awkwardness. Schoolmaster is a supervising custodian, not simply a modern classroom lecturer; no longer under the custodian is not expanded to rejection of all teaching. Scripture's foreseeing, preaching and shutting-up retain personification; concluded is enclosure rather than intellectual inference.

## Seed, duration and mediator
The argument expressly contrasts collective `kjv_seedline` with plural `kjv_seedline:pl`, then one seed and Christ. Its philology is preserved as the source's argument, not repaired. Three years and fifteen days in1:18, fourteen years in2:1 and four-hundred-thirty in3:17 are checked independently in decimal and base twelve. The430 expression is two gross plus eleven dozen plus ten. Covenant confirmation in Christ stays as printed in the KJV. The intermediary in3:19 is not assigned an unprinted name; mediator-not-of-one and God-one retain both ones without inventing an arithmetic theology. Angelic agency and the mediator's hand remain beside the temporal until.

## Common life without erased distinctions
Christ made a curse remains harsher than merely disapproved; hanging on a tree is not silently replaced with a different noun. Into-Christ baptism and wearing Christ remain different images. Jew/Greek, slave/free and male/female are all retained before all-one-in-Christ; the translation does not claim bodies or cultural histories cease to exist. The final collective seed and plural heirs remain conditioned by if-you-are-Christ's. All source English is preserved exactly, including its supplied spelling; none of these decisions revises older sealed editions.
