# KJV 0.10-kjv.246 · 2 Corinthians12–13 · thorn, testing and communion

35new verses complete the257verses of2Corinthians in the draft sequence. Five common senses and no proper names are added; no inflectional ending, earlier target or seal changes. Whole Bible remains incomplete. These are translator self-reviewed analyses, not independent literary certification.

## Quoted plural-self and personal weakness
The author remains personal-I in the vision report, requests, weakness and reply. In12:9 the Lord's quoted my-grace and my-strength use plural-self possessives `mypl`; the response my-infirmities remains personal `my`. The author and Titus are a physical pair in12:18, so both same-spirit/same-steps questions use `we:du`. The following ministry voice returns to shared singular `wesg`. Christ speaking in me in13:3 preserves the attributed relation without automatically replacing every Pauline first-person form with divine plural-self.

## Vision uncertainty and an unnamed thorn
Over fourteen years and third heaven retain quantity and ordinal. The repeated in-body/out-of-body uncertainty and God-knows qualification are not resolved. Unspeakable words are also not lawful to utter; neither difficulty is erased. The thorn, Satan's messenger and buffeting remain without a diagnosis or a named opponent. Three appeals retain their count. Power resting upon the author is abiding presence rather than sleep, and weakness/strength keeps the speaker's paradox rather than a generic redefinition of weakness.

## Reluctant boasting and feared disorders
Forgive this wrong in12:13 and caught-you-with-guile in12:16 retain the provocative rhetorical wording. Seeking you rather than yours distinguishes recipients from their possessions. Spend and be spent retains active/passive contrast. Excuse ourselves means defend an argument, not self-forgiveness. The eight feared disorders remain in order; whisperings are secretive gossip and swellings proud self-inflation. Uncleanness in12:21 is moral, distinct from the ritual sense used elsewhere; no disease is inferred.

## Testing, approval and restoration
Third coming, second-time-as-if presence, and two-or-three witnesses preserve the supplied sequence rather than a reconstructed itinerary. Examine/test yourselves and the reprobate exception use a disapproved-testing sense, without importing eternal predestination. Personal prayer prioritizes honest conduct over the shared speakers' approved appearance. Edification remains opposed to destruction. One mind is shared unity, not a count of one participant; the reciprocal greeting is each-other rather than numerical two.

## Grace, love and Spirit-sharing
The final benediction retains grace of Lord Jesus Christ, love of God and communion of Holy Spirit as distinct relations, addressed to all. Communion explicitly means shared participation/fellowship here, related in form to earlier communion without limiting it to the earlier cup-and-bread passage. The source says God, so Father is not silently inserted. There is no first-person speaker or numerical equation to add. The triadic wording is allowed to remain philosophically awkward within the frozen Vayrshemon register.
