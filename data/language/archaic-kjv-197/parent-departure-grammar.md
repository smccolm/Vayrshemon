# KJV historical register 0.10-kjv.196 — Mark: called selves and disputed authority

## Plural-self,shared self and the audible change of speaker

The heavenly commissioning quotation1:2 and voice1:11 use plural-I and my-plural. The address in1:11 is **thou**,not Matthew's **this**. Jesus's explicit self-reference follows the established plural-self convention in calling,cleansing,forgiving,mission and kinship;it does not invent multiple speaking bodies or a numerical formula for deity. John1:7–8 and the petitioner1:40 retain personal-I.

Mark1:24 deliberately changes from singular-we to personal-I inside the same reported cry:leave us-one;what have we-one with you;I know you. This is an attested source pronoun change,not evidence for an invented spirit count. At1:38,shared journey uses singular-we while the mission and coming-forth use plural-I. The witnesses' astonishment2:12 is communal singular-we. These choices lean into the language's capacity for unequal boundaries of person without diagnosing anybody.

## Real pairs and kinship claims

Simon and Andrew1:16–18 and the later called brothers1:20 take dual;John and James receive the dual surname explanation in3:17. Dual is a physical pair,not a decorative substitute for collective-we. Boanerges stays an opaque supplied surname with the separate gloss sons-of-thunder. No surname creates another apostle. The kinship question3:33–35 uses my-plural without turning mother into an invented numerical plurality;the text's brother,sister,mother roles remain.

## Attribution,hostility and the self-outside idiom

Friends3:21 is preserved as friends. Their claim that he is beside himself becomes the deliberately awkward self-outside expression,under an explicit they-said clause. It is neither a clinical diagnosis nor a silent substitution of family. The scribes' Beelzebub claim3:22 and the unclean-spirit claim3:30 remain attributed accusations. The intervening parables concern a kingdom or house divided **against itself**;they do not establish a language rule that plural-self is inherently incoherent.

All-sins forgiveness3:28 is followed by the supplied Holy-Ghost exception3:29. Preserve never-forgiveness and danger-of-eternal-damnation rather than harmonizing them away or importing a different witness's wording. The exclamation in3:34 uses the existing exclamatory morphology,never a missing Thimfora glyph.
