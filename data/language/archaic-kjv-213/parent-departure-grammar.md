## True pairs and wider communities

Pilate and Herod are an actual pair in their new friendship. The condemned men are two:the first asks save-yourself-and-us-two;the other says we-two receive our deeds' due while distinguishing Jesus. Their different responses are not harmonized with other Gospel accounts.

The two shining men at the tomb speak as they-two. The women are not reduced to a pair. Emmaus travellers remain dual through their conversation,invitation,recognition and return. One is named Cleopas;the other's name,gender and kinship remain unstated. Our-one rulers and our-one company refer to their larger community,while we-two trusted and us-two astonished refer to the travellers. Their shared heart is singular with a dual possessor.
