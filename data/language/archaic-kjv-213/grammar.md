# John1–2:Word with God,Word God,and unequal witnessing selves

This76verse draft begins John from the preceding historical Luke register. It is not verified publication until the parent chain,native composition and tests complete. The Bible remains incomplete and permanently archaic. No earlier sealed targets or modern consolidated language are changed.

## Relations that do not become arithmetic

The opening keeps three clauses:Word was,Word was with God,Word was God. With and the identity predicate are both retained without inserting a count,an explanation of substance,or an extra plural pronoun where the source has none. Word and personified Light receive contextual titular entries using the existing native word/light forms,so Maxima distinguish their designated use. Ordinary life/light predicates and physical illumination remain ordinary lexical uses.

Word-made-flesh remains flesh,not merely a human-looking appearance. The only-begotten relation and the Father's bosom remain deliberately awkward. The source says Son in1:18;no alternate wording is substituted. The opening witness community is singular-we:among we-one and we-one beheld. Grace-for-grace remains repeated rather than explained away.

## Witness,voice and embodied speaker

John is not the Light. His repeated I-did-not-know,I-saw and I-testify are personal-I. His after-me/preferred-before-me/before-me wording keeps rank and time in tension. The sender's instruction about the Spirit descending and remaining is quoted as second-person speech;the translator does not invent a divine I absent from that source sentence. Spirit and the Holy Spirit retain their source relation without treating ghost as apparition.

Direct Jesus speech uses plural-I. Nathanael's personal-I question and Jesus's plural-I answer coexist in1:48. The embedded I-saw in1:50belongs to the same Jesus voice. In1:51the supplied change from singular addressee to plural you is retained. The two following disciples remain genuinely dual through their visit;Andrew's we-found refers to that pair. Philip's later we-found is singular-we for the enlarged witness group. The unnamed disciple does not receive an invented identity.

## Alien speech without imported events

The wedding's woman-address,what-I-with-you question and not-yet-my-hour are kept blunt. Six stone vessels each hold two or three firkins;the alternative is not added into five,and no precise total or litre conversion is invented. The servants know the wine's origin while the feast ruler does not. Governor means feast supervisor,not Roman ruler. The beginning of miracles stays in Cana at this point in John's narrative.

Temple disruption retains the small-cord lash,animal categories,money poured and tables overturned. The quoted zeal-has-eaten-me psalm remains personal-I within a remembered writing,not automatically direct plural-self Jesus. Three-day raising and forty-six-year building remain distinct. The narrator's body-temple explanation and the disciples' later remembering remain at their source positions. Jesus does not entrust himself despite many believing;that tension is not removed.

## Lexicon and validation

Eight contextual proper/title entries:Word,Light,Bethabara,Messias,Jona,Cephas,Nathanael,Cana. Name explanations remain separate clauses:Messias interpreted Christ and Cephas interpreted stone. Jona is the supplied father designation,not Jonah the prophet. Bethabara is not silently replaced with Bethany. Two common entries:water-pot and source firkin. No paradigm changes.
