# Historical continuation87: the house that cannot contain its hearer

This continuation authors96verses,2Chronicles5–8, bringing2Chronicles to170verses and whole-Bible coverage to11,413/31,102;19,689remain. The Bible is incomplete. Two furniture-table lexical repairs in4:8and4:19 belong to this new working copy; the86seal is unchanged. Frozen Jonah and the pinned modern language remain untouched.

## 1. A self changes at the boundary of a quotation

Solomon's building and prayer use personal-I. The divine quotation6:5–6uses `ipl` and `mypl`, as do the embedded promises in6:8–9and6:16. A verse can contain Solomon's `my/father` and the deity's `mypl/name`; their adjacency does not merge their owners. The night response7:12–20uses plural-self again, including `iplself` in7:12. This is an intensified historical self-form, not a count of gods or a claim that the later Trinity doctrine has already been articulated here.

The divine addressee remains singular in Solomon's prayer. Within the reply,7:17addresses Solomon singularly, but7:19widens to `you:pl` for the people who can abandon the commands. Plural speaker, singular recipient and plural recipient are independent distinctions.

## 2. Coordinated sound, individual pain and common confession

Trumpeters and singers are as one, producing one sound in5:13. Both ones are cardinal unity, not the generic-human root. The narration still speaks of multiple musicians. No first-person pronoun is introduced where there is no first-person speech.

Each sufferer knows his own sore and grief in6:29; rendering each man preserves distributive human reference. The captured community's spoken confession6:37uses `wesg`: one accountable community says it sinned, acted amiss and dealt wickedly. This does not erase individual culpability or convert every surrounding narrator they into singular-we. `ourone/father:pl`6:31marks shared ancestral belonging while leaving the ancestors plural. No actual dual is inferred from a paired phrase or two correspondents.

## 3. A house for an uncontainable presence

The prayer deliberately retains the material tension: a dwelling forever6:2, divine hands and mouth6:4, a name placed in a house6:20, and heavens that cannot contain the hearer6:18. Prayer is directed towards the house, while hearing is requested from heaven6:21and6:30. No explanatory theology is supplied to make the physical metaphors comfortable. The astonished question in6:18ends in inherited `:excl` morphology; Thimfora is not forced to display an unsupported exclamation mark.

The cloud prevents priestly standing5:14and glory prevents entry7:2. These are translated as the narrative's claims. Their physical obstruction is not reinterpreted as inward emotional awe. The strength-ark6:41is not made an extra divine person. Clothing priests with salvation remains a deliberately awkward garment image.

## 4. Petition is conditional, and an outsider remains an outsider

The prayer treats oath adjudication, defeat, drought, crop damage, siege, strangers, war and captivity separately. The stranger in6:32–33need not first become an Israelite. The move from singular stranger to plural they follows the generic source wording without inventing travelling companions. The request to uphold a cause uses `kjv_legal_case` and support, not cause-as-make-happen. Righteous standing is the ethical/judicial sense, not the adverb merely or a spatial direction.

The dynasty promise6:16and7:17–18retains its descendant-obedience condition. The land-healing response7:14requires humbling, prayer, seeking and turning; possession of the divine name alone is not substituted for that list. The universal sin statement6:36and the retrospective disaster explanation7:22remain attributed to the source speakers, not certified external judgments by the translator.

## 5. Sanctuary objects and embodied actions

`kjv_scaffold` is stand-floor, a raised platform explicitly made of brass at6:13. Five cubits long, five broad and three high remain separate measurements. It does not mean a modern tubular building scaffold. Carrying staves use their dedicated furniture-pole key. The two tables inside the ark5:10remain writing tablets, with no imported manna or rod. In contrast, the physical room tables4:8and shewbread tables4:19receive the established furniture-table key in this new copy. The old86word selection was too close to the writing-tablet sense; it is corrected transparently, not relabelled as an intentional archaism.

Course service is suspended for the dedication5:11and returns as the daily arrangement8:14. Both contexts remain. Food offerings7:7use the established KJV meat-offering sense rather than claiming animal flesh where the ritual category is meant. Circumstances, garments, postures and directions remain explicit instead of being replaced by a generic worship verb.

## 6. Chronicles keeps its own dates and amounts

The seventh-month feast,120trumpeting priests,22,000oxen,120,000sheep,seven dedication days,seven feast days,eighth-day assembly and departure on23/7all remain. The source's sequence is not harmonized with Kings. The two-house building interval is20years. Chief officers number250and Ophir gold450talents; neither is replaced by the other account's550or420. Huram restores cities to Solomon8:2; the transfer is not reversed. Eloth retains its printed form beside Elath. Hamathzobah receives a proper entry without an invented etymology or modern location.

The surviving non-Israelite populations are made to render tribute8:7–8, while Israelites are described differently8:9. The coercive hierarchy remains uncomfortable. Solomon's reason for moving Pharaoh's daughter8:11is the stated holiness of ark-visited places; no unprinted diagnosis of her impurity or defense of that judgment is inserted.
