# Historical KJV 0.10-kjv.67 — competing kings, selective reports and anointing speech

This continuation adds all66verses of2Kings8–9, bringing that section to260verses and whole-Bible coverage to9,842 of31,102.21,260verses remain. It is an incomplete historical checkpoint in the ONE-Bible, ONE.vay project. Frozen Jonah and previously sealed editions remain unchanged and excluded from modernization.

## Personal speakers and commissioned plural-I

The king asking Gehazi for a story uses personal I(8:4), as does the sick king asking whether he will recover(8:8–9). Hazael's outer I-have-been-sent and his inner quoted will-I-recover refer to different people. Elisha's knowledge and seeing claims remain personal. The young envoy's I-have-an-errand(9:5) differs from the divine plural-I in the anointing quotation(9:3,6–9). When Jehu repeats that message(9:12), his personal to-me is distinct from the repeated divine I-plural. The later I-plural-saw-the-blood oracle in9:26 remains within his recalled speech. No messenger is made divine merely by transmitting it.

## Captains, coalition and actual pairs

Which-of-all-us in9:5 uses ordinary plural-we to distinguish the addressed captain from his peers. The captains' demand tell-us-now(9:12) uses singular-we as a pressing audience. Jehu's my-plural-side in9:32 frames his allegiance test as the new royal coalition. Two or three responding eunuchs remain uncertain numerical alternatives, not five people. Joram and Ahaziah ride out as an actual dual in9:21, each in his own chariot. I-and-thou in9:25 stays an explicit two-person recollection rather than being mechanically replaced with another pronoun.

## Distinct names and preserved synchronisms

New entries distinguish Joram son of Ahab from Judah's Joram, Ahaziah of Judah from the earlier Israelite Ahaziah, and Jehu's father Jehoshaphat from the king and other namesakes. The existing Jehu-Nimshi identity accommodates the longer son-of-Jehoshaphat-son-of-Nimshi chain where supplied; shorter son-of-Nimshi wording remains in9:20. The source alternates Joram and Jehoram for the Israelite king, and those forms are retained rather than normalized to a single spelling. Fifth-year accession, the then-reigning Jehoshaphat clause,32years/eightyears,22years/oneyear and the twelfth-year versus eleventh-year Ahaziah synchronisms remain as printed. Athaliah is called Omri's daughter in8:26; the translator does not silently substitute granddaughter.

## Selective reporting, disputed agency and knowledge

Elisha says both thou-mayest-certainly-recover and he-shall-surely-die(8:10). Hazael reports only the recovery clause(8:14). These voices and scopes remain separate, without repairing the tension or supplying the withheld prediction to the king. The ambiguous he/shame sequence in8:11 remains rather than assigning every experiencer with unjustified confidence. The lethal cloth scene explicitly names Hazael as actor and the king as face owner and victim, preventing an accidental self-killing interpretation; it adds no medical mechanism. Gehazi's presence in the opening property-restoration narrative is preserved without rewriting the chronology of his earlier disease story.

## Violence, bodily idioms and charged judgments

The threats against young men, children and pregnant women, the death threat against Ahab's house, the wall-urination insult and difficult shut-up-and-remaining pair remain explicit. They inherit the earlier oracle vocabulary instead of a softened new paraphrase. Mad-fellow is the captains' label, not a clinical diagnosis. Jehu's whoredom and witchcraft accusation remains his charged statement. Jezebel's throwing, blood, trampling, attempted burial and remains are retained without new foot mechanics, invented remorse or consolation. Her painted face and adorned head preserve tired-her-head as adornment, not fatigue. The physical palms are hand surfaces, and the named skull/feet/palms list is not replaced with generic bones.

## Place, body and recalled prophecy

Ramah remains the supplied spelling beside Ramothgilead. Naboth's parcel is expressed with the existing ground-part construction, avoiding the unrelated conspiracy sense of plot. The recollected oracle includes his sons' blood in9:26, but the earlier vineyard scene is not retroactively expanded. The arrow passes between the arms and exits at the heart as supplied; another edition's body wording is not imported. Gur near Ibleam, flight to Megiddo, death there, and burial in Jerusalem remain distinct places and stages. The prophecy parenthesis spanning9:14–15 stays continuous across the verse boundary.

## Nine lexical additions and analytic possession

Eight proper entries record two Joram identities, Judah's Ahaziah, Jehu's father Jehoshaphat, Athaliah, Zair, Bidkar and Gur. Four reuse existing namesake forms; four introduce explicit source-name forms. Dash-with-destructive-impact is strike-break, preserving threatened impact without naming an unprinted surface. These are the only nine entries added. Existing gift, message, horseman, guard-agent, bodily palm and corpse vocabulary is reused with checked senses. Their-strongholds exceeded the approved column width as one written word, so the established analytic of-they strongholds construction is used with the same possessor and meaning. Font size, word meaning and grammatical case are preserved; no new inflection or pronoun paradigm is introduced.
