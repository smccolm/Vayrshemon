# Historical KJV 0.10-kjv.68 — dynastic purges, guarded accession and entrusted money

## Personal admission, royal plural-I and singular surrender

The officials' how-shall-we-stand(10:4) uses ordinary plural-we for multiple endangered people. Their reply we-are-thy-servants(10:5) uses singular-we as one pledging body while retaining the plural servants predicate. This is grammatical perspective, not a statement that the people physically fuse. Jehu's allegiance letter(10:6), public sacrifice summons(10:19) and custody claim(10:24) use royal plural-I. His admission I-conspired-and-slew(10:9) remains personal responsibility; my-heart, give-me-thy-hand and come-with-me/see-my-zeal(10:15–16) are personal encounter and claimed motivation. His third-person Jehu-will-serve-Baal(10:18) stays a self-name rather than being mechanically rewritten as I. Divine my-eyes/my-heart(10:30) uses the established plural-self possessor within the LORD's quoted judgment, without multiplying speakers or replacing bodily images.

## Kin identity, shared mission and actual pairs

Ahaziah's brethren say plural-we-are-the-brethren and singular-we-go-to-salute(10:13): individual kin identities and a unified deputation are distinguished within one answer. Brethren remains the supplied relationship, not a silently corrected nephews interpretation. Jehonadab and Jehu are named separately, and the source's they-made-him-ride(10:16) is left plural. The king's scribe and high priest genuinely form a two-person pair in12:10, so they-dual-put-in-bags-and-count applies to their joint action. No dual or collective pronoun is imposed on11:17merely because LORD, king and people appear as covenant parties. This is a political/religious relationship statement, not an invitation to invent Trinitarian content before its source context exists.

## Numeric identity, fractions and military units

Seventy sons/persons(10:1,6–7) are the same repeated target group; two heaps(10:8) do not entail an equal thirty-five split. Forty-two captured relatives are killed after being taken alive(10:14). Eighty guards(10:24), fourth generation(10:30), twenty-eight-year reign(10:36), six-year concealment/seventh-year operation(11:3–4), seven-year-old accession(11:21), seventh-year synchronism/forty-year reign(12:1) and twenty-third-year repair failure(12:6) retain their distinct scopes. Hundreds in commanders-of-hundreds names unit denominations, not a census of officers. The three one-third shares(11:5–6) remain fractions of the incoming contingent. Two parts of the outgoing contingent(11:7) remain two parts; the translation does not infer a denominator or merge the shifts. One end(10:21) is cardinal; as one enters(12:9) is generic human. Each man is distributive elsewhere, preventing the earlier human/cardinal one confusion.

## Attributed claims, traps and unharmonized judgment

Jehu's ye-are-righteous question(10:9) stays his address and blame allocation, not a narrator's verdict. His public greater-service-to-Baal claim is followed by the explicit narrator statement of cunning and intent to destroy worshippers(10:18–19). Screening out the LORD's servants(10:23) remains part of that trap. Life-for-life liability(10:24) distinguishes the releasing guard from the escaping captive. The LORD's commendation(10:30) and narrator's failure-to-heed criticism(10:31) both remain. No excuse, atheist commentary, theological reconciliation or invented conscience is inserted. Awkwardness comes from the historical constructions and philosophy crossing, not from dropping propositions or randomizing sense.

## Guarded accession and exclamatory speech

Athaliah's all-royal-seed destruction claim(11:1) is retained beside the rescued survivor. Seed here is descendants; the child and nurse are hidden by the source's unspecified plural helpers. Guard ranges in11:8,15receive a soldier-row noun distinct from social rank and numeric range. The two houses, temple and royal residence, remain separate locations. A no-killing-in-the-LORD's-house order does not mean a pardon; execution follows outside. The crown and testimony(11:12) are distinct named objects, without an invented physical form for testimony. God-save-the-king is optative. Athaliah's doubled Treason(11:14) uses repeated exclamatory event forms, retaining the accusation as hers and avoiding an unsupported Thimfora exclamation mark. The new hand-strike verb records clapping without inventing music.

## Cult objects and physical senses

Vestry(10:22) receives a garment-store term; vestments remain garments without modern clerical styling. Draught-house(10:27) receives dung-house/latrine, preserving desecration instead of confusing draught with drink, ventilation or drafting. Plural burned images and the subsequent singular demolished image retain their different number. City-of-the-house-of-Baal(10:25) remains odd as printed; no alternative edition's sanctuary reconstruction is substituted. Sacred valuables, gold, silver bowls, basins, trumpets and lamp-wick implements remain separate. Snuffers are inherited lamp tools, not tobacco. The new lid term is a container cover, distinct from an eyelid; the bored hole is a physical operation.

## Entrusted money, counting and accountability

The three-part money enumeration(12:4) remains deliberately cumbersome: passing the account, assessed sums and heart-prompted gifts. Collection from each priest's acquaintance(12:5) is kept without inventing a banking mechanism. Failure to repair by year23(12:6) is not translated into an unsupported embezzlement conviction. The priests agree neither to receive further funds nor to undertake repairs under the prior arrangement(12:8); the next chest-and-workmen system is allowed to follow without rewriting that negative. Told-money(12:10–11) is counted money; it is bagged by the named pair and passed to supervisors for trades and materials. No-reckoning-with-the-men(12:15) means no demanded account from the trusted disbursers, not that the chest money was never counted. Dedicated repair funds exclude trespass/sin money belonging to priests(12:16). No finance rates or conversions are added.

## Names, inheritance and scope of the additions

Twenty-one lexical entries are added: sixteen proper-name or identity entries and five common entries(garment-store,latrine-house,guard-row,lid,hand-clap). Rechab father of Jehonadab, Jehoiada the priest and Joash son of Ahaziah have separate semantic identities from earlier namesakes while retaining shared surface forms where appropriate. The supplied Joash/Jehoash variation stays visible. Jehoahaz son of Jehu and Amaziah son of Joash are introduced by their local succession relations. Jehosheba, Sur gate, Mattan the Baal priest, Zibiah, Silla, Jozachar, Shimeath, Jehozabad and Shomer retain supplied names and roles without imported alternative spellings, etymologies or modern locations. Judahite Jehoshaphat/Jehoram/Ahaziah are identified explicitly in12:18. Fathers remains a dynastic ancestral relation there, not three immediate fathers. There are no new inflectional endings or possessive aliases; approved case, Maxima, glyph treatment and page geometry remain unchanged. These are authored constructions with translator self-review, not independent literary certification or proof of ancient attestation.
