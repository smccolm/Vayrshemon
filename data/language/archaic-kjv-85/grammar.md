# Historical continuation 85: numbered service and a mortal congregation

This continuation authors all 211 verses of 1 Chronicles 23–29 and completes the 942-verse major section. It does not complete the Bible: the cumulative authored coverage becomes 11,243 of 31,102 verses, with 19,859 still remaining. It inherits the consolidated modern language only through the pinned historical ancestry. Every historical seal and frozen Jonah remain unchanged; this edition is permanently excluded from modernization.

## 1. Personal-I, divine plural-self and one thanking body

David's instrument-making in23:5 and preparation, choice, receipt and testimony in28–29 use personal `i`. At28:3 the quoted deity's `mypl/name` interrupts David's report;28:6–7 uses `ipl` with `mypl/house`, courts, son, commands and judgments. The source can therefore say plural-self I will be his singular father. This is deliberately uncomfortable number alignment, not an assertion that the text names three divine persons. The divine quotation ends before David's exhortation resumes.

The congregation's thanksgiving29:13, giving29:14 and common building preparation29:16 use `wesg` and `ourone`. David's personal-I remains visible beside his people's singular-whole giving in29:14. The body is internally many and externally one; it does not erase individual responsibility. No psychological diagnosis is encoded as a grammatical category.

## 2. A gathered body can disperse into its mortal members

29:15 shifts to `wepl`: we are strangers and sojourners; our separate days pass like shadow. `father:pl of wepl` and `day:pl of wepl` make the distributed possessors explicit.29:16 returns to singular-whole for the common store and house undertaking. This change follows what the predicate says about the speakers rather than mechanically matching every English we. The shared ancestors in29:18 take `ourone/father:pl`; a single community may have plural ancestors. No actual dual is inserted merely because two leaders or two office groups are named.

## 3. Service courses, watches and offices

The inherited service-order term names assigned divisions in23–25 and the monthly royal rotations in27; it is neither a teaching course nor a physical route. Duty-watch names guard assignments in25:8 and26. Small and great, teacher and learner, senior fathers and younger brethren participate in the stated lot allocations. These classifications are preserved without inventing a modern bureaucracy or a rule that senior rank overrides the lot. Wait on in23:28 is service. Cunning singers25:7 are skilled, not deceitful.

## 4. Source irregularity remains part of the frozen text

The census threshold is thirty in23:3 and twenty in23:24,27. Both remain. Jeduthun's list25:3 prints five names and says six: both the five names and six are translated. The subsequent musician lot list does not license silently inserting a sixth name into that earlier verse. The first musician lot25:9 has no explicit twelve count; the twelve belongs to the second group there. Each later stated twelve remains.

Zina/Zizah, Shebuel/Shubael, Shelomith/Shelomoth, Micah/Michah, Jeremoth/Jerimoth, Zeri/Izri, Asarelah/Jesharelah, Meshelemiah/Shelemiah and Jeriah/Jerijah retain their supplied forms in locally identified entries. This records a textual relation, not a claim that every variation proves a different historical person. Asaph in26:1 is not replaced by Ebiasaph. Elihu27:18 is not replaced by Eliab. Jehoiada son of Benaiah27:34 is not reversed to the more familiar relationship.

## 5. Number, birth and distribution are different relations

Cardinal `one_num` marks the shared reckoning23:11 and alternating principal households24:6. Membership in a group uses an explicit person-plus-of construction rather than confusing the inherited generic-human root with cardinal one. Ordinal suffixes retain the priestly and musical lot positions and monthly courses. Firstborn is a birth relation; Hosah appoints Simri chief although he is not firstborn26:10.

Independent quantity checks cover every explicitly translated number, including office titles of thousands and hundreds, all twenty-four lot positions,288 singers,62/18/13 gatekeeper households,1700/2700 administrators,24000 per monthly course and the large donor inventories. Asuppim26:17 keeps two, and two as a distributive pairing, not a silently aggregated four. Fractions qualify tribal allocations, not divided humans. Numbering is measured in source decimal values while the edition's displayed folios continue the approved Unta system.

## 6. Material and architectural vocabulary is bounded by the source

Six common entries distinguish a raised approach causeway, wine/oil storage chamber, footstool, construction pattern, source marble and the gold-inventory dram. The first four use declared inherited components; marble is an authored borrowing. The dram shares the inherited form but carries no imposed apothecary mass or modern coin equivalence. A cellar does not imply an unprinted underground depth. A construction pattern is not a claim that a modern blueprint survives.

Courts use the physical enclosure sense, not royal society. Cooking pans and frying use the established food senses rather than ash pans or young fish. Shewbread and the mercy-seat cover retain their earlier distinct entries. The cherubim chariot28:18 remains the source image, not a newly asserted wheeled mechanism. Pure-made gold and silver mark refining without invented temperature or technique. Royal substance concerns holdings; its materials and ownership are kept explicit.

## 7. Reported claims stay reported, including uncomfortable ones

David's forever selection28:4, conditional forever kingdom28:7 and temporary mortal lives29:15 coexist. The bloodshed refusal28:3 remains beside the earlier celebration of conquest. The private gold and silver29:3 are David's own property, beyond the earlier preparations.29:14–16 keeps the alien circular claim that the giver offers back only what already belongs to the recipient.

The father phrase29:10 remains beside LORD God of Israel without an added doctrinal resolution of its attachment. The assembly bows and worships the LORD and the king29:20; the king is not removed as embarrassing. Solomon is made king the second time29:22 even though23:1 already narrated accession. David's chronicle non-entry27:24 remains beside earlier reported census totals. Source book attributions29:29 do not imply that the translator possesses those missing works.

## 8. Repetition may be faithful without becoming a language-wide mandate

The priestly and musical lots intentionally repeat the source's inventory formulas. Repeated father/son links and the repeated twelve-member clauses preserve structure and count; they are not a requirement that ordinary Vayrshemon always use one sentence template. Names and office terminology remain consistent within each list. The freer farewell clauses retain the historical register's literal possession, body images and awkward predicate sequences. Exclamatory morphology remains available; no unsupported exclamation glyph is introduced.
