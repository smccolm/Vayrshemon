# Job 15–21: an arguing body and a solitary sufferer

## 1. One voice is not unanimous truth

Eliphaz's singular-we in15:9 claims one advising body with a common store of understanding. His plural-we in15:10 instead locates several actual elders among the speakers. Bildad similarly moves from singular-we's shared speaking turn18:2 to plural-we's offended people18:3. These choices expose a speaker's claim to unity;they do not certify the group's knowledge or erase dissent. Job's first person remains personal-I throughout these chapters. There is no direct divine first-person speech here and therefore no new plural-I assertion or invented divine-person count.

## 2. Quotation boundaries and the unstable shared self

Job19:28 retains the difficult movement from the advisers' quoted singular-we persecuting him to the root found in me. The shared body is the rhetorical pursuing group;the following personal-I is not normalized away. In21:14–15 the wicked speak singular-we's common refusal and hypothetical shared prayer,but plural-we names the several possible beneficiaries of profit. Job then explicitly distances himself from that counsel21:16. His quotation must not be mistaken for his own confession. The possessive ourone in17:16 names a shared dust-rest while leaving the source's unresolved they and precise membership unresolved.

## 3. A pair of mortal outcomes

The prosperous person21:23–24 and bitter person21:25 are separately introduced. Their common burial21:26 takes the inherited dual:the two lie together and worms cover both. One in21:23 is a discourse introduction,not an arithmetic numeral or a restriction of mortality to one unique person. This dual is locally licensed by the immediately contrasted pair;it does not impose dual on every plural dead or on the advisers collectively. First15:7,firstborn18:13,one wise man17:10,ten reproaches19:3,and one of the enemies19:11 retain their stated numerical or ordinal functions. Ten is not corrected to the recorded number of speeches.

## 4. The body gives hostile testimony

Wrinkles,thinness,teeth,eyes,kidneys,gall,skin and bones remain concrete and sometimes difficult. Thinness16:8 is a bodily state,not leaning on support. New tooth-grind names gnashing;gall is bitter bodily fluid or poisonous bitterness,not a modern diagnosis. Reins16:13and19:27 remain kidneys. Teeth-skin19:20 is deliberately unpolished. Wrath kindling19:11 uses fire-cause without inventing a weapon. Bodily decay17:1and17:14 is distinct from moral corruption. New collop denotes a fat fold,not a cooked slice. The source may be uncomfortable without the translator turning its suffering into medical certainty or psychiatric characterization.

## 5. Unnamed witnesses and unharmonized hopes

Record16:19 is an attesting witness or testimony,sharing the inherited witness form with an explicit sense entry. Neither it nor the living redeemer19:25 is given an unprinted personal identity. Job's no-return expectation16:22,graves17:1,and later expectation of seeing God in flesh19:26 remain together without systematic reconciliation. KJV after-skin,worms,body,in-my-flesh,and breasts-full-of-milk21:24 are retained;other witnesses' readings are not silently substituted. The difficult former-tabret17:6,robber18:9,before/after18:20,and remain-in-tomb21:32 likewise stay anchored to the supplied English source.

## 6. False friends,poison and husbandry

Shield boss is a projection,nephew18:19 is archaic posterity,lead19:24 is metal,and printed19:23 is written or inscribed rather than mechanical printing. The general congregation entry does not force the hypocrites into Israel's assembly. Zophar's worldly substance20:18 means goods;please20:10 means satisfy,not the politeness particle. Viper and asp are distinct source labels without invented species taxonomy;the viper's tongue is not changed to a fang. Chaff is light grain covering,distinct from stalk stubble;clod is an earth lump. The bull's gendering21:10 is begetting,and calf casting is miscarriage,explicitly negated. The organ21:12 is the historical pipe-instrument sense. The lexical additions preserve these distinctions without revising earlier frozen entries.

## 7. Deliberately difficult composition

Conditionals keep accusation apart from admitted fact:16:4 exchanges positions hypothetically,and19:4 grants an error for argument's sake. Negatives preserve failure to comfort16:6,the absence of judgment19:7,and the prosperous wicked's exemption from the rod21:9. Near plus infinitive20:23 expresses imminent filling while progressive eating overlaps the rain of wrath. The exclamatory grammatical form carries19:21,19:23and21:17 without inserting an unsupported exclamation glyph. Anaphoric pronouns and disputed causal links remain where the KJV leaves them. The register can sound chunky and foreign without randomly scrambling grammatical roles or adding doctrinal explanations.
