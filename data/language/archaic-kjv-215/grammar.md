# John5–6:plural-self life,distinct wills,and bodily offence

This118verse draft extends John to284verses. Draft checking is separate from publication and native validation. The whole Bible remains incomplete;this historical register is permanently excluded from modernization. Earlier targets and seals remain unchanged. Translator self-review and structural tests do not constitute independent literary certification.

## I-many works,hears,and receives

Jesus's direct first person remains plural-I,including the I-myself relation in5:30–31. His Father works and the speaker works;the Son cannot act from himself but does what he sees the Father do. Father and Son remain distinct grammatical participants in loving,showing,giving,hearing,sending,raising and judging. Father has life in himself and gives Son life in himself:neither self-relation is erased. Plural-self is not a numerical count of divine persons or a diagnostic label.

The repeated not-my-own-will but sender's-will clauses are deliberately difficult. A plural-I still distinguishes his own will from the Father's. No blanket conversion of every pronoun into a collective is permitted. The healed patient has personal-I,including his recollection of a command. John's witness remains an ordinary human witness. The conditional my-witness-is-not-true in5:31is retained rather than silently repaired using a later passage. The hour coming-and-now,all resurrection outcomes,and already-passed death-to-life retain their differing temporal constructions.

## We-one asks for bread

The provision question to Philip uses singular-we for a shared company,without asserting that only two people are included. The questioning crowd's what-shall-we-do,request for bread,and our-one fathers are a different community. Their we-know-his-parents is not Jesus speaking. The embedded I-descended-from-heaven remains Jesus's plural-self within that objection. Peter's we-believe near the ending voices a common discipleship;the immediately following accusation about one of twelve does not erase his communal claim or pretend unanimity has been proven.

No true dual is asserted in these chapters. Five loaves,two fish,twelve baskets,about five thousand men,two hundred pennies,and an approximate twenty-five-or-thirty furlongs retain source quantities. Two fish are an ordinary counted noun phrase rather than an invented pronoun pair. Pennies and furlongs remain source units without modern conversions. Thirty-eight years and first entrant remain explicit in the healing narrative.

## Flesh,food,and mutual indwelling

The claim my-flesh-is-food and my-blood-is-drink stays bodily and awkward. It is not smoothed into teaching,spiritual encouragement,or a selected sacramental doctrine. A new common drink-substance entry distinguishes what is drunk from the act of drinking and from an offering. Its transparent drink-food construction supplies the needed noun without a new inflectional paradigm. Indeed appears twice. Flesh profits nothing later in the same discourse remains beside the earlier flesh-as-food claims;the translator does not resolve their tension.

The eater dwells in plural-I and plural-I in him. Mutual indwelling preserves distinct grammatical locations and participants,not numerical sameness. The living Father sends,the speaker lives by Father,and the eater lives by the speaker. Living here is being alive,not income. Many disciples really leave after finding the saying hard;their departure is not rewritten as eventual agreement.

## Supplied text and contextual vocabulary

The Bethesda sheep market,five porches,waiting sick people,and full angel/first-entry verse remain as supplied. The unnamed feast is not assigned a festival identity. The warning against further sin is preserved without inventing a biography or cause of disability. John's feeding retains the Jesus-to-disciples-to-seated-people chain and the crowd's intended coercive kingship. The sea crossing retains immediate arrival. The single boat observation and parenthetical other boats remain separately scoped.

Three contextual proper entries identify Bethesda,Tiberias and Simon the father of Judas;Simon is not merged with Peter. Source religious accusations remain historical speaker statements,not translator judgments of modern groups. Ordinary lamp-light is not the personified Light entry. Commit-all-judgment uses giving rather than the lexical commit-perform sense. Sealing uses marking with a seal;prove uses testing;water disturbance uses disturb. Legal just uses righteous;number uses numerical reckoning. The approved50pt Thimfora,22pt reading columns,38pt leading,Maxima,aligned paragraphs,page geometry and Unta numbering are unchanged.
