# Daniel7–9: visionary roles,unresolved times and the confessing communal self

## 1. Personal witnesses and the prayer's singular-we
Daniel's visions and distress use personal I. Gabriel's8:19 and9:22–23 first-person interpretation also stays personal;delivering a divine message does not make its speaker divine plural-I.9:5–18 shifts to singular communal we/us and our-one possessives for the people across kings,princes,fathers,judges,sins and desolations. Those possessed nouns remain genuinely plural.9:16's personal I beseech thee sits within communal our sins and fathers;9:18's my God sits beside our supplications.9:20 explicitly confesses Daniel's own sin and the people's sin. The same speaker can be bodily personal and representative without a random agreement change. There is no divine first-person speech or actual-dual pronoun in these82verses;the source's distinctions take priority over increasing pronoun frequency.

## 2. Beasts,body parts and preserved arithmetic
The four winds and four beasts are literal source counts;reciprocal one from another is not a separate cardinal. The first lion-like beast receives a man's stance and heart through unnamed passives. The second is a bear animal,raised on one side with three ribs. Leopard wings and heads each number four. The fourth's ten horns and three removed former horns remain,without naming historical empires not yet supplied. Former correctly translates the first horns as the earlier set,not an added ordinal. Thousand thousands and ten-thousand times ten-thousand retain their products rather than becoming innumerable. Two ram horns,selected higher one,four replacement horns,four winds and2300days remain exact. No metrics,dates,three-and-a-half-year calculation or numerical Trinity is inserted.

## 3. Ancient of days and the approaching figure
Thrones cast down retains the supplied KJV reading without replacing it with an alternate placed reading. Ancient-of-days gains a source-role compound. White garment,wool-like hair,fire throne/wheels,stream,attendants,judgment and opened books remain distinct. One like the Son of man is an indefinite approaching figure,not a counted divine person;like,with clouds,to the Ancient,and they brought him near all remain. The roles are not silently merged or identified with later personal names. The same son-of-man phrase later addresses Daniel in8:17 and therefore cannot force one referent by string matching. Judgment given to the saints remains ambiguous between authority and favorable verdict. Saint uses a set-apart community/being sense,not later canonization.

## 4. Defeat,dominion and the divided time phrase
The horn really prevails against saints until the intervention;the text does not say only apparent defeat. Exhaust translates wear out rather than clothing. Time and times and the dividing of time preserves the supplied words,without supplying half or converting to years. The other beasts lose dominion yet have lives extended for season and time. Fourth beast/fourth kingdom and ten horns/ten kings retain both occurrences;another following king is not explicitly relabelled eleventh. Kingdom given to the people of saints,whose kingdom and serve him retain collective/singular shifts instead of resolving every antecedent. Daniel's closing distress and keeping the affair in his heart remain beside the opening written sum.

## 5. Interpreted identities and deliberately unresolved agents
Ulai and Gabriel get contextual name entries;Shushan and Elam reuse inherited identities. The ram's west/north/south directions do not acquire an unprinted east. The goat does not touch ground,no wings added. Choler is anger,waxed is became,host is army rather than hospitality,and practised is acted. The little horn's from one of them,by him,granted host and not-his-own power retain their compressed agents and antecedents. Only where8:20–21 supplies Media,Persia and Grecia are they named;no named first king or four successors is added. Craft in8:25 is deceit and by peace remains peace. Broken without hand remains unnamed agency. Evening/morning in8:26 is not used to halve8:14's2300days. None understood at8:27 remains despite the interpretation just given.

## 6. Collective confession without softened divine agency
Darius's stated Ahasuerus parent receives a contextual identity rather than an equation with the Esther king. Jeremiah's seventy years is explicit as years and distinct from later weeks. The prayer keeps four opening confessions,failed listening,near/far dispersion,face-shame and the kings/princes/fathers' guilt. Shame of faces in9:7 becomes source singular face in9:8. Mercy and forgivenesses remain plural. God confirms words by bringing great evil and watches over evil before bringing it;the active verbs are not replaced by passive permission. Evil names the harmful event in the source,not an extra translator accusation. Past failure to pray remains beside present prayer. Personal I petition,communal responsibility,my God/our God,and third-person Lord's sake coexist without smoothing. The three Lord addresses are rhetorical repetition,not a divine count.

## 7. Seventy weeks and an untranslated chronological problem
Gabriel is called man and is caused to fly swiftly,without added wings or a named causing agent. His personal I offers skill/understanding. Seventy weeks is kept as weeks;the source-cultural week entry does not authorize converting the prophecy into years. All six9:24purposes remain,including ritual reconciliation,vision/prophecy sealing and anointing the substantival most Holy without choosing person or place. Messiah adds the anointed-person title without an imposed later name. Seven and62weeks remain separate,not a substituted69. The cut-off Messiah not for himself preserves the KJV phrase. Flood,determined desolations,one week,midweek cessation,overspreading abominations,and final pouring upon the desolate retain their unresolved references. No date or doctrinal timetable is supplied.
