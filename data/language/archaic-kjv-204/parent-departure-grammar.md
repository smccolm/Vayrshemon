## From crew-we to personal responsibility

The response of the witnessing crowd is singular-we (5:26). Direct Jesus speech retains plural-I throughout calling,forgiveness and teaching. In6:42 the quoted would-be helper is personal-I inside the teacher's hypothetical speech;the quotation does not become a divine declaration.
