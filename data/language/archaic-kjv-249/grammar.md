# Historical KJV register 0.10-kjv.249 — Ephesians1–3

## One acting community, two prior constituencies

Ephesians2:14–18 makes the singular-we distinction productive. The two peoples named through circumcision/uncircumcision and near/far remain two groups; the resulting shared body is one. `wesg of two group:pl` says that the acting we is one whole with those two constituencies. It does not count two physical bodies, so physical `we:du` is intentionally absent. Two/one are retained in2:14–16 and2:18 rather than erased by the pronoun. Through him, by one Spirit and to the Father are distinct relations; no numerical identity or diagnosis is inserted.

The author uses personal-I for his hearing, writing, service, imprisonment and prayer. Shared singular-we marks the community's blessing, inheritance, former conduct, enlivening and access. Christ-in-your-hearts and the inner man do not automatically create plural-I: the plural-self choice remains contextual, as explicitly documented for the shared living subject of Galatians2:20. The KJV itself does not print a divine first-person speech in these three chapters.

## Gift, works and the compound body-building image

The not-of-works clause in2:9 and created-for-good-works in2:10 are both retained. Workmanship denotes what has been made, not the skill of its maker. The text's movement among body, household, building, growth, temple, roots and foundations remains mixed and deliberately chunky; it is not flattened into a single modern organizational metaphor. Chief corner stone, foundation of apostles and prophets, and building together have distinct roles. Killing enmity does not kill either people-group.

The economy of redemption, purchased possession, inheritance, pledge and riches remains audible. Riches uses the established wealth noun here because wealth is the actual metaphor; no material prosperity promise or transaction theory is added. Discursive course, attributed circumcision labels and shared citizenship use their contextual senses.

## Preserve the supplied witness and its unresolved relations

The present source includes at Ephesus1:1, understanding-eyes1:18, fellowship of the mystery3:9, all-created-by-Jesus-Christ3:9, and Father-of-our-Lord-Jesus-Christ3:14. All remain. Whole family3:15 is not replaced with every family, and in Christ3:6 does not gain an unprinted Jesus. Faith-of-him3:12 retains its genitive instead of silently becoming faith-in-him. The that of2:8 retains its broad reference; the translation does not independently settle its scope.

Dispensation is entrusted ordering/administration, not a chart of later theological ages. Fellowship in3:9 is separately recorded as the supplied shared-participation reading. Mystery means a concealed matter now made known; it is not automatically an intrinsically unknowable contradiction. The as-now qualification in3:5 prevents strengthening the claim into absolute prior ignorance.

## Dependent clauses, exaggeration and sacred awkwardness

The linked clauses1:15–19,2:4–7 and3:1–7 preserve their dependency across verse boundaries. Personal-I can remain implicit after its introduction; no new speaker is invented at each numbered verse. Exceeding power, less than the least, knowing love beyond knowledge, fullness filling all in all and world-without-end remain strained expressions. The four dimensions in3:18 are breadth, length, depth and height without invented measurements.

Source claims are translated as source claims. This edition neither endorses nor repairs their theology. Personal prayer, corporate identity and Spirit indwelling remain grammatically distinguishable without modern clinical language.
