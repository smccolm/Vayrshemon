# KJV historical register 0.10-kjv.93 — service-body, imperial self and remainder

An incomplete continuation of the ONE-Bible edition: 2 Chronicles 29–32 adds 117 authored verses. Twenty earlier contextual sense errors are repaired only in new working copies. The register remains deliberately archaic and excluded from modernization. The modern consolidated standard and every prior historical seal remain unchanged. These are authored literary decisions and translator self-review, not claims of independently certified translation.

## One service-body, several people fed

The inherited singular-we `wesg` represents the coordinated temple service reporting its completed cleansing in 29:18–19. Hezekiah's personal `my/heart` in 29:10 holds his resolve, while the threatened community from which wrath should turn is `wesg`. His paternal address to the Levites as sons of the crown uses `mypl/son:pl` in 29:11, without a biological paternity claim. In 31:10, by contrast, `wepl` reports multiple people's sufficient eating and surplus: bodily recipients are not flattened into the earlier service-body. Context motivates the alternation; it is not randomized ornament.

## The imperial plural-self and a quoted common defense

Sennacherib's `ipl` and `mypl` in 32:13–17 gather crown, ancestral conquests and military agency into one speaking imperial self. They do not multiply his physical bodies or import a clinical diagnosis. The defenders' `wesg` and `ourone` in 32:7–8 claim one besieged common body. In 32:11 those forms recur inside the enemy's quotation of Hezekiah's promise; they do not convert Sennacherib to the defenders' God. No Trinity count is supplied in these verses. The plural-I/singular-we resources remain available for later passages when their discourse warrants them.

## Remainder is not repose

`rest` (dlen) denotes resting, repose or metaphorical settled support; `remain` (dlenÛemin) denotes staying or remaining. The construction `remain:cv part` expresses a remainder. It replaces erroneous repose-root uses in Exodus 28:10; Leviticus 5:9; Numbers 31:8,32; Deuteronomy 3:13; Joshua 10:20;13:27;17:2,6;21:5,34; 1 Chronicles 24:20; and 2 Chronicles 9:29;13:22;20:34;24:14;25:26;26:22;27:7;28:26. Existing case suffixes attach to `part`, for example `remain:cv part:n`. This narrow error repair changes no paradigm and modernizes no historic form. Sabbath-rest, relief from war, the ark at rest, and the people resting on words in 32:8 retain the repose expression. New 30:6 remnant and 32:32 remaining acts use the remainder construction from the outset.

## Interrupted clauses and shared negation

Verse boundaries do not manufacture new sentences. The pardon request in 30:18 deliberately continues through the qualifying relative clause in 30:19; the opening circumstance in 32:2 continues to 32:3. The distribution clauses in 31:15–19 retain the source's crowded syntax. The coordinated `nor` and `neither` clauses share the preceding negative scope, as in 29:7,30:3 and 32:15. They must not be read as a positive command to offer or believe merely because the second finite word lacks an additional negative suffix. This preserves the inherited construction rather than adding a new universal negation rule.

## Ritual senses, material senses and awkward agencies

Ritual uncleanness uses `kjv_impurity_ritual`, clean status `kjv_clean_ritual`, sacrificial fat `kjv_fat`, and reconciliation `kjv_reconcile_ritual`. The service courses use `kjv_service_order`, the shewbread table `kjv_table_furniture`, the temple court `kjv_court_enclosure`, and the porter `kjv_gatekeeper`. Corn remains grain; substance is goods; comfortable speech is comforting, not soft furniture. Reassurance and ritual healing receive no invented medical diagnosis. The narration's God leaving Hezekiah to test him so that he might know his heart in 32:31 remains epistemically awkward; no omniscience explanation or substitution of show-to-others is inserted.

## Calendar positions, durations and quantities

The 29:17 first-day/first-month, eighth-day, eight-day duration and sixteenth-day/first-month sequence is preserved without an inclusive-counting correction. Passover is deliberately in month two, day fourteen. The first seven-day feast and the additional seven days remain separate; the repeated decision and fulfillment in 30:23 do not create a third week. Four groups of seven animals, 70/100/200 burnt offerings, 600/3000 consecrated animals, and donor-specific 1000/7000 versus 1000/10000 gifts retain their categories. The male registration threshold of three years and Levite threshold of twenty are not reconciled into one adult age. The literal one-heart and one-altar use cardinal `one_num`, never the lexical oneness noun. No 185000 death count or fifteen-year extension is imported from Kings into this shorter account.

## Local names and inherited identity

Forty-three proper-name entries preserve local relationships without merging homonyms. Abijah, Hezekiah's mother, remains the supplied form beside Abi in Kings. Cleansing and store rosters retain distinct fathers and assignments; same-spelled names do not prove identity. Existing Jerusalem-Gihon is used for the watercourse, not the Genesis river. Gad is the prophet/seer and Zadok the priestly ancestral house. Surface forms may be shared by distinct named persons; dictionary entry counts therefore differ from newly coined word-form counts. No additional common root, inflectional ending or pronoun paradigm is introduced here.
