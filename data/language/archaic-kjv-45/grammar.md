## The public self has a body but is not always that body

Saul's warning to the Kenites uses plural-I for the destructive expedition acting through its king. His later insistence on obedience and admission of sin use personal-I: responsibility, fear and requested honour are his. His claim that we destroyed the remainder uses singular-we for the expedition as a whole, contrasted with the people he blames for sparing the valuable animals. The language therefore marks a shift in responsibility without endorsing the defense.

Goliath identifies his individual body as a Philistine and offers it for combat using personal-I. The proposed political servitude uses singular-we and whole-possessive ourone, since he claims to bind his side by his personal outcome. His public defiance in17:10 uses plural-I for representative force, then returns to personal me for the opponent's approach and dual-we for the two prospective combatants. This is an authored discourse distinction, not a claim about Hebrew morphology, a mandatory royal plural or extra physical bodies.

## Quotation boundaries protect personal and divine reference

Divine instruction and recollection use plural-I. In16:2 the deity supplies Samuel's future words, I am come to sacrifice. That embedded I is Samuel's personal-I, as is the actual explanation in16:5. Carrying a divine commission does not turn the messenger into the deity. David likewise comes personally in the LORD's name; he does not become divine plural-self by invoking that name.

Samuel's waiting company uses plural-we in16:11; the servants' our lord uses plural possessors of wepl. They are several participants, not a fabricated pair. In17:47 Israel receives the enemy into singular-we's plural hands: collective unity does not erase bodily plurality. The true dual remains available for the two proposed combatants.

## Repentance is allowed to contradict itself

The same inherited change-of-mind stem is used for the divine statement in15:11, Samuel's denial in15:29 and the narrator's statement in15:35. Replacing these with unrelated senses merely to remove a contradiction would modernize the theological difficulty. The stem indicates reconsidering a prior intention, without importing the morally guilty sense of human repentance into every occurrence.

Samuel's refusal to return in15:26 remains beside his return in15:31. Saul's claim of obedience remains beside his admission of sin and fear of the people. His request for honour before elders stays explicit. Thy God is not softened into our God in his sacrificial explanations and plea.

## Violence and value judgments remain attributed

The command names men, women, infants, sucklings and animals. The translation neither removes those categories nor invents their individual guilt. Agag's survival and the destruction of all the people are separate supplied claims. Selective sparing of good livestock contrasts with destruction of rejected things; refuse is rejected material, not the verb declining an offer.

The analogy of rebellion to witchcraft remains an analogy, not a new event in which Saul performs magic. In15:33 this supplied text says the sword, not thy sword. The translation does not add the possessive. The subsequent killing is retained without added graphic detail. David's and Goliath's violent predictions similarly remain attributed speech.

## Anointing does not tidy appearance, spirits or recognition

The warning against outward appearance remains beside David's ruddy beauty. The seven sons passing before Samuel are not added to the three named earlier to invent ten rejected sons; the later eight-son statement remains. Ruddy is a colour description without a modern ethnicity or diagnosis.

The evil spirit is explicitly from the LORD or God in both narration and reported speech. No substituted Satan, merely permitted agency, modern diagnosis or universal treatment claim is introduced. Harp playing brings the narrated relief. The servant's warrior description of David remains beside Saul's later description of him as inexperienced youth. Saul's named summons, affection and correspondence with Jesse remain beside his later questions about David's father. The translator supplies no invented legal purpose or chronology to make these smooth.

## Historical objects need separate lexical senses

The new physical shield noun explicitly converts the inherited protect/shield stem. Target-shield, mail-coat, greave, sling-tool and sheath distinguish equipment from actions, practice marks and writing. The sling noun is separate from the inherited slinging verb. Camp-trench is distinct from the old verbal scar-cutting sense; baggage reuses the already established carriage sense. Musical cunning uses existing skilled, not deceit. The reason question uses explanatory reason, not the verb cause.

The KJV target is retained as a defensive shield.
