## Vine and cultivator

The plural-self is the vine,the Father the cultivator,and the addressed people branches. These are related roles,not three counted persons. Fruit-bearing is produce,not the inherited birth verb bear. Purging retains the plant-cleansing image and the following clean-through-word statement. Branch removal,wither and burning remain,including the source shift from a singular nonremaining person to plural gathered branches. Nothing-without-me is not weakened to little. Servants become friends because what was heard from Father was made known;that information chain remains alongside obedience and appointment.

The quoted hated-me-without-cause clause retains the psalm's personal-I inside the plural-self's framing discourse. Earlier psalm seals are unchanged. Both-seen-and-hated/both-me-and-Father is coordination rather than an imposed physical dual. No source dual is introduced in these chapters. A cloak for sin retains the covering image rather than replacing it with a newly invented legal acquittal.

## Distinct Spirit speech and the disciples' communal hearing

The Comforter is sent by the speaker from Father and proceeds from Father. The source does not supply a numerical doctrine or a later dispute about procession,and neither is added. The Spirit hears,speaks,receives,shows,and glorifies. Not-speaking-of-himself preserves received speech rather than treating every speech act as one undifferentiated self. Sin,righteousness and judgment each retain their supplied explanation.

The disciples' what-does-he-mean uses singular-we. Their quotation retains Jesus's plural-I,so quote boundaries determine the pronoun referent. Their later we-are-sure remains a reported communal assertion before Jesus's do-you-now-believe question. No witness certainty is silently converted into narrative certification. The childbirth analogy uses actual birth morphology here;bear-many-things elsewhere is endure. I-will-see-you remains that direction,not changed into you-will-see-me. Veiled-saying is a new hide-word lexical entry for the proverbs contrasted with plain speech,distinct from the older concise-maxim entry.

I-do-not-say-that-I-will-pray keeps negation on the saying clause;it does not become a universal refusal to pray or a correction of14:16. Alone-yet-not-alone and peace-beside-tribulation retain both sides. Each-to-his-own remains unexpanded rather than inventing a particular home or destination.

## A singular-we with an explicit one predicate

The prayer directly addresses Father with singular thou while its speaker uses plural-I. Third-person thy-Son and the name Jesus Christ remain where printed. Only-true-God address,pre-world glory,finished work,and pre-foundation love stay explicit without added doctrinal arithmetic. In17:11,21and22the Father/speaker community uses singular-we;the lexical predicate one remains beside it. We-one-are-one is intentionally awkward and semantically different from a physical pair count. The people are also to be one,and the repeated directional relations I-in-them/thou-in-I/they-in-we remain clauses rather than a replacement pronoun.

All-mine/yours reciprocates possession without erasing giver and recipient. The prayer for these/not-world and its later extension to future believers both remain. Not-of-world stands beside not-removal-from-world. Son-of-perdition is son-of-destruction without adding an unprinted name. Sanctifying self does not assert prior impurity. The source's one predicates are checked individually;reciprocal each-other is not counted. Existing morphology and lexicon are reused except the single contextual veiled-saying entry,no new proper names or endings.
