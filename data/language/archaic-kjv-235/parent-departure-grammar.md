# Romans 4–6: many descendants, one shared life, and disputed accounting

## The divine plural-self and the letter's singular-whole

The cited I-have-made-you in 4:17 is divine plural-self. Abraham remains the singular addressee made father of many nations. The identity structure is therefore not a flat conversion of every first person into the same number: divine I-plural speaks to a personal you about many descendants. The citation keeps that asymmetry without naming an unprinted count of divine persons.

The argument-we in 4:1/9 and the us-all in 4:16 represent a shared speaking community. The believers addressed through 4:24 and chapters 5–6 likewise use singular-we for the whole. Our Father Abraham, our Lord, our offences and our justification retain communal possession. Our hearts in 5:5 remains a plural possessed noun: a singular-whole possessor can possess several hearts. This grammar does not require the members to share one literal organ.

Our old man in 6:6, in contrast, keeps its singular noun. The common prior self and body-of-sin image sits beside many bodily members and the communal we. The translation deliberately lets that feel awkward. It does not turn old man into an unmentioned elderly individual, a named ancestor, or a clinical explanation of personality. The personal I in 6:19 explicitly marks speaking after the manner of humans; it differs from both communal we and the quoted divine plural-self.

## Crediting is not counting

Counted, reckoned and imputed in chapter 4 use the established accounting-of-standing vocabulary. They are not the ordinary counting of physical objects and not the noble title count. Reward reckoned as debt differs from grace. Believing in the one who justifies the ungodly is retained without changing the recipient into an already deserving person.

Circumcision remains a sign and a seal of the righteousness of faith held in the uncircumcised state. The repeated circumcision/uncircumcision terms and the not-only relations are necessary to the argument. They are not shortened by silently dropping a negation. The world-heir promise is to Abraham or his seed; seed uses the descendant sense, not a new literal plant reference or an inserted single later descendant.

About a hundred years is explicitly approximate. The supplied considered-not in 4:19 remains, beside his now-dead body and Sarah's womb-deadness. These are the source images; no medical diagnosis, modern reproductive account or reading from a different textual witness replaces them. Staggering in unbelief is the source faltering metaphor. The text does not diagnose a gait problem.

## One, many and all must not be normalized

Chapter 5 repeats one man, one offence, one sinner, one righteousness and one obedience against many or all. Each printed one remains numerical. The undefined many receives no invented total. The two all-men clauses in 5:18 both remain all; the many of 5:19 remains many. Neither is changed to settle later arguments about salvation's scope.

The second one in 5:16 leaves its noun unstated as the source does. The three ones in 5:17 retain their separate positions: one man's offence, death reigning by one, and life reigning by one Jesus Christ. The latter one is not the indefinite person-one of 5:7. Likewise, once in 6:10 is an explicit occurrence count, distinct from the shared death imagery elsewhere.

Death passing to all because all sinned in 5:12 is retained without substituting an unstated inherited-guilt mechanism. Sin present before law, sin not imputed without law, and no transgression where there is no law remain separate statements. The translator does not eliminate their awkward relation by inserting a commentary into the verse.

## Peace, admission and received atonement

We have peace in 5:1 keeps the supplied indicative. Access by faith in 5:2 retains that printed relation. Access is entry-way, a relational admission image without a literal door or fee. Experience in 5:4 is tested-standing in the patience-to-hope chain, not merely having undergone an event or travelled widely.

The love of God in 5:5 keeps its supplied possessive relation and pouring image. Holy Ghost is spirit, not an apparition. God, the Son whose death reconciles, and the Spirit given to the community remain distinct referents in the argument without added numerical arithmetic.

The received atonement in 5:11 shares the established historical atonement form but records the reconciliation relation of this passage. It is not an instruction to perform the earlier altar procedure. Death reconciles and life saves in 5:10; present justification and future salvation in 5:9 remain different tense relations. The free gift is grace-gift, distinct from a debt or wage and from an object freed from captivity.

## Dead together, free into service

The rejected question in 6:1 asks about continuing in sin for grace to abound. Its answer uses existing exclamatory morphology. The later question in 6:15 has its own refusal. Neither becomes an endorsed instruction, and no unsupported exclamation glyph is introduced.

Baptized into Christ and into his death remain repeated relations. The translation adds no immersion or sprinkling procedure. Buried with him, planted together, old man crucified, body of sin destroyed, and alive from the dead keep the supplied bodily images rather than becoming abstract doctrinal summaries. Freed in 6:7 is retained as freed rather than silently substituted with justified.

Members are body-parts, not an organization's roster. Mortal body means a body capable of dying, not already dead. The source's singular body with plural addressees remains. The body-parts can be presented as instruments of unrighteousness or righteousness; the word instruments need not invent weapons or a musical performance.

Free from sin followed by servants of righteousness remains a deliberate reversal. Free from righteousness in 6:20 is also retained, although uncomfortable. The supplied form-of-doctrine delivered-to-you relation remains in 6:17. Iniquity unto iniquity in 6:19 is repeated rather than simplified. Sin's wages and God's gift in 6:23 are different economic relations, with no invented salary or earning of the gift.
