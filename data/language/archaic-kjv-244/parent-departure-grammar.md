# KJV 0.10-kjv.243 · 2 Corinthians1–4 · comfort, veil and shared life

This frozen historical continuation adds77verses and9lexical senses (6common,3contextual proper). All prior targets, inflectional paradigms and seals remain unchanged. Whole Bible remains incomplete. Source-critical notes and explicit analyses accompany every verse; this is translator self-review, not independent literary certification.

## Shared suffering and personal testimony
The sender community speaks as `wesg`, singular-whole we, with `ourone` possessions. It includes three named proclaimers in1:19; no physical dual is licensed. Personal plans, appeals and witness testimony use `i`. The quotation “I believed” in4:13 remains a human personal-I before the community adopts its pattern. Suffering, despair and perplexity do not themselves create plural-self. Our-one hearts remain plural in1:22,3:2,4:6; our-one body is singular in4:10. The inward/outward man in4:16 is a contrastive image, not two literal persons or a diagnosis.

## Comfort, pledge and difficult correspondence
Comfort moves from God to the community and onward to those in any trouble. All/any scopes, suffering/comfort alternatives and affliction above strength remain. Delivered/delivers/will-deliver in1:10 retains three tenses. Conversation means conduct; simplicity guileless integrity; lightness fickleness; earnest a pledge. Second benefit is ordinal. Yes-yes/no-no, the painful letter, sufficient punishment by many and forgiveness-plus-comfort stay deliberately uneasy. In-part grief and in-Christ's-person forgiveness are not converted to confident modern reconstructions of an offender or ecclesiastical office.

## Aroma and distinct written things
Death-to-death and life-to-life aromas retain repeated relations and the question of sufficiency. One recipient/other indicates classes, not a count of individuals. The KJV causes-to-triumph and corrupt-the-word readings remain. Epistle `letter` in3:1–3 is distinct from written-law letter in3:6. Our hearts, ink, Spirit, stone tablets and flesh tablets preserve the source contrasts. Tablet uses the established writing-tablet sense, not meal furniture. The source's unflattering death-ministry still has real glory; in-this-respect qualifies the later no-glory comparison.

## Veil, mirror and Spirit
Moses' veil, the end of what passes away, blinded minds and the unspecified “it” that turns remain without harmonizing their referents. The polemic is attributed to this text. “The Lord is that Spirit” and “Spirit of the Lord” both remain explicit; the translator adds no numerical Trinity. Uncovered-face mirror-beholding in3:18 follows the supplied KJV, not another witness's reflecting formulation. Shared singular-we can include all participants while their transformation is into the same image.

## Earthen life and temporal limitation
God of this world remains the printed designation without an inserted proper name. Trouble/not-distress and perplexity/not-despair are rhetorical pairs. Jesus' dying and life, mortal flesh, death in us/life in you, and by-Jesus resurrection stay distinct. Light affliction is low weight, counterposed with eternal glory-weight; it is not illumination. Temporal means time-bounded, contrasted with eternal. The seen/unseen negation scopes remain explicit and are not expanded into a translator-supplied metaphysical system.
