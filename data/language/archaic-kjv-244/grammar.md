# KJV 0.10-kjv.244 · 2 Corinthians5–8 · reconciliation and willing gifts

These notes and the verse-by-verse back-readings are translator self-review, not independent literary certification.

## Shared bodies, personal testimony and quoted plural-self
The tent, clothing, ministry and ambassador images use shared singular-we. Our-one mouth and heart in6:11 are singular; our-one hearts in7:3 remain plural. Personal I-say, trust, grief, advice and testimony use `i`. Divine speech quoted in6:2,6:16–18 uses `ipl` and `mypl`: dwelling among the people, being their God and receiving sons and daughters. The singular father and sanctuary predicates remain; no numerical Trinity is added. Beside-ourselves in5:13 remains a rhetorical alternative contrasted with composure, not a diagnosis or a license to replace communal-we with plural-I.

## Clothing and reconciliation without explanatory repairs
The earthly tent-house, divine building and clothing-upon overlap deliberately. The writer desires over-clothing rather than unclothing; mortality is swallowed by life. One died for all retains its cardinal and if/then wording. Each body-deed recipient is distributive, not another counted one. Relational reconciliation is distinguished from earlier ritual reconciliation. God-in-Christ, the world, their trespasses, the envoy mediation and made-sin predicate remain explicit; sin-offering is not substituted as an explanatory repair.

## Difficult comfort, grief and the painful letter
Decision-regret in7:8 differs from repentance toward salvation in7:9–10. The personal speaker does not regret though he did regret; the change is not erased. Care means attentive concern, not careful manufacture. The supplied our-care-for-you in7:12 and your-comfort in7:13 remain. The seven responses in7:11 include diligence, clearing, indignation, fear, longing, zeal and revenge. Innocence-clearing records an attributed response, not an invented tribunal. Exclamatory morphology carries the force without a Thimfora exclamation mark.

## Willing giving and equality
Forwardness in8 is eagerness, not spatial frontness. Personal advice and the denial of command coexist with comparison to others. Giving is evaluated by what someone has, not what they lack. Their deep poverty and generosity-riches retain the paradox. Your abundance/their lack and their abundance/your lack remain reciprocal, with equality repeated; no unprinted levy, amount or modern political system is added. First giving selves and first willing mind are precedence expressions; the preceding-year interval is not expanded into a precise date.

## Envoys, pairs and public accountability
Titus and an unnamed gospel-praised brother form the pair with whom another brother is sent in8:22. In8:23 Titus is treated separately from the two unnamed brothers, so the dual there refers to that different pair. The collective them in8:24 is not forced into a dual for all three. No proposed later identity is inserted for either brother. Selection by assemblies, administration of the gift, the supplied your-ready-mind wording and honest conduct before both Lord and people remain explicit.
