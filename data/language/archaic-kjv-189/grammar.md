## Speaking identity and physical pairs

Jesus's first-person utterances and their object forms use plural-I; my-pl appears in sake/name/Father constructions. This is the chosen speaking-identity register, not a new count of deities. The centurion,scribe,bereaved disciple and woman use personal I. The centurion's being under authority does not make his self-description a royal plural.

The boat company says singular-we in shared danger. The two possessed humans are a physical dual in narrative; their voiced plea and the explicitly plural devils' subsequent joint request use singular-we. That grammatical choice expresses shared petition,not an inferred number of spirits or a clinical diagnosis. John's disciples' singular-we is joined to Pharisees as another group. Our-one infirmities/sicknesses remains communal possession inside the Isaiah quotation,without altering the older Isaiah target.

The two blind men use explicit we-dual in their request,they-dual in narration,and you-dual in Jesus's answer. Jesus plural-I and human you-dual coexist in one question. Dual possession is expressed analytically with "of they-dual/you-dual" rather than inventing a possessive paradigm. The outgoing two at 9:32 are distinguished from unnamed bringers of another man. Neither a plural body-part noun nor wine-and-bottles "both" creates an extra physical pair count.

## Authority, willingness and embedded voices

The leprosy supplicant asks whether Jesus is willing; "I will" answers willingness,while "I will come" is future action. The centurion's three commands retain their obedient responses. The Father's Spirit speaking in the commissioned people retains the negative "not you" beside a singular Father and plural addressees. Reception connects disciples,Jesus and the sender without collapsing every referent into one pronoun or adding a numerical Trinity formula.

Blasphemy and devil-prince accusations remain attributed speech. Narrated source spirit-possession and sickness categories are not equated with contemporary medical diagnoses. The ruler's already-dead daughter report remains beside Jesus's sleeping claim without harmonization or an inserted diagnosis. Deliberate repetition in commissions,acknowledgment/denial and allegiance comparisons remains.
