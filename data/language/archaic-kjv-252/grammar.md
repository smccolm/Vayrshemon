# Historical KJV register 0.10-kjv.252 — Colossians1–4

## Shared affliction, communal life and distinct participants

Colossians1:24 uses contextual `ipl` and `mypl` for the explicitly shared Christ-affliction filled in the author's flesh for Christ's body,the assembly. This extends the shared-living register of Galatians2:20 and Philippians1:21 into shared affliction; it neither diagnoses distress nor multiplies biological bodies. The immediately following ministry appointment1:25 returns to personal-I. Working-in-me1:29 stays personal-I because influence or agency alone is not an automatic plural-self trigger.

Singular-we presents the ministry community's thanks,hearing,prayer,deliverance,redemption and proclamation as joined action. Our-one life3:4 preserves the source's our while the following you stays second person. Flesh-absent/spirit-present2:5 is one author's relational contrast,not a reason to create plural-I. Physical dual is confined to4:9's two messengers,Tychicus and Onesimus. Named persons,shared bodies and divine relations keep distinct grammatical roles. All divine fullness bodily is not a numerical count.

## Image, fullness and the difficult divine relations

Image of the invisible God,firstborn of every creature,firstborn from the dead,head of body/assembly and preeminence remain distinct source expressions. Created by him and for him retain agency and purpose;consist means hold together. The source's all things,visible/invisible and four authority categories remain without an invented hierarchy. All fullness1:19 and all fullness of Godhead bodily2:9 are not flattened into the same generic label.

The supplied mystery-of-God-and-of-Father-and-of-Christ2:2 remains awkwardly explicit rather than replaced by another witness's shorter text. Through his blood1:14 remains. Christ in you1:27 and life with Christ in God3:3 remain relations;no unprinted divine first-person speech or doctrinal arithmetic is introduced. Reconciliation retains the repeated by-him and himself relations,with the author's parenthetic I-say1:20 left personal.

## Body, handwriting and disputed ordinances

The full body-of-the-sins-of-the-flesh2:11 and without-hands wording remain,as do shared burial/rising and faith of God's operation2:12. No baptism mode is added. The supplied you-forgiven-all-trespasses2:13 stays you. Handwriting of ordinances is a written record in erasure/removal/cross-fastening imagery,not a penmanship style. Principalities and powers are displayed in triumph without invented human victims or practical violence.

Philosophy is wisdom-seeking,with the source's separate vain-deceit,human-tradition/world-rudiments and not-Christ criteria left explicit. Rudiments inherits the Galatians entry rather than inventing an atomic theory or named spirit list. Hath not seen2:18 remains the supplied negative;intruding and puffed mind receive contextual senses without diagnosis. Bands in the growing body are binding connections,not groups of people. Touch/taste/handle prohibitions are quoted as the questioned ordinances. Will worship,humility,body neglect and the difficult final not-in-any-honour-to-flesh-satisfaction2:23 remain instead of a silently confident explanatory paraphrase.

## Clothing, household instructions and named correspondence

Mortify3:5 retains the moral body metaphor tied to its named vices without a practical self-injury instruction. Put-off/put-on keeps clothing imagery,including embodied bowels of mercy. Forbearing others means enduring patiently,not restraining oneself. Charity is love,and God peace3:15 retains God rather than another witness's Christ. Psalms,hymns and spiritual songs remain separate labels even when inherited forms overlap. The song destination remains the Lord.

The historical household asymmetry remains,including own husbands,unfree servants,all things,heart sincerity,and obligations against bitterness,provocation and partiality. Masters owe what is right and equal;the text is not falsely modernized into employment. In4:8,the messenger is to know your state,not the reverse. Marcus is Barnabas's sister's son,not a silently changed cousin. Jesus called Justus is a different person from Jesus Christ and earlier Justus namesakes. Nymphas has his house in this source. Epaphras remains distinct from Epaphroditus,and his great zeal4:13 is retained. The letter from Laodicea remains unnamed and is not reconstructed or identified with another book.
