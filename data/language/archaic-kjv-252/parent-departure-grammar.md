# Historical KJV register 0.10-kjv.251 — Philippians1–4

## Shared living, personal agency and the one community

Philippians1:21 uses `ipl`: to I-many, living Christ is. This is a contextual continuation of Galatians2:20's shared-living construction, not a universal replacement of Paul's I. Personal-I remains in thanksgiving,uncertainty,physical life,travel,grief and the remembered ancestry. The next verse's flesh-life and choice return to personal-I. Between two1:23 counts the stated alternatives,not selves; death and departure remain reported desires without an added method or instruction.

Singular-we marks the community acting in worship3:3,qualified shared maturity3:15,common walking3:16,example3:17 and expectation3:20. Possessive our-one keeps the communal Father,Lord,citizenship and body relations. The community still has many individual members. Physical dual occurs only at4:2,where two separately named women receive the same appeal. No first-person divine speech is manufactured in2:5–11; the source's God,Christ,himself,Father and Lord relations remain in their printed grammatical roles. No divine arithmetic or diagnosis is introduced.

## Keep the difficult form and servanthood passage difficult

The translation keeps in-form-of-God,thought-not-robbery-to-be-equal-with-God,no-reputation,form-of-servant and likeness-of-humans as distinct constructions. It does not silently substitute a different grasping formulation,turn no reputation into nonexistence,or settle the metaphysical relation. Fashion means appearance. Humbling and obedience culminate in the specifically named cross death; exaltation and name-giving retain God as their agent and him as their object.

Every knee retains heaven,earth and under-earth locations without equating the last with a newly specified realm. Every tongue's confession and glory to God the Father remain separate. Working one's salvation2:12 stands beside God's working both willing and doing2:13. The source's unresolved agency tension is deliberate in this archaic register. Offering upon the recipients' faith sacrifice2:17 remains conditional and figurative rather than a completed death report.

## Cutting, reckoning and pursuit

Concision is a new cutting-group noun with the source's polemical bite,kept distinct from circumcision in3:3. Dogs and evil workers are not sanitized,nor expanded into an accusation against an entire unprinted ethnicity. Eight-day circumcision is ordinal. Israel stock,tribe of Benjamin,Hebrew of Hebrews and Pharisee retain the author's personally claimed credentials. Persecution and blamelessness remain juxtaposed rather than morally reconciled by the translator.

Count is reckon,not numeral enumeration; former gains become losses,and the raw dung image remains. Faith of Christ and by faith3:9 retain different relations. Apprehend means seize in the active/passive pursuit image,not simply understand. Not already perfect3:12 and as many as perfect3:15 remain in tension. Mark3:14 is a goal,mark3:17 noticing; neither uses the inscription sense. The full same-rule and same-thing clauses of3:16 are preserved. Vile body3:21 means lowly condition,not moral filth or a medical diagnosis.

## Bodily affection, civic belonging and material help

The bowels idiom stays bodily and awkward in1:8 and2:1. It is not erased into a general feeling. Conversation1:27 is conduct; conversation3:20 is a new heavenly civic-belonging sense,distinct from purchased Roman citizenship and spoken conversation. Palace1:13 remains the supplied palace rather than a silently substituted guard designation. Naturally caring2:20 means genuine concern; Epaphroditus's sorrow2:26 concerns what the recipients heard about his illness.

Bishops and deacons are separate role nouns without a later institutional hierarchy. Yokefellow is a common metaphor,not an invented person's name. Moderation means gentle forbearance; careful4:6 means anxious,but care4:10 is concern. Flourished-again4:10 is renewed growth,not music,and the existing care despite absent opportunity remains explicit. Honest4:8 is honourable,just righteous,and virtue moral excellence. All six qualities and two if-any clauses stay. Giving,receiving,account,fruit,fragrance and sacrifice preserve the gift passage's mixed metaphors. Caesar's household does not imply Caesar himself joined the community. Explicit through-Christ4:13 and with-you-all4:23 follow the supplied KJV.
