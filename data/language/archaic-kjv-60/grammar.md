# Historical continuation60: dynastic fractures and the widow's accusation

## 1. Royal treaty-self and divine quoted self

Asa's treaty negotiation in15:19 uses ipl for the public royal party, gift-sender and party from whom Baasha should withdraw; my/father remains the genealogical relation. In16:2–3 the LORD's ipl/mypl belongs to a new quotation centre addressing Baasha. In17:4,9 divine ipl commands ravens and a widow, whereas Elijah's17:1 i standing before that god and my/word remain the prophet's personal delegated speech. A messenger's authority does not automatically make the messenger the divine plural self.

## 2. A real pair alongside collective singular-we

The widow explicitly names me and my son in17:12. The eating-and-dying we is therefore we:du, a true pair; it is not replaced by plural individuals or singular-whole merely to maximize use of a favoured paradigm. This preserves the earlier people's singular-we as a distinct political/household-whole strategy.17:15 separately mentions she, he and her house. Do not use that later household phrase to erase the earlier explicit pair, or infer a precise total household census. No new wesg is inserted into passages containing no suitable collective first-person assertion.

## 3. The widow's bleak expectation and priority of food

The meal is flour, the dress action preparation, and the little oil remains in a cruse. Two sticks are counted. The anticipated eating followed by death remains, not replaced by reassurance. Elijah's cake is requested first, before food for the woman and her son; reversing that order would change the scene's ethical discomfort. The promise then names flour-barrel and oil-cruse separately until rain. The new deplete verb is empty-become, avoiding an ambiguous rubbish/waste noun. It describes exhausted supply, not the claim that no individual grain ever leaves the container.

## 4. Personal challenge, bodily breath and soul-return

The widow's17:18 question accuses the visitor of recalling her sin and killing her son. Elijah's17:20 question asks his god whether he has brought evil on the host widow by slaying the child. Both remain questions with personal i/my, not newly established narrator facts or softened orthodox paraphrases. The bodily lack of breath, loft ascent, three stretches, soul-return and downward reunion are preserved as narrated. Three counts the stretches, not divine persons; no medical diagnosis or mechanism is supplied. The woman's final now-I-know remains her stated recognition, contrasting with chapter13's false message without inventing a universal religious truth test.

## 5. Dynastic violence and object relations

In16:4 dogs and birds are the agents and dead members of Baasha's house are marked objects. The repeated wall-urinating insult remains direct; one in16:11 is a human anaphor, unlike cardinal one_num in ages or counts. Kin and friends remain in the threatened/actual scope. The source's no-breath-left claim and attribution to a prior word are retained without inventing a body count or a separate translator endorsement of killing relatives. Zimri's house-burning death is stated at the source's level, without added procedure or speculation about his psychology.

## 6. Chronological and relational tensions

Mother Maachah daughter of Abishalom is retained in both15:2 and15:10; do not silently write grandmother or replace Abishalom by another parallel form.15:6 still names Rehoboam, followed by Abijam's own war in15:7. High places survive beside Asa's perfect-heart claim. The because-he-killed-him ending in16:7 keeps its ambiguity and uncomfortable relation to fulfilled judgment rather than assigning an invented named victim. Samaria's hill/city naming in16:24 remains beside the prior Samaria mention. Hiel's foundation in Abiram and gates in Segub keep the supplied opaque relation; no invented construction-sacrifice procedure or death mechanism.

## 7. Quantities and material senses

Accession ordinals are distinct from reign durations. Abijam's18th-year/3years, Asa's20th-year/41years, Nadab'ssecond-year/2years, Baasha'sthird-year/24years, Elah's26th-year/2years, Zimri's27th-year/7days, Omri's31st-year/12years/6atTirzah and Ahab's38th-year/22years are not silently reconciled. Half the chariots differs from two factions, each called half the people. Two silver talents are the hill price, without modern conversion. A league is an alliance, not the inherited distance unit; left treasury is remaining, not the left-hand direction; light thing is small in seriousness, not illumination; rear altar is erect, not rear a child. Wood is building material, not a forest, and foot disease is not assigned a modern diagnosis.

## 8. Lexical and proper-name additions

Thirty proper records retain named royal, prophetic and local identities. Jehu son of Hanani is not automatically a later royal Jehu; Jehoshaphat son of Asa is not the court recorder; Ahijah father of Baasha is not the Shilonite prophet. Abiram and Segub are Hiel's sons in this context. Six common records add Bethelite, Tishbite, barrel, morsel, loft and deplete. Existing forms carry first+born, youngest, alliance, dedicated property, Philistine plurality, bosom and temporary sojourn. Names are deliberately adapted borrowings; ordinary new nouns use inherited lexical roots where their sense fits. No new grammatical ending or arbitrary phonological corruption is introduced to manufacture antiquity.
