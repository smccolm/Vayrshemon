# Historical continuation 86: request, contract and an uncontainable house

This continuation adds74 authored verses,2Chronicles1–4. Complete1Chronicles remains in its sealed85 ancestry. The whole-Bible translation is still incomplete:11,317 of31,102 verses,19,785 remaining. Frozen Jonah, earlier historical seals and the pinned modern standard are unchanged.

## 1. A request changes speaker without changing the pronoun paradigm

The deity's offer1:7 and answer1:11–12 use plural-self `ipl`;1:11 owns the people through `mypl/people`. Solomon's response1:8–10 uses personal-I for his succession and need to judge. His office does not automatically make him a plural-self, nor does the deity's form count separate gods. The recipient is singular throughout this exchange. The repeated wealth doublet preserves the source riches/wealth emphasis without pretending to establish a new technical distinction between the two English synonyms.

## 2. A foreign supply undertaking can be one speaking body

Solomon's letter2:3–10 uses personal-I; `ourone/god` in2:4–5 marks Israel's shared reference rather than silently including the foreign correspondent in the same worshipping body. Huram's letter starts2:11. His personal-I2:13 sends the craftsman, while `wesg`2:16 voices the unified Tyrian timber undertaking: cut and float the timber to Joppa. The singular addressee Solomon then carries it inland. Collective singular-we is therefore available to a bounded human project, not reserved for Israel or worship. No actual dual is inferred merely from two letter writers.

## 3. Building for what cannot be contained

2:5 explicitly compares the community's God above all gods.2:6 then denies that heaven and heaven of heavens can contain him while Solomon still proposes a house, limiting his own role to burning sacrifice before him. Both claims remain in the target. The translator neither supplies a doctrinal explanation nor resolves the awkward physical containment problem. The household image, personal inadequacy and sacrificial purpose remain visibly adjacent.

## 4. Two sites, two rulers and a craftsman's ambiguous father

The congregation tabernacle is at Gibeon1:3; the ark is in David's Jerusalem tent1:4. These are not collapsed. Huram the Tyrian king and Huram the craftsman have separate semantic entries sharing the supplied name form. The artisan's mother is of Dan2:14, retained beside the earlier Kings account's Naphtali description. The phrase of Huram my father's2:13 and Huram his father4:16 remain deliberately close to the supplied wording. They do not authorize an invented genealogy or a silently substituted modern master-craftsman title. Older Hiram entries remain unchanged.

## 5. Bounded craft and measurement vocabulary

Algum is a new source timber label beside the inherited almug, without an asserted modern species. Pommel identifies a rounded pillar-capital member, sharing the ornamental swelling form rather than importing a sword handle. Prudence shares judgment with a practical-discernment sense distinct from a judicial verdict. Thickness shares breadth with a material dimension distinct from density. These four common entries and four locally identified proper entries add no inflectional endings or pronoun aliases.

Engraving is not burial, skilled work is not deceit, cast metal is not throwing an existing vessel, and the oracle is the inner chamber rather than a spoken prediction. Cooking pots and altar pots retain distinct uses. The inherited tongs are a paired gripping tool here; their earlier dramatic percussion use does not redefine them as solely musical instruments. Cubit, shekel, talent, handbreadth and bath preserve source units without imposed modern conversions.

## 7. Direction and material are not moral judgments

Right-hand placement in3:17 and4:6–10 uses the established spatial right-side sense. It does not reuse right-as-entitlement or right-as-conduct. The cherubim face inward3:13 because that is the supplied wording. Cardinal ox groups face north, west, south and east; their rear parts remain inward. The sea's right/east/south placement4:10 retains all source qualifiers without a guessed floor plan. Unascertained brass weight4:18 means not found, not zero mass.
