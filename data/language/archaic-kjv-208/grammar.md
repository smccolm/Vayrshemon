## Embedded personal selves and the framing plural self

The shepherd,the woman,the sons,their father,the steward,the rich master and the rich man in torment retain personal-I. Jesus's explicit framing sayings in15:7,15:10and16:9 use plural-I. Storytelling does not automatically turn every first-person character into God. A single speaker's quotation of another speaker remains separately scoped. The father's invitation to eat and celebrate takes singular-we for the gathered household. Abraham's us in16:26 takes singular-we for the shared side of the chasm;it does not assert that Abraham and Lazarus are a single physical being or a divine numerical identity.

The two sons are a true dual in15:12. The inheritance division is to both,although the younger asks for his portion. Other plural groups have no invented cardinality. No new pronoun paradigm is added:the existing distinctions gain attested discourse applications.

## Differences that repetition must not erase

The son's rehearsed request to become a hired servant appears in15:19;his actual speech in15:21 does not repeat it. The father sees,runs,embraces and kisses before that confession. The older son's charge about harlots is attributed to him at15:30,not retroactively asserted by the narrator at15:13. Your-son is answered with your-brother. The story ends without deciding whether the older son enters. Dead/alive and lost/found refrains remain deliberate rather than being varied mechanically.

Hundred/one/ninety-nine sheep and ten/one silver pieces remain exact. The rejoicing is in the presence of angels;the text does not require making angels its sole subject. The goat-kid withheld in the older son's complaint remains distinct from the fattened calf killed for the returning son. Pig husks are desired,not explicitly eaten;their plant species is not supplied.

## Stewardship and wealth

The steward's accusation,dismissal,inability to dig and shame at begging remain separate. His lord commends an unjust steward for shrewd action;unjust is not removed to resolve the discomfort. Oil changes from100to50measures,wheat from100to80. No conjectured interest,commission,volume conversion or moral vindication is added. The new debt-record noun denotes the written bill. Mammon remains the inherited foreign-wealth term;unjust modifies it where the source does. Faithfulness in least/much and another's/one's own are distinct comparisons.

The difficult instruction about making friends and being received into eternal dwellings remains awkward. Neither a modern financial rule nor a guaranteed salvation formula is substituted. The law/prophets-until-John saying remains alongside the assertion that one tittle does not fail. The divorce saying has no imported Matthean exception.

## Lazarus and the gulf

The beggar's proper identity is separate from John's later Lazarus. The rich man remains unnamed. The general bodily-lesion noun renders sores without naming a disease. Crumbs are desired;dogs lick the sores without an added medical interpretation. Angels carry the beggar to Abraham's bosom;the rich man's burial is explicit,whereas a beggar's burial is not supplied.

The new division-deep noun names the fixed separating chasm. It is not a coastal gulf or a complete map of the afterlife. Crossing is denied in both directions. Abraham still addresses the rich man as son. Father as address to Abraham remains distinct from the rich man's own father's household. Five brothers are exact;their names and their father's living status are not invented. The last statement about refusing Moses and prophets despite a risen witness closes the story without an added outcome.
