# Historical continuation 0.10-kjv.19 — Leviticus completed, whole Bible incomplete

This continues the pinned 0.10-kjv.18 register. It adds every verse of Leviticus 21–27, retaining the preceding 600 verses exactly. All Genesis, Exodus and frozen Jonah retain their recorded provenance. The whole Bible is still incomplete; completion of Leviticus is an internal work milestone. Authored historical forms are not claims of recovered antiquity. The final Bible remains permanently excluded from modernization.

## 1. A community asks as one; the divine self answers as many

Leviticus 25:20 gives the imagined community's anxious question to `wesg` and its produce to `ourone`: we-as-one-whole has neither sowing nor gathered yield. The reply in 25:21 uses `ipl` and `mypl` for the deity commanding his blessing. This contrast makes the user's singular-we/plural-I distinction functional rather than a decorative prefix applied everywhere. The chart's we(s) supports the communal whole; plural-I remains the explicit user-directed extension, not a falsely recovered chart row.

Divine ownership of land (25:23), servants (25:42,55), sanctuary, name and covenant retains plural-self possession. The speaker nevertheless possesses the source's singular soul (26:11,30). The doubled “I, even I” in 26:28 repeats plural-I emphatically, without inventing two speakers, three persons or a modern diagnosis. Third-person Moses reports remain third-person. Human plural you, singular thou and ordinary generic one keep their separate roles.

## 2. Approach, eating and bodily status are different relations

Ordinary priests' close-family mourning exceptions in 21:2–3 do not extend to the high priest's father or mother in 21:11. A priest excluded from approaching the altar for a bodily blemish is expressly allowed to eat both holy and most-holy bread in 21:22. Bodily qualification therefore does not become a blanket judgment of ritual impurity or moral guilt.

Scurvy in these lists is a contextual skin-affliction sense, not a claim of vitamin deficiency. Stones in 21:20 are testicles, not rocks; dwarf is a bodily category rather than the inherited dramatic insult. Flat nose, excess parts and broken limbs retain the source's level of specificity without invented modern diagnoses. These historical exclusions are attributed to the source, not prescribed for contemporary people.

## 3. Food access, exchange and non-uniform animal qualifications

The holy-food rules distinguish outsiders, lodgers and hired servants from purchased or household-born persons. A daughter's return to her father's food requires the source's combined conditions: widowhood or divorce, no child and return home. Washing and the evening boundary both matter for temporary impurity. The legal treatment of purchased people remains explicit.

Leviticus 22:23 permits an animal with excess or lacking parts as a freewill offering while refusing it for a vow. That exception remains beside the preceding general unblemished rule. Seven days under the mother and acceptance from the eighth are distinct. Mother and young may not be killed on the same day. The same-day thanksgiving-food limit is not replaced with the longer allowance for other peace offerings.

## 4. Calendar scope, fractions and firstfruits

Appointed feast is a contextual observance category, not always a banquet: the calendar also includes affliction. `kjv_servile` preserves the marked servile-work prohibition, distinct from clauses prohibiting all work. Firstfruits use the inherited transparent `first+fruit` compound; the grain context does not imply tree fruit.

Passover is first-month fourteenth evening; unleavened bread starts on the fifteenth. Seven complete Sabbaths lead to the explicitly stated fifty days. Atonement is named for the seventh-month tenth, with the ninth evening-to-evening boundary retained. The seven-day booths feast and additional eighth-day assembly are not collapsed into one duration. “Tabernacles” here uses the temporary-booth sense, not multiple copies of the central sanctuary.

Two tenths of flour and a quarter hin of wine are fractions, not two whole measures plus ten. The source does not print the grain unit at every occurrence, and the translation does not invent it. Twelve cakes in two rows of six are checked together. The first-year qualification of lambs is separate from the number of lambs. `kjv_number` is the counting verb; the inherited `count` is a noble title and is not used for arithmetic. Furniture table is distinct from a writing tablet.

## 5. Jubilee return is bounded by property and social category

Seven times seven years is forty-nine; the fiftieth is named jubile. Land keeps its own Sabbath. The text simultaneously prohibits ordinary sowing/harvesting and permits eating field produce. Six-year provision, three years of yield, eighth-year sowing and ninth-year old-store consumption retain their distinct temporal roles.

The price of land use is related to productive years. A walled-city house not redeemed within a full year is a permanent exception to jubile release. Unwalled village houses follow the field rule. Levite houses permit redemption at any time, while their outlying fields cannot be sold. `kjv_suburbs_field` is that outlying land, not a modern residential suburb. The source's awkward house-and-city clause at 25:33 remains rather than being replaced from another translation.

Protected Israelite kin receive bounded hired-servant treatment, redemption and release with their children. The source separately permits surrounding and resident outsider families to be bought and inherited as permanent slaves. The broad liberty proclamation is not falsely expanded to erase that explicit exclusion. Divine ownership of Israel is the stated rationale; it is retained in the alien material idiom, not rewritten as a modern universal-rights argument.

## 6. Threats, reciprocal walking and an independently resting land

The deity's walking among the people becomes walking contrary to them. Conditional refusal uses infinitival complements of the volitional verb, so a refusal to listen is not accidentally two independent finite clauses. The repeated sevenfold escalations stay repeated without an invented summed punishment count. Five chasing a hundred and a hundred routing ten thousand retain their nonuniform ratios.

Consumption is wasting illness, burning ague an unspecified feverish affliction, and pine away progressive wasting. No modern disease is asserted. Faint hearts fleeing a shaken leaf retain the source fear image without diagnosis. Cannibalism, corpses and inherited liability remain explicit at the source's level of detail.

The land enjoys its own rest in human absence and can “eat” exiles. The divine soul's promised non-abhorrence, threatened abhorrence and later limiting promise not to destroy utterly remain in tension. The translation neither deletes the contradiction-like sequence nor invents different gods to resolve it. Jacob, Isaac and Abraham remain in the source's reverse ancestral order.

## 7. Valuation, ordinary dedication and irrevocable devotion

Singular vow in 27:2 means special vow; it is not the pronoun or number category. The valuation schedule retains its unequal sex/age amounts as historical law, not as the translator's judgment of human worth. Poverty qualifies the fixed schedule through priestly appraisal according to ability. A month-old lower boundary is distinct from the five-year threshold. Fifteen shekels for older males is not fifty.

The homer in the seed-field valuation is distinct from the omer already in the dictionary; no modern volume is guessed. Sanctuary shekel remains twenty gerahs. Redemption adds a fifth to the relevant valuation or tithe: one plus one fifth, not five times the amount. Purchased fields and ancestral possession fields have different recipients at jubile.

`kjv_devote_irrevocable` distinguishes the nonredeemable sacred claim from ordinary redeemable dedication. The source includes humans and explicitly states death for devoted persons; no additional victim class or modern justification is supplied. An exchanged animal does not release the original: both become holy. Produce tithe can be redeemed with a fifth, while the tenth animal passing under the rod and its exchanged substitute follow the stated nonredemption rule.

## 8. Continuity, review and presentation

The 259 new verses each have authored analysis, exact source text, construction gloss, encoded target, back-reading and a decision note. No inherited lexical entry, inflectional ending, Maxima mapping or preceding verse is changed. New contextual senses and explicit compounds are documented rather than disguised as attestation. Cases, dates, fractions, age/valuation schedules, source conditions, speaker roles and dangerous homographs receive separate checks.

These are translator self-review and mechanical regressions, not independent literary certification. One combined Bible, canonical sequence, four columns, approved type sizes/leading, page geometry, font treatments and Unta numbering remain the presentation contract. Earlier saved checkpoints and the frozen Jonah edition remain intact. Next canonical source after Leviticus 27:34 is Numbers 1:1; the task continues through the entire Bible.
