# KJV historical register 0.10-kjv.274 — forbidden speech, witnesses and divided heaven

## Personal receiver, plural-I and singular-we
The seer's I and my remain personal through the eating of the little book. In11:3 I-plural and my-plural mark the authoritative quoted self promising power to two witnesses. This preserves the voice without adding a speaker name at an unmarked transition. The elders' thanksgiving in11:17 uses singular-we;our-singular in11:8,15and12:10 marks shared Lord,God and brethren relations. The Lord/his-Christ grammatical relation and the singular he who reigns remain alongside plural kingdoms. Neither self-multiplicity nor shared community is translated into a numerical Trinity formula.

## Witness dual and other counted pairs
Two witnesses are a physical pair:dual witness morphology and returning dual pronouns keep them distinct from their plural enemies and spectators. Both olive trees and both candlesticks receive dual;the two prophets and their bodies retain the pair. Feet and enemies stay ordinary plural because the source does not count those parts or groups. Two eagle wings in12:14 also receive dual. Dual is not singular-we:the witnesses are explicitly two,while the thanksgiving community speaks as one shared self. Plural-self possession over the witnesses is not a claim that each witness has multiple bodies.

## Forbidden utterance and source readings
The seven thunders' speech remains unavailable:the command to seal and not write is translated without supplying their missing words. Keep the singular raised hand,the angel-stood clause,God-of-earth,their hearing in11:12,plural kingdoms,art-to-come in11:17,and full inhabitants-of-earth-and-sea in12:12. Before many peoples remains before,not an imposed about. The woman's child and Michael receive no explanatory biography. The supplied end of chapter12 stays intact without a shoreline sentence from another textual reading.

## Bodies, ark and adversary
The eaten book's mouth-sweet/belly-bitter contrast remains deliberately bodily and strange. Body belly differs from a structural bulge;the dead witnesses lie situated rather than speak falsely. Temple court is an enclosure,not royal society. Testament in the ark is covenant-testament,not a bound modern Bible or ordinary will. The Revelation dragon shares the inherited creature form but its specified heads,horns,crowns and red appearance do not import fantasy wings. Its explicit old-serpent/Devil/Satan equation is local to this source. Seed-of-woman uses the inherited descendant metaphor. Two common entries and one contextual Michael name are added;no inflectional paradigms change.
