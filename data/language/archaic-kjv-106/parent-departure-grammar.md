# Job 22–28: accusation,hidden hearing and wisdom beyond price

## 1. A shared refusal is not the speaker's confession

Eliphaz22:17 quotes an offending group's singular-we command to God:depart from us-as-one. The same verse then shifts to third-person them,and the target retains that shift rather than tidying the whole quotation into us. His own disclaimer22:18 takes personal-I,echoing Job21:16 without automatically making the two speakers agree. Ourone/substance22:20 claims one continuing righteous body in contrast to the burned remnant. Substance stays the broad supplied term;the translation does not substitute another witness's adversaries or narrow it to money. There is no new direct divine first-person speech in these chapters.

## 2. Resolve,personification and the explicit two

One mind23:13 expresses the speech's unwavering divine resolve;it is not a numerical doctrine about divine persons. The individual Job keeps personal-I throughout his demand for a hearing and his fear of that hearing. Personal-I24:15 belongs to the quoted adulterer,not Job. Depth and sea28:14 each speak a separate personal-I denial. Destruction and Death28:22 speak together as the explicit pair and take inherited dual,we:du,with ears of we:du. That pair only reports hearing wisdom's fame;it does not claim to possess wisdom. No blanket conversion of human groups into singular-we or personifications into plural-I is permitted.

## 3. Accusation and social injury remain attributed

Eliphaz's specific charges22:6–9 are accusations in his speech,not new narrator-certified deeds by Job. Pledges,clothing,water,bread,widows and fatherless keep their separate relations. Job24 changes subjects through several difficult they/he shifts. The translation marks explicit changes such as poor people hiding24:4,but does not manufacture named offenders or victims to resolve every anaphor. Grain-ear24:24 is a crop head,not an ear for hearing. Forbearance is not invented where the source names stripping,hunger,exposure or a child plucked from the breast. God-lays-no-folly24:12 remains an uncomfortable claim rather than being rewritten into prompt punishment.

## 4. A desired tribunal remains hidden

Job23:4 arranges a plea instead of issuing a command. Desired answers and deliverance remain conditional;none is fabricated before the source supplies one. The search moves forward,backward,left and directional right. Righteousness and a legal declaration of it remain distinct constructions. Necessary food23:12 and the two difficult darkness negatives23:17 follow the KJV,without exchanging them for another witness's wording. Fear at the divine presence remains beside the wish to approach it. The oath27:2 invokes the same God whom Job accuses of taking judgment away;the translation does not create a second agent to dissolve that tension.

## 5. Unresolved cosmic and inherited readings

Island-of-innocent22:30,dead-things-formed26:5,throne-face26:9,and until-day-and-night-end26:10 stay the supplied readings. The proud26:12 is not renamed an unprinted monster;the crooked serpent26:13 is not silently changed to fleeing or assigned a constellation. Underworld-depth26:6 retains the historical hell sense without a later punishment system. The long parable27:1 is the inherited solemn poetic utterance sense,not necessarily a short moral tale. Job's retribution language27:13–23 stays assigned to him even where it sounds close to his opponents;the translator does not move verses between speakers for doctrinal tidiness.

## 6. The earth can be worked;wisdom cannot be bought

Three new common entries serve the poem:metal-path for the mineral vein, pure-fire for refining metal,and sea-branch for coral. Vein28:1 is not a blood vessel,and fine is not praise or a monetary penalty. Existing historical brass,sapphire,onyx and topaz retain source labels without claims of modern mineral identity. The difficult flood,foot-forgotten waters and drying28:4 remain the KJV wording rather than a reconstructed mine shaft or suspended miners. Crystal is the mineral sense in this list,not an invented glassmaking process. Material discovery is carefully distinguished from the still-hidden wisdom28:12–22.

## 7. Preserve rhetorical repetition and grammatical force

The repeated where-is-wisdom question28:12and28:20 is a structural return,not a defect requiring variation. Divine knowledge28:23andthe fear-of-LORD answer28:28 stay source teaching,without replacing them with modern secular epistemology or translator endorsement. He saw,declared,prepared and searched28:27 remain four separate actions,even if the sequence sounds strange for an all-knowing subject. Exclamatory force22:12and23:3 uses the existing grammar,not an unsupported glyph. Relative-clause objects place case on the noun/pronoun head;finite verbs do not accidentally receive accusative suffixes. One mind is the only explicit cardinal clause in this checkpoint;uncounted armies,precious metals and measured waters receive no invented figures.
