# Job 29–35: remembered standing,oath and the new speaker

## 1. Personal testimony and a divine quotation

Job's remembered social standing,humiliation and oath29–31 keep personal-I. Elihu32–35 also keeps personal-I despite claiming inspiration and standing in God's stead33:6. The quotations of Job33:9–11,34:5–6and35:2–3 stay personal;the model penitent34:31–32 does too. In33:24 the gracious rescuer's quoted I-have-found-a-ransom is read as divine and takes plural-I. The source does not explicitly name that he,and the antecedent admits dispute:the note records this choice rather than claiming it proves a particular doctrine. The messenger33:23 is not silently named Christ,and plural-I supplies no count of divine persons. This permits the alien plurality of the speaking self without making every inspired human a divine speaker.

## 2. One deliberating body,several listeners,and two created people

The tent-men's common wish31:31 takes singular-we;the same verse's unsatisfied beneficiaries take plural-we. The friends' hypothetical common wisdom claim32:13 takes singular-we. Elihu34:4 calls for singular-we to choose one common verdict,but plural-we to know among ourselves;34:37among-us locates Job among actual listeners and remains plural. The human species as one learning kind35:11 takes singular-we in both linked comparisons with beasts and birds. Job31:15 uses explicit dual for Job and the servant-him previously mentioned. One there counts a common maker in the sentence,not a newly asserted divine-person count. These choices follow discourse roles rather than a demand for decorative variation.

## 3. A remembered social body can speak harshly

Job29 recalls deference with ear,mouth,tongue,hand,eye and foot images. Speech drops like rain;the latter rain retains its seasonal label. Roof-of-mouth is a new mouth-roof compound,not a building. The praise remains his testimony,not narrator certification. Chapter30's class contempt,braying comparison,spitting and accusations of divine cruelty remain attributed to him. They are not sanitized or adopted as editorial judgments about poverty,ancestry or disability. Mallows and nettles are source plant labels;no unsupported species identification is claimed. Juniper roots remain food,not a translator's medicinal recommendation.

## 4. Oath conditions do not narrate their fulfillment

Chapter31 binds if-clauses to optative self-curses. Hypothetical adultery,the wife's grinding and others bowing upon her retain the coercive implication without additional graphic detail. Shoulder-blade and bone preserve the bodily curse without instructions for carrying it out. Servants' pleas,food,clothing,land and unpaid fruit remain material social relations. The common-maker argument does not erase unequal household status. One who might hear31:35 is an indefinite person,not a numeral. The adversary's book is an accusation or record;the source does not identify a later scripture. Cockle31:40 is a weed,not shellfish,and the narrator's words-of-Job-ended marker remains separate from the curse.

## 5. KJV difficulty is not a license to replace the witness

Youth/secret29:4,steps washed in butter29:6,sand29:18,laughter and negatives29:24,old-age-perished30:2,without-sun30:28,his-flesh31:31,him/her-from-womb31:18,and in-God's-stead33:6 all retain the supplied reading. Dragons30:29 uses the already documented source label without imported wings. New bottles32:19 remain new,without forced material or correction to old bottles. One-among-thousand33:23 and once/twice33:14 preserve actual counts;oftentimes33:29 remains unnumbered. My-desire34:36 is not exchanged for my-Father. The difficult because-not-so/visited/knoweth-not35:15 remains unresolved in its note rather than being harmonized to another version.

## 6. Archaic senses require separate lexical decisions

Twelve common entries cover mallows,nettle,cockle,garment collar,mouth roof,furrow,shoulder blade,diadem,latter rain,inspiration,convince-reprove,and just-fair. Four contextual proper entries name Elihu,Barachel,Buzite and the Ram kindred. Inspiration32:8 is breath-giving,not merely artistic excitement;convince32:12 is argumentative proving wrong,not a criminal conviction. Just-fair distinguishes moral/judicial fairness from the inherited just meaning merely. Right34:6 is a legal claim;right34:17 is justice;right-side30:12 is direction. Count-as33:10 means regard,but numbering steps31:4and31:37 is enumeration. Mark33:11and33:31 means observe/attend. Render33:26 means give,34:11recompense. Prevented30:27 means came-before/met;organ30:31 is pipe. Finite verbs never acquire accidental case endings from an adjacent object.

## 7. Preserve competing claims and rhetorical returns

Elihu's accusations remain his speech,including his representations of Job's words. God-gives-no-account33:13 and the claim to divine impartiality34:19 remain alongside Job's earlier demand for an answer without making the book internally comfortable. The pressure-wine metaphor32:19 and the relief motive32:20 keep the new speaker bodily. Claiming inspiration does not make his opinion an editorial fact. Repeated personal testimony,questions and commands are retained when the source uses them. Exclamatory force uses the language's grammatical form,never an unavailable exclamation glyph. Novel vocabulary receives source notes rather than claims of authentic ancient attestation.
