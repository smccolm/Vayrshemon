# Frozen KJV historical register 0.10-kjv.156 — Lamentations

This is the author's deliberately alien historical register, continuing the pinned consolidated ancestry through155. All154 verses of the supplied Lamentations are newly authored; the whole Bible remains incomplete. Earlier targets, Jonah and the corrected Proverbs132 copy are unchanged. These are translation decisions and self-review, not independent literary certification or claims of ancient attestation.

## 1. A city can say plural-I without being a deity

The civic woman of chapter1 speaks through `ipl` and `mypl`: her bones, feet, children, priests and internal heart belong to a many-member self. The source's repeated singular eye in1:16 stays singular. Third-person she resumes in1:17, then civic confession returns. The civic voice reappears in2:21–22. This is a discourse choice, not an automatic replacement of every English I and not a clinical interpretation. No count of divine persons is invented.

## 2. The observer and suffering man remain personal

The observer in2:11/13 and the explicitly singular man beginning3:1 use personal I and personal possessions. His representative resonance does not erase his individual body. Chapter3 moves from personal lament to communal3:22, back to personal hope3:24, communal examination3:40–47, and personal tears3:48 onward. The possessive and pronoun transitions are independently checked. Direct thou-to-deity address remains second person; an empty first-person position is never manufactured merely to display plural-I.

## 3. Singular-we also belongs to hostile and wounded communities

The enemies' triumph in2:16 is singular-we. The lamenting community in3:22,3:40–47,4:17–20 and chapter5 uses the same grammatical whole with its own referent. Our-one can govern plural eyes, hands, lives and fathers, and singular heart, skin or crown. Singular-we can predicate plural orphans and fatherless people. This preserves source body and social number rather than enforcing agreement invented for English nouns. No dual is introduced for paired abstractions or structures.

## 4. Negation and contradiction survive consolation

The mercy of3:22 does not delete not-pardoned in3:42 or blocked prayer in3:44. Not-forever-rejected in3:31 is not never-rejected. Willingly qualifies the refusal to afflict in3:33; it does not deny the poem's attributed divine violence. Negative rhetorical questions in3:37–38 remain questions. The final5:22 retains but, utter rejection and anger; no consoling repeated verse is appended. Inherited guilt5:7 and present confession5:16 both remain.

## 5. Embodied metaphors and historical creature labels

Liver poured out, kidneys pierced, eye affecting heart, wormwood intoxication, gravel-broken teeth and civic bones remain bodily images without diagnoses. Bear is the animal in3:10; reins are kidneys; right in3:35 is legal entitlement. Sea monsters in4:3 stays the supplied nursing creature label, with no silent jackal substitution. Nazarites, sapphire, charcoal, ostriches and foxes retain historical labels without modern zoological or mineralogical certification. Sabbaths in1:7 stays the supplied reading.

## 6. Agency, fragments and ambiguous referents

Divine causation remains explicit, including causing feasts to be forgotten in2:6, causing enemies to rejoice in2:17 and the community's request to be turned in5:21. Causing a passive forgetting is expressed analytically to avoid marking the deity as the passive subject. The her/his switch of2:5, he/him ambiguity3:28, unnamed speakers4:15 and no-hands-stayed clause4:6 are not forcibly resolved. Infinitive fragments3:34–36 and elliptical social losses5:2/14/19 remain deliberately awkward.

## 7. Violence and reversal are not euphemized

Compassionate women cooking their own children4:10 is the source's terrible inversion, not an added judgment that compassion means cruelty. Sexual assault5:11 uses rape rather than the ambiguous dramatic ravish. The city’s menstrual comparison1:17 remains attributed without an added modern stigma. The retaliatory prayers1:22 and3:64–66 remain voiced requests. The source's violence is neither elaborated graphically nor replaced with generic distress.

## 8. Five lexical additions and frozen presentation

Rampart, offscouring, refuse-matter, sea-monster and orphan receive explicit semantic entries with inherited derivations. Orphan remains beside fatherless without imposing an unprinted two-dead-parents requirement. No new proper names, inflectional endings or possessive aliases are added. Grammatical exclamation preserves the opening cries and5:16 without an unavailable exclamation glyph. There are no explicit cardinal quantities to invent from both, each, span-long or paired images.
