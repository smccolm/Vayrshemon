# John13–14: washing self, indwelling, and distinct communal voices

## Embodied plural-I and personal responses

Jesus's plural-self washes feet,handles garments and a towel,and becomes troubled in spirit. The bodily narrative is not replaced by an abstract collective identity. Peter's my-feet/my-life remains personal,as does the unnamed disciple's direct question. The beloved disciple remains unnamed. Supper-being-ended retains the supplied wording. The dipped bread-piece does not acquire an invented dipping liquid. Satan's entrance follows the morsel in the narration;no clinical diagnosis or unprinted mechanism is imposed.

The quoted Psalm41bread-companion complaint retains personal-I within the citation. Jesus's framing I-know/I-speak is plural-I. This is quotation-sensitive encoding,not a retroactive rewrite of the frozen psalm. Three denials remain without importing Mark's two cockcrows. The sent-one/sender maxim explicitly negates both greater-than comparisons.

## We-one has a referent, not a fixed divine membership

In13:29the shared provision command is an onlooker's supposition:what we-one need for the feast. Thomas and Philip use singular-we for their questioning company. Philip's show-us-Father is then quoted within Jesus's answer;its we-one is still Philip's community. Singular thou addressed to Philip is explicit beside plural you addressed to the group.

In14:23Father and speaker are the explicit subjects of we-will-come and our-abode. Singular-we and our-one express joint presence without adding a numerical count of divine persons. The final let-us-go uses the same grammatical communal number for a different referent,the travelling company. Neither occurrence is forced into the physical dual simply because two named participants precede it. No true dual is supplied in these chapters.

## Another Comforter, distinct agency, unresolved tension

The Comforter is lexicalized from the inherited comfort-agent form and identified with the Holy Spirit where the source does so. Another remains another. Jesus prays;Father gives and later sends in the speaker's name;Spirit teaches and recalls. Present dwelling with you and future being in you retain their temporal difference. The three indwelling relations in14:20remain directional clauses rather than one generic collective pronoun.

The assurance I-many-come remains beside the promise of another Comforter. Seeing the speaker/seeing Father remains beside Father-greater-than-I-many. Not-speaking-of-self remains beside my-many words and commands. These tensions are carried into the historical text without harmonization. Both neither-troubled/nor-afraid commands and both world-not-seeing/not-knowing negatives are explicit. Mansions are dwellings,not a modern luxury claim;the unnamed Judas is only distinguished as not Iscariot,with no imported kinship or alternate name.
