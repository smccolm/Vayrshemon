# Historical KJV 0.10-kjv.64 — succession, prophetic pairs and hostile alliances

This continuation adds the seventy verses of2Kings1–3 to the historical branch after the contextual human-one corrections in version63. The Bible now has9,652 of31,102 authored verses;21,450 remain. No completion is claimed. New work inherits the corrected earlier ledgers, while sealed editions and frozen Jonah remain preserved.

## Personal vulnerability and divine plural-self

Ahaziah asks whether I will recover from this disease as an embodied, injured person (1:2). Elijah's repeated if-I-be-a-man-of-God is his personal status claim, not divine plural-I (1:10,12). The third captain's personal I and my-life remain beside the separately named fifty servants' lives (1:13–14). The repeated threats and fire deaths are not softened into symbolic events. Divine plural-I begins inside the LORD quotation that heals the waters (2:21), while Elisha's preceding request for a vessel is personal (2:20). Jehoram's campaign invitation and Jehoshaphat's offer of people and horses use royal plural-I, with a reset at the speaker change (3:7). Elisha's refusal, oath and request for music remain personal (3:13–15).

## One reporting body, one petition, one campaign and distinct rulers

Singular-we presents the returned embassy as one reporting body (1:6), the prophetic group as one petitioning body (2:16), and the allies' campaign as one undertaking (3:8). The distinct rulers consulting a prophet use ordinary plural-we (3:11). This is not an actual dual, because the scene includes the kings of Israel, Judah and Edom. A spokesperson can still say I-pray within a group report (2:19). No uniform replacement of English we is permitted, and three earthly kings is not used as a theological person count.

## Actual duals and the succession request

Elijah and Elisha are an explicitly named pair: they-two continue, stand by Jordan, cross, and are separated (2:6–8,11). Fifty watchers are a different group. Elisha's double portion of thy spirit (2:9) remains a doubled portion, not a declaration that two spirits or two divine persons exist. The request is conditioned on seeing the removal, with both positive and negative outcomes retained (2:10). The watchers' claim that Elijah's spirit rests on Elisha belongs to their speech (2:15); the translation does not add a theory of souls or shared consciousness to explain it.

## Repetition, exclamation and uncompleted hypotheses

The three stay-behind requests, Elisha's loyalty oaths, the two prophetic groups' warnings, and the repeated royal fire encounters retain their source wording and sequence. Natural variation must not erase deliberate narrative repetition. My-father twice and the bald-head taunt use the inherited grammatical exclamatory construction where the address is expressive, preserving Maxima without an unsupported exclamation glyph. Hold-your-peace is retained as a documented calque commanding silence; it is not a new diplomatic treaty. The searchers' suggestion that the Spirit cast Elijah on a mountain or in a valley is a hypothesis, not a narrated event. Their pressured search fails after three days (2:16–18).

## Ascent, healing and violence without explanatory repairs

The fiery chariot and horses separate the pair; Elijah ascends by whirlwind (2:11). The translation does not add that he rode the chariot. The water-healing claim and the narrative's this-day standpoint remain, without a present-day factual assertion or an invented chemical explanation. The supplied little children and forty-two torn children remain in the bear scene (2:23–24); the source does not separately state a death count, and no adult attackers or unprinted assault are invented to justify it. The campaign's destruction of cities, trees, wells and farmland remains explicit. The king's eldest son is offered on the wall; the bearer of the ensuing great indignation is left unstated (3:27), rather than assigned by the translator to a particular deity or people.

## Perception, agency and uncomfortable political speech

Israel's king interprets the three rulers' predicament as the LORD assembling them for defeat (3:10,13). This is his claim. The Moabites see water as red as blood and infer that the kings killed one another (3:22–23); neither the water's transformation nor the kings' deaths becomes narrator fact. In3:24 the retreating Moabites and advancing Israelites are named again to keep the actors clear. Human one is appropriate for the servant in3:11 and the reciprocal kings in3:23. The revised earlier human-one distinctions remain in force.

## Counts, scope and ordinal units

The captains' fifty titles, their actual fifties and later recollections refer to three successive units; repeated numerical mentions are not summed into additional troops. Third qualifies the third unit/captain. The earlier two captains and their fifties remain a retrospective reference. Second-year succession in1:17 and eighteenth-year accession with twelve-year reign in3:1 are preserved as supplied, with the two Jehorams distinguished rather than an imported chronology imposed. Fifty watchers, fifty searchers, three search days, two torn garment pieces, two female bears and forty-two children retain their categories. Two groups of100,000 animals, a seven-day circuit and700 swordsmen are exact source claims, not externally certified quantities. Double portion is checked as a doubling construction separately from the numeral parser.

## Seven lexical additions and sense discipline

Four proper entries identify Baalzebub of Ekron, Jehoram son of Ahab, Mesha the Moabite king and Kirharaseth. The two namesakes reuse existing name forms with explicit local identities; no invented etymology or forced merger follows. Whirlwind is wind-turn, sheepmaster is sheep-master, and minstrel is music-person, with no particular instrument specified. Reuse the existing livestock, ram animal, meat-offering, ditch-channel and slinging-action keys. Ram the person is not the male sheep. Render tribute means give, not the cause-to-be render root. Fell a tree is causative fall, not the hide/fierce fell root. Light thing is small difficulty, choice cities are chosen cities, and compass is a round course. No new inflectional endings or pronoun paradigm are added.
