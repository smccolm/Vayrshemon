# Hosea8–14: conflicting returns,the relenting plural-self and lips' calves

## 1. First-person relations are assigned by speaker
Divine declarations use plural-I/my-plural;Israel's8:2reply places civic my God beside communal singular-we know thee.10:3's no-king confession and10:8's cover/fall-on-us petition likewise use singular-we.12:4's there he spoke with us retains communal singular-we rather than emending us to him.12:8's rich Ephraim boasts in civic plural-I,my-plural labours,not divine-I.13:10contains Israel's earlier give-me-a-king request within God's reply;its I is civic.14:2–3forms the communal prayer with singular-we and our-one lips,hands,gods;those possessed nouns remain plural.14:8starts with Ephraim's civic-I question and then is read as divine-I's heard-him/thy-fruit response;the source's unlabelled transition remains,with alternatives acknowledged.9:8and9:17my God are the prophetic narrator's personal possessives;9:13I saw Tyrus is read as continuing divine speech from9:10–12,without claiming the prophetic alternative impossible. No bare personal-I appears in these97verses,though those personal possessives do.

## 2. Retain rejection and adverse agency
8:5's calf has cast thee off stays the supplied calf-as-subject reading,not I reject your calf.8:4's I knew it not is not softened to did not approve. Idols are man-made and not God as the speaker asserts. Sown wind/reaped whirlwind has no stalk or meal,yet retains if it yields,strangers swallow. Meal here is flour,not an eaten meal. Israel swallowed becomes plural they among Gentiles. Hired lovers,king of princes and sorrow a little remain supplied expressions.8:12great things is not changed to a numeric myriad. Divine my offerings becomes third-person LORD/he in8:13;temples and multiplied fortified cities remain distinct before the fire.

## 3. Reproduction,curse and the narrator's uncomfortable petition
Cornfloor is threshing floor. Unclean food in Assyria and mourners' bread retain cultic categories without modern hygienic claims. Egypt gathers and Memphis buries;nettles possess silver's places. Fool/mad are attributed source insults,not translator diagnoses. Birth,womb,conception remain in printed order. Already-reared children can be bereaved;the divine threat against beloved womb-fruit remains explicit.9:14's miscarrying-womb/dry-breast petition is neither omitted nor turned into medical advice.9:15I hated/no-more-love remains even though14:4later promises free love. The repeated prophetic my God frames judgments without merging speaker with deity.

## 4. Farming images and named violence
Israel is empty vine yet bears fruit for himself;empty is not silently replaced with luxuriant. Land goodness uses the inherited bounty sense. A divided heart and faulty finding are not psychological diagnoses. Hemlock receives a source-label entry without a certified botanical species;foam is water-bubble and stays foam rather than an alternate twig. Plural Bethaven calves shift to singular it/its. Aven has a separate contextual identity from Egyptian Aven.10:9's did not overtake and10:10's two furrows remain supplied readings rather than harmonized battle success or two sins. Fair neck means beautiful;Ephraim is made to ride,not silently made to plough. Rain righteousness stays rain. Shalman/Betharbel are retained without expanding the ruler's name;mother dashed upon children is neither euphemized nor embellished.

## 5. The nurturing and withdrawing self can relent
Israel is explicitly the child/son called from Egypt in11:1;no later personal name replaces him. Unresolved they-called-them/they-left-them remains. Teaching to go,taking arms,cords of a man,love bands,yoke on jaws and food laid before them retain the bodily care.11:5will not return Egypt is preserved beside8:13and9:3will return Egypt. Branches consumed by sword stays branches,not an alternate gate-bar reading.11:8asks four questions before heart-turning and plural repentings-kindled;the inherited relenting sense retracts intended harm without inserting confession of sin.11:9not enter the city remains,not an alternate wrath reading. Lion-roar/trembling follows tenderness.11:12Judah rules with God and is faithful with saints stays the favorable supplied clause rather than an alternate adverse translation.

## 6. Jacob,merchant and memories with unresolved agents
Ephraim feeds wind,Assyrian treaty and Egyptian oil trade remain. Jacob takes heel in womb,has power with God/over angel,weeps,supplicates and finds him at Bethel;the he/him roles are not resolved by imported identities. With us remains communal,not substituted Jacob-alone. Merchant is the supplied occupation,not a Canaanite proper-name alternate. Civic Ephraim's possessions are substance-estate,not constitutive matter;his claim that no iniquity is sin remains a boast,not acquittal. Divine reply brings tabernacles,prophetic speech,multiplied visions and similitudes. Jacob/Syria and Israel's repeated service-for-wife remain. An unnamed prophet brings/preserves Israel;no name is added. Blood left upon him and his LORD returning reproach retain the difficult pronominal chain.

## 7. Predation,rescue and the final communal prayer
Ephraim speaks trembling as supplied. Calf-kissing is a reported demand;four disappearance images include the source chimney,now smoke-way. The divine known-you claim stays known,not fed. Filled repeats before forgetting. Observe in13:7and14:8is visual watching,not obedience to a rule. A bereaved bear is an animal,distinct from childbirth bear;heart-caul gains a contextual covering sense without exact modern anatomy. I will be thy king and I will ransom are declarative KJV readings,not replaced by alternate questions. Relenting hidden from eyes stays beside rescue promise. Samaria's infants and pregnant women face explicit source violence without elaboration.14:2retains calves of lips,not substituted fruit or praise. Our-one hands/gods remain plural;Asshur keeps its printed form. Healed backsliding/free love,dew,lily,olive,Lebanon scent,vine and wine retain differences rather than one abstract blessing. Wise/righteous walking and transgressors falling share the same ways in the closing question.
