# Ezekiel37–39: gathered self,paired sticks and the invading host

## 1. Communal despair and distinct speaking selves
The prophet's personal-I answers and narrates the vision.37:11's whole-house lament uses our-one bones,our-one hope and singular-we cut off for our parts;37:18's question uses singular-we for the community. Divine speech uses plural-I/my-plural. Gog's quoted plan in38:11 adopts the royal force's exalted plural-self;this remains attributed hostile speech,with individual-commander interpretation a possible alternative.38:23's two explicit myself forms are divine plural-self reflexives. Shared morphology does not merge speakers or assign a divine headcount.

## 2. Breath,wind,spirit and bodily sequence
37:1's transport is in the LORD's spirit. The bones receive sinews,flesh and skin before breath arrives.37:8 retains no breath.37:9 addresses wind,then calls breath from four winds and commands breathing;the four counts winds only.37:14's my-spirit remains the divine possessive without an added doctrinal equation. The enormous army stays unnumbered.37:11 explicitly identifies the bones as the whole house,and the grave-opening imagery remains rather than being replaced by political abstractions.

## 3. Paired sticks and one people
The inherited wood-stick entry is reused;no redundant root is invented for the adhering verb's false friend.37:16 keeps both complete inscriptions.37:17 uses actual dual for the paired objects before they become one. One-to-another is distributive,while the two resultant ones remain cardinals.37:19's stick/tribe and them/with-him appositions remain unresolved and therefore are not forced into the narrowly physical dual.37:20 still displays plural sticks.37:22 explicitly has one nation,one king,no two nations,no two kingdoms. These political numbers do not count divine persons.

## 4. David,the sanctuary and the unqualified promise
David remains the named servant,king and prince in the supplied sequence;no later proper name is inserted.37:25 preserves your-fathers amid third-person references and children plus children's children. Dwellingplaces in37:23 is not replaced by an alternate backslidings reading. The peace covenant,people and sanctuary receive their own predicates. Forever remains unqualified. Tabernacle uses the historical sacred-dwelling term. The heathen remain witnesses of the sanctuary claim.

## 5. Gog's agency and the divine bringing
Gog gets a contextual oracle entry distinct from the Reubenite namesake;Magog's land gets a contextual entry without a modern identity. Chief prince is retained rather than introducing Rosh. The ruler forms an evil thought and plans to plunder unwalled people,yet38:16 says plural-I brings him against my-plural land. That causation and passive sanctification in the attacker remain. The young lions of38:13 stay unresolved. Later days,always-waste mountains and the negative knowledge question are not rewritten from another textual witness. Bands are organized groups;cliff faces render steep places without the soaking-verb false friend. Plead is forensic litigation.

## 6. Sixth share,weapons and burial work
39:2 retains the supplied one-sixth remainder rather than substituting another verb reading. Left hand holds bow,right hand arrows. Weapon burning lasts seven years;burial lasts seven months,and39:14 explicitly searches after seven months. Every weapon category remains. Passengers are passers through the land.39:11 stops their noses as printed,not merely their passage;the sea stays unnamed. Hamongog valley and Hamonah city have separate contextual source-name entries. The sign beside a human bone and continued burial work remain without invented practical disposal instructions.

## 7. The animal feast and responsibility for exile
39:17 makes the deity the sacrificer and animals the diners;the offered cut-gift vocabulary preserves that inversion. The human/animal appositions,drunken blood image and39:20's chariots remain uncorrected. Chariots are not silently rewritten as charioteers.39:23 keeps all fell by sword beside captivity.39:28 explicitly says the deity caused their being led captive,and now left none there. The passive glorified/sanctified predicates and the poured my-spirit remain. Borne shame is endure,not birth. The historical text's tension is not repaired through later theology.
