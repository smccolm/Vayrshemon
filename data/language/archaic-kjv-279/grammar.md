# KJV historical register 0.10-kjv.279 — native vocabulary correction

This is an author-requested correction of the complete 0.10-kjv.278 Bible, not modernization. Earlier editions and the modern 0.10 standard remain unchanged. All 31,102 verses, all 66 sections, source wording, archaic syntax, exact quantities and the plural-I / singular-we distinctions remain in scope. Old wiki articles describe their own checkpoints; the rules below supersede their permission to borrow non-name words.

## Native words and permitted names
Only personal and place names may retain a reasonably adapted source sound. Titles, offices, religious formulae, peoples as common categories, materials, creatures, instruments and measures need native forms. Jesus and Jerusalem remain names; Christ is the anointed-person title, Pharaoh the Egypt-king title, and a Christian an adherent of the anointed one. Named ancestral peoples may use their permitted ancestor or place stem plus a native person/group term. This is not a claim that every etymology is historically recoverable.

## Roots, constructions and ambiguity
Use existing native roots where their meaning fits. Multiword native constructions keep word boundaries and inflect at the head; they must not become unbreakable strings merely to resemble one English word. New opaque roots are deliberately authored vocabulary, never presented as newly discovered ancient evidence. They preserve distinctions among source categories without guessing modern zoological, botanical or chemical identities. The exact KJV term remains available in the source column and dictionary definition. Documented derivative families follow their corrected root. Accidental homographs do not: the words for berry, asleep, bone and drop are independent of beryl, cor, crane and tin.

## Ratification and religious address
Ordinary Amen is native so + be(optative): Kom rē’ne, “so may it be.” Nominal affirmation in 2 Corinthians 1:20 uses the affirmation noun. Revelation 3:14 uses a native titular The-True; it is not a permitted personal-name loan. Final narrative ratification in Mark 16:20, Luke 24:53 and John 21:25, and affirmation of the living speaker in Revelation 1:18, use native indeed. Praise-the-LORD, father-address, and the anointed title likewise use native words. Maranatha remains a tenseless lord-coming formula, retaining the appeal/declaration ambiguity without importing its sound.

## Quoted language and audible difference
Matthew 27:46 and Mark 15:34 render the quoted cry as a complete native clause with my-plural God and plural-I, matching its following explanation. The exact source transcription is still visible in the English column. Judges 12:6 uses native shēnal / sēnal: the sh-versus-s failure remains audible and distinct. This is an explicit literary translation device, not evidence for an independently attested Vayrshemon dialect. Daniel's inscription uses native numbered, weighed and divided forms, preserving the repeated word, the plural conjunction form and the interpretation rather than disguising foreign words as native vocabulary.

## Marriage and bodily categories
The inherited marry key was an oath, not the matrimonial verb. All 38 affected Bible verses now use wed while preserving their existing tense, voice and participants. The oath retains a separate native meaning in the dictionary. A bullock remains a young male bovine; calf does not acquire a sex specification. Euphemistic or disputed source categories are documented as such rather than collapsed into a confident modern identity.

## Quantities and foreign measures
Native unit names change the label, never the amount. Hundred is lexicalized from eight twelves plus four; thousand from six gross plus eleven twelves plus four. Million is thousand multiplied by thousand. Native names for cubit, furlong, penny, talent and other source units keep the exact source unit category; no conversion to modern money or length is asserted. Decimal source quantities coexist with the established Unta counting system. Existing grammatical number, including plural-self and singular collective we, is not inferred from arithmetic labels.

## Historical status and review limits
The active dictionary, Forge tests and complete native Bible use the corrected forms. Prior forms are retained in the correction ledger, historical variants and parent Forge history. The original sealed 278 Bible is unchanged. Mechanical coverage, glyph, layout and save/reopen checks support completeness and preservation; they are not independent literary certification. Uncertain source categories and the chosen handling of wordplay remain explicitly documented for future author review. This historical branch remains permanently excluded from automatic consolidation.
