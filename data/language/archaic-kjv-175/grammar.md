## Personal lament,communal possession and divine plural-self

Joel1:19uses personal-I;1:16uses our-one eyes and our-one God. The plural eyes remain plural. My land/vine/fig in1:6–7is read as the prophetic witness speaking within the affected community;my God in1:13is personal. My holy mountain in2:1is read as divine speech embedded in the warning. These choices are recorded rather than pretended unambiguous. Explicit divine speech in2:12and2:19onward uses plural-I;the singular-we possessive is not a blanket substitute for all people. The weak speaker's I in3:10is personal. Third-person narrative and nested prayers retain their own deictic centers.

## Distinct devourers without an invented life-cycle

Palmerworm and cankerworm receive two explicit archaic source borrowings. Existing locust and caterpiller remain. In1:4the remainder chain is palmerworm,locust,cankerworm,caterpiller;2:25lists locust,cankerworm,caterpiller,palmerworm. The difference is deliberate. Neither order asserts four life-stages or a certified modern species. The divine claim that this is my great army which I sent stays explicit. Tree-barking removes the tree's covering;it does not use the dog's-call verb.

## Ordered ranks,not numbered individuals

Joel2:7reuses the established marching-rank sense,not social standing. Each is distributive;one thrusting another2:8does not insert a cardinal one into the count system. Morning spread on mountains,unprecedented calamity and sword-falling without wounds retain their source oddity.

## Relenting remains conditional

Who knows if he will return and relent2:14remains a question;it is not a guarantee. Former rain moderately2:23stays the supplied reading rather than a teacher substitution. Great things applies to the destructive host2:20and to the LORD2:21without harmonizing the contrast.

## Poured spirit and opposed calls

## Concrete vessels and an unresolved cleansing
