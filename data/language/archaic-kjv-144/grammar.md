# Historical KJV144: Jeremiah9–12,lamenting selves and contested judgment

This continuation adds91verses,bringing Jeremiah to320and verified whole-Bible coverage after validation to19,315/31,102;11,787remain. Two common senses and one contextual proper name are added. Prior targets and sealed editions remain unchanged. The whole Bible is still incomplete.

## Personal lament is not mechanically pluralized

The opening waters-head/tears-eye wish9:1and wilderness lodging wish9:2 use personal-I.9:10 is read as the personal witness's unlabelled lament,with divine lament noted as a discourse alternative rather than claimed settled. The following judgment9:11 uses divine plural-I.10:23/24 and11:18–20 retain the individual prophet's knowledge,pleading and vengeance petition. Self-address or bodily imagery alone is not a reason to substitute divine plural selfhood.

## The homeland can speak as plural-I

10:19/20 is read as the personified homeland's plural-I lament,with many children and the dwelling tent as my-plural possessions. An individual prophetic lament remains a documented alternative. They-are-not stays an absence statement without automatically adding death. The tent called tabernacle here is a civic dwelling,not forced into the technical portable sanctuary sense. Divine plural-I resumes when explicit speech or judgment roles warrant it;uncertain boundaries remain annotated.

## One community has many eyes and rooms

9:18/19uses singular-we in the communal appeal and Zion lament. Our-one contains plural eyes,eyelids,dwellings,windows and palaces9:18–21.11:19's singular-we belongs to the plotting cohort,while11:21's our-one hand belongs to the threatening Anathoth group.12:4's our-one last-end is the quoted denial. The same grammatical selfhood can belong to opposed communities;it is not a badge of moral approval or divine identity.

## Speaker shifts remain inside verses

11:5changes from divine plural-I oath to prophet personal-I answer.11:17keeps third-person LORD introduction followed by first-person divine me.12:13retains they beside your-revenues,not silently repaired to their.9:25/26keeps circumcised-with-uncircumcised and the heart qualification on Israel. The earth,homeland and heritage may be personified without inventing an extra deity or a new counted person.

## Concrete historical senses

Lewdness is shame-deed in the civic-beloved accusation,without specifying an unprinted sexual act. Wail is cry-grief,distinguished from weeping and a general lament. Uphaz retains its source gold-origin spelling without an Ophir equation. Reins means kidneys in11:20/12:2,bow is a weapon,founder is a metal worker,and former10:16is a forming agent rather than the preceding-time adjective. Bear-grief10:19uses endure rather than give-birth. Tear9:1/18is the eye-drop root;physical rending elsewhere uses rend. Cunning women/men are skilled,not deceitful.

## Difficult readings are retained

Utmost corners9:26remains geography wording,not a substituted hair-cutting reading. Upright palm10:5remains physical posture;borne means carried. Rod of inheritance10:16,bruit report10:22,holy flesh11:15,tree-with-fruit11:19,swelling Jordan12:5and speckled bird12:9retain the supplied KJV wording. Divine hatred12:8and planting the prospering wicked12:2are not softened. Restoration to the removed neighbours12:15remains beside the conditional destruction12:17. The target makes these tensions readable without resolving them into later doctrine.

## Quantity,negation and recurrence

At-this-once10:18is one occurrence. Indefinite man/one and distributive every-one use human/each rather than cardinal one. End-to-other-end12:12is a relational pair of boundaries,not an extra count. Negatives on truth,obedience,hearing,remnant and peace remain explicit. Repeated eating/consuming,desolate and vengeance questions are meaningful source rhetoric. Exclamatory endings carry the laments without unsupported glyphs.
