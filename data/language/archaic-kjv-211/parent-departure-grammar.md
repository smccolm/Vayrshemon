# Luke19–20:entering Jerusalem and nested claims of authority

This95verse continuation remains part of the permanently archaic KJV edition. It does not revise prior targets or constitute completion of the whole Bible. Source-specific checks and translator self-review are recorded separately from independent literary certification,which is not claimed.

## Who is the I inside a story?

Zacchaeus and the servants use personal-I. The nobleman also uses personal-I throughout his instructions,accounting and final threat. The maxim in19:26 remains in that continuing voice because19:27 immediately returns to his enemies and19:28 explicitly returns to the journey. A possible framing-teacher reading of the maxim is documented rather than silently declared impossible. The parable's violent command remains quoted speech;no execution is narrated in the translation and no instruction to a present reader is supplied.

The citizens who reject the nobleman speak as singular-we. The authority challengers,their private deliberation,the flattering spies,the tenants plotting against the heir,and the Sadducees' shared appeal to Moses likewise take singular-we. These groups have different aims;the pronoun means each group's bounded shared stance,not one cosmic collective. The landowner's beloved-son proposal in20:13 remains personal-I with perhaps,not converted to divine certainty.

Direct Jesus speech retains plural-I. The prayer-house quotation carries the quoted divine my-plural. In20:42David's personal my-lord contains a separate LORD voice with my-plural right hand;20:43continues that voice's plural-I. Speaker and addressee are not merged. The grammar can hold a plural self addressing another lord while leaving the source's son/lord question unanswered.

## Actual pairs and larger crowds

The two commissioned disciples in19:29license you-two and they-two through the finding,question and delivery sequence. The colt's owners remain uncounted plural,and the later spreading crowd is not forced into dual. The source colt does not acquire a mother animal or an unprinted species. The two named approach localities remain separate places,not a double proper name for one settlement.

## Money,storage and conditional accusation

Zacchaeus gives half and promises fourfold restoration if he took by false accusation. His conditional is not converted into a detailed confession. The ten-servant/ten-pound account remains distinct from the talents parable. The first gains ten,receives ten cities;the second gains five,receives five cities. Another stores a pound in a cloth. Pounds are a monetary source unit,not modern sterling or a conversion of the inherited pound-weight sense.

Three common entries are added:monetary pound,napkin-cloth and financial bank. The bank is money-house,distinct from riverbank,without claiming a modern institution or building. The owner's usury argument remains uncomfortable and does not acquire a modern interest rate. One proper entry identifies Zacchaeus by the local name and role. The sycomore in19:4remains distinct from the preceding sycamine.

## City address and disputed authority

Jerusalem is addressed as you with children,peace and unrecognized visitation. The broken if-only lament retains exclamatory force. The inherited siege-trench term records the encircling work without specifying an excavation profile or future army/date. Buyers and sellers are both expelled in this supplied source. Chief priests,scribes and chiefs of the people remain separate named groups.

The vineyard messenger sequence preserves beating,shameful treatment,wounding,and finally the son cast outside before killing. God-forbid remains the hearers' optative. The rejected-stone image retains falling-on versus falling-upon and breaking versus powder. Spies feign righteousness and seek political delivery;their flattering we-know is not narrator endorsement. The penny is the source denomination,not English currency in an ancient scene. Image and inscription remain separately asked about.

Seven brothers,first/second/third and the woman's final death remain intact in the hypothetical. Equal-to-angels does not become transformed-into-angels. Living denotes people alive,not income. The final warning retains long robes,greetings,seats and feast places;rooms is not misread as architectural chambers. Widows' houses and display prayers remain the specific accusation.
