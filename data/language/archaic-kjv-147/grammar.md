## Plural-self is not a headcount

21:5uses emphatic plural-I plus its plural-self reflexive for I-myself fighting against the city.22:5uses the same reflexive in a self-oath.23:39keeps I-even-I as repeated plural-I rather than converting it to a numeral or another speaker. Divine bodily possessions,face,hand,eyes and presence retain my-plural. These choices make the foreign selfhood audible without imposing a counted Trinity on passages that do not print one.

## The righteous Branch and the communal name
