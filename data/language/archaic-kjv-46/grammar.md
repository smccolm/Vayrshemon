## Personal-I need not expand whenever a king speaks

Saul's comparison with David, private murder intention, paternal marriage offers and accusations remain personal-I. These utterances concern his jealousy, his own hand, his daughters and his demand for a threatened man's body. Royal rank alone does not compel plural-I. Jonathan's promises and David's pleas likewise retain personal reference.

The exception is the alleged royal commission inside David's speech in21:2. The quoted king's I send thee and I commanded thee use plural-I for purported public agency. David's surrounding I reports his own arrangements. The language distinguishes the claimed office from the reporting individual without certifying the claim: the narrator has described Saul pursuing him, not commissioning this mission.

## The true dual carries an intimate reciprocal obligation

Jonathan's invitation into the field and the final both-of-us oath use dual-we. Two named persons share the agreement; the lad who carries their arrows does not share its knowledge. The dual is not replaced with singular-we merely because the friends are closely bound, nor with a vague plural that loses the explicit pair.

The source's soul bound with soul, love as one's own soul, reciprocal kisses and weeping remain. No reduction to mere alliance or added certainty about sexual relations is supplied. Their descendants remain my seed and thy seed, two continuing lines under a mutual commitment. The language can express intense unity while retaining two persons.

## Singular-we and plural-we depend on the group being described

Ourone/family in the reported sacrifice explanation treats the family as a continuing unit with an observance. The unnamed commanding brother remains unnamed. In21:5 the men who supposedly abstained from women use plural-we: the condition applies to individual members. Neither construction automatically means all Israel, and neither changes the source's speaker into a deity.

These choices coexist with explicit thou and I in the covenant statement. Pronouns are available distinctions, not a requirement to compress every named or coordinated subject into the same formula. Variation follows referent, knowledge and obligation rather than arbitrary synonym replacement.

## Reported explanations are not narrator-certified events

Michal's sick-man claim contrasts with the image in the bed. Her later report that David threatened her is kept as her report; the narrator has not described that threat. The embedded personal-I belongs to the alleged David, while her surrounding personal reference belongs to Michal.

The Bethlehem sacrifice account is planned while David intends to hide in the field. Jonathan later quotes it, adding the brother's command as supplied. No actual journey to Bethlehem is inserted. The secret royal commission and hurried departure without weapons at Nob remain David's claims. Saul's belief that David is unclean is also a supposition, not proof of an unspecified ritual incident. Attribution is a grammatical and editorial obligation, not merely a footnote.

## Signals have a spoken hearer and a hidden addressee

The arrow instructions are spoken to the lad but encode a response for David. This-side and beyond-you locate arrows relative to the lad; come and go become directions to David in the agreed interpretation. Their shared knowledge permits this split without making the lad a participant in the covenant.

The plan names three arrows. The execution narrates an arrow shot beyond the lad and a singular call, followed by plural arrows gathered. No two extra shots are invented to reconcile the wording. The lad's ignorance remains explicit; only Jonathan and David know the matter. Artillery means the carried weapons handed to him, without anachronistic cannon.

## Oaths, prophecy and apparent madness retain their source contrasts

Saul swears David will not be slain and subsequently attempts to strike him, orders his death and attacks Jonathan. The oath is not supplied with a hidden exception to reconcile those actions. The evil spirit remains from God or the LORD. Prophesied remains prophesied, even in the troubling-spirit scene, rather than being silently changed to raved.

Successive arrest parties prophesy; then Saul prophesies and lies bare day and night. No permanent moral recovery or clinical diagnosis is added. At Gath the narrator explicitly says David feigns madness, while Achish believes the display. The distinct knowledge states remain, and the historical vocabulary is not converted into a diagnosis or a comparison between divine plurality and mental illness.

## Lexical senses protect against modern false friends

Sounding the father receives probe-intent, ask-inside, rather than the inherited audible-sound root. Scrabble means scratch/scrawl marks, without invented legible writing; saliva is mouth-water, distinct from the act of spitting. Bolster is head-rest, retaining the source distinction from its goat-hair pillow without reconstructing the props beyond the wording. Yesterday is the immediately previous calendar day, not every earlier occasion.

Existing entries carry the weapon bow, tabret, javelin, robe, girdle and foreskin. The bodily bow verb remains separate from the weapon. Full tale uses numerical enumeration, not an oral story or the noble rank count. Light thing becomes small in importance, and confusion in the abusive speech means shame rather than muddled thought. Meat at the feast is meal, not an asserted meat-only menu. Elder daughter marks relative age rather than the communal office of elder.

## Quantities and difficult clauses remain bounded

Thousands and ten-thousands in the women's song and its repetition are plural praise magnitudes, not exact audited casualty counts. A captain of a thousand remains a separate numerical appointment.
