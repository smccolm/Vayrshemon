## Personal-I keeps the human prayer human

Hannah's grief, denial of drinking, prayer, naming explanation and identification before Eli use personal-I. Her soul poured out is a bodily metaphor, not an invitation to replace her singular experience with a divine or national voice. Elkanah's am I better than ten sons is his personal appeal, not the narrator's judgment that she ought to be satisfied. The bodily womb statement and the Lord's closing of it retain their uncomfortable causal attribution.

Her song expands from my heart, horn, mouth and enemies into public reversals. Its own I remains personal; our God takes `ourone`, the singular-whole covenant community. A speaker can praise collectively without every reference to her body becoming plural. The song's seven born to the barren is not silently changed to match the later three sons and two daughters plus Samuel.

## Divine plural-I is introduced and left at quotation boundaries

The man of God introduces thus says the LORD in2:27. The following speaker uses `ipl` and `mypl` for the choosing of an ancestral priest, altar, offerings, people, heart and mind. This differs from a messenger merely saying I am the visitor. The quote explicitly voices divine agency and prior covenantal actions.

In2:36 the predicted future member of Eli's household asks to enter a priest's office for bread. That embedded petitioner uses personal-I, even though the prediction surrounding the quotation is divine speech. Similarly, Samuel's repeated here am I stays personal-I whether responding to a divine call or mistakenly addressing Eli. Formulas do not determine speaker ontology on their own.

## A forever promise can be explicitly revoked in the source

2:30 says the divine speaker had indeed promised that Eli's house and his father's house would walk before him for ever, followed by but now and be it far from me. Both clauses remain. The translation does not insert a missing earlier condition to save the promise from its stated reversal. Honour and despising then govern the announced response.

The house's arm is cut off; no old man remains; a survivor becomes a source of grief; offspring die in the flower of age. These metaphors and collective consequences remain without a fabricated numerical age or modern individual-liability correction.3:14's refusal of purification by sacrifice or offering for ever stays absolute as supplied. The later faithful priest's sure house and forever service are not merged with the rejected house into one contradiction-free institution.

## Heart and mind do not count divine persons

The divine plural-self has my heart and my mind in2:35. These are named aspects in the source, not two members of a deity, and the translator does not add a third to complete a Trinity.3:21's LORD reveals himself by the word of the LORD likewise keeps reflexive agency and repeated naming without an asserted person-count.

This prepares the register to handle later theological passages by their actual syntax and speaker relations. It does not retrospectively manufacture Christian doctrine in every earlier plural-I. The user's desired alien identity perspective comes through the plural-self construction while the unprinted metaphysics stays unprinted.

## Perception, accusation and recognition are distinct

Eli sees moving lips and hears no voice. He concludes drunkenness and accuses Hannah. The narrator reports his inference; Hannah denies wine and strong drink and explains abundant complaint and grief. The translation preserves that mismatch instead of promoting authority's inference into fact. Request uses a spoken-capable word rather than the older dictionary's written-petition sense.

Samuel mistakes repeated calls for Eli's calls. The third time Eli recognizes the source and gives a response containing LORD; Samuel's actual response in3:10 lacks that vocative, so it is not inserted. Both spoken Samuels remain. Not yet knowing the LORD describes Samuel's position before revelation, unlike the preceding priestly sons' culpable not-knowing. Eli's dim eyes and inability to see are physical setting details, not a translator's diagnosis of spiritual blindness.

## Ritual exploitation stays concrete

The three-toothed hook, four vessel names, taking what the hook brings up, demand for raw meat before the fat is burned and threatened force all remain. Presently means immediately in the offerer's request. The servant's I will take it by force is personal-I in coercive speech, not divine entitlement. The young men's sin and people's abhorrence of the offering stay explicit.

The report of the sons lying with women at the tabernacle door retains the source euphemism. No consent, victim ages or explicit sexual detail is invented. Eli's rebuke is followed by refusal to listen because the LORD would slay them; that causal direction is not reversed for theological comfort. Hannah's yearly coat and visits continue beside her lifelong lending of Samuel, preserving care rather than narrating an unsupplied abandonment.

## A community can expect rescue and still be defeated

Philistine speech first says God has come, then speaks of these mighty Gods who struck Egyptians with plagues in the wilderness. Singular and plural remain, as does their wilderness localization. These are reported enemy claims, not silently corrected narrator theology. Their repeated quit yourselves like men is rendered as conduct yourselves like men, not leave or surrender.
