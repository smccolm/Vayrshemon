## Plural-I has a speaker,not a universal divine meaning

44's divine address uses plural-I and my-plural words,face,fury,anger,name,law and servants.45:3quotes Baruch with personal-I,my-sorrow and my-sighing;the quoting deity does not absorb the quoted person's identity.45:4/5resumes divine plural-I.46:5's final says-LORD locates its seeing-I in the oracle;46:8gives personified Egypt a civic plural-I boast.46:18/25–28return to the divine speaker. Shared morphology therefore distinguishes collective selfhood from biological number without declaring every plural-I divine.

## The dissenting assembly and the women's our-men

44:15introduces men who knew their wives' rites and a large group of women.44:16–19uses singular-we for the answering community;44:19's our-one-men phrase preserves the female-associated viewpoint rather than replacing it with a generic family reference.44:20/24explicitly address men and women or all women again.44:25quotes the vow in singular-we. Both beside paired groups and paired speaking/doing is retained without inventing a two-person dual.46:16uses singular-we for the fleeing soldiers' shared proposal of return;our-own-people and land-of-birth do not assert that every soldier has one birthplace.

## Opposing explanations of disaster remain opposed

44:17claims prosperity during the Queen-of-heaven rites;44:18claims deprivation after stopping them.44:21–23answers that the rites caused the disaster. Both causal accounts retain their speakers. Queen of heaven stays a title relation without an imported deity identification.44:19asks whether cakes and libations were made without our men;no consent law is invented.44:25's repeated you-will-perform-vows remains as printed without turning it into an unambiguously sincere divine endorsement.

44:9retains five repeated wickedness phrases.44:12keeps repeated consumption and the four judgment labels.44:14retains none-escape beside except-escapees;44:28retains the later few survivors rather than harmonizing away the tension. ## Numbers and the two warriors

45:1and46:2retain fourth-year dates.46:12has one mighty warrior stumbling against another and both falling;dual-they marks that illustrative pair,not a two-person army.46:16each-on-other is a distributive collapse among many and receives no cardinal or dual.44:28few has no invented exact count. Other repeated whole/all expressions retain their supplied scope without false census precision.

## Bodily and military images remain difficult

44:22bear means endure.46:10keeps the sword devouring,filling and becoming blood-drunk beside the sacrifice image.46:11's many medicines and no-cure are the oracle's irony.46:20retains destruction coming toward the fair heifer rather than substituting another version's insect.46:22keeps voice like serpent without adding a particular hiss.46:26retains later inhabitation beside devastation;46:28distinguishes not-total-destruction from not-unpunished.47:3keeps weak hands as the reason fathers do not look back.47:6addresses the sword as thou;47:7answers with it under an assigned command. No added moral verdict or modern explanation resolves those images.
