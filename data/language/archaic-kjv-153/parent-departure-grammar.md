## Plural-self repents while personal witnesses speak

39:16–18uses divine plural-I and my-plural words in the promise to Ebedmelech.40:4is Nebuzaradan's personal release offer;40:10Gedaliah's personal undertaking beside communal-us;40:15Johanan's personal proposal of secret killing.42:4/9/19–21retain Jeremiah's personal voice around the divine quotation.42:10–12/17and43:10/12resume divine plural-I.42:10repent-me receives an archaic reflexive object,plural-I plus plural-self-accusative. This is an application of the existing reflexive paradigm,not a new ending or a denial that the source describes repentance over enacted evil.

## The remnant's singular-we can promise and refuse

41:8uses singular-we for the ten survivors' joint appeal;the explicit ten still counts distinct men.42:2/3/5/6uses singular-we and our-one for the remnant's petition and oath. They initially say thy-God,the prophet answers your-God,and they then say our-God.42:13/14quotes the communal determination to seek safety in Egypt;42:20quotes their earlier promise back to them.43:2/3uses our-one and singular-us in the accusation against Jeremiah and Baruch. Shared identity does not imply narrator agreement or moral approval.
