## One divine speaker,plural-I and singular-we
Isaiah6:8 keeps the quoted deity's plural-I in whom shall I send beside singular-we in who will go for us. The prophet's enclosing I heard and responding here am I remain personal-I. This encodes internally plural selfhood and a communal singular reference without asserting three bodies,three gods or an unprinted council roster. In1:18 singular-we includes the addressed community in reasoning together; it is not interchangeable with the divine self-reference in6:8.
