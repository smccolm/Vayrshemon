## Singular-we belongs to more than one community

The safety-denying people5:12,the hypothetical communal questions5:19/24,the attacking siege cohort6:4/5,the refusing people6:16/17,the frightened inhabitants6:24/26,and temple worshippers7:10 each speak singular-we. The same form does not certify shared identity across turns. At8:8 it is a claim to wisdom;at8:14/15/20 it is fear,disappointed hope and not being saved. Our-one holds plural hands6:24 and the communal God. Speaker annotations identify who owns each collective self.

## Personal witness and plural divine speech

The prophet's social inference5:4/5,frustrated warning6:10/11,and sorrow8:18/21 use personal-I. The I-will-pour6:11 follows the personal voice under the supplied wording;the next says-LORD6:12 explicitly restores divine plural-I. My-people6:26and8:19/21/22 belongs to the lamenting witness within the community. At8:19 personal report,civic questions and divine plural-I reply occur within one verse. Personal what-have-I-done8:6 remains a hypothetical individual self-question quoted inside divine speech. Personal myself8:18 is not rewritten as divine plural-self.

## Temple repetition does not count divine persons

The temple-of-LORD triple7:4 is retained as rhetorical repetition,not an implied trinitarian enumeration. The divine my-name/my-people possessives retain plural-self grammar without assigning a number of internal persons. Human plural faces7:19and plural nations remain ordinary multiplicity. Distinct speaker roles,reflexives and noun plurality matter more than maximizing the visible incidence of a pronoun.

## The source may contradict expectations

7:10 keeps delivered-to-do-abominations rather than changing it to delivered-despite.7:16 forbids prayer,cry and intercession;the deity refuses to hear.7:22 retains did-not-speak-nor-command about burnt offerings/sacrifices at the Exodus despite earlier ritual legislation. No merely,only or outside harmonization removes that tension.7:31 retains both not-commanded and not-entered-my-heart. The opaque he-made-it-in-vain and pen-in-vain8:8 remain the KJV wording.6:27 keeps tower/fortress rather than substituting assayer from another rendering.

## Material and animal senses stay exact

Battlement is wall-top,retaining architectural KJV wording rather than vine-branch replacement. Bird-cage is bird-enclosure without a specified mechanism. Snort is nose-blow,distinct from neighing. Rending into pieces5:6 uses rend,not tear meaning an eye-drop. Bow is the weapon,lead is metal,founder is a metal worker,fan is not used for the earlier winnowing action,and crown/stock distinctions remain inherited. Turtle8:7 is turtledove;crane and swallow are birds. The corrected swallow-bird sense from132is inherited. No modern species identification is asserted.

## Negative scope and uncomfortable images

Repeated peace-peace/no-peace6:14and8:11,not-a-full-end5:10/18,and no-burial8:2 stay explicit. Children intended to eat bread5:17 are not turned into the object of eating. Heart,ear circumcision,burning words,wooden people and rejected silver remain concrete metaphors. Corpses7:33 remain dead bodies;fray is frighten-away. Black8:21 remains mourning imagery rather than a racial identity. Despair8:3 is translated as a source statement,not advice to a reader.

## Natural variation preserves meaningful recurrence

Witness statements,dialogue turns,commands,conditional clauses and lament keep their differing structures. Each replaces distributive every-one without adding cardinal one. First7:12 is temporal priority;only-son6:26 is uniqueness without an invented count. The repeated temple phrase,peace formula and indictment are source rhetoric and intentionally recur. Variation does not license deleting repetition that carries meaning. Exclamatory endings mark woe and disappointed health without unsupported punctuation glyphs.
