## Plural-I as office and authority, personal-I as embodied speaker

Benhadad's ownership demand and Ahab's submission use plural-I for the ruler as office and domain (20:3–6,9). The boast distinguishes a personal self-curse from followers of the military ruler (20:10). Ahab's report about his family and valuables remains personal (20:7), as do Benhadad's plea to survive (20:32), Ahab's garden wish (21:2), his resentment (22:8,18), disguise (22:30), and wounded body (22:34). Jezebel's promise of seizure uses the coercive royal plural-self (21:7); that choice does not excuse or obscure her authorship of the letters in Ahab's name. Royal treaty commitments reset plural-I at the change of king in 20:34. Jehoshaphat's offer of people and horses speaks as the crown (22:4), and Ahaziah's proposed servants are royal possessives (22:49). Divine plural-I belongs to the quoted oracles (20:13,28,42;21:21–22,29), not to their human messengers as private persons. The person categories follow discourse role, never a count invented for the Trinity.

## Singular-we, plural-we and the actual two

Syrian officers speak as one military body in their terrain-theology forecast (20:23,25). The same singular-whole construction marks Israel's territorial claim and proposed campaign (22:3,15). Ramoth is our-whole possession, not a plurality of disconnected owners. By contrast, the attendants who put sackcloth and cords on their separate bodies use plural-we with of-we-plural loins and heads (20:31); the spokesperson's I-pray remains personal. The two kings consulting a prophet use the actual dual (22:7–8). Do not make every assembly singular-we or every pair plural-I. The source's shift from thy singular to ye plural in 20:28 is retained.

Province is land-rule; Jezreelite is the named place-person; infantry is foot-warrior. Forbear is self-keep, the alternative to campaign, not an ancestor. Deputy is stand-rule-person. Armour-joint is distinct from the body's joint, and driver is drive-person. Wound-inflict explicitly derives a verb from wound-cause. Abominably is the narrator's abhor-way evaluation; subdued walking is quiet-way rather than a diagnosis. Existing related zero conversion remains available for search, consent, sealing, fasting, increase and warfare in their event constructions; it does not license unrelated English homographs.
