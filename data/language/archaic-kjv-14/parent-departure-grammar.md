# KJV historical register 0.10-kjv.13 — one pledged voice, name-bearing messenger and difficult judgments

This continuation inherits v12 and authors Exodus 19–24, 169 new verses. With Genesis and unchanged frozen Jonah it contains 2,244 of 31,102 authored verses; 28,858 remain. It is an incomplete checkpoint toward ONE entire Bible in ONE .vay. Final historical text is permanently excluded from modernization. All interpretation notes are translator self-review, not independent certification or recovered ancient attestation.

## Divine self, exclusive relation and external plurality

The Sinai first person uses `ipl`, `mypl` and reflexive `iplself`: I-plural brought the community to my-plural self, claim all earth, and prohibit other gods before me. Internal self-plurality differs from the separately counted gods. Singular thou addresses the covenant party while plural ye can address its members; switches in the source remain. No pronoun operation automatically converts third-person God, LORD or he to first person. Jealous possession and visitation of fathers' iniquity upon descendants remain uncomfortable source claims rather than receiving a modern moral repair.

## One pledged voice and two human delegates

All people answering together in 19:8 and with one voice in 24:3 use `wesg`; the pledge after reading the covenant in 24:7 repeats it. The fearful request for mediated hearing in 20:19 likewise speaks for the whole. Moses and the newly named Joshua in 24:14 use the inherited dual construction, distinct from the Aaron/Hur pair left to judge. The servant's first-person declaration in 21:5 remains ordinary personal-I: a named family does not automatically turn every human speaker into a corporate self.

## A name in another agent

Exodus 23:20–23 keeps the sent angel, his voice and the deity's speaking as distinct but linked roles. My-plural name is in him remains an indwelling/name-bearing construction, not merely a label attached outside him. The translation does not resolve whether this entails identity, delegated authority or another theological account. He does not pardon remains a stated limit. The he-to-I transition in 23:25 is retained. These are useful historical resources for later identity passages, not a claim that the still-untranslated Trinity passages have already been completed.

## Bodily revelation and constrained access

Descent, summit, darkness, fire, voice and heaven coexist as source locations/images. Seeing thunder and trumpet-noise in 20:18 retains the sensory crossing. Fear not beside his fear before your faces remains a deliberate tension. Leaders see God, feet and a sapphire-like paved work, then eat and drink; no later prohibition is imported to cancel this scene. Moses alone comes near while others ascend only to their permitted distance. The altar-step restriction keeps its stated bodily-exposure reason.

## Servitude, family constraint and unequal liability

Six years of bought Hebrew service, seventh-year departure, master-owned wife/children, ear-piercing and for-ever service remain explicit. A sold daughter's distinct release and marriage provisions are not rewritten as modern employment or voluntary adoption. The personal-I pledge of staying follows family separation pressure; the translation does not certify free consent. Rod-beating, the money rationale, eye/tooth release and the fixed servant compensation retain status distinctions. Judges follows this KJV even where other readings might differ. The source is preserved without endorsing these rules as present-day advice.

## Conditional cases and evidence

Known dangerous behavior, notice, failure to confine and fatal result are separate animal-liability conditions. Ox-for-ox, five-for-one, four-for-one and double restitution remain different cases. The oath, witnessed remains, absent/present owner and theft exception structure entrusted-property rules. The pregnant woman's fruit departs and no mischief follows remains ambiguous; no miscarriage/premature-birth decision or unstated harm recipient is inserted. Great/hard judicial matters from the prior version remain distinct from physical matter. An audit checks the conditions as well as numeric multipliers.

## Land, rest, strangers and hostile promises

No partiality toward the poor and no distortion of their justice coexist. Stranger-heart invokes remembered foreignness. Rest benefits animals, dependants and outsiders; fallow produce feeds poor people and then beasts. The text also commands exclusion and gradual expulsion of named peoples. Its stated danger of a desolate land and multiplying beasts is retained without making the policy a modern humanitarian claim. The unnamed river stays unnamed. Liquors are produce liquids; kid is a goat, not a child; hornets remain the source designation without an imposed allegory.

## Material lexicon, numbers and preservation

New senses distinguish horn-goring from blood-shedding gore, blow-mark stripe from color stripe, sacred pollution from physical dirt, and attendant minister from modern office. Tables are writing tablets; Sapphire remains the historical label without modern mineral certification. Third/fourth generations remain separate, thousands in 20:6 stays indefinite without adding generations, twelve pillars match twelve tribes, two halves divide blood, and forty days plus forty nights do not become eighty days. Overlong inflected refresh expands to a passive infinitive plus capacity verb under the purpose clause, preserving sense without reducing type size. Parent entries, suffixes, Maxima, reading map, approved layout/paint and frozen Jonah remain unchanged. Structural checks do not prove literary equivalence.
