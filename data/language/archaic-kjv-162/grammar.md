# Ezekiel25–28: civic boasts and maritime lament

## 1. Divine self and attributed civic self
The narrative introductions use personal-I. Divine speech uses plural-I and my-plural;no unprinted singular-we or dual is inserted. Tyrus's boast in26:2 and27:3 uses civic plural-I. The prince's I-am-God claims in28:2 and28:9 are attributed exalted-self quotations,immediately challenged by man-not-God. The form marks a speaker's adopted selfhood,not a translator certificate of divinity. At the oracle formula the same plural-self morphology belongs to the deity. This keeps discourse attribution essential rather than assigning a fixed identity to every pronoun form.

## 2. Gloating and neighboring peoples
The aha quotation receives an explicit gloating interjection sense sharing inherited ah;the exclamatory morphology supplies force without an exclamation glyph. Hands clapping,feet stamping and heart contempt remain separate. Despite in25:6 is contempt,not a concessive connective. Palaces of the eastern men,Rabbah's camel stable,Moab's named frontier cities and Cherethims follow the supplied wording. No modern ethnicity or geographic location is inferred from a source label.

## 3. Tyrus's siege and absolute predictions
26:1 gives an eleventh year and first day but no month number. Daughters in the field remains a civic kin expression. Nebuchadrezzar keeps his source spelling and king-of-kings title. War engines,axes and garrisons follow the KJV categories. He shifts to they in26:12.26:14's no-more-built and26:21's never-found-again are not qualified to match outside history.26:20 positively sets glory in the living land;no alternate negative reading replaces it.

## 4. Shipbuilding trades and semantic additions
The lament distinguishes mariners,pilots,rowers and calkers. Ship-guide and ship-close are authored lexical compounds for navigation and caulking;bench and ebony receive precise material/structural senses. The bench is not assumed always wooden when the source says ivory. Gebal's elders remain the skilled workers. Senir fir,Lebanon cedar,Bashan oak,Chittim ivory,Elishah coverings and Ashurite builders retain their own relations. No invented modern ship plan,sealant or species identification is inserted.

## 5. Commercial inventory without euphemism
Fairs are markets and occupy means trade in this chapter.27:13 explicitly trades human persons as well as brass vessels;the translation does not hide that as labor services. Horsemen remains beside horses and mules. Ivory horns,Pannag,white wool,Dan and Javan going to and fro,precious chariot cloths and ships singing follow the supplied readings rather than alternate reconstructions. Pannag's place-or-commodity status remains unresolved. Eden in the trading list has a separate contextual entry from the explicitly named garden in28:13.

## 6. Lament,collapse and repetition
The long cargo/personnel roll in27:27 is complete,including calkers and commercial agents. Pilots' cry shakes suburban grounds;the oar-holders descend to land. Wallowing uses bodily rolling,not a scroll. Baldness,sackcloth,ashes,bitter crying and lament remain separate actions. Merchants' hissing and the absolute never-more conclusion remain source rhetoric. Repeated all,sea,merchant and wealth expressions carry the lament's accumulation rather than being randomly replaced for superficial variety.

## 7. Prince,king,cherub and divine passives
28:2 calls the addressee prince;28:12 begins a king's lament. Thou-sealest-up-the-sum remains a compressed image without a calculated number. All nine gemstone labels plus gold remain in order;tabrets and pipes follow the supplied musical wording without a new instrument design. The covering cherub and fire-stones are retained within the king-address;no Satan name or later supernatural biography is added.28:16 keeps the unexplained they and profane-as-common. Glorified and sanctified remain passive divine predicates in28:22 and28:25.28:23 keeps wounded-shall-be-judged rather than substituting another reading.

## 8. Coverage and evidential limits

The only explicit numeric values are26:1[11,1];named cargo lists do not create extra cardinals. All verse anchors,pronoun attributions and inherited target identities are checked. This is translator self-review and structural validation,not independent literary certification. Modern0.10,frozen Jonah and all earlier sealed editions remain unchanged.
