# Ezekiel21–24: paired cities and the mourning sign

## 1. Contextual self,community and pair
The narrator uses personal-I at speech boundaries and in his enacted mourning sign. The deity uses plural-I and my-plural. The abrupt should-we-make-mirth question in21:10 is read as a shared audience question within the oracle;24:19 is the questioning community's singular-we. This does not turn every plural group into a singular collective. Two named civic sisters in23:2 license dual they in their shared clauses,and dual you at23:40 and23:49. Their sons,daughters,lovers and attackers remain ordinary plurals. Inherited possessives do not encode that dual distinction;the explicit pronouns do. No new pronoun paradigm or divine person count is invented.

## 2. Sword address and exact source quantities
Chapter21 addresses the sword directly,then returns to the narrator and the road sign. Double the third time retains2and3 without calculating an unsupplied six. Two ways,both twain,one land and two ways again retain their printed repetitions. One-way-or-other is idiomatic directional choice rather than a cardinal. The triple overturn remains three repeated verbs without an added number. The blade point is a new semantic entry distinct from pointing with a finger. Private chambers are not latrines,and furbishing is weapon polishing.

## 3. Difficult readings and conflicting judgments
The sword cuts off both righteous and wicked in21:3–4;chapter18 is not rewritten to harmonize that declaration. Wrapped-up sword,arrows made bright,captains in21:22 and the first-person question in21:30 follow the supplied KJV instead of alternate readings. The unnamed prince and the one whose right it is remain unnamed;right here is entitlement.22:16 preserves take thine inheritance in thyself.22:25 says prophets,while22:27 separately says princes. These choices record source fidelity,not a claim that every textual ambiguity has been solved.

## 4. City accusation and office distinctions
Chapter22 keeps civic feminine reference beside the source's it/her changes. Fatherless,widow,stranger,poor and needy remain distinct categories. Set light by means esteem little,lead is metal,and ravening is predatory devouring rather than a bird. Priests' sacred/common distinction is separate from ritual clean/unclean. The deity's passive I-am-profaned remains;the furnace comparison retains both metal lists in their own orders and the leaving-there clause. The hedge gap is an opening,not the inherited gape verb.

## 5. Paired civic allegory without embellishment
Aholah and Aholibah are explicitly Samaria and Jerusalem. The elder/sister relation and shared mother remain,with no invented etymology. The sexual and punitive images are translated directly in their source terms,without added physical description or endorsement. Dote here is excessive attachment;bear in23:4 and23:37 is actual birth within the metaphor,whereas23:35 and23:49 use endure for liability. Flesh/issue retains the source's coarse comparison without substituting a clinical account. The two cities are dual only when both are the referent;the ambiguous final they in23:3 and their adornments in23:42 are not forced into that pair.

## 6. Reversal,possessors and unresolved references
Her mind in23:17 becomes my-plural mind in23:18. Desire for the attractive officers returns as the threat of those same groups. The attackers judge according to their judgments,not a silently substituted divine standard. Sabeans remains the supplied people label,not drunkards. The text's cup,shards and body metaphors remain concise;break the sherds is not changed to gnaw.23:40 preserves the shift from paired ye to singular thou. The final recognition of plural-I does not remove the two addressed civic identities.

## 7. Pot and mourning sign
Chapter24 adds precise senses for pot scum and head tire. Scum does not assert rust versus foam;the existing culinary seasoning verb handles spice. Bones under the pot,with lies,uncovered blood and active-purge/failed-purging remain as printed. The final they-judge in24:14 is not changed to I. The prophet's wife dies at evening between the two morning actions.24:22's I-have-done is personal Ezekiel,not the deity;24:24 names him in third person and resumes divine plural-I. No-mourning beside mourning-one-to-another in24:23 remains an unresolved source tension.

## 8. Coverage,quantities and limits

Same day,each,another and every side do not create counts. Source-critical anchors,pronoun boundaries,quantities and all earlier target texts are checked;this is translator self-review and structural validation,not independent literary certification. Modern0.10,earlier sealed historical editions and frozen Jonah remain unchanged. The entire Bible remains incomplete.
