# Hosea1–7: rejected names,civic bride and the returning singular-we

## 1. Distinguishing divine,civic and personal speakers
The divine first-person voice uses plural-I and plural-my. The nation's personified female voice in2:5,7,12uses its own civic plural-I and plural-my inside divine narration;the final they say,my God in2:23is also the people's civic possessive. This is a shift of speaker,not God calling himself his own God. Hosea's first-person receipt,purchase and address in3:1–3stay personal I.6:1–3forms the people's communal singular-we appeal;7:5's our king uses the corresponding our-one possessive without replacing the narrator's surrounding divine speech.5:14's I,even I retains both emphatic divine plural-I occurrences. The pronouns do not assert a number of divine persons or turn genuine plural children,prophets,kings,houses,heavens and body parts into single objects.

## 2. Names that can reject and restore a relationship
Hosea son of Beeri,Gomer daughter of Diblaim,the child Jezreel,Loruhamah and Loammi get contextual entries. Earlier human/place namesakes are not merged. The child's Jezreel form stays visibly the same as the place named in the blood charge. Ammi/Ruhamah preserve the affirmative addresses rather than normalizing away their relationship to the earlier negative names. The explanatory no-mercy/not-my-people clauses remain translated rather than replaced by etymological glosses alone. Ishi and Baali remain distinct quoted address forms;the marital/master-Baal associations are recorded without inventing an additional verse. Jareb remains the supplied royal designation,not an imposed historical identification. All twelve additions are contextual names/address forms;inherited ordinary vocabulary supplies the rest.

## 3. The coercive marriage analogy is not softened
Wife and children of whoredoms,the first son's bare him,subsequent births without him,and weaning remain distinct. Judgment on Jezreel blood and Jehu's house is not harmonized away against earlier narratives. Litigation against mother repeats,the marriage relation is denied,and the threat includes stripping to birth-nakedness,making wilderness and killing by thirst. The woman's own stated motive for return is better provision,not an inserted confession. Six lover-provisions,wool/flax covering,public exposure,ended festivals and destroyed vineyards remain concrete. Therefore I allure follows the threats without an added apology. Comfort and betrothal coexist with coercion;the translator neither endorses the treatment nor replaces the source's agents with passive happenings.

## 4. Naming,nonhuman hearing and incomplete reciprocal speech
The I/my versus LORD their God shift in1:7and first-person versus LORD in2:20are retained without invented identities.1:11's one head is an explicit numeral,while collective Judah and Israel do not automatically become a numerical dual.2:18includes covenant partners among beasts,birds and ground-creepers;the bodily lie-down verb is permitted only in its actual bodily context.2:21–22repeats hear across God,heavens,earth,crops and Jezreel instead of silently changing every verb to answer.2:23moves she/they/thou through the restoration promise.3:1's woman remains unnamed and friend remains friend;the purchase is15silver pieces plus one homer and half homer barley,without supplying shekels. Flagons of wine stays the supplied KJV wording,not raisin cakes.3:3's so I also for thee stays elliptical;no explicit abstinence vow is added.

## 5. Priests,cult acts and unresolved social accusations
No truth,mercy or God-knowledge anchors a forensic dispute. Blood touches blood and eat the people's sin remain literal source constructions;the latter is not silently changed to sin offerings. Priestly rejection and forgetting children remain divine active speech. Stocks are wood,not foot restraints or financial shares;staff is the rod used in the consultation image. Oak,poplar and elm remain supplied tree names.4:14's I will not punish your daughters/spouses stays negative while the accusation turns to the others. Israel's backsliding heifer and lamb in a large place retain their difficult juxtaposition. Her rulers love Give ye and wind binds her in her wings preserve abrupt voice and gender shifts. No source-absent bribe object or named perpetrator repairs the prose.

## 6. Withdrawal and a communal response that is questioned
Mizpah/Tabor snares,deep slaughter,unfindable deity,strange children and a devouring month retain their unsettling images. The command followed in5:11is not given an invented issuer. Moth/rottenness and lion/young-lion are distinct divine destructive comparisons;tear is rending,never eye-tears. King Jareb cannot cure despite the appeal.5:15withdrawal precedes6:1–3's singular-we appeal:he tore,will heal;struck,will bind;after two days revive,in the third raise. Those units are not replaced by a later named resurrection story. The next divine questions interrupt the appeal;goodness vanishes like dew. Thy judgments in6:5is not emended to my judgments,like men in6:7does not become proper-name Adam,and by consent in6:9does not become a substituted place.6:11retains he set harvest/when I returned without deciding harvest's outcome for the reader.

## 7. Oven politics,unawareness and exclamatory accusation
7:1's would-have-healed is unrealized,not completed healing. Inside thief and outside robber-band remain. False words distinguish lies from bodily lying down and lying in wait. The baker's raising phrase is kept without inserting an unprinted object;dough,kneading,leavening,overnight sleep and morning flame remain. Our-one king introduces communal possession inside the political report. Unturned cake,unknown strength-loss,unnoticed gray hairs and foolish heartless dove retain concrete awkwardness. Divine netting and chastisement are active;7:13uses two exclamatory be forms without unsupported exclamation glyphs. Assembling for grain/wine is not replaced by a different self-cutting reading. Return,but not to the Most High remains a failed destination,not automatically repentance.
