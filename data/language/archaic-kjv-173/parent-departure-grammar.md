# Daniel10–12: embodied witnesses,paired kings and the closed book

## 1. Personal interpreter and actual paired kings
Daniel's bodily mourning,fear,speech loss,questions and confessed non-understanding use personal I. The sent interpreter also speaks personal I in10:11–14,20–21and11:1–2;being sent or delivering a divine message does not force plural-I. Michael's prince role is named without making him the unnamed interpreter.11:6joins the two opposing kings with actual dual they/themselves;11:27explicitly pairs both kings at one table and likewise uses actual dual. Neither pair becomes singular-we. There is no quoted divine first-person declaration or first-person communal confession in these79verses,so plural-I and singular-we are not inserted merely to increase their frequency. Previously established divine and communal usages remain intact in the inherited text.

## 2. Embodied distress without a modern diagnosis
Three full weeks of mourning recur as three whole weeks without pleasant bread,flesh,wine or self-anointing. This is not translated as no food of any kind. The twenty-fourth day of the first month is explicit. The linen-clad figure's beryl,lightning,fire-lamp and polished-brass comparisons stay comparisons;no identity or wings are supplied. Daniel alone sees while companions only quake and flee. Comeliness becomes bodily corruption,not moral guilt;no strength is repeatedly retained. Deep sleep face-groundward,knees,palms,touched lips,speechlessness and absent breath retain their sequence. Greatly beloved and repeated be strong do not remove fear. No clinical label explains the experience.

## 3. Princes,local identities and exact waiting
Michael gains one contextual proper-name entry distinct from earlier human namesakes. Existing Hiddekel,Uphaz,Persia,Grecia and Chittim entries are reused. The Persian prince resists21days;Michael is one of chief princes;the speaker remained with the kings of Persia,plural,without adopting another textual reading. Your prince in10:21names Michael's relation to the addressees rather than the interpreter's possession.11:1retains Darius the Mede's first year and repeated even I. Three future Persian kings and a fourth richer king are not assigned outside historical names. Multiple heavenside agents do not entail a numerical Trinity.

## 4. Succession,alliances and pronouns left unresolved
The mighty king's broken kingdom divides to four winds,not posterity and not his prior dominion. The compressed south-king/one-prince and he/him comparisons remain. Estate denotes inherited social position rather than money. The south-king's daughter,her begetter,bringers and strengthener are given up;the marriage diplomacy is not made successful. Actual dual refers only to paired kings,while unnamed bringers remain ordinary plural. Princes carried with gods and vessels stays princes rather than an alternate idols reading. South's king coming into his kingdom retains the supplied attribution. Sons become an unnamed singular campaigner;armies,hearts,arms,food-sharing betrayers and ambiguous he/his are not replaced by names from a commentary.

## 5. Political violence and contested worship
Choler is anger;forecasting devices is planning,not weather;fenced cities are fortified and the raised mount is siege earthwork. Fattest places preserve a richness metaphor. Flattery,robbers,mischief,corruption and accusations belong to the reported prophecy. The daughter of women and corrupting her remain unsoftened. Both kings speak false words at one table:lies never compile as bodily lying. Intelligence with covenant-forsakers means communicating with them. God of forces stays an opaque source title;desire of women is not assigned an orientation or substitute deity. God's hierarchy and kingly self-magnification do not become translator doctrine. Escape exceptions for Edom,Moab and chief Ammonites remain distinct from Egypt's non-escape. Libyans and Ethiopians at his steps are not forced into a named military relation. Plural seas and palace tabernacles remain at the unnamed final holy mountain.

## 6. Understanding can fail after instruction
Understanding ones teach many but fall through sword,flame,captivity and spoil;little help stays little. Some fall to be tried,purged and whitened. Whitening retains the source image without a racial interpretation. Daniel10:1says he understood;12:8explicitly says he heard but did not understand. Neither is rewritten to force one cognitive state across the whole account. The final response closes and seals the words rather than supplying the requested explanation. Wisdom and understanding distinguish groups in12:10without making the translator certify the distinction.

## 7. Awaking,divided time and allotted rest
Michael stands for the people's children during unprecedented trouble;deliverance is qualified by being found written. Many dust-sleepers awake,some to eternal life and some to shame/eternal contempt;many does not become all. Wise shine like firmament and teachers like stars. Two other figures stand at the river banks;one asks the linen-clad man upon waters. The oath raises both named hands and swears by him who lives forever,without merging swearer and referent. Time,times,half remains uncomputed:unlike7:25's dividing of time,this passage actually says half,but still does not name years.1290days and1335days are separately preserved,not replaced by their45-day difference. Rest and standing in one's allotted lot remain distinct,without an invented date or modern doctrinal paraphrase.
