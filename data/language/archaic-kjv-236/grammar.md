# Romans 10–12: the shared body and the borrowed root

These 78 drafted verses extend Romans to 336 verses. Projected authored coverage is 28,267 of 31,102, leaving 2,835. Drafts require publication, native composition, tests and viewed proofs before joining verified coverage. Earlier targets and seals are unchanged; the historical KJV remains permanently excluded from modernization. Structural checks and translator self-review are not independent literary certification.

## Many members, one we

The letter's personal I continues in testimony, requests, apostolic self-description and the Elias quotation. Elias's I-alone-left in 11:3 remains personal: isolation and fear do not automatically trigger plural-self. The hypothetical grafted recipient's I in 11:19 is personal as well. In 10:19 personal I-say and the quoted divine I-provoke occur together; the analysis keeps their referents distinct rather than choosing one number for the whole verse.

Divine quotation uses plural-self in 10:19–21, 11:4/27 and 12:19. My-plural hands in 10:21 and my-plural covenant in 11:27 use matching possession. To-myself in 11:4 is the co-referential plural-self reflexive, while the seven thousand men remain an independently counted human group. No count of divine persons is inferred.

The preaching we of 10:8 and the quoted our-report in 10:16 are singular-whole community forms. Romans 12:4–7 makes this choice especially visible: we-one have many parts in one body; we-one, being many, are one body in Christ. The explicit numeral one stays numerical, and many stays uncounted. Singular-we does not erase the members, their mutual relation, or their different functions. The source says not all members have the same office, not that no member has an office. Our-one service belongs to the shared whole while individual teachers, exhorters, givers, rulers and mercy-givers retain separate roles.

Bodies in 12:1 stays plural and sacrifice stays singular. The awkward shared offering is not regularized into either one physical body or many separately specified sacrifices. No new bodily procedure is supplied by the translator.

## Hearing, confession and partial negation

Zeal without knowledge in 10:2 retains both the attributed zeal and its limit. The testimony verb is witness-giving, not carrying a physical record. Christ as end of law in 10:4 keeps end without deciding in the verse whether it means termination, goal, or both. The mouth/heart and confession/belief relations remain distinct in 10:8–10. The argument's ascent and descent parenthetical explanations remain in full.

The three questions in 10:14 preserve their dependent sequence: calling on the unbelieved, believing the unheard, and hearing without a proclaimer. Sending remains necessary in 10:15, with the complete gospel-of-peace and good-things wording. Not all obeyed in 10:16 is explicit partial negation. It does not become all-did-not or nobody-obeyed. The source word-of-God in 10:17 remains, without substituting a different textual witness.

Sound in 10:18 is audible proclamation, not a healthy condition or a depth measurement. Both all-earth and world's-ends scopes remain. Gainsaying in 10:21 is against-speaking, not gain as profit. The printed disobedience and speech-opposition descriptions remain distinct. The hand-stretching divine self remains plural-self with plural hands.

## Intercession against, not only intercession for

The supplied Elias passage says intercession to God against Israel. The previous for-asking advocacy vocabulary is not forced into an accusatory relation. A distinct against-ask sense records this use, with God as addressee and Israel as opposed party. The translation does not add a legal verdict or imply that all intercession is hostile.

The response's 7,000 is exact: the Unta analysis is four times twelve cubed, plus seven times twelve, plus four. Those men have not bowed the knee to Baal's image. The divine reflexive adds no arithmetic to that count. The following present remnant has no supplied total, so it is not assigned 7,000 by inference.

Both halves of the grace/works antithesis in 11:6 remain, including otherwise-work-no-more-work. Election as the collective obtaining agent and the rest blinded in 11:7 are retained as the source's construction; no modern medical mechanism is invented. Slumber-spirit, eyes not seeing and ears not hearing remain with the supplied until-this-day phrase.

The table in 11:9 is a meal-table, not a writing tablet. Snare, trap, stumblingblock and recompense all remain predications of it. The quoted darkening of eyes and bowing of back are not converted into the translator's own injunction. The stumbling/falling question and its rejection remain beside the Gentile-salvation and jealousy-provoking purpose.

## The recipient does not carry the root

The olive-tree comparison preserves some broken branches, a wild recipient grafted among the remaining branches, and shared root and nourishing richness. Fatness means that fertility, not a moral judgment or a numerical amount of bodily fat. Bear-the-root means support, so the reversal is explicit: thou does not support the root; the root supports thee.

Office in the personal apostolic claim is apostleship, whereas office in 12:4 is bodily function. Emulation in 11:14 means rival desire, not computer emulation or merely copying another's accent. Saving some stays partial. Casting-away as world-reconciliation and receiving as life-from-dead remain metaphors without a new timetable for literal resurrections.

Natural and against-nature in 11:24 remain part of the supplied horticultural comparison. They do not import an unprinted sexual classification or a theory of ethnic biology. The goodness/severity contrast keeps its condition: goodness toward thee if continuing, otherwise cutting off. The other branches can be grafted again if they do not remain in unbelief. Both negations are preserved.

Blindness in part and until Gentile fullness in 11:25 retain both limits. Own conceit is one's self-estimation, not the older fancy or ornamental-token sense. All Israel in 11:26 remains all, with out-of-Sion and the removal of ungodliness from Jacob as supplied. The source's father's spelling in 11:28 is retained rather than silently rewritten. Enemies and beloved remain different stated relations, not resolved into one label.

Without repentance in 11:29 records change-of-mind or reconsideration of gifts and calling. It does not assert repentance from moral wrongdoing by God, and does not rewrite older uses of repent. The all-enclosed-in-unbelief and mercy-upon-all clauses in 11:32 preserve both alls. Concluded here is enclosed, not a logical deduction. The final of-him, through-him and to-him relations are not redistributed among unprinted divine persons.

## Conduct in the shared community

Reasonable service in 12:1 is rational service. The mind-renewing transformation and testing of the good, acceptable and perfect will in 12:2 retain all descriptions. Prove is testing or discerning here, not merely proving a theorem. Soberness in 12:3 is measured self-judgment and does not reduce to lack of alcohol. Every person receives a measure without an invented proportion or hierarchy.

Wait on ministering in 12:7 means attend to service. Exhortation is explicit urging toward conduct. Giving with simplicity, ruling with diligence and showing mercy cheerfully each retains its paired manner. Love without dissimulation means love without pretending. Cleaving to good is adherence, not splitting it. Brotherly affection and mutual preference retain the reciprocal relation without numeric one or physical dual.

Instant in prayer means persistent, not a momentary duration. Hospitality is stranger-welcome, not an institution of medical care. Condescending to low-estate people means association with those of low social station, not modern patronizing behavior. Estate is used here in its established social-condition sense, not landed property. Honest conduct before all includes honourability rather than only factual accuracy.

Peace with all retains if possible and so-far-as-within-your-power. Lieth is capacity, not falsehood or reclining. The self-avenging prohibition contrasts with divine plural-self repayment. Give-place-to-wrath retains its supplied relation without inserting an unnamed possessor. Feeding the hungry enemy and giving drink to the thirsty enemy remains beside the uncomfortable fire-coals-on-head image; the translation neither erases that image nor expands it into an added torture instruction. The passive/active reversal of being overcome by evil versus overcoming evil with good remains.
