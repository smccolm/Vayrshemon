# KJV historical register 0.10-kjv.11 — hardening, one worshipping whole and pass-over

This continuation inherits v10 and authors Exodus 7–12. With all Genesis and sealed Jonah it covers 1,916 of 31,102 verses. It is an incomplete checkpoint toward ONE complete Bible in ONE .vay. Earlier files remain preserved. The final historical Bible remains permanently excluded from modernization. These are authored interpretations and translator self-review, not independent certification or ancient attestation.

## Plural divine self and external divine plurality

The divine first person uses `ipl` (rôun) and `mypl`. Exodus 12:12 juxtaposes that internally plural speaking self, one named LORD, and the explicitly plural gods of Egypt. Those are different grammatical relations; no count of internal divine persons is inserted. Divine third-person self-designation in 9:5 stays third person, as does narration. Later Trinity passages remain untranslated and are not counted as completed work.

## The singular-we whole resists partition

Moses' `wesg` (ly) in 10:9 includes young, old, sons, daughters, flocks and herds. `ourone` marks one collective's possession while its members stay plural. Pharaoh's men-only and later children-without-livestock concessions attempt to split that declared whole. In 10:25–26 the one worshipping body insists on its animals while admitting ignorance of the exact future offering. Future commemorators in 12:27 say our-whole houses: their present group identifies with the remembered body without erasing plural houses or generations. Egyptian advisers' us and Egyptians' fearful we remain ordinary plural participation.

## Royal office and personal confession

Pharaoh's decrees and permissions can use `ipl`; his personal confession, request for forgiveness and request for blessing use `i`. Each transition is recorded per verse. The ritual community's singular-we and the ruler's office-self compete within conversation instead of acting as blanket ethnic labels. Evil before you in 10:10 preserves the unresolved threat/intent ambiguity. Sarcastic force is contextual, without an invented exclamation glyph. The inherited exclamatory resources remain available.

## Hardening without harmonization

Divine hardening, Pharaoh hardening his own heart, ambiguous he and passive constructions remain distinct. The future threat in 9:15 follows the KJV rather than another translation's counterfactual. Livestock dying, later livestock threatened, no green thing remaining and later animal departure are not repaired with invented imports or narrowed quantifiers. Keeping these tensions preserves source claims rather than certifying their historical resolution.

## Prophetic mouth and bodily figures

Aaron's prophet role in 7:1 is an authorized mouth, not necessarily a future-predictor. Moses as god to Pharaoh is a role description, not divine personal identity. God's finger, hand and the rod's handlers retain their bodily figures and distinct agents. The rod swallows rods as stated. Tactile darkness stays darkness that can be felt, without a weather explanation. The dog moving tongue in 11:7 remains a body idiom rather than generic peace.

## Ritual distribution and pass-over

Pass-over is an alien calque for the rite, event and victim by metonymy in kill the passover. Singular lamb/it distributes over households; it does not imply one national animal. Small households may join by soul-count and eating capacity. The source lamb explicitly comes from sheep or goats: that category stretch remains. Male/first-year qualification, three door positions, innards, leftover burning, girded loins and unbroken bones stay concrete. The destroyer in 12:23 receives no invented name or forced identity equation with the named deity.

## Participation, purchased people and shared rule

Stranger, foreigner, hired servant and bought-for-money servant remain distinct. Purchased human servitude and bodily circumcision are not softened into voluntary employment or abstract initiation. The initial stranger prohibition stands before admission for a willing sojourner and all his males. One law for home-born and stranger follows those conditions; it is not expanded into context-free modern equality. The social discomfort remains intentional.

## Material senses, quantities and preservation

New senses distinguish boil lesion from boiling, fly insect from flight, door post from travel-post and historical livestock disease from a modern diagnosis. Rie and hyssop retain source designations without disputed modern species substitutions. Coast means region. Leaven substance differs from leavening action. Borrow, lend and plunder coexist. Quantities include 80/83, three/seven days, dates 10/14/21, approximately 600,000 men besides children and 430 years; inherited Unta values remain. No new numeral base, suffix paradigm, Maxima mapping, typography or paint treatment is introduced. Compilation and layout checks do not prove literary equivalence.
