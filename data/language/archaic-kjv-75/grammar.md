# KJV historical continuation75 — exile, maintenance and contextual right

## First-person restraint within a collective language
These fifty verses have no source first-person I or we. The plurality of armies, captives and officials does not license inserting plural-I or singular-we. Gedaliah's commands in25:24 address you; they remain second-person. The established personal-I, plural-I self and singular-we whole retain their contextual distinction. Future divine and Trinitarian passages are still to be translated; no later doctrine is backfilled into the fall of Jerusalem.

## Captivity numbers and coexisting calendars
Keep three years of Jehoiakim's service, Jehoiachin's eighteen years of age and three months of rule, the capturing king's eighth year, and Zedekiah's twenty-one years of age and eleven years of rule distinct. Ten thousand captives in24:14 coexists with seven thousand warriors and one thousand artisans/smiths in24:16; no invented two-thousand category completes the arithmetic. Siege dates, destruction date and the thirty-seventh captivity year use separately labelled units. Second priest is rank; three door-keepers, five royal attendants and sixty men are counts. Ten companions in25:25 does not include Ishmael in the ten. The inherited Unta system represents these quantities, not a decimal-looking digit substitution.

## Displacement, destruction and withheld pardon
Would not pardon becomes pardon + refuse, preserving unwillingness without converting it into inability. Defection uses loyalty-change, distinct from the inherited defect meaning flaw. Forts use siege-fortress, while the plain is level terrain rather than unadorned style. The source says the sons are killed before Zedekiah's eyes, the eyes put out, and the captive bound; the bodily sequence is retained. Temple burning, wall destruction, deportation and execution are separate actions. The narrative's theological causation remains attributed to its narrator; translation does not certify it.

## Office, occupation and material provision
Vinedresser is vine-care-person; husbandman is field-work-person, with no marital presupposition. Allowance is fixed-gift: recurring material maintenance, not permission. Daily amount, every day and all days of life remain the source's cumbersome overlapping scopes. Continual eating is expressed as eating without cessation in a habitual context, not a claim of literally uninterrupted chewing. Nebuchadnezzar reuses an existing proper-name entry; its dictionary association with a different episode does not add that episode here.

## Genealogy and unstable royal identity
Mattaniah is the captive king's paternal uncle; Zedekiah is his bestowed royal name. Jehoiakim and Jehoiachin remain distinct. The two Seraiahs are chief priest and son of Tanhumeth, respectively. Ishmael son of Nethaniah is distinct from Abraham's son; Elishama here is keyed to Nethaniah's genealogy. Gedaliah's parent and grandparent remain explicit. Evilmerodach is an opaque source name, not a claim that its bearer is evil. Mizpah is keyed to the local episode without asserting an unprinted equation with every earlier similar name.

## Material inventory and unresolved measurement
The brass sea is the inherited temple vessel. Gold in gold and silver in silver retain the material repetition. Without weight is without a weighing, not zero mass. The pillar is eighteen cubits and its chapiter three cubits in this source passage; do not normalize it to a five-cubit description elsewhere. Wreath-work, pomegranates, snuffers, spoons and firepans remain their source categories without fabricated engineering or modern liturgical specifications.

## Contextual right-sense repairs in new copies
Twenty earlier uses were reviewed: sixteen need a contextual lexical repair; four correctly retain entitlement/claim. Genesis18:25 and moral evaluations in2Kings use conduct-right. Numbers18:18,20:17,22:26 and2Kings11:11,12:9 use direction-right. Numbers27:7 uses conduct-right adverbially for speaking correctly.2Kings10:15 uses sincere for reciprocal heart-alignment. Deuteronomy21:17, Ruth4:6 and2Samuel19:28/43 retain the inherited entitlement sense. These are error repairs in new versioned working copies, not modernization. All sealed ancestors and frozen Jonah remain unchanged. The existing modern consolidated language is not rewritten.
