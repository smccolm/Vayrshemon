# Historical KJV 0.10-kjv.36 · Tribal selves, personal voices, difficult songs

Judges 1–5 adds 145 authored verses, bringing the incomplete whole Bible to 6703/31102. The 59 additions comprise 39 source names or referent distinctions and 20 common senses or particles. Earlier targets, the modern register and frozen Jonah remain unchanged. No pronoun or case paradigm is rewritten. This deliberately awkward edition remains excluded from later modernization.

## Two tribal selves can make a dual we

Judah speaks to brother Simeon in Judges 1:3 as plural-I: the named tribe has internal plurality while speaking as one self. My-plural allotted portion belongs to that self. Its invitation uses dual-we for the two explicitly named tribal partners. This is a pair of corporate actors, not a claim that only two soldiers fight. Singular thou and thy address the other tribe as the source does. The brother image remains even though the actors are communities.

Israel's for us in 1:1 is singular-we asking who will act for the community. The unnamed number of spies in 1:24 is plural-we, not automatically dual because Joshua earlier had two spies. Ordinary kings, captains, Deborah and Jael keep personal I. These contrasts extend the requested plural-I/singular-we system while refusing a uniform pronoun replacement across all speakers.

## A messenger speaks the covenant-maker's I

The angel in 2:1–3 claims to have brought the people from Egypt and sworn the covenant. Plural-I follows that divine speaking authority, with my-plural covenant and voice. The narrator calls the speaker angel before and after the direct speech. Both framing and claim remain. The translation does not add an ontology explaining whether the messenger is identical with the deity, an emissary quoting without an explicit quote marker, or something else.

Deborah's speech in 4:6–7 similarly embeds the LORD's command and promise. The promise I will draw and deliver is plural-I. Her own I will surely go with Barak in 4:9 is personal I. Ehud's I have a message from God (3:20) remains personal I: a human asserting divine authorization does not thereby become the divine speaker. The source Spirit in 3:10 is retained separately; no unprinted Trinity count or clinical claim is introduced.

## Recollection that does not make the story tidy

The opening after Joshua's death is followed by a retrospective account of Joshua dismissing the people and dying. Past-perfect constructions mark supplied anterior actions without moving or removing the repeated material. Timnathheres retains its spelling beside Joshua's Timnathserah. Deborah the judge is distinguished from the earlier nurse Deborah; Heber the Kenite and Eglon the king are distinguished from ancestral or place referents with the same names.

Jerusalem's narrated capture and its continuing Jebusite population both remain, including Benjamin where the source names Benjamin. The LORD with Judah and Judah's inability to expel people with iron chariots remain side by side. The later claim that the LORD deliberately left nations and did not deliver them to Joshua is not harmonized with earlier absolute conquest language. Tribute is coerced payment, not expulsion or a voluntary alliance. Asherites dwelling among Canaanites retains the source's reversal of the host relationship.

## Changed action, punishment and responsibility

The deity sells the people, strengthens an opposing king and has a hand against them for evil. Those are explicit predicates with their source agents. Relenting in 2:18 uses the inherited change-of-adverse-intention sense rather than implying a confession of wrongdoing, but the change itself is not denied. The no-more-expulsion declaration is a negative decision following the covenant claim, not a translator's explanation of how the two must agree.

Prove in chapters 2–3 uses test, an actual trial, not establish a proposition as true. Teaching war in 3:2 is not softened into teaching only wisdom or peace. Devotional whoring uses the established source metaphor and does not invent a separate sexual episode. Baal/Ashtaroth as cultic referents and groves as cultic objects or sites have separate semantic identities from ordinary woodland or prior place names. The exact physical form of the groves is not supplied by the translation.

## Human intention and delayed knowledge

Would dwell and would not allow use the volition verb, distinct from future tense. Barak's symmetrical if-you-go/I-go and if-you-do-not/I-do-not conditions retain both halves. The prediction that his honour will pass to a woman does not prematurely insert Jael's name. Jael's repeated invitation and no-fear language remain beside the subsequent violence rather than being replaced with narrator commentary about deception.

Ehud's private message is his claim. The servants' covers his feet inference remains an inference expressed with the source euphemism, and their delayed discovery remains sequential. Gift carriers are not invented as accomplices. The text's terse physical injury descriptions are retained without extra technique or gore. Head-temple, haft, hammer and goad name the supplied objects and body region; temple never silently becomes a sacred building. Filth preserves the dirt-came-out clause without adding a medical mechanism.

## The personal singer and the paired singers

The song opens with Deborah and Barak, but I, even I and I Deborah explicitly identify a personal voice. Those lines remain singular I, including my heart and my soul. Mother in Israel is preserved as the poem's political and social self-description; the grammar does not make it an assertion of biological maternity or automatically a plural-self pronoun. The soul apostrophe retains singular thou addressed to the singer's own soul.

Singular thou addressed to the LORD coexists with plural divine self-reference elsewhere. The final prayer changes from thy enemies to them that love him; that shift remains. An archaic direct-address particle `a` marks source O where it appears, with no inherent number or divinity. Imperative and optative exclamation continues to use the established verbal ending rather than an unsupported exclamation glyph. The new particle changes vocabulary, not the pronoun or inflectional paradigm.

## Song repeats, contradicts and imagines

All four awaken calls, the repeated help of the LORD, the pransings and the bow/fall/lie sequence remain. Source repetition is not the same as accidental translator repetition and is not removed by a natural-variation heuristic. Stars fight in their courses, mountains melt and the river sweeps away: no explanatory weather report or astronomical theory replaces their poetic agency. Horsehoofs are broken in the supplied wording, not silently changed to thundering.

The poem's butter and lordly dish remain beside the prose milk bottle. Its smote off his head wording remains beside the prose temple injury. Sisera's mother and her ladies imagine success, coloured cloth and a girl or two for each man. The translation retains that disturbing anticipation as their imagined spoil, neither narrator confirmation nor consensual romance. The questioning and repeated colour/needlework expressions keep its countervoice and tragic ignorance visible.

## Specific senses without unnecessary replacement

Highway and byway distinguish principal and side routes without introducing motor roads. Coastal breaches are openings along a coast, not a confidently reconstructed modern harbour. Rehearse is tell/recount, while the inherited performance-rehearsal word is avoided. Governors reuse ruler. Writer's pen remains a pen rather than a silently substituted staff. Lusty means strong/vigorous here, not sexual appetite. Meat in the under-table recollection is food, whereas the source fat adjective and fat tissue noun remain distinct.

Message as sent word differs from an errand as assigned work. Parlour reuses the chamber form with a scoped source sense; summer and privacy are separately stated. Gird is clothing fastening, not a newly coined verb. Pitched his tent in4:11 uses set; the inherited pitch noun means sticky resin and is explicitly excluded from that clause. Proper names are explicitly authored or inherit a declared source-name form with a separate referent key. No untranslated English fallback is used as target vocabulary.

## Verification and continuation

Each of the 145 verses has source-exact English, authored analysis, compiled Vayrshemon and gloss, a back-reading and an editorial note. Independent source-quantity expectations preserve seventy kings; eight, eighteen, forty and eighty years; nine hundred chariots; about ten thousand versus exact ten thousand; six hundred; and the poem's forty thousand question. A cubit remains a source unit without conversion, and the girl-or-two phrase remains distributive imagined allocation rather than a total captive count.

Pronoun referents, quoted authority, agency, negatives and disputed lexical senses have explicit checks. Word-width validation found no overwide word. Native checks must preserve the approved geometry, 50/22 pt text, 38 pt leading, Maxima, paragraph alignment and Unta folios. These checks and translator self-review do not constitute independent literary certification. Continue Judges 6:1; 24399 Bible verses remain untranslated.
