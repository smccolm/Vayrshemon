# Ezekiel43–45: returning glory,graded service and the prince's responsibility

## 1. The voice from the house and plural-self
43:3 preserves the personal narrator's startling when I came to destroy the city. The translation does not replace I with he to make the agency easier.43:6 distinguishes the heard speaker out of the house from the man standing beside the witness. From43:7,the throne,feet,name and dwelling belong to the divine speaker's plural-I/my-plural register. This morphology does not multiply thrones or bodies.44:2 retains the LORD's third-person self-reference inside his speech rather than forcing every divine utterance into first person. The narrator remains personal-I. No communal-we or actual-dual forms are needed in these83verses. The source spirit transports the witness without an added personal name or doctrine of counted divine persons.

## 2. Proximity,royal corpses and conditional disclosure
43:7–9 keeps the kings' carcasses in high places and the threshold/post proximity between royal or communal space and divine space. Singular their threshold/post contrasts with plural my thresholds/posts. The wall between me and them remains. The hanging opening place-phrases and forever dwelling are preserved.43:10 shows the house to the house of Israel,retaining the wordplay and shame-purpose.43:11 begins IF ashamed and repeats all forms twice;its exits,entries,ordinances and laws remain a deliberately cumbersome inventory. The two this-is-the-law declarations in43:12 are not edited down. These are preservation choices,not endorsement of the speech's accusations.

## 3. Altar units and shifts of actor
The long cubit remains cubit-plus-handbreadth. Settle uses the existing architectural ledge term,not the ordinary settle verb. Exact heights,widths,half-cubit border and eastward stairs remain. The four horns and four corners have separate counts.43:21 changes from thou taking the animal to HE burning it;the agent is not silently regularized.43:26 explicitly consecrates THEMSELVES rather than dedicating the altar,and the self-reference remains human plural. The second day,seven-day duration and eighth-day continuation are distinct. Salt and specified animal qualifications remain source ritual descriptions without added procedures.

## 4. Shut gate,prince and differentiated Levite service
44:2's no-human-entry rule remains beside44:3's prince using the gate's porch and eating bread before the LORD. The tension is not solved with an invented architectural mechanism or a later messianic proper name. The apostate Levites bear liability yet retain gate,house and slaughter duties;the Zadok-line Levite priests receive the nearer sanctuary and fat/blood service. Endure renders bear iniquity and shame,avoiding the childbirth false friend. Strangers' heart and flesh uncircumcision are both retained as attributed source restrictions. The you/they shifts in44:7 remain. The translation does not endorse the exclusions or add a modern ethnic mapping.

## 5. Garments,contagious holiness and kin exceptions
Linen and no wool remain material rules. Bonnets,breeches and sweat-causing girding use inherited garment vocabulary.44:19 repeats outer court and explicitly says NOT sanctify the people with their garments;this is not rewritten as avoid defiling them. Cropping hair stands between shaving and allowing long locks;poll means cut hair. The widow prohibition keeps its priest-widow exception.44:25 lists father,mother,son,daughter,brother and a sister without a husband as exceptions to corpse contact;the allowed defilement remains defilement. The seven days in44:26 follow cleansing. Holy/common and ritual-clean/unclean remain two distinctions,without collapsing both into moral good/evil.

## 6. Divine inheritance and political limits
44:28 preserves plural-I is their inheritance and plural-I is their possession as two statements,with no land possession given to them.44:30 shifts from your dough to thine house and retains the priest's causative blessing clause.45:8 forbids my-princes oppressing my-people;45:9 demands removal of violence,spoil and exactions. Exactions uses the extortion-event form rather than a blanket synonym for every contribution.45:17's prince's part is duty,not a numeric fraction.45:22 explicitly requires a sin offering for HIMSELF as well as all the people. That difficult implication is not erased to accommodate later theology.

## 7. Land measures,capacity ratios and the maneh
45:1 explicitly calls the25,000length reeds;later ellipses remain ellipses. Twenty-five thousand is24×1000+1000 using established arithmetic.45:2 has500by500 without an explicitly repeated unit,and50cubits surrounding it.45:5 says twenty chambers,not towns. Honest balances/ephah/bath avoid the merely sense of just. The ephah and bath are each a tenth of a homer.45:12 retains20gerahs per shekel,and the separate20,25,15shekel clauses for maneh rather than replacing them with a single total or another standard. The two sixth-share levies in45:13 are sixths of an E P H A H from a homer,not sixths of the homer.45:14's cor is a homer of ten baths,and the ten-bath definition repeats. Maneh and cor receive explicit source-unit entries;neither gets an invented metric value.

## 8. Calendar,sense record and frozen scope
The first-month first-day sanctuary rite,seventh-day erring-person rite,first-month fourteenth-day Passover and seventh-month fifteenth-day feast remain in this text's own order.45:20's simple gets a recorded unwitting/lacking-understanding sense sharing the old simple form.45:23 retains all four printed sevens;45:24 keeps the one-ephah animal measures and one-hin-per-ephah oil ratio.
