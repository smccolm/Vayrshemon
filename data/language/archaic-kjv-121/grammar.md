## 1. Wisdom's personal voice is not automatically divine

Wisdom is introduced as feminine public personification1:20–21and speaks personal-I in1:23–33. Her my spirit, my words and outstretched hand belong to that voice. She names the LORD in third person1:29. The translation therefore does not impose divine plural-I, a fixed number of divine persons or an identification with a later theological figure. The existing plural-I remains available when the source supplies that discourse role; religious vocabulary alone does not trigger it.

The instructional parent uses personal possession and personal-I. Chapter4remembers the parent's own father teaching him; the quoted my in4:4belongs to that earlier father. Chapter5:12–14stages the future learner's regret in personal-I. Chapter7first presents the teacher as eyewitness, then the woman's personal-I14–17, then returns to the teacher24. These nested speakers are recorded at their verses. None is merged into a single divine narrator.

## 2. The robbers' shared purse and the private dual

The gang's invitation1:11–14uses plural-we for multiple participants, bodies, victims and houses. When the proposal becomes one common purse1:14,the shared owner is singular-we. This extends the communal identity distinction to a culpable group: singular-we does not mean morally approved, holy or benevolent. One purse is an explicit count, not merely a metaphor for unspecified cooperation.

The woman addressing the young man7:18uses the established dual: we-two take our fill and comfort ourselves with loves. The immediate encounter supplies the pair. Neither a third participant nor a communal singular-we is introduced. The inherited plural possessive/reflexive forms are interpreted by their explicit dual antecedent; no new possessive paradigm is invented. Physical plural-you remains in the public addresses to children and Wisdom's audience. No plural-I or our-one is required in these202verses.

## 3. Learner types and historical vocabulary

Four common entries are added. kjv_naive,experience-lack,labels the simple or unseasoned hearer,distinct from physical simplicity,innocence and the resistant fool. kjv_discretion,before-think,names practical forethought rather than confidentiality or a court verdict. kjv_ant,small-gather,is an authored creature label for the source's provisioning example,without a modern species claim. kjv_fold_limb,bend-together,distinguishes folding hands from the existing proportional multiplier fold and architectural fold-panel.

Subtilty1:4uses practical prudence; subtil of heart7:10uses strategic cunning. The source contexts differ and do not demand a single morally loaded synonym. Dark sayings1:6are riddles,not absence of illumination. Sound wisdom2:7and3:21is dependable insight,not auditory sound. Just is righteous; froward is contrary/perverse,not forward. Naughty6:12means wicked,meat6:8means food,and want6:11means deprivation. No inflectional endings,compiler rules or pronoun aliases change.

## 4. Source tensions in teaching and desire

Proverbs praises understanding while3:5warns against reliance on one's own understanding. Both are retained. Wisdom's refusal and mockery1:26–30are not softened into a neutral mechanism. The source's paternal chastening analogy3:11–12remains punitive teaching and rebuke rather than merely pleasant encouragement. Maternal law remains alongside paternal instruction1:8and6:20.

Chapter5:16positively disperses fountains abroad;17insists they be exclusively the addressee's. The printed positive form is preserved rather than silently rewritten as a prohibition. Verse6keeps thou as the subject of pondering and the awkward lest construction; an alternate she-does-not-ponder reading is not substituted. The navel/marrow3:8,heart-tablet3:3and7:3,and speech through feet/fingers6:13retain their embodied strangeness. No smoothing into a modern self-help register.

## 5. Distinguishing desire, pressure and death images

Ravished with love5:19–20means absorbed or delighted here,not sexual assault. The breast,hind,roe and bosom images remain rather than being euphemized. The later forced by flattering lips7:21retains narrated pressure without adding a physical assault mechanism. Harlot attire,the woman's peace-offering claim and the narrator's judgments remain attributed: reported ritual piety is not automatically validated by the narrator.

Goodman7:19is husband,not a statement that the absent man is morally good. The scheduled return remains unspecified rather than being turned into a lunar date. Aloes7:17uses the existing aromatic-wood label,not the modern succulent. Carved works7:16retains figured/cut work in the textile setting without asserting a specific craft reconstruction. Hell5:5and7:27uses the historical burial-inside/deathward-region sense,not an imported fiery-punishment doctrine. The supplied fool/stocks simile7:22remains rather than adopting an alternate animal reading.

## 6. Direction, bodily sense and numbers

Right paths4:11are morally upright; right on4:25is straight ahead; right hand3:16/4:27is the bodily side. Straitened4:12means narrowed,not straightened. Fast hold4:13is a strong grip,not speed or fasting. Issues4:23means life's outflow,not problems. Lie in wait and lie down remain postural senses; lying tongue6:17and lies6:19are false speech. These are explicit contextual checks rather than global source-word replacement.

The quantities are one purse1:14,firstfruits rank3:9,two sword edges5:4,six then seven6:16,and sevenfold6:31. The six/seven list is an ascending rhetorical enumeration,not13; sevenfold is a multiplier,not seven separate stolen objects. Firstfruits is an initial portion,not a specified percentage. Generic one,only beloved4:3,many years,many wounded and repeated little do not introduce exact totals. The thief may be undeserving of contempt6:30yet still owe sevenfold/all-house restitution31; that tension remains.
