# Ezekiel17–20: inherited guilt and refused consultation

## 1. Personal narration,plural divine self,communal aspiration
Ezekiel's narrative openings and20:49 protest use personal-I. Divine first person uses plural-I and my-plural;20:5 and20:9 retain the explicit plural-self reflexive. Singular-we appears only in20:32,the community's quoted wish to share the outsiders' identity. This is contextual person selection,not a mechanical substitution for every English I or we. No dual pronoun or counted divine persons is added.

## 2. Riddle and interpretation retain their separate voices
Chapter17 first presents eagles,cedar,vine and water,then names kings and political relations after a new speech boundary. The translation does not name the birds prematurely. Another eagle and a tender twig are not cardinals. Irrigation,growing sprigs,fruit-producing and vegetative flourishing use their actual plant senses. A new flourish-plant entry distinguishes living growth from a musical fanfare;unchanged inherited dictionary entries remain intact.

## 3. Covenant possessors and judicial contention
The human king's oath becomes my-plural oath/covenant in17:19. That attribution shift remains explicit. Plead in17:20 and20:35–36 is forensic litigation,not asking a favor. Bands in17:21 are groups;bond in20:37 is a covenant obligation. Neither is the physical restraint sense. Life oaths and my-plural bodily images preserve the awkward historical register without adding a new theology.

## 4. Generational responsibility without harmonization
Chapter18 distinguishes righteous father,violent son and considering grandson. Father's guilt and son's guilt are symmetrically excluded in18:20. Past wrongdoing not mentioned in18:22 is paired with past righteousness not mentioned in18:24. The source's fairness challenge retains equal/unequal and negative questions. My way in18:25 becomes my ways in18:29. The imperative to make a new heart in18:31 remains beside the earlier divine gift of a heart;neither text rewrites the other.

## 5. Lament and unresolved imagery
Chapter19 remains a lament. Female lion,whelps,chains,knew-desolate-palaces and in-thy-blood follow the supplied KJV rather than alternate readings. One whelp is counted;another is relational. Authority is held,not birthed. Fire comes from the vine's own rod;the final doubled lament receives no invented restoration. The unknown historical identities remain unknown in the translated text.

## 6. Repetition and resistant refusal
Chapter20 deliberately repeats name-motivation,hand gestures,statutes,sabbaths and rebellion across generations. These are meaningful source refrains,not defects to replace with arbitrary lexical variety. Would not listen in20:8 is refusal,not the counterfactual should-have-listened construction. Withdrawal of the hand in20:22 differs from its lifting in20:23. Fathers' statutes and my-plural statutes remain distinct possessors.

## 7. Difficult divine agency remains difficult
20:25 says plural-I gave statutes that were not good and judgments by which they would not live.20:26 actively attributes pollution in gifts to plural-I. These predicates are not reduced to permission or silently reconciled with20:11.20:41 retains plural-I will be sanctified as a passive predicate.20:39 preserves the bitter go-serve-idols imperative and its condition. The translation records the text's force rather than supplying a doctrinal repair.

## 8. Exact quantities,lexical discipline and limits
One whelp and the seventh-year,fifth-month,tenth-day date are the only explicit numerical values in these119verses. Every wing,all sides,firstfruits,another and man-to-man do not create extra quantities. Bamah remains a source name without a fabricated Vayrshemon pun. South,flaming flame,green/dry trees and the closing parable complaint remain. Every verse has source-critical anchors and a review note;this is translator self-review and structural validation,not independent literary certification. Earlier sealed editions,modern0.10 and frozen Jonah remain unchanged. The whole Bible is still incomplete.
