# Frozen KJV historical register 0.10-kjv.159 — Ezekiel13–16

These117 newly authored source verses continue the historical register through158,bringing Ezekiel to361verses. Earlier targets remain unchanged;the whole Bible is incomplete. The decisions below are authored translation choices and self-review,not independent literary certification or claims of ancient attestation.

## 1. Plural self that speaks,acts,deceives and answers itself

Narrative openings retain personal-I. The extended divine addresses retain plural-I and my-plural,including the explicit by-myself14:7 as plural-I-self. The active I-have-deceived-that-prophet14:9 is not softened into permission or transferred to an invented agent. The same plural self gives,judges,remembers and is passively pacified16:63. No divine-person cardinal is added. No source first-person communal we occurs in these chapters,so singular-we is not inserted merely to showcase the paradigm.

## 2. Attributed rival revelation and unstable pronouns

Prophets speak from own hearts and follow own spirit13:2–3. Their LORD-says claim remains adjacent to LORD-did-not-send13:6 and plural-I-did-not-speak13:7. They-would-confirm13:6 retains its pronoun without assigning a new agent. Three exclusions13:9 concern assembly,written record and land entry. The source's women-prophets accusation13:17–23 retains arm-hollow pillows,headcloths,all statures and hunting souls to make fly without inventing a magical mechanism. The opposed should-not-die/should-not-live13:19 remains a quoted moral claim,not a translator's verdict.

## 3. Three exemplars,four judgments,one fire,half the sins

Explicit three in14:14/16/18,four judgments14:21,one fire15:7 and half16:51 are retained exactly. Verse14:20 names Noah,Daniel and Job without adding an unprinted three. Daniel receives a contextual identity entry;the source spelling is retained without replacing it with Danel or merging an earlier royal/priestly namesake. Each/every-one is distributive. Both sons-and-daughters and both ends name paired categories/parts,not dual-person pronouns. The no-offspring-delivery clauses do not become rescue of a family by association. Verse14:22's survivors and promised comfort remain beside the preceding devastation.

## 4. Construction,plants and awkward source images

Untempered morter is preserved as the supplied inadequately prepared wall coating,not silently changed to whitewash or a modern recipe. Daubing is spreading a coating;the hailstones are directly addressed13:11. Foundation-discovered13:14 means exposed. The vine-wood comparison15:2–6 concerns material usefulness and burning,without inserting a claim that grapes have no value. Meet is fitting. Deserts are dry lands;tear is physical rend;divine is the divination verb when it governs divinations. Former estate16:55 is previous condition and therefore legitimately uses the temporal former.

## 5. Jerusalem's life history and repeated forms

Birth and nativity16:3 receive distinct labels;the figurative Amorite/Hittite ancestry remains. The four omitted infant-care actions16:4,the blood-setting and repeated live16:6 remain. Bodily soiling uses defile rather than the narrower sacred-profane verb. Development,garment-edge covering,oath and covenant16:7–8 retain their sequence;mine is analytic of-plural-I. Material inventories preserve embroidery,badger skins,fine linen,silk,bracelets on hands,the forehead jewel and the stated foods. Forehead is not changed to another reading's nose;wax means become,fashioned means formed,fair means beautiful,meat means food.

## 6. Hostile sexual and violent allegory without expansion

The source's accusations remain attributed to the speaker and addressed to the personified city. Feet-opened16:25,great-of-flesh16:26 and his-it-was16:15 remain unexpanded;no additional sexual or anatomical detail is supplied. The child-sacrifice and public-exposure threats remain in concise source terms. The crucial reversal of payment16:31–34 stays visible:the city scorns wages,gives gifts and hires lovers while receiving no reward. Imperious means domineering,not an imperial office. Prominent constructed sites are distinguished from social eminence. No clinical diagnosis or modern ethnic claim is invented.

## 7. Comparative sisters and unresolved covenant ending

The singular-thy/plural-your shift16:45 remains. Samaria at the left and Sodom at the right16:46 retain spatial orientation;elder/younger are sibling comparisons. Sodom's stated pride,full bread,idleness and neglect of poor/needy16:49 remain explicit,without an imported substitute offense. Half-the-sins16:51 and more-righteous16:52 are comparative;justification does not assert absolute innocence. Restoration includes Sodom and Samaria16:53–55. Syria16:57 remains Syria. The nevertheless-covenant turn,sisters becoming daughters,but-not-by-thy-covenant16:61,and passive pacification with a silenced mouth16:63 remain without a supplied harmonizing explanation. The difficult negatives16:16/43/47 are retained as printed.
