# KJV historical continuation 0.10-kjv.2 — WORKING, incomplete Bible

The requested book is the whole Bible, not an individually delivered biblical section. All 66 sections belong in one final `.vay`. The present authored continuation covers Genesis 1–11. Jonah remains the separately frozen 0.10-kjv.1 text and is included unchanged in the combined working document. This is not the complete KJV. Do not freeze the working Bible, claim its missing sections are translated, or promote this branch to modern `current.json`.

The inherited frozen grammar and compiler remain the starting point. There are no new inflectional suffixes, font mappings, Maxima rules or painting changes. These are authored uses of an imagined historical language, not claims of ancient attestation. Each new verse carries an authored analysis, source string, target string, gloss, back-reading and note. Back-readings are explanatory self-review, not independent proof of equivalence.

## 1. Strained conceptual accommodation

Continue above-owner for God, name-lord for LORD, life-self for soul, word-good-give for blessings conferred, and apart-reserve for hallowing. A human blessing God uses the inherited praise/bless verb; the deity is not being given fertility or made more sacred by a human. The garden account adds fixed-stretch for firmament and above-region for the cosmographic heaven. These do not silently change the passage into modern cosmology.

Genesis 6 needs **back-heart-pain**, retrospective regret over a completed making. Jonah's **intention-back-turn** concerns withdrawal of a proposed calamity. They are related in English repent but are not interchangeable target entries. Neither expression adds a confession of moral sin to the deity. Keep the source's anthropomorphic heart, eyes, smelling and remembering instead of explaining them away in the translation.

## 2. Appositional names and coordinated possessors

LORD God as a clause subject is name-lord followed by above-owner with one nominative anchor. Inside a possessed phrase, `kjv_lord+kjv_god/voice` is a compound title possessing the voice, not `kjv_lord/kjv_god/voice`, which would mean the LORD's god's voice. Coordinated titles and paired conceptual heads can be compounded before one possession chain: `good+evil/knowledge/kjv_tree`, the knowledge-tree of good-and-evil; `kjv_heaven+earth/kjv_generations`, heaven-and-earth's origin-account. The construction is intentionally weighty and restricted to transparent coordinated heads. It must not replace all ordinary conjunctions with compounds.

Possessors bind to their head. `wepl/image` is the image of plural we; the collective-singular `we` must not silently erase the quoted plural us in Genesis 1:26, 3:22 or 11:7. The plural speech does not commit the translation to one theological explanation. `his/self+image` reinforces own-image, rather than making ownership itself the image. Numeric or descriptive modifiers must not accidentally become possessed predicates.

The inherited free relation `of` is also available: fruit-from of garden-trees, or tree of good-and-evil's knowledge. Use this analytical alternative when several possessors would make an unwieldy word. The native 50 pt line-width check exposed that problem in Genesis 3:2 and 3:8. Meaning and participant attachment stay the same; the font is not shrunk and the long word is not cut at an arbitrary glyph.

## 3. Reference, relatives and unfinished sentences

Long relative objects may be announced, qualified and resumed with thatone before their finite verb. This is deliberate translationese, not permission to lose the subject. Genesis 2:19 names Adam as the naming agent; 2:21 keeps Adam's ribs distinct from the deity's; 3:8 gives the walker and hearers different subjects with the inherited different-subject converb.

A verse boundary is a reference boundary, not necessarily a sentence boundary. Retain the unfinished lest clause of 3:22, the coordinated list of 10:15–18, and the opening introduction of 6:1–2. Pagination may split at reviewed clause boundaries without adding new target sentences or capitalization. It must not put finite-tense plus ablative on a verb just to mean from those who; put case on the pronominal head, then its relative clause.

## 4. Nominal uses and lexical sense boundaries

The inherited language permits roots in nominal positions: offering from offer, punishment from punish, the water's gathering, ground-working and covered/bare states. These uses are documented; they are not an automatic English suffix stripper. Flesh-know explicitly carries sexual know, while ordinary know continues to mean knowledge. The source's understatement becomes awkward, but the event does not vanish.

Sense-specific entries prevent familiar-word errors: food for meat in Genesis 1; animal-inclusive food in Genesis 9; harp/string-music versus organ/breath-music; story versus ark storey; pitch/bitumen versus throwing; brass as the source's ancient copper-alloy category; bow/arched-string versus bowing one's body. The flood chest-ship is not automatically the later covenant chest. Gopher, bdellium and the source stone names are retained without pretending their ancient identifications are certain.

## 5. Source quantities and old counting

Retain older tren for three and tatus't for second, with inherited ordinal -'t available on count phrases. Other source-number names keep their inherited values. A compound `nine+hundred` is nine times the decimal unit hundred, not the Unta digit 900. Conjunction adds, so `nine+hundred and six+ten and nine` is 969. Source counting units are not reinterpreted in base twelve. Page folios continue to use Unta normally.

The Genesis 5 before/after/total ages are checked arithmetically. Preserve the exceptional Enoch clause: he was not because God took him, without adding a death. Genesis 11 contains before/after ages without stated totals; the translation must not insert calculated totals into the source. Preserve 120 years in 6:3 without replacing days with an interpreted lifespan or countdown.

Repeated counts are distributive: seven-seven / two-two in 7:2–3 follows by sevens and by two. The source's accompanying male/female phrase remains. Do not silently choose fourteen, erase the two-of-each instruction in chapter 6, or confuse forty days of rain with 150 days of prevailing water. The ark's 300 × 50 × 30 cubits stay source measures, without one forced modern unit conversion.

## 6. Genealogies, people names and place names

Each adapted name has an explicit dictionary entry and source occasion. There is no unknown-word transliteration fallback. Personal Lot is a separate lexical key from Jonah's chance-token lot. Cain, Cainan and Canaan; Seba and Sheba; Methusael and Methuselah; Jabal and Jubal; Mesha and Meshech remain separate. A name reused in different genealogies does not assert that the persons are identical. The person Haran and the settlement Haran have separate semantic identities with the same spelling.

Nations-table eponyms follow the supplied source. They are not automatically equated with modern national, racial or religious categories. Source lists preserve their counts and descent direction; begetting an object does not reverse into being begotten by it. The birth of several sons in a summary formula does not itself assert simultaneous triplets.

Adapted names do not automatically reproduce Hebrew wordplay. Woman/man, Eve/living, Noah/comfort, Peleg/dividing and Babel/confounding are retained as the source's explanations, with phonetic losses noted. Do not claim a fabricated native etymology merely because it sounds attractive.

## 7. Deliberate unresolved readings

Keep the disputed masculine pronouns of 4:7, the compressed violence poem of 4:23, the source's sons of God and giants, Enoch's taking, the ark window's one-cubit instruction and the ambiguous he in 9:27. The target must not quietly solve them by replacing pronouns with a preferred name. Use notes to expose the uncertainty.

The KJV's uncomfortable claims remain its claims: curse on the ground, relational subjection, all-flesh destruction and blood-for-blood. In 9:25 the curse names Canaan. It must not become a curse on every descendant of Ham or a modern racial group. Genesis 9:22 does not explicitly describe a sexual act, and none is inserted. Awkwardness is conceptual and syntactic; factual drift, omissions and accidental reversals are defects.
