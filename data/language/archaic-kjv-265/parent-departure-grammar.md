# Historical KJV 0.10-kjv.264 — James

This continuation adds all 108 verses of James. After sequential publication and verification, projected coverage is 30,375/31,102; 727 remain. The deliverable remains one complete Bible in one .vay with all 66 major sections. Draft analyses and source indexing are not verified translation. Structural checks and translator self-review are not independent literary certification. No earlier targets are revised; this historical edition remains permanently excluded from modernization.

## Shared we, personal argument, and divided allegiance

The community begotten as firstfruits in 1:18 uses singular-we. The teachers facing greater judgment in 3:1 and the erring speakers in 3:2–3,9 retain that communal number while their mouths, bodies and acts remain plural. Our-singular Lord, father Abraham and bodily members in 2:1,21 and 3:6 express shared relation, not a literal shared anatomy. The trading party in 4:13,15 uses singular-we inside quoted plans; the conditional Lord-willing statement governs both living and doing. Singular-we also occurs in the difficult indwelling-spirit clause 4:5, the reckoning of the enduring happy in 5:11, and Elias's shared human susceptibility in 5:17.

The tempted speaker's I in 1:13 and the argumentative speaker's I in 2:18 remain personal-I. No divine first-person quotation or physical dual is asserted in this section. James's double-minded at 1:8 and 4:8 receives a divided-commitment compound, explicitly separate from plural-I and from any mental-health diagnosis. Ordinary my-brothers and the usher's my-footstool are not reassigned divine number. One God in 2:19 and one lawgiver in 4:12 remain genuine numerical assertions; the register never deletes source numbers to force an identity theory.

## Testing, work and the law

Trials in 1:2,12 use the established trial sense; enticement to evil in 1:13–14 uses tempt. This distinction does not rewrite earlier sealed narratives or claim that every historical occurrence has one sense. Wanting is lacking, glass is a mirror, naughtiness is wrongdoing, and conversation is conduct. The perfect-law image keeps liberty, continuing and doing the work. One point in 2:10 is one precept of the law, not the inherited gesture verb point.

Partiality is social favour given to the splendidly dressed visitor, not the modern sense of incomplete. Both questions in 2:4 remain. Convinced in 2:9 is conviction as a transgressor. Mercy rejoices against judgment in 2:13; the wording is not silently replaced with a preferred paraphrase. The supplied without-thy-works in 2:18 and dead in 2:20 remain. Justification by works and not by faith only in 2:24 is retained without harmonizing it away against Romans. Abraham and Rahab remain the supplied exemplars, with sacrifice, occupation and alternative route intact.

## Tongue, nature and contradictory speech

Actual horse-bits and a ship's rudder ground the tongue analogy. Matter in 3:5 means combustible material extent; no particular forest is inserted. Its exclamation is carried by exclamatory morphology, never the unsupported Thimfora exclamation glyph. The broad course-of-nature fire image in 3:6 receives nature-course, rather than a newly imposed doctrine of reincarnation or a specific anatomical circuit. Hell uses the punitive source term here, not Jonah's deathward burial-inside term. Unruly in 3:8 and God in 3:9 preserve the supplied readings.

Sensual in 3:15 means natural or creaturely impulse, not specifically sexual action. Confusion in 3:16 is disorder, not a clinical condition. The full no-fountain-both salt/fresh wording of 3:12 remains; fresh water is nonsalt water, not water never previously used. The first/then wisdom sequence and both without clauses of 3:17 remain distinct. Speech, action and bodily images retain their tensions rather than being smoothed into a systematic doctrine.

## Desires, property and the frail future

Kill in 4:2 remains kill. Both adulterers and adulteresses in 4:4 remain present. The spirit in 4:5 remains the subject lusting to envy as printed; no substitution of God jealously longing, no invented quotation source. This linguistic decision is recorded without claiming to resolve the source's interpretive problem. God resisting pride and the readers resisting the devil use opposition, not the engineering sense of resistance alone. The year of the trading plan carries no invented explicit one numeral. Its vapour comparison and conditional life remain.

Metal canker in 5:3 gains a corrosion sense sharing the rust form, distinct from plant-devouring creatures and bodily decay. The gold-corrosion rhetoric is not corrected by modern chemistry. Wages withheld by fraud cry; Sabaoth remains the title and is not confused with Sabbath. As in a day of slaughter in 5:5 retains as. The righteous he in 5:6 is not assigned an unprinted personal name. The farmer waits for both rains; no calendar dates are added.

## Endurance, care and restoration

The end of the Lord in 5:11 is retained without asserting God's death. Condemned in 5:9 and faults in 5:16 preserve the supplied readings. Prayer, elders, oil, raising and forgiveness remain source practices and claims. The if-he-has-sinned clause of 5:15 does not become an assertion that sickness proves sin. Confession and prayer in 5:16 are reciprocal. Elias's three years and six months remain exact; no chronology is altered to fit another passage. The closing a-soul and multitude-of-sins stay without an added owner.

Four common entries: divided-commitment double-minded, course-of-nature, sensual-natural and metal-canker. One contextual name: James, without invented parentage or a forced namesake merger.
