## Quoted selves and actual pairs

The centurion's personal-I reaches Jesus through two successive groups of intermediaries:elders and friends. The elders speak as singular-we and our-one nation. Their claim of worthiness contrasts with his own unworthiness;do not merge the judgments or put the centurion physically into the encounter.

John sends two disciples. They report us-two were sent,then repeat the wider community's question,do we-one await another (7:19–20). The answering you-two addresses their actual witnessed pair. The two debtors and the girl's two parents are actual duals as well. No cardinal is inferred from a crowd or from the name Legion.

Jesus's direct first-person speech remains plural-I. His plural-self possession distinguishes one head from plural feet without inventing extra anatomy. His shared travel invitation is singular-we (8:22),as is the endangered boat's plea (8:24). The questioned man's I in8:28 remains personal. Luke's narrator explains the name Legion because many devils entered;Mark's quoted plural-we is not inserted. In8:45 Peter and companions echo Jesus's who touched I-many:the quotation retains its original referent. No present-day diagnostic analogy is asserted by these person-number choices.

## Unnamed woman and named supporters

The woman at Simon the Pharisee's meal is not named. Retain her source sinner designation without inventing the particular sin or identifying her with Mary Magdalene. Mary called Magdalene is explicitly named in8:2,alongside Joanna,Chuza's wife,and Susanna. Their support from their own goods is stated. The seven devils are the source count,not an explanation of the preceding woman's history.

Simon the Pharisee is a contextual name identity distinct from Peter and the leprous host elsewhere. The given Mary component and the called-name Magdalene are expressed separately;the full compound name is not doubled. Joanna shares a name form with Luke's earlier ancestor without becoming that person.

## Body,mercy and unsettled judgments

The widow's only son,her compassion-directed address,the touched bier,restored speech and return to mother all remain. Royal courts use the royal-household sense,not sanctuary enclosures. The blind,lame,leprous,deaf,dead and poor report is complete. Wisdom retains her children rather than a harmonized works reading.

## Luke-specific parables and healings

The seed on rock lacks moisture;do not import the sun-scorching cause. The fruitful yield is hundredfold alone,not an added thirty or sixty. Preserve honest good heart,keeping and patience;worries,wealth and pleasures are three choking conditions. Heed how you hear retains the seeming possession distinction. The redefined kin both hear and do.

Gadarenes remains the supplied place name. Many pigs are unnumbered;no two-thousand count is imported. The narrator's explanation of many spirits,deep,restraints and right mind remains without a modern diagnostic category. The commission says what God did;the resulting report says what Jesus did. Keep those lexical distinctions without adding an identity equation.

Jairus's only daughter is about twelve and dying before the interruption. The woman has twelve years of bleeding,spent all livelihood on physicians,and touches the garment border. Healing virtue is efficacy,not moral merit. Retain good comfort,immediacy,public disclosure and the subsequent death report. The girl's returned spirit,food and parents' silence instruction remain;do not insert Mark's Aramaic phrase. Sleeping and knowing she was dead stand together as supplied.
