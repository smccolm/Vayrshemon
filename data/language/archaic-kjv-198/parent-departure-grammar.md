## Many selves versus a shared singular we

The parable comparison4:30 and shared voyage4:35 use singular-we;the endangered crew4:38 and disciples' food-purchase question6:37 do likewise. The local community6:3 shares a singular-we while naming the carpenter's brothers and unnamed plural sisters. These are speech communities,not numerical singular headcounts.

Mark5:7 begins with personal-I in the reported appeal. At5:9 the speaker gives the collective name **Legion** and explicitly explains **we are many**. That declared collective self motivates **my-plural name**;the many then takes **plural-we**,not the communal singular-we default. All the devils' petition5:12 continues plural-we. This is a deliberately contextual extension of usage,not a new pronoun paradigm. The he/them switch5:10 remains.
