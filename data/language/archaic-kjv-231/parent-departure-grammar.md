## The narrator's singular-we and the elders' singular-we

The travel narrative uses singular-we for a working community moving,waiting,sailing,praying and being welcomed. Acts20:5–6 and13–15 establish that community beside Paul's separate walking route. Acts21:1–18 continues it without naming its narrator by external inference. The companions preceding the narrator at Troas are they;the group sailing from Philippi is we. The same pronoun morphology does not entail identical membership across every scene.

The elders' we-say/we-have in21:23 and we-wrote in21:25 are a different singular whole. The sentence subjects are identified by the speech frame,not by inventing new pronouns for every cast. Our-way,our-course and our-baggage are communal possession in21:5,7,15. Paul's our-Lord in20:21 is confessional communal possession;Ananias' our-fathers in22:14 is ancestral communal possession. These are not arithmetic claims about Lord,fathers or travellers.

No physical dual is inferred for this batch's plural escorts,companions,wives,children,elders or soldiers. Two chains counts objects. Four daughters and four vowed men are separate groups. The captain's four thousand alleged murderers remains a question about identity,not the narrator's confirmed identification of Paul.

## Paul as personal speaker inside a divine plural-self narrative

Paul's farewell and defence use personal-I,including his grief,labour,knowledge,ignorance and responsibility for persecution. His tears are eye-drops and his hands service his own needs and companions. The first-person grammar does not turn an apostle into a divine plural-self.

The reported Jesus voice in22:7–8 changes person class within Paul's narrative: I-fell/I-heard is personal,while why-persecute-me and I-Jesus-of-Nazareth use plural-self. The same distinction recurs in22:18's testimony-concerning-me and22:21's I-send-thee-far. The addressee remains singular. A quoted divine plural-self does not make the narrator's autobiographical I plural,or imply that every speaker's voice has multiple owners.

The captain's tell-me and I-obtained-citizenship in22:27–28 are personal-I too. Paul's answering I-citizen-born is a separate personal speaker. The grammar of reported speech retains these boundaries without adding a diagnostic account of anyone's identity.

## The difficult divine relations remain difficult

Acts20:28 retains the supplied church-of-God,purchased-with-his-own-blood construction. Holy Spirit makes overseers;the church belongs to God;the purchase uses his own blood. The translation does not substitute church-of-the-Lord,remove own,or replace blood with an abstract payment. There is no printed divine first person here,so the translator does not insert plural-I simply because the content is theologically awkward.

Acts20:22's spirit-bound journey to Jerusalem remains beside21:4's through-the-Spirit instruction that Paul should not go there. The prohibition is not turned into an implied warning whose contradiction disappears. Agabus' enacted prediction in21:11 has him bind his own limbs with Paul's girdle,then report Spirit speech about Jews binding the owner and delivering him to Gentiles. His own is the actor's own body,not Paul's. That third-person prediction does not acquire invented first-person divine speech.

Acts22:9 keeps saw-the-light,were-afraid,and heard-not-the-voice. Earlier9:7 retains its heard-the-voice/no-man reading unchanged. The translation does not silently replace heard with understood to reconcile the supplied accounts. A frozen awkward edition must preserve such seams,not repair them into a polished synthesis.

## Allegation, supposition, testimony and coercion

The elders report in21:21 that Paul allegedly teaches Jews to abandon Moses,not circumcise children,and not follow customs. Both negative instructions remain within the reported allegation. The proposed purification and paid expenses in21:24 are meant to show those reports are nothing and that he keeps the law;the translator does not independently rule that theological dispute resolved.

The Asia accusers allege temple pollution in21:28. Verse29 distinguishes their prior sight of Trophimus in the city from their supposition that Paul brought him into the temple. An assertion about seeing him does not become an assertion about an unobserved temple entry. Trophimus remains the named Ephesian introduced among the Asia companions.

Paul's readiness in21:13 extends beyond being bound to dying. His22:20 confession keeps consent to Stephen's death and guarding the killers' clothes. The new martyr noun is witness-death,a slain witness;it does not declare every witness already dead. The captain's examination by scourging in22:24 stays explicitly violent. The uncondemned condition in22:25 and the fear caused by having already bound a Roman in22:29 remain legally significant.

## Civic freedom and historically concrete things

The new citizenship noun derives from citizen+state. It renders this-freedom in22:28 as the Roman civic status under discussion,rather than general freedom of movement or a claim that Paul was formerly enslaved. The captain obtained it with a great sum;the sum remains unspecified. Paul was a citizen by birth. Birth is distinguished from carrying by soldiers in21:35.

The new thong noun derives from leather+bind and names the binding straps of22:25. The troop band in21:31 retains the established group sense,while bands loosed in22:30 use the restraint noun. Carriages in21:15 is baggage,not a vehicle. Charges in21:24 is paid expense,not a command or a criminal count. Estate-of-elders in22:5 is their collective council,not land ownership. Count-my-life-dear in20:24 is esteem,not noble rank. The20:26 record construction uses witness,not a written-file verb.

The nineteen contextual proper entries preserve the source's route and companions. Gaius-of-Derbe is not automatically equated with the earlier Macedonian Gaius. Secundus stays a name rather than becoming second. Coos,Phenicia and Trogyllium keep the supplied forms and stop sequence. Mnason's old-disciple description supplies no numerical age. Philip the evangelist is the member of the Seven,not silently the apostle. Hebrew remains Hebrew in21:40/22:2;no Aramaic correction is imposed.
