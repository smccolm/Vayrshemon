# KJV historical register 0.10-kjv.276 — vials, the city-self and broken commerce

## Which I speaks
The witness-I remains personal in seeing and hearing. The guide angel's I-will-show and I-will-tell in17:1,7remain personal;being heavenly does not alone license plural-I. The divine thief-like interruption in16:15uses plural-I. My-people in18:4uses plural-self possession in the heavenly address without an inserted speaker name. Babylon's quoted I-sit-a-queen in18:7is civic plural-I:the city is one grammatical queen and widow-denier while its speaking self contains many inhabitants and relations. All three boast predicates remain. No first-person we is invented where the source supplies none.

## Temporal repetitions and numerical limits
Preserve each vial ordinal,three spirits and three city parts,approximate one-talent hail weight,one-of-seven and seven-vial references,seven heads/mountains/kings,five fallen,one present,eighth-of-seven,ten horns/kings,one hour,one mind,all three double expressions,one-day plagues and each one-hour lament. The beast was,is not,and yet is remains the supplied paradox. Do not resolve it into a biography or replace yet-is with an alternate future reading. The source weight remains approximate and unconverted;the various hours are retained in their clauses rather than added together.

## Body, luxury and trade senses
Sore is a painful lesion sharing wound,without inventing accidental trauma or a skin diagnosis. Scorch is heat-hurt;gnaw is tooth-eat. The city's bird-cage is bird-prison. Luxury is rich-delight for delicacies and living deliciously,not mere tasty food or physical fragility. Thyine timber is retained by an explicit historical source-label loan without a guessed species. Admiration in17:6is astonished wonder,not moral approval. Filthiness uses the foulness abstraction,not a literal discharge. Craft in18:22is artisan skill rather than deceit. Six common entries and one contextual Armageddon name are added;no inflectional paradigms change.

## Do not smooth the supplied readings
Keep Lord and shalt-be in16:5,another-out-of-altar in16:7,earth-and-whole-world kings in16:14,he-gathered in16:16,and temple-of-heaven in16:17. Keep plural blasphemy-names in17:3,where13:1had singular name. Keep horns-upon-beast in17:16,not horns-and-beast. In18:6retain rewarded-you. In18:17retain all-company-in-ships. In18:20retain holy-apostles-and-prophets. Name interpretations supplied by the angel are translated as source statements;no modern city,political group or institution is assigned to the images.

## Refrains and uncomfortable inventories
Fallen-fallen,Alas-alas,and the repeated no-more-at-all clauses stay repeated. Emotional emphasis uses the inherited exclamatory form,including the city's maritime lament question,without the unsupported exclamation glyph. Every material and commodity in18:12–13is retained in order. Slaves and souls-of-men remain distinct adjacent entries;the translation neither sanitizes the human trade nor endorses it. All victim groups in18:24remain,including all slain upon earth. The wrath and vengeance speech remains source speech,not instructions to current readers.
