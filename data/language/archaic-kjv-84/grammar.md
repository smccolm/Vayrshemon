## 1. A human plural-I can voice a contingent

Joab's `ipl` in19:12 speaks for the force he leads: if the Syrians overpower me-as-force, help me-as-force; I-as-force will help you if Ammon is stronger. This follows the established parallel construction in2Samuel10. It is not a divine title or diagnosis. In19:13, `wesg` joins the forces in one undertaking for `ourone/people` and the cities of `ourone/god`. Two named commanders do not force dual speech when the utterance includes their contingents. Actual dual remains available where a speech act specifically designates a pair; it is not a quota to insert.

## 2. Personal-I carries the census responsibility

David's kindness19:2 is personal reciprocity. His census order21:2, confession21:8, distress21:13, insistence on his own guilt21:17 and purchase21:22,24 use personal-I.
