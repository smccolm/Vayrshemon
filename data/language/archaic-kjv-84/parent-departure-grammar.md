# Historical continuation83: sung identity, the house promise and royal records

This working register continues the historical branch after82. It does not modernize any sealed edition. These87 individually authored verses cover1Chronicles16–18; the entire Bible remains incomplete. Interpretations are translator self-review, not independent certification or claims of ancient Vayrshemon attestation.

## 1. Plural-self speech can hold singular relational roles

The quotation in16:18 uses `ipl` for the giving deity. In16:22, `mypl` retains that speaker's possessors. The long quotation17:4–14 uses plural-self recipient, speaker and possession consistently. In17:13, `ipl his/father be:fu` deliberately makes the plural-self speaker occupy the singular father relation; `he mypl/son be:fu` retains the singular son. The philosophical awkwardness is intended. The grammar does not supply a number of divine persons or turn this royal promise into an explicit later Trinity passage. The named LORD inside first-person speech in17:10 remains a source shift rather than a second invented speaker.

## 2. Corporate prayer and plural witnesses are different constructions

The song's `ourone/god` in16:14 and `ourone/salvation` in16:35 mark shared belonging. The scattered people ask to be gathered with `wesg`: the desired body is one despite dispersion. In17:20, `wepl` and `ear:pl of wepl` retain the several hearing witnesses. David's personal domestic comparison17:1 and self-question17:16–17 use `i` and personal possessors. His third-person self-reference17:18 stays third person. Every quotation boundary resets speaker selection; no global I-to-plural or we-to-singular replacement is allowed.

## 3. Repetition and exclamation belong to the song

Repeated singing, giving, peace/blessing and enduring mercy are retained as liturgical parallelism. This is intentional repetition, unlike an accidental overused grammatical prefix. Wishes use optative morphology; imperatives remain commands. The emphatic thanks in16:34 uses `:imp:excl`, never an unsupported exclamation-mark glyph. Amen remains the inherited ritual assent. The new `kjv_exult` is joy-glory, a verbal act of glorying, while `glory` continues to mean the noun splendour/honour. Trees sing, seas roar and mouths issue judgments in the source's material imagery; the translation keeps the estrangement rather than smoothing it into abstract doctrine.

## 4. World firmness and figurative seed require their own senses

The inherited `stable` derives from horse-house and cannot translate world stability in16:30. The target uses passive establishment. `kjv_seedline` retains descendants and the plant metaphor in16:13 and17:11 without inserting an unprinted named heir. Forest replaces raw wood for the singing trees16:33. Suffered in16:21 becomes permitted; wonders use the deed/sign noun; expressed by name16:41 becomes called/designated by name. `kjv_deplete` handles wasting people17:9, avoiding rubbish. Estate and high degree17:17 use social standing/rank, not land ownership or angles.

## 5. House remains an uncomfortable hinge

The cedar house, curtained ark dwelling, house promised to David, and divine house/kingdom remain connected by the source house word. Material residence and continuing household are not forced into a single modern architectural explanation.17:5 retains tent-to-tent movement;17:6 retains judges, not tribes from another version. Nathan's first advice17:2 and the night's prohibition17:3–4 remain separate. David's return to personal speech17:16 does not inherit the divine plural-self voice. Requests for establishment are wishes, not assertions that the whole promise has already occurred.

## 6. Worship has assignments, materials and more than one location

The ark chest in David's tent and the tabernacle at Gibeon16:39 remain distinct. Burnt sacrifices and peace offerings are separate categories. Each man and woman receives the supplied bread, flesh and wine-flagon wording16:3; no alternative raisin reading or exact vessel capacity is invented. The repeated Jeiel16:5 is retained. Obededom's repeated mentions16:38 remain, with the second expressly identified as Jeduthun's son; the ledger does not force that local entry into an unproven equation with the earlier householder. Hosah the porter is separate from Hosah the place. Continual duties coexist with each day's required work and the morning/evening schedule, without claiming that each individual performed without interruption.

## 7. Chronicles' campaign particulars remain its own

Gath and towns18:1, Hadarezer's supplied name, Hamath and Euphrates18:3,1000chariots/7000horsemen/20000infantry and100reserved chariots18:4,22000Syrians18:5, and Abishai's18000Edomites18:12 are retained. Do not import measuring-line violence absent from18:2, change agents or harmonize other-account counts. Footmen use `kjv_foot_soldier`, not the domestic foot-servant entry. Hamstringing uses the existing concise injury verb. The reserve is expressed as the supplied chariot complement without inventing horses per vehicle. Royal justice and divine-preservation claims remain narrator attributions beside the conquest narration.

## 8. Diplomatic purpose and record offices are not interchangeable

The new `kjv_congratulate` is joy-greet: acknowledging another's success. Hadoram's mission18:10 separately asks welfare and congratulates victory; Tou's existing war provides the source explanation. Tou and Hadoram retain the supplied forms instead of Toi/Joram substitution. Tibhath and Chun remain the brass-supplying cities. The brass sea is the large basin, not an ocean. Jehoshaphat is a record-keeping official, Shavsha a scribe. Abimelech son of Abiathar18:16 remains as printed instead of a silent Ahimelech correction or reversed genealogy. David's sons remain chief about the king18:17, without importing a stronger office from another account.
