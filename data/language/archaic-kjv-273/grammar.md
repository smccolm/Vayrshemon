# KJV historical register 0.10-kjv.273 — sealed tribes and trumpets

## Personal witness and communal seal-work
The seer's personal I remains in seeing,hearing and the exchange with an elder. In7:3 the angelic seal-workers use singular-we and our-singular. In7:10,12 our-singular belongs to the collective praise. Servants,foreheads,faces and the many gathered groups retain their own plurals. There is no quoted divine first-person speech requiring plural-I in these chapters. Distinguish social shared identity from the explicit two coming woes in9:12:the latter has counted dual morphology on the woes,not a claim that the events are living persons.

## The supplied tribal list
The144000total is twelve-times-twelve-times-thousand;each of the twelve named tribes retains its12000. Preserve Juda,Reuben,Gad,Aser,Nephthalim,Manasses,Simeon,Levi,Issachar,Zabulon,Joseph,Benjamin in that order. Do not add Dan or substitute Ephraim to harmonize another list. Contextual Manasses names the tribe,not the earlier named king. The great unnumberable multitude remains a separate described group;the translation does not assert an unprinted equation or separation doctrine.

## Fractions, times and repeated judgments
The seventh seal has approximately half an hour's silence,not a precise clock measurement. Third-part scopes remain distinct from all-grass and unquantified fountains. All five explicit thirds in8:12 remain;night likewise supplies the source relation without an invented additional written fraction. Five months occurs twice and stays twice. Four angels in9:15 are prepared for an hour,day,month,year;do not sum these into a modern duration or predict a date. The horsemen's two-hundred-thousand-thousand is exactly200000000. Keep ordinal trumpets distinct from counted angels and fractional victims.

## Visionary materials and names
Bottomless pit is without-bottom-pit,an explicit abyss term with no measured depth or geological model. Jacinth is a supplied historical appearance/material name without a certain modern chemical identification. Wormwood as star designation shares the plant word's form while keeping its contextual naming role. The ordinary martial breastplate term is used rather than the priestly gemstone breastpiece. Palm-tree material differs from palms of hands;tears are eye-drops;hail is frozen weather,not a greeting. Abaddon and Apollyon are the two source language-specific names of the same angel. Three common entries and three contextual names are added;no paradigms change.

## Keep the source's awkwardness
White robes washed in blood remain white-in-blood. Keep angel in8:13 rather than substituting eagle from another reading. Triple woe uses the established exclamatory morphology three times,without an unsupported exclamation glyph. Retain all as/like comparisons in the locust description,and both tail-head and mouth-tail relations. In9:19 their-power remains as supplied. The refusals to repent and all listed objects and offences remain without editorial smoothing. Nothing in the translated visions assigns their violent images to modern people or offers present-day action instructions.
