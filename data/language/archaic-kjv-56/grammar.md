## 1. Divine plural-I and a personally inadequate king

The LORD's speech in3:5,12–14 uses `ipl` and `mypl`: the giver of wisdom, riches and honour speaks as a plural-self. The clauses retain a single quoted speaker centre, not a translator-supplied number of divine persons. The dream boundary in3:5 and15 remains explicit. The language does not transform a dream encounter into a narrated waking appearance.

Solomon's **I am but a little child / I know not how** in3:7 stays personal `i`. His request **that I may discern** in3:9 also remains personal: the human capacity needed for office, not a claim that an institution feels uncertainty. The descriptive little child is his self-diminishing statement, not an inferred birth date. Divine plural-I therefore does not mechanically force the recipient into plural-I.

The unasked riches/honour in3:13 and conditional lengthened life in3:14 remain different promises. The condition is not dropped to make a smoother prosperity formula. In6:12–13 the plural-self's dwelling among Israel remains conditioned by the preceding conduct requirements. The house does not by itself replace the if-clause.

## 2. Actual dual witnesses are not a singular collective

The two women in3:16 are two persons. In3:18 the testifying woman's **we were together / save we two** takes actual `we:du`, repeated because the absence of other adult witnesses is central to the allegation. It does not deny that the infants existed. Their contradictory exchanges in3:22 use `they:du` while each quoted my returns to the particular speaker's personal possession.

The initial allegations about overlying, death and a midnight exchange remain testimony, not omniscient medical or criminal findings. The narrator only identifies the living child's mother in3:26. That evidential change is explicit in the notes. The king's threat to divide the child is an imperative; no narrative clause says the child was divided. Two halves remain a proposed division, not two whole children or a performed act.

Hiram and Solomon in5:12 are likewise **they two**, `they:du two`, parties to a league. The presence of an alliance does not automatically convert two rulers into singular-we. This preserves the user-directed plural-I/singular-we system alongside ordinary dual reference.

## 3. A royal body can undertake work while a person considers a request

Solomon's political rest in5:4 and building purpose in5:5 use royal `ipl`. In the nested quotation in5:5 the LORD's `ipl` has a different referent from the outer royal `ipl`; the quotation boundary resets the centre. The text does not imply that Solomon is the divine speaker.

Hiram personally **considers** in5:8 with `i`, then commits his operating royal body through `ipl` to timber delivery. In5:9 `mypl/servant:pl`, conveyance, unloading and `mypl/kjv_household` belong to the organized household. These are concrete agents and obligations, not a vague majestic flourish.

Solomon's **among us** in5:6 takes `wesg`: the domestic polity as one body lacking the comparative Sidonian timber skill. The claim does not abolish individual workers or say the Sidonians are one person. The source's admitted craft dependence is retained rather than replaced with national superiority.

## 4. Bodily imagery and source discomfort are preserved

Solomon's marriage affinity with Pharaoh is `kjv_affinity_marriage`, wed-kin. The unnamed daughter is taken and moved as the source states; the translation supplies neither consent nor a romantic narrative. The two litigants retain the source's harlot label without extra moral condemnation, invented ages or diagnoses.

The mother's **bowels yearned** remains bodily inward longing through `kjv_bowels` and the explicit `kjv_yearn` sense. It is not modernized into a clinical account or flattened into a generic assertion of kindness. Suckling is expressed by causative suck, the child being caused to suck; it does not reverse agent and infant.

Largeness of heart in4:29 stays broadness of heart, an expansive-capacity image beside the sand comparison. It is not anatomical pathology or automatically generosity. Israel's fear of the king after judgment remains fear, not merely admiration. The comfortable vine/fig scene and universal praise remain the narrator's rhetoric, not independently verified population statistics.

## 5. The administrative list is not silently reconciled

Abiathar remains among the priests in4:4 despite his preceding removal. Geber's **only officer in the land** remains beside the twelve-officer system. No invented chronology or district boundary makes these statements tidier. Son-of-Hur, son-of-Dekar, son-of-Hesed, son-of-Abinadab and son-of-Geber remain patronymic-only designations; no forenames are manufactured.

Azariah son of Zadok and Azariah son of Nathan have separate local identity records. Nathan father of the officials is not forcibly identified with the prophet or David's son. Ahiah son of Shisha, Ahimaaz the officer, Baanah son of Hushai, Jehoshaphat son of Paruah, Shimei son of Elah and Uri father of Geber likewise retain local evidence. Geber as a father and Geber son of Uri are not collapsed solely because the forms match.

Baana remains beside Baanah, Adoniram beside Adoram, Basmath beside Bashemath, and the supplied Sochoh/Bethshean/Zartanah spellings remain intact. These records neither assert different historical individuals automatically nor erase the source's distinguishable relations. The month names Zif and Bul are not replaced with modern calendar dates.

## 6. Food, cargo and labour have distinct senses

`kjv_food_provision` is food-prepare. Meal in4:22 is inherited `kjv_flour`, grain material rather than a served meal. The30fine-flour and60meal measures stay different; neither receives an assumed litre conversion. `kjv_horse_stall` is horse-house, animal lodging rather than a market stand or delay. Dromedary is an explicit source-label borrowing; it is not silently replaced with another witness's swift steeds or assigned a precise species claim.

`kjv_raft` is wood-vessel, the floats conveying timber. `kjv_unload` is carry-out, cargo discharge rather than bodily discharge or dismissal. The annual oil amount in5:11 is20measures, not a larger number imported from a parallel account. Hire in5:6 uses inherited wages, preserving compensation rather than only the act of engaging a worker.

`kjv_labour_levy` is work-gather, a levy of people distinct from tribute goods. The30,000levy,10,000monthly rotation, one month abroad/two home,70,000carriers,80,000hewers and3,300overseers remain separate categories. No volunteer status or modern employment contract is added. `kjv_stonesquarer` is stone-shape, the supplied craft category rather than an imported ethnic identification.

## 7. A house that contains an oracle is not a spoken answer

`kjv_oracle_chamber` is god-chamber, the inner sanctuary linked to the most holy place. It differs from `kjv_oracle_reply`, a consulted utterance. The ark put there is the covenant chest, not Noah's vessel. `kjv_windowlight` records the historical light-admitting opening; narrow lights are not dim electric lamps. No definitive reconstructed window cross-section is asserted.

`kjv_beam_support` is beam-hold for narrowed rests. The purpose clause in6:6 uses the existing passive optative to express beams not being fastened into the walls; this also keeps the word within the approved column without changing font size. Chambers rest structurally through passive support in6:10, not the bodily ease sense of rest. `kjv_winding` is turn-way and `kjv_stair` up-step. Spatial right uses the existing right-side entry.

`kjv_ceiling` is inside-roof; the source's floor/ceiling-wall phrasing remains awkwardly visible. `kjv_inner` shares inside with a structural adjective sense. The supplied middle-door entrance, sides of the20-cubit space and oracle forepart are not silently rewritten using another plan. The repeated completion statements remain in narrative order.

## 8. Fractions, panels and dimensions must not collapse into homographs

`kjv_fifth_share` is part-five, exactly1/5; `kjv_fourth_share` part-four, exactly1/4. These are fractions, not ordinal positions or counts of posts. The exact dimension intended by the source's **part of the wall** relation is left unspecified. `kjv_door_leaf` is door-part, not foliage. `kjv_fold_panel` shares turn with physical folding, distinct from the proportional fold multiplier. Two fir doors have two leaves each.

The whole house is60×20×30cubits; the described inner oracle is20in each dimension and the front temple40long. Porch orientation, chamber widths5/6/7, chamber height5, two cherubim each10high and5+5wing span are checked independently. The40,000horse stalls remain40,000; no silent four-thousand correction. Three stone rows and one cedar row remain distinct materials.

The480th year from departure, fourth regnal year and second month are ordinal dates, not lengths to add. Completion is eleventh year/Bul/eighth month, with the explicitly stated seven years retained beside the named dates. No modern year conversion or recalculated month-exact duration is substituted.
