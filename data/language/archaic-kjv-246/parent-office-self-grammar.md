# KJV 0.10-kjv.8 — personal witness, office-self and shared liability

This continuation inherits v7 unchanged and adds Genesis 39–44: 209 authored verses. Genesis 1–44 plus frozen Jonah account for 1,407 of 31,102 verses; 29,695 remain beyond this checkpoint. The one complete Bible remains the required deliverable. This deliberately awkward historical register is permanently excluded from modernization. Authored historical forms are not claims of ancient attestation.

## 1. Personal witness and a household claiming injury

Joseph's private refusals, remembered dreams, assertions of innocence, interpretation and tears use ordinary singular-I. Potiphar's wife changes perspective: her account of an encounter uses singular-I, but her accusation that a Hebrew was brought to mock us uses singular-we, claiming the household as one injured body. The claim is hers; grammar does not endorse it or transform her accusation into narrator fact. Delegated household authority does not remove Joseph's enslavement. The dedicated prisoner and custody terms do not imply a just conviction.

The shared-self forms are selected by discourse role, not mechanically by plural English pronouns or intensity. This prevents the requested internal plurality from erasing the real differences between an individual witness, an institution and a collaborating group.

## 2. A ruler's I encompasses an office without becoming a god

Pharaoh's dreams use singular-I: one sleeper sees, wakes and reports. In the public commission of Genesis 41:40–44, plural-I voices the office-self that commands house, people and territory; its possessive is mypl. I am Pharaoh combines a singular title with an internally plural speaking authority. The court's search for a suitable administrator uses singular-we, a selecting body.

This is an explicit Vayrshemon interpretive choice, not a historical claim about Egyptian pronouns and not a divine identification. Joseph's personal divination claim in 44:15 remains singular-I. Pharaoh's reference to the Spirit of God remains his third-person attribution; no named Trinitarian person is inserted. Divine causal action in the sons' naming explanations stays third-person God, with a single causative predicate rather than doubled do + cause.

## 3. Dreams distinguish images, quantities and attributed meanings

Royal cup-bearer, custody, dream interpretation, woven basket, vine, cereal grain-ear and court magician receive explicit senses. Ear of corn is not a hearing organ or specifically maize. Rank grain is luxuriant, not noble. White baskets retain the supplied reading without substitution of a different scholarly interpretation. Pressed grapes are not given an invented fermentation interval.

Lifting the head remains parallel in the two interpretations: restoration in one, lifting the head off the body and execution in the other. Three branches and three baskets each correspond to three days; they are not added into six. The two Pharaoh dreams repeat the same seven abundant and seven famine years, not fourteen years of abundance. Joseph's claims about God revealing what will happen are represented as his claims. The impossible lean cattle remain dream images, not rationalized zoology.

## 4. Kin-self, travelers and shared wrongdoing

Genesis 42 separates ten travelers, twelve brothers, one absent brother and the youngest left behind. Singular-we claims sons of one father; plural-we describes the separate men as witnesses. Shared guilt for the brother uses singular-we, but Reuben's I told you remains personal dissent. Jacob's bereaved personal-I shifts to plural-I when all these things are against me: the endangered kin-self extends the earlier two-band and few-in-number usages. Joseph and Simeon are absent or presumed lost in his speech, not narrator-certified deaths.

Genesis 43:8 contrasts the traveling plural-we with a household singular-we that must survive, then unpacks that whole into travelers, father and children. Kinship possessives such as ourone/father can occur beside personal my/father: viewpoints remain chosen by the speaker. The source father's two sons focuses Rachel's children inside his speech without deleting the other sons from the narrative.

## 5. One liable group can be unpacked into separate bodies

The brothers' extreme offer in 44:9 proposes death for the cup-holder and slavery for the rest. The steward says according to your words but changes the terms to one servant and the others blameless. Both the wording and the discrepancy remain. Found does not mean stolen: the narrator has already ordered the cup planted.

Judah's 44:16 singular-we speaks as one liable family, then explicitly unpacks that whole into plural-we and the cup-holder. God has found guilt is his interpretation; the target does not turn it into a factual admission of stealing the planted cup. His personal surety and final offer to remain instead of Benjamin return to singular-I. Collective identity does not make people interchangeable or authorize sacrificing a dependent. My lord, thy servant and as Pharaoh retain their ceremonial indirection; the last is authority comparison, not identity equivalence.

## 6. Embedded reports keep their speakers and their contradictions

Nested first persons follow the quoted speaker: Joseph inside Judah's plea, Judah inside his earlier pledge, and Jacob inside the family recollection. Reported questions need not be inserted retroactively into earlier narration. The brothers' report that all money was discovered at the lodging remains beside the earlier one-sack discovery followed by unloading at home. The steward's divine-treasure explanation stands beside the narrator's restoration order. The suspected slave trap is the brothers' fear, not the narrator's explanation of the dinner invitation.

Counterfactual past-perfect retains its conditional frame: had we not delayed, we had returned twice is not a claim that two returns happened. Their refusal to listen to Joseph is a past refusal, not an accidental future will-not. Father's death if Benjamin is lost remains a prediction in the plea. Joseph's alleged divining is a source claim of sign-reading, distinct from the adjective divine and without translator certification of supernatural efficacy.

## 7. Bodies, food and alien religious cadence

Bowels yearning and life bound in another's life keep their embodied metaphors, without a modern psychological diagnosis or an invented biological merger. God forbid remains an awkward deity-invoking optative, not a flattened certainly not. If I am bereaved, I am bereaved keeps its repetitive personal resignation. Native exclamatory morphology carries the money-discovery cry; no unsupported exclamation glyph is introduced.

Load a donkey means put cargo on it, distinct from feeding or filling its body. Inn is a lodging stop, not necessarily a commercial hotel. Food mess is a portion, not disorder. Almonds remain distinct from generic nuts; balm, honey, spices, myrrh, nuts and almonds preserve all six gift categories. The three separate dinner groups and the Egyptians' stated taboo remain attributed social practice, not translator endorsement. Merriment does not by itself require a claim of intoxication.

## 8. Exact measure, naming and permanent preservation

One fifth is expressed as one of five equal parts, not one twelfth merely because Unta counts duodecimally. Benjamin's portion is five times the others', not five additional portions. Double purchase money and returned money are separate objects without an invented total weight. Numbers, ages, time spans, ordinal search order and symbolic correspondences receive independent source checks. Joseph's statement about all food remains beside the earlier fifth-part recommendation without editorial harmonization.

Zaphnathpaaneah is an adapted proper form without invented etymology. Potipherah has a separate referent from Potiphar. Manasseh and Ephraim keep source naming explanations beside their names. Existing dictionary entries, paradigms, case mapping and approved rendering remain unchanged. Every new verse has an exact compiler fixture; selected Forge page tests illustrate the semantic contrasts. Compilation, quantities and self-review are not independent literary certification, and this checkpoint is not completion of the whole Bible.
