Stolen waters and secret bread17are her claim,not the narrator's endorsement; the deathward warning18returns outside that invitation.

The first-person meal invitation9:5and life-extension claim11remain in the Wisdom discourse. ## 4. Wealth, labor and unharmonized maxims

The short maxims make categorical claims about righteousness,wealth,poverty,life and death. The translation preserves them as the source's generalizations rather than converting them into modern empirical guarantees. Slackness leading to poverty10:4remains beside poverty as the poor person's destruction15. Generosity increasing wealth11:24–25is not given a new financial mechanism. The grain-seller11:26remains a seller rather than being rewritten as a free distributor.

The supplied no-sorrow blessing10:22,no-evil-to-the-righteous12:21,and no-death pathway12:28remain absolute in wording. Their tension with earlier Job and Psalms is not erased. Knowledge can be taught and also concealed12:23. The source's social and theological inconsistency is part of this deliberately alien historical edition,not a compiler error to smooth away.

## 5. Agency, repeated lines and negative scope

Violence covers the wicked mouth10:6/11in the supplied direction; it is not inverted into the mouth concealing violence. Both occurrences use the same clause. The chattering-fool-falls refrain10:8/10also remains identical. Prating means chatter,not praying. Lies and lying lips are false speech; lie in wait12:6is ambush posture.

Though hand joins hand11:21,the wicked shall not remain unpunished. The analysis expresses unpunished as a negative passive adjective and not-remain as the negative finite predicate. Repeating a negation feature twice on a single verb is rejected by the compiler; the intended two levels of scope are explicit. Seed remains descendants without a new count. The righteous are recompensed11:31with the broad repayment sense,not a forced pleasant reward.
