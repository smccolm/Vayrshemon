# KJV historical register 0.10-kjv.14 — indwelling self, embodied service and sanctuary craft

This continuation inherits v13 and authors every Exodus 25–31 verse: 243 new verses, 906 total Exodus verses. With all Genesis and unchanged frozen Jonah, 2,487 of 31,102 verses are authored; 28,615 remain. This is an incomplete checkpoint toward ONE entire Bible in ONE .vay, permanently excluded from modernization. Decisions below are authored historical language development and translator self-review, not recovered ancient attestation or independent literary certification.

## Plural self dwelling as singular God

The divine first person retains `ipl` and `mypl` when receiving offerings, showing patterns, giving testimony, meeting the community and dwelling among it. In 29:45–46 the same plural-I is predicated as their God, a singular relational title. The language leaves that deliberately awkward self-number/title-number relationship visible. It does not turn two cherubim into two divine persons or recast every third-person LORD/he as first-person speech. In 31:17 the voice moves from me-plural to third-person LORD/he; that source transition remains. Later Trinity passages are still untranslated and are not claimed as completed evidence.

## Community, mediator, name-bearer and craftspeople

In 29:42 the speaker meets plural-you but speaks to singular-thou, preserving community and mediator. Aaron carries twelve names and the community's judgment on his shoulders/heart as one human representative; this does not automatically make him plural-I. Bezaleel, Aholiab and the wider wise-hearted makers remain distinct recipients of skill. The spirit-of-God wording is retained without imposing a count or identification of divine persons. No gratuitous singular-we is inserted where the source has no collective first-person speech; contrast is useful only if the pronouns do not become decorations on every clause.

## Object senses, material names and bounded uncertainty

New explicit senses distinguish ark-chest from flood vessel, furniture-table from writing tablet, furniture crown-rim from head crown, pillar fillet-band from meat, sanctuary vail from face veil, and incense altar from the larger burnt-offering altar. Casting metal differs from throwing an object. Waving an offering differs from a water wave. KJV meat offering refers here to food/grain, not another portion of flesh. Technical roots and names are authored; gem/mineral, timber and aromatic labels preserve the supplied English without claiming modern species or mineralogical certainty. Ouch means a stone mounting; talent and hin remain source measures. New material compounds use the existing morphology and alphabet, not random placeholder roots.

## Exact dimensions and distributive construction

Numeral `+` compounding multiplies components: `two+ten` is twenty. Addition uses `and`: `two+ten and eight` is twenty-eight; `two+hundred and five+ten` is two hundred fifty. The old `kjv_three` remains distinct from modern three. Fractions preserve unit scope: two cubits and a half, a quarter of one hin, and one tenth part of flour. No unstated flour base unit or metric conversion is inserted. Twenty-eight/four linen curtains differ from thirty/four goat-hair curtains; five-plus-five and five-plus-six groups differ. Six branches and seven lamps are not conflated. Two shoulder stones bearing six names each differ from twelve individually named breastpiece stones. Per-unit counts, repeated placements and indefinite one forms are retained and independently audited.

## Material continuity without speculative diagrams

Of-the-same and one-beaten-work keep the stated unity of lampstand parts. The mercy-seat's integral cherubs remain spatially separate from the voice between them. Relative-position clauses preserve inside/outside, under/above, opposite and north/south even where the KJV's joinery description is difficult. The corner-board wording is not repaired into a guessed diagram. Four silver-based inner pillars differ from five brass-based door pillars and four court-gate pillars. Source object-personification his remains awkwardly visible. The plural candlesticks in 25:34 is preserved rather than harmonized to a singular elsewhere.

## Bodily service, contact holiness and threatening consequences

Animal blood is applied to named places, sprinkled on persons and garments, and poured at the altar base; entrails, caul, kidneys, rump and dung remain concrete. Anatomical right uses `kjv_right_side`, never entitlement-right. Parts burnt outside camp differ from parts burnt on altar and flesh eaten by named participants. Atonement extends to objects as well as people without being reduced to a modern apology. Whatsoever touches becomes holy remains the source contact claim. Iniquity of holy things, death clauses for improper approach and sabbath work, and cutting-off formulas are retained without moral modernization or an invented precise punishment mechanism where the source leaves it open.

## Recipes, restrictions and source tensions

The ointment recipe keeps 500/250/250/500 shekels and one hin; the incense has four equal-weight named ingredients. The shekel equals twenty gerahs; the census offering is half a sanctuary shekel, with the same amount demanded of rich and poor and an inclusive twenty-year threshold. The broad prohibition on pouring oil upon man's flesh remains beside the explicit priestly anointing command without inserting ordinary/unauthorized into the translated sentence. Always-burning language remains beside evening-to-morning tending. All-vessels brass in 27:19 remains beside earlier gold objects. These tensions are documented, not silently harmonized.

The inherited morphology, Maxima mapping, 50 pt script, 22 pt reading/gloss/English, exact 38 pt leading, approved page geometry and actual three-font color treatment remain.
