# Hebrews1–3: quoted selves, Son, shared household and rest

Five contextual common entries are added; there are no new proper names or paradigm changes. Earlier canonical quotations and all sealed targets remain unchanged. This is an incomplete Hebrews section inside the still-incomplete whole Bible project, not an independently certified literary translation.

## Divine plural-I across attributed voices

The Father-voice quoted in1:5 and1:13 uses `ipl/mypl` while addressing a distinct thou/Son and speaking of he. This follows the existing heavenly-voice treatment without inserting two,three or any count of internal persons. The direct address O God in1:8 and **God,even thy God** in1:9 are both preserved. Their tension is not resolved by deleting a title or changing a third-person relation into first-person speech.

In2:12–13 Hebrews assigns earlier-scripture words to the sanctifier/Son in its present argument. That attributed voice uses plural-I here; the older Psalm22 personal speaker and older prophetic edition remain sealed unchanged. Quotation in a new discourse can have a newly assigned speaker without retroactively modernizing its source. The speaker's brothers and God-given children remain distinct from the speaking centre. Likewise **I will put my trust in him** preserves he rather than collapsing the relation into self-trust.

The Spirit is explicitly the speaker introducing3:7–11. The quotation's me/I/my in3:9–11 therefore continues the established divine plural-self; your fathers/that generation/they remain the opposed physical people. The initial **his voice** stays third person. No divine physical dual or diagnostic analogy is asserted.

## Communal singular-we and distributive members

`wesg` marks recipients of Son-speech in1:2,the warned listeners in2:1–5,the community's not-yet seeing in2:8–9,and the house/participants/inference of3:6/14/19. `ourone` marks sins,profession and confidence. A singular collective can be predicated of plural sharers,sons or heirs without reducing its members to one body or one legal beneficiary. The if-conditions on holding confidence remain rather than becoming automatic membership guarantees.

All of **one** in2:11 is preserved without supplying Father,substance or an unspecified human referent. Both coordinates sanctifier and sanctified; it does not make the many sanctified people into exactly one second individual. No physical dual is introduced in these chapters.

## Lexical distinctions and deliberately alien images

`kjv_person_being_hebrews` shares substance for1:3's underlying being/person;it does not use the ordinary human-person noun or possessions. Exact image and brightness retain the representational/light imagery without specifying a later metaphysics. `kjv_captain_salvation_hebrews` extends captain to the leading agent of salvation,not an invented army. Perfect in2:10 is full accomplishment through sufferings,not an assertion of prior moral fault.

`kjv_fold_cloth_hebrews` licenses physical folding of the heavens-as-vesture image,distinct from a multiplier or sheepfold. `kjv_slip_away_hebrews` is away-flow with the heard things as object,keeping the supplied let-them-slip wording rather than replacing it with another reading's we-drift-away. `kjv_tempt_trial_hebrews` explicitly extends tempt to trial/challenge,including enticement where context supports it;the tested one is not asserted to yield. The wilderness provocation remains a named kind of event,not an inserted place name or medical diagnosis.

Profession in3:1 is acknowledged confession,not a paid occupation. Right hand is positional,not moral correctness. Wax old is become old. Seed of Abraham is offspring. Reconciliation in2:17 retains the high-priest/people-sins atoning context rather than reducing it to two friends making peace. Rest is an entered condition in the argument,not simply sleep or the remaining items in a list.

## Source-critical retention and quantities

Keep plural **worlds**,**by himself** and **our sins** in1:2–3;spirits rather than a substituted winds in1:7;the full works-of-thy-hands clause in2:7;every man in2:9;and the supplied **took on him the nature** in2:16 rather than an alternate helps translation. Hebrews3:1 retains Christ Jesus. Hebrews3:16 retains the supplied statement **some...not all**,not an alternative question about all who left Egypt.

The source does not name the witness in2:6,so no David name is inserted. Forty years in3:9 stays attached in Hebrews' position instead of being shifted to match the already sealed Psalm paragraph. Both local forty occurrences are three twelves plus four. At first in2:3 and all of one in2:11 retain explicit numerical expectations. Firstbegotten is a lexical category; one in a certain place is indefinite-person selection.
