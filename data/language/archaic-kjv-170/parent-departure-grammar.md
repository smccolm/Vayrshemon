# Daniel1–3: captive names,royal selves and communal refusal

## 1. Personal I,sovereign plural-I and communal singular-we
The eunuch chief's endangered head in1:10,Daniel's petition in1:12,his thanksgiving and intervention in2:23–24,Arioch's self-credit in2:25 and Daniel's denial of superior wisdom in2:30 use personal I. The king's assertions,demands,threats and decrees use plural-I and its possessive:one body speaks as the gathered sovereign authority. This is a discourse extension of the historical pronoun distinction,not a psychological diagnosis or a newly counted plural of kings. The companions,Chaldean court specialists and Daniel's representative interpretive promise use singular-we where the source says we/us. Our-one-faces in1:13 keeps the possessive communal but the faces plural.3:24's king invokes the shared court act with singular-we;3:25 returns to his own sovereign seeing with plural-I. No divine first-person speech or Trinity is fabricated in these chapters,and no actual-dual pronoun is needed for the three companions or paired body parts.

## 2. Captivity,education and imposed names
The Lord actively gives Judah's king and vessels into the captor's hand. The captor carries them to his own god's treasury. Court selection retains bodily qualification,wisdom,skill,learning and the Chaldean tongue;science is knowledge,not a modern laboratory discipline,and meat is food. Four original names are retained alongside four explicitly assigned court names,with each renaming relation recorded. Contextual identities distinguish the captives from earlier genealogical namesakes without claiming Daniel's identity or nonidentity with Ezekiel's exemplar. Ashpenaz,Melzar and guard-captain Arioch remain supplied roles,not invented biographies. The third-year arrival,three-year training,second-year dream and first-year Cyrus endpoint stand as printed without chronological harmonization.

## 3. Food trial and bodily individuality
Daniel's resolve not to defile himself appears twice. Tender love in the eunuch chief is preserved without inventing romance. Worse liking refers to bodily appearance. The ten-day test,ten-day agreement and ten-day outcome each remain explicit;four youths and tenfold comparison are separately counted. Pulse reuses the legumes sense already in the dictionary,not heartbeat. Our-one faces and the other children's collective singular countenance deliberately differ in number. Source fairer/fatter describes the narrated outcome;no medical mechanism or endorsement of a modern diet is added.

## 4. The king's vanished thing and specialists' limits
The thing is gone from me remains the supplied KJV wording at2:5/8 rather than an alternate firm-decree reading. Gain time is the king's accusation;false corrupt speech is not the bodily lie verb. The dismemberment and dunghill threats are retained beside gifts,rewards and honour. The specialists' plural gods whose dwelling is not with flesh remains their quoted claim;Daniel's God in heaven is a separate response. Soothsayer gains an explicit divination-person compound because the role list distinguishes it from magician and astrologer. Syriack remains the printed language designation. Daniel's personal revelation follows the companions' singular-we prayer;Arioch's I found claim remains even though Daniel approached him. The awkward for-their-sakes clause in2:30 is not silently replaced by a smoother purpose clause.

## 5. Image materials,kingdoms and unassigned history
Gold head,silver breast/arms,brass belly/thighs,iron legs and mixed feet remain in their source sequence. The broken materials in2:35 are iron/clay/brass/silver/gold;2:45 orders them iron/brass/clay/silver/gold. Neither list is normalized. Without hands retains negated agency rather than naming a cutter. Third and fourth kingdoms are explicit ordinals;another is not a separately added count. Bear rule is hold authority. Partly broken remains broken rather than weak. They mingle with human seed stays an underspecified plural,without imported ethnic assignments or named later empires. Reciprocal one another is not a cardinal. The kingdom not left to other people,violent destruction,and forever standing remain independently stated.

## 6. Worship directed at Daniel and the coercive court
2:46 explicitly makes Daniel the king's worship object and recipient of offering and sweet odours. The translation does not redirect that action to God or excuse it with an unstated mediating doctrine. God of gods and Lord of kings remain the king's later acknowledgment. Chapter3 repeats all officials at invitation and assembly. Sheriff uses the inherited local royal officer sense,not a modern police institution. Instrument lists are authored individually:cornet,flute,harp,sackbut,psaltery,dulcimer in3:5/10/15,but no dulcimer in3:7. Flute records the musical-tube sense;sackbut and dulcimer are cautious source loans,not asserted reconstructions of ancient instruments. One language group is not substituted for the source's peoples,nations,and languages metonymy.

## 7. Refusal without a guarantee and the fourth figure
We are not careful in3:16 means not anxious,not negligent. If so,our-one God can deliver and will deliver;but if not,we-one still refuse. Both the assurance and countercondition remain without smoothing the tension into guaranteed survival. The furnace's one seven times more retains the printed awkwardness as one and seven,without calculating eightfold or a temperature. Hosen adds leg-clothing separately from coats and hats. The executioners die;three bound men fall;the king sees four loose men and a fourth form like the Son of God. This is the king's attributed comparison,not an inserted name Jesus or a counted Trinity.3:28's his angel stays distinct wording without a forced identity equation. Only the three named men emerge. Bodies,hair,coats and smell remain separately observed.
