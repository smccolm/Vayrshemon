# Daniel4–6: exposed kings,foreign writing and commanded reverence

## 1. Royal plural-self and the recovering personal body
Nebuchadnezzar's proclamation uses sovereign plural-I through the recounting of his fear,dream and boast. In4:34 the exposed narrator lifts his own eyes and recovers understanding with personal I and my.4:36 returns to plural-self as counsellors,lords,kingdom,honour and majesty are restored;4:37 closes as the public king. This is an explicit discourse choice distinguishing embodied recovery from institutional authority,not a new person paradigm or a diagnosis. Daniel's my-lord/my-counsel remains personal;the queen's parenthetic I say in5:11 is personal;Belshazzar's demands and offers use royal plural-I. The accusers in6:5 speak singular-we as a concerted hostile body. Daniel's6:22 I/my God remains personal;Darius's6:26 decree uses sovereign plural-I. No divine first-person or actual-dual pronoun is invented.

## 2. Watcher,holy beings and uncounted times
The watcher and holy one in4:13 are paired descriptions followed by singular he,not two newly counted visitors. Holy-being gains a sacred-person compound separate from the inherited divine title Holy One;watchers and holy beings become plural in4:17 as printed. The tree's its/his shifts,root stump,iron/brass binding,dew and beast-heart remain. Band reuses physical binding,not a group of people. The seven times in4:16/23/25/32 remain without a supplied calendar unit;they are not silently called seven years. One hour and twelve months remain independently exact. The indefinite they driving,feeding and wetting the king is not filled with named angels or captors. Heavens rule remains plural metonymy.

## 3. Bodily humiliation without diagnostic rewriting
Flourishing in the palace is prosperity,not a musical fanfare or literal flowering. Food for all is not exclusively meat;leaves are actual vegetation. Daniel's wish that the dream fall on enemies is optative,not a different interpretation. Break sins by righteousness and iniquities by mercy to the poor retains its conditional possible lengthening of tranquillity. Grass like oxen,hair like eagle feathers and nails like bird claws remain comparisons,not literal animal transformation. No modern psychiatric explanation is added. Reason returning means understanding;staying God's hand means restraining it. Both how-great/how-mighty exclamations use the existing exclamatory morphology rather than an unsupported punctuation glyph.

## 4. Court ancestry,unnamed queen and attributed gods
Belshazzar is distinct from Belteshazzar. The repeated source father/son relation to Nebuchadnezzar is retained without importing a reconstructed pedigree. The unnamed queen is not identified as mother or wife. Her repeated thy-father,the-king,I-say-thy-father remains emphatic and personal. Holy gods in her speech and gods without holy in5:14 retain their difference. The banquet command includes gold and silver vessels,while the actual bringing in5:3 specifies gold only.5:4 orders gold/silver/brass/iron/wood/stone;5:23 orders silver/gold/brass/iron/wood/stone. The material lists are not normalized. The writing hand remains only its visible part and fingers,not an added whole apparition.

## 5. Foreign inscription and unrepaired wordplay
MENE,MENE,TEKEL,UPHARSIN remain four quoted tokens,including the repeated MENE. The source interpretation headings are MENE,TEKEL,PERES;PERES is not substituted into the quoted inscription. Four explicit source loans retain the foreign forms and their adjacent numbered/finished,weighed/lacking,and divided/given interpretations. This preserves the fact of foreign wordplay without fabricating a native etymology. Number uses the numerical verb,not the noble title count;wanting is lack,not desire. Daniel refuses gifts yet reads;the king later gives scarlet,chain and third-ruler proclamation without an invented acceptance speech. The third rulership is kept without an imported explanation of the first two. Belshazzar's death that night names no killer. Darius the Median gets a contextual identity distinct from the earlier rebuilding king,and his about-sixty-two age retains both quantity and approximation.

## 6. Bureaucratic unanimity claims and continuing prayer
The120princes and three presidents remain exact;Daniel is first as printed,not merely one of three. President-officer adds a chief-governor compound,not a modern elected presidency. The proposed whole-realm promotion remains the king's thought. All presidents agreed is the conspirators' claim despite Daniel's exclusion;it is not corrected into narrator unanimity. Their singular-we is hostile communal agency. Thirty-day petitions to any God or man except the king,unchangeable law claims and lethal penalty remain. The source's already-open Jerusalemward windows are not turned into a newly defiant opening. Daniel continues three daily kneelings,prayers and thanks as before. The investigators find prayer;the claim of disregard is their accusation.

## 7. Rescue,collective punishment and coercive praise
The king is displeased with himself,labours until sunset,and says God will deliver. His later lamenting can-he-deliver question stays a question;the translator does not force uniform confidence. Stone,royal signet and lords' signet remain distinct. Fasting is food abstention;instruments of music remains the supplied reading without speculative substitution. Daniel's personal innocence claim concerns this case. The source says belief explains his lack of injury without turning it into a general promise of safety. The accusers,their children and their wives are all thrown to lions;all bones break before reaching the den bottom. No family guilt is invented and none of the victims is omitted. The following peace greeting and compulsory trembling/fear decree remain beside that violence. Darius and Cyrus are joined as separate named reign references without an imposed identity equation.
