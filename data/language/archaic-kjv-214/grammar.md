# John3–4:singular-we within plural-I speech,and communities at a well

This90verse draft extends John to166verses. Publication and native verification remain separate from drafting;the whole Bible remains incomplete and permanently archaic. Prior targets and seals are unchanged. Structural checks and translator self-review are not independent literary certification.

## I-many says,we-one know

John3:11explicitly shifts from plural-I introducing speech to singular-we speaking,knowing and testifying,with our-one witness rejected by plural you. The source does not enumerate that we. It is not translated as three persons,an automatically identical Father/Son group,or the same community as Nicodemus's earlier we-know. Nicodemus speaks for an unspecified group;its membership is not invented.

The thee-to-ye shift in3:7is retained. Born-again remains the supplied wording and Nicodemus's womb/old-age objection stays literal and awkward. Water and Spirit remain named without choosing one sacramental explanation. Wind and Spirit are distinct native words in the source comparison;no invented cognate claim is made. The Son of man who is in heaven remains in3:13even while the discourse has an embodied speaker. Giver,Son and Spirit retain distinct grammatical roles without added arithmetic.

John the witness remains personal-I in not-Christ,sent-before,and I-must-decrease. No new quotation boundary is invented for3:31–36. No-one-receives remains beside the next receiver clause;the source's rhetoric is not normalized. God's not-by-measure giving of Spirit stays nonnumeric. The severe condemnation/wrath wording is preserved as the historical text,not adopted as the translator's judgment.

## A woman,her community,and the speaker's community

The woman uses personal-I for her requests,knowledge and testimony. Her our-one father Jacob,our-one fathers and expected telling-to-we-one refer to her inherited community. Jesus's we-one-know-what-we-worship in4:22is a separate bounded community claim. Later townspeople's we-one-have-heard is their own shared testimony. These uses do not merge every we into one universal mind.

Jesus's plural-self asks for an ordinary drink and is explicitly tired. His repeated living-water promise remains awkward beside a deep water-well and missing drawing equipment. The woman wants relief from thirst and the trip. Five husbands remain without invented reasons or moral biography. Jesus's quotation I-have-no-husband retains the woman's personal-I inside his speech;it does not state his marital status. The woman's all-I-ever-did report likewise stays quoted personal speech inside narration.

The food image proceeds from a practical misunderstanding to doing the sender's will and finishing his work. Sender and worker remain distinct. The paired sower and reaper use dual for the two roles within the image,not a head-count of the field workers. Four months remains a quoted expectation beside fields already white. The nobleman's son is not a centurion's servant;the seventh-hour recovery,yesterday and whole-house belief remain local source details.

## Source and vocabulary

The4:2parenthesis explicitly distinguishes Jesus from baptizing disciples without rewriting3:22. Samaritan dealings is relation-do,one new common entry broad enough for social interaction rather than commercial trade alone. Four contextual names identify Nicodemus,Aenon,Salim and Sychar. Jacob's water-well uses the established noun rather than adverb well;well-said retains the adverb. Wage,fever,yesterday,worship and serpent use their existing intended senses.
