# Historical KJV 0.10-kjv.103 — Job: a shared yesterday and an impossible hearing

This continuation adds174authored verses inJob8–14,bringing the working Job text to334verses. It inherits the pinned modern0.10through the historical KJV chain without rewriting any earlier sealed text. The Bible remains incomplete. The register is deliberately awkward and foreign;its constructions are authored editorial choices,not claims of ancient attestation or independent literary certification.

## 1. One present generation, two disputing parties

Bildad8:9uses singular-we and matching ourone for the present generation as one limited knowing body:only of yesterday,its days a shadow. This rhetorical collective does not make the former authorities infallible. Job's personal-I remains separate,including the I attributed to him by Zophar11:4. The personified place in8:18also says personal-I;being a nonhuman speaker does not automatically produce a divine plural-self.

Job9:32–33uses dual-we for the imagined two parties,Job and God,who might meet in judgment. The following both makes the pair explicit. This is not singular-we:the entire complaint concerns parties who cannot form a workable hearing because their power is unequal. The missing daysman would stand between them. No divine first-person speech is present in these chapters,so none is invented merely to display plural-I. Job13:22and14:15retain alternating thou/I in the desired exchange.

## 2. Authority can be challenged without changing the narrator

Bildad's if-your-children-sinned remains conditional. His assertion that God never casts away a blameless person is allowed to conflict with Job's assertion that blameless and wicked are both destroyed. Zophar's lies accusation and less-than-deserved-punishment claim remain his own. Job's no-doubt-you-are-the-people line remains ironic in context without inserting a literal not. The friends' ancient authority and Job's own eyes and ears remain rival claims to knowledge.

The translation does not issue a new verdict resolving every assertion. The narrator's earlier blamelessness,the LORD's without-cause statement,and Job's inability to know the court scene remain distinct levels of evidence. In9:17Job independently protests wounds without cause;this does not give him an invented memory of the divine conversation.

## 3. Wet plants, fragile supports and source names

Rush-plant shares the inherited reed form. Flag-plant is water-leaf,not a banner. Hypocrite is false-face,the supplied moral category in the friends' and Job's arguments,not a modern diagnosis. The web and unstable house remain separate successive images. Roots seeing stones,a place denying a removed occupant,and the joy of replacement remain awkward rather than being converted into a smooth botanical explanation.

Arcturus,Orion andPleiades receive three explicit name forms. Their supplied KJV labels are not corrected using another textual witness,identified with an unprinted star group,or expanded with mythology. The southern chambers remain unnamed chambers. A new plant-organism noun distinguishes a growing plant from the inherited planting verb;root-leaf is a historical cultural calque,not a universal botanical definition.

## 4. Judicial speech without the wrong human category

Daysman is between-judge,the absent intervening adjudicator,not a calendar-worker or an inserted named savior. Cause in13:18is a plea arranged for hearing,not a causal mechanism or an order issued to someone. Judicial justification uses the inherited judge-right sense. The accept-person idiom in13:8and10is rendered accepts-face/accepts-faces,keeping partiality visible without classifying the divine referent under the human-person noun. This local idiom is documented rather than hidden as a literal word-for-word substitution.

Job's imagined hearing retains its conditions:remove the hand and remove fear,then either party may call and the other answer. His confident expectation of vindication remains an expectation,not a completed judgment. The opposing parties are not merged into one voice to simplify the grammar.

## 5. Ordinary senses cannot be left to accidental English homonyms

Post in9:25is run-messenger,a swift courier. Stocks in13:27is foot-bind,a restraint. Ditch is dig-ground,a low dug channel. Water wearing stones uses water-rub,erosion,not clothing. Fashioned in10:8is shaped,not clothing-fashion. Mark in10:14is observe the sinner;the printed heel-mark in13:27keeps the physical distinguishing-sign word. Spoiled counselors and princes are stripped,not indulged or spoiled food. Straitened nations are narrowed,not straightened. Habitation explicitly names a dwelling place rather than leaving only an event nominal.

Spatial length and breadth in11:9are compared explicitly;the inherited longer form for duration is not used for earth's extent. Hell-depth shares the received alien religious form but records the local underworld-depth sense without silently importing the base dictionary's later punishment interpretation. The source's hell label remains;the gloss does not pretend another witness's word was printed.

## 6. Questions, conditions and exclamatory force remain different

Job10:14says if-I-sin;7:20said I-have-sinned. Those are not interchangeable. Job9:29also remains conditional. Negative rhetorical questions retain their scope across parallel verbs. The bodily birth lament in10:18and the desired silence in13:5use the inherited exclamatory grammar rather than an unsupported exclamation glyph. The grave wish in14:13is an optative,while14:14asks whether a dead person lives again;neither is silently made an assertion of certainty.

The supplied affirmative I-will-trust in13:15remains alongside I-will-maintain-my-ways. A different witness's no-hope reading is not imported. Remembrances and bodies in13:12remain the KJV's words,not replacement maxims and defenses. Dig-about-yourself in11:18,changes-and-war in10:17and the final flesh-and-soul suffering in14:22remain difficult.

## 7. Number has a local task, not a doctrinal one

One of a thousand in9:3keeps both numbers and their relation rather than manufacturing a decimal probability. One thing in9:22counts the stated point. Double wisdom in11:6is the source's difficult comparison,not a claim that divine consciousness has two persons. One man mocking another in13:9is a hypothetical human comparison. Two things in13:20are the requested removals explained next. Not one in14:4counts possible agents;the target does not use the inherited generic-human one and thereby narrow the question.

Numbered months and steps remain counting expressions without invented totals. The celestial names do not acquire an invented star count. Exact source quantities are audited independently of font-slot number rendering.

## 8. Mortal hopes are allowed to change within the speech

Job's swift days,unheard voice and impossible lawsuit lead into the plea to be left alone. The tree may sprout while the human dies. The heavens-until limit in14:12remains,and the following hidden-in-grave wish remains a wish. Change in14:14is not assigned a modern resurrection mechanism. The desired call-and-answer is followed again by counted steps,sealed transgression,eroding hope and a person who does not know his sons' fortunes. The translation preserves this movement instead of forcing a single systematic afterlife doctrine onto the chapter.
