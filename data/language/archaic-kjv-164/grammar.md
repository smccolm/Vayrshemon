# Ezekiel33–36: communal claims,shepherd judgment and caused renewal

## 1. Plural-self,singular-we and an actual political dual
The prophet's narration and the quoted inviter in33:30 use personal-I. Divine speech uses plural-I/my-plural;I-even-I in34:11 and34:20 repeats that form rather than substituting a reflexive.33:10 gives the despairing house singular-we and our-one sins.33:21's our-one captivity places the personal narrator within the one captive community.33:24 deliberately says singular-we are many:one claiming body with many members,contrasted with Abraham's explicit one. Seir's mine in35:10 is civic plural-self and its we is singular-we;the deity resumes the same plural-self form at the next oracle formula.35:11's two occurrences of them use actual dual for the two nations/countries named in35:10. Myself there is divine plural-self reflexive.36:2's hostile ours is singular-we. None of these choices invents a divine headcount.

## 2. Warning duties and conduct reversals
33:2–9 retains the distinct responsibilities of the listener and watchman,including the hearer's iniquity beside the watchman's blood liability. Hearing is not automatically heeding.33:12–19 retains every condition and negative:past righteousness does not cover later wrong,and past sins are not mentioned after the stated turn and restitution. Pledge restoration and return of robbed goods remain concrete acts. Every-one judgment is distributive each,not a numeral. The unequal-way accusations retain their speaker opposition.

## 3. City news and an aesthetically consumed oracle
33:21 keeps the printed twelfth year,tenth month,fifth day. One-that-escaped is a person,not a new count.33:22 keeps the evening-before/morning-arrival sequence and the unusual until clause;dumb means speechless rather than unintelligent.33:24's many-member singular-we is an attributed possession claim,not an endorsed conclusion.33:30 retains against-thee rather than changing it to about-thee. The hearers' mouth-love and coveting heart remain opposed;the lovely song/instrument comparison criticizes hearing without doing. Exclamatory force continues through morphology,not an exclamation glyph.

## 4. Failed care and the consuming shepherd
34:4 retains all five neglected care actions:strengthening,healing,binding,returning and seeking. The no-shepherd statement remains beside my-shepherds without a corrective qualification. Meat is food;fat in34:3 is material,while fat/lean animals later express bodily condition. Sheepfold is an enclosure,not a multiplier. The shepherds' mouths consume the flock until it is rescued.34:16 retains destroy-the-fat-and-strong and feed-with-judgment rather than an alternate protective reading. Care and judgment coexist without editorial smoothing.

## 5. One shepherd and human flocks
34:23 explicitly counts one shepherd and names David.34:24 distinguishes that prince from the divine speaker. No later messianic identity or Trinity enumeration is inserted.34:27 uses yoke bands as restraints and retains served-themselves-of-them as exploitation.34:29's plant of renown remains unresolved rather than becoming a named species or person.34:31 explicitly calls the flock humans.36:38 returns to festival flocks of humans;the image is kept as a metaphor rather than an actual animal census.

## 6. Seir and the contested land
35:5 retains the time when their iniquity had an end.35:6 says not-hated-blood and repeats blood's pursuit.35:8 shifts his to thy;35:9 shifts thou to you. The two nations and two countries in35:10 name a paired political claim,not four different counted lands. The subsequent dual is limited to its two antecedents;the mountains in35:12 remain plural. Idumea retains its supplied form.36:3 gains disgrace-name for infamy,a public reputation term. Despiteful means contemptuous;borne shame means endurance,not childbirth. Agricultural shooting is growth,tilling uses land-cultivation,and old estates means former states rather than invented property titles.

## 7. Name-motive,indwelling and agency
36:17 retains the menstrual-separation comparison through the historical ritual vocabulary without asserting a modern medical claim.36:20 keeps the unresolved they-to-them speaker seam and quoted his-land.36:21's pity is for the divine name;36:22 and36:32 explicitly deny acting for the people's sakes.36:23 distinguishes active sanctifying of the name from passive sanctification of the divine self. New heart/new spirit and stone/flesh heart remain material metaphors.36:27's my-plural spirit and caused walking retain divine ownership and causation without an added voluntary choice or a counted Trinity.36:37 permits enquiry beside chapter20's refusal;these historical assertions are not collapsed into one timeless rule.

## 8. Coverage and evidence
These117verses add one common compound for infamy and no new proper names or inflectional endings. Explicit numbers are33:21[12,10,5],33:24[1],34:23[1],35:10[2,2]. Every verse has source anchors,back-reading and an editorial note;pronoun sets and the one actual-dual reference are separately checked. The release continues its immediate historical parent while preserving all older targets,modern0.10 and frozen Jonah. This is translator self-review and structural validation,not independent literary certification. The complete Bible remains unfinished.
