## A shared self is not an erased participant

Jesus retains plural-I and my-plural in direct teaching,the bread/body and cup/blood sayings,and the prayer. The meal company is singular-we in22:8,while Peter and John are you-two/they-two through their errand and answer we-two in22:9. These are different referential scopes inside one scene. No arithmetic doctrine follows from the grammatical plurality.

The Father appoints to Jesus,who appoints to the disciples. In22:42the praying plural-self explicitly distinguishes my-will from the addressed Father's will. The translator does not merge the two wills,explain away the cup,or remove the strengthening angel and agonized prayer. Sweat is as blood-drops,the supplied comparison,not a medical assertion. These deliberately awkward relations are allowed to remain awkward.

Simon is named twice before a plural-you warning in22:31. The prayer in22:32then singles him out with thee/thy/thou. Peter's prison-and-death promise and three denials use personal-I. In22:61his memory contains Jesus's quoted plural-I;the quotation does not make Peter the plural speaker. The attendants' sword question and council's conclusion each use singular-we for their own bounded group,not one continuous community.

## Source-specific tensions retained

The widow's two mites are contrasted with abundance and her entire livelihood. Temple predictions retain some disciples being put to death alongside the promise about no hair perishing;no harmonizing explanation is inserted. All trees remain beside the fig tree. Worthy remains the supplied wording. Days of vengeance,pregnancy and nursing,universal snare and this-generation remain with their source scope and without present-day identifications.

The supper retains the cup before bread and the cup after supper. Luke's for-you wording does not acquire Matthew's many/remission clause. Betrayal money has no imported thirty-silver count. Twelve apostles,twelve tribes,three denials,two swords,and about one hour remain exactly where supplied. Luke's cock-crow does not acquire Mark's twice detail. The anonymous striker and high-priest servant do not acquire Peter/Malchus names. The right ear is healed. Suffer-thus-far remains a terse allow-thus-far;it is not expanded into approval of violence. The sleeping disciples' sorrow is retained.

## Two contextual dictionary additions

Benefactor is benefit-give-person as a reported ruling honorific,not narrator endorsement. Domestic hall is house-middle for the high-priest house's communal fire setting,distinct from the governor's hall. Neither term asserts an architectural plan. No new proper name or pronoun paradigm is needed.

The borrowed Iscariot designation is retained beside Judas. The source's blasphemous speech is reported without invented insults. The council's final you-say-that-I-am reply remains attributed rather than becoming a bare yes. Both refusal-to-answer and refusal-to-release carry explicit negation.
