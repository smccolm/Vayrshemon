## 1. The commanding plural self and the exposed personal self

Absalom's wish to be made judge in15:4 begins with personal `i`; the justice he imagines dispensing through office uses `ipl`. Ahithophel in17:1 personally selects the proposed force, then uses `ipl` for action as its commander in17:1–3. These are projected agencies in proposals, not assertions that either plan has already happened.

David's imagined divine reply in15:26 uses `ipl`, followed by David's personal **here I am**. The quotation boundary resets the referent. The condition is retained: the narrator has not independently reported that God spoke this refusal. Personal `i` also carries David's uncertain travel, prayer, hoped-for return and proposed bodily presence at battle. At18:33 his wish to have died instead of Absalom remains a bereaved father's singular self. Commanding many does not make every bodily experience plural.

## 2. Rival singular-we bodies, countable people and actual pairs

David's fleeing household uses `wesg` in15:14,19,20. Absalom's deliberating political body uses the same singular-whole construction in16:20 and17:5–6. Hushai's promised overwhelming force uses `wesg` in17:12–13. These are rival wholes with different speakers and referents, not one permanently shared entity. The repeated first-person form is licensed by local participation, not merely by the English spelling we.

The people addressing David in18:3 use `wepl`: individual lives can be counted, halved and compared with the king's value. The thousand-shekel refusal in18:12 uses **hearing of we(pl)** for multiple witnesses of the command. This distinguishes counted persons from the unified actor of flight or a projected attack.

The priests addressed in15:27 form a real pair, `you:du`, with a son belonging to each. The sons of Zeruiah addressed in16:10 are also an actual dual. The hidden messengers in17:18–21 use `they:du`, sharply distinguishing them from the plural pursuing servants. Dual is grounded in the named pair, not guessed from a small or intimate group.

## 3. Political performance stays inside its speech frame

Absalom's assurance that every legal case is good, his complaint that no royal hearer is appointed, his vow and the trumpet proclamation remain his political acts. The two hundred accompanying invitees explicitly know nothing of the plot. Their presence cannot make them knowing conspirators. The stolen-hearts image remains concrete.

Hushai's doubled **God save the king** and profession of service are performed speech after David's counter-counsel instructions. His assertion of loyalty does not override the disclosed mission. Ziba's claim about Mephibosheth is reported by Ziba, without narrator certification. David's grant to Ziba is nevertheless an actual action in the narrative.

Shimei's accusations and divine explanation belong to Shimei. David's explanation that the LORD told him to curse remains David's response, not an inserted independent oracle. Hope that the LORD may repay good remains uncertain. The dual address to Zeruiah's sons preserves David's separation from the proposed retaliation.

## 4. Good counsel, extravagant counsel and interrupted information

Ahithophel proposes twelve thousand men, an immediate attack and killing the king only. Hushai proposes delay, a vast gathering and personal leadership by Absalom. His promises leave not one of David's company and even drag a city into a river with ropes. These are rhetorical claims; the translation neither executes the plans prematurely nor supplies engineering explanations for the extravagant images.

Hushai calls the first advice not good **at this time**. The narrator later calls Ahithophel's advice good and says the LORD appointed its defeat. These distinct evaluations remain distinct. Ahithophel's counsel being esteemed **as if** consulting God's oracle is a comparison, not a statement that every proposal is divine revelation.

The well episode retains the woman's deceptive report and the narrator's different location of the fugitives. The young female messenger is given no invented sexual role. Ahimaaz's final claimed ignorance remains his answer, not certified ignorance; Cushi's indirect death message remains indirect. Recognition of a runner, expectation of good news, victory framing and the father's question are separate stages of knowledge.

## 5. Material senses and seven common additions

`kjv_oracle_reply` is god-word as an answer consulted, distinct from a later architectural sanctuary sense. `kjv_pulse_food` is bean-food, a legume provision rather than a heartbeat. `kjv_dart` shares the javelin surface form while recording the source's pointed shaft weapon without asserting a bow-shot arrow or modern reconstruction. `kjv_third_share` is an exact part-three, one-third share rather than third-in-order.

`kjv_legal_case` is judgment-matter, a specialized lexical compound for a cause brought for judgment. It is neither the inherited causative verb nor physical substance in a petitioner's case. `kjv_gatekeeper` is gate-guard, the source porter rather than a burden-carrier. `kjv_covering` is cover-thing over the well, without assigning an unstated material.

Existing entries retain water well versus well as an adverb, grain versus maize, ground grain versus earth, wilderness, saddle, loaf, bottle, cord, pit, armor, pillar, avenging and tumult. Bare feet are expressed directly. Affliction uses experienced suffering; falsehood uses the false-lie event, not an unrelated nominal root. Plain is terrain; bear is the animal, not begetting; right is the body's side where the source is spatial.
