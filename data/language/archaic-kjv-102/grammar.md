# Historical KJV 0.10-kjv.102 — Job: the court, the sufferer and the confident friends

This continuation authors Job1–7,160verses,within the permanently historical KJV register. Its deliberately foreign constructions descend from the pinned0.10consolidation through the historical chain. They are editorial choices,not recovered ancient Vayrshemon. The Bible remains incomplete. Previously sealed texts,including Jonah,are unchanged. These checks establish coverage and internal consistency;they do not claim independent literary certification.

## 1. Divine plural-self does not take over the sufferer's personal-I

The LORD's my-servant in1:8and2:3uses the plural-self possessor,mypl. The divine me in2:3uses plural-I as the addressed court speaker. Neither construction enumerates divine persons. Sons-of-God remains the printed relationship without converting it into a Trinity or replacing it with an unprinted class of angels. Satan is given a local proper-name entry for this court challenger;later Christian biography is not inserted into the scene.

Each disaster messenger's I-alone-escaped remains personal-I. Job's naked-I,his birth curse,his demand for an answer and his absent-I at7:21also remain personal. Eliphaz's I-have-seen and I-heard refer to his own claimed experience. A personal testimony is not made collective merely to intensify the register. The source's my-spirit and my-soul remain ordinary personal possessors.

## 2. Singular-we can make a contested claim to a common voice

Job2:10uses singular-we for one shared receiving condition:good and evil received from God. This construal does not reduce the line to a counted husband-wife pair. Eliphaz4:2uses singular-we to claim the companions' intervention as one speaking body;5:27uses it for their claimed common examination and conclusion. These are rhetorical assertions of unity,not the narrator certifying that every friend agrees or that their conclusion is true. The following individual who/himself in4:2remains individual. Job6addresses the companions with ordinary plural-you;the group of three does not become a dual.

## 3. Claims stay inside their speakers' arguments

The narrator calls Job blameless before the disputes. The LORD repeats that assessment and explicitly says without cause in2:3. Satan's predictions of a curse remain predictions. Fire-of-God in1:16is the messenger's designation. Eliphaz's innocent-never-perishes question,his plough-and-reap theory and his promised immunity remain his argument. His night vision includes an unidentified spirit,an indiscernible form,partial hearing and a reported voice. None is relabelled a verified divine revelation.

Job's death-as-rest,grave-as-no-return and divine-surveillance complaints remain his expressed perspective. The translation supplies neither doctrinal harmonization nor a narrator's endorsement. Source qualifications matter:2:10says with-his-lips,not every possible inward thought;6:14leaves he ambiguous between sufferer and friend. Job7:20retains I-have-sinned as printed rather than changing it into if-I-have-sinned.

## 4. A small noun system for material suffering and ordinary work

Potsherd-scraper is pot-part,not an intact vessel. Boils use the inherited lesion sense;sole is the foot surface and crown here is top-of-head. Untimely-birth is before-time-birth,without a modern medical subtype. The moth entry is cloth-wing,a historical image name,not a biological assertion about every species. Grain-shock is corn-heap,a gathered mass,not fright. Hireling is wages-work-person,the hired worker rather than the employer. The weaving shuttle is thread-carry,distinct from modern transport. Couch is lie-bed.

Fatherless is without-father;it does not assert both parents died. Holy One receives a nonhuman title entry so the divine referent is not narrowed to the inherited human one. Right-speech is true-speech for fitting or well-grounded words,not entitlement or direction. Satan's proper-name entry counts separately from the twelve new common senses;the other eight proper entries identify Job,Uz,three friends,two designations and the raiders.

## 5. The KJV's difficult images stay difficult

Job3:8retains mourning rather than importing another witness's monster. Knees prevent in3:12means meet or receive,not obstruct. Desolate places built for kings are not explicitly identified as tombs. The strange return thither in1:21is not solved by an added grave. Before-eating in3:24remains before,not instead of. The repeated negatives in3:26remain negative even though a comfortable-before-disaster story might seem easier.

Job4:17retains more-just-than and more-pure-than,not a substituted before-God construction. Excellency in4:21stays the supplied abstract term. Job5:24retains not-sin,not another witness's nothing-missing. Swallowed words in6:3are not changed to rash words. Blackish ice,paths going to nothing,a covenant with field stones and a place that knows its absent occupant remain personifications and strange images. Whale in7:12is retained as the supplied creature label rather than replaced by a different witness's monster.

## 6. Sense distinctions carry the argument

Substance in1and6is goods,not material essence. Counted offerings use numerical reckoning,not the noble title count. Without cause is without reason,not a legal lawsuit. Eliphaz's cause entrusted to God in5:8is a plea or case. Disappointing devices means defeating plans. League with stones is covenant,not distance. Giving up the ghost follows the inherited release-breath death idiom. Lie in6:28is false speech;lie in3:13and7:4is bodily reclining. Consume for destruction or disappearance uses the historical ruin sense,not eating. Meat in6:7is food,not necessarily flesh.

The animal voices remain distinct:braying for the ass and the inherited cattle-low verb for the ox. Headlong counsel is carried in haste rather than adding a fictitious first-place number. The grain shock and workman's shuttle are concrete images supporting the speeches;they are not decorative vocabulary substitutions.

## 7. Repetition, quantities and exclamatory grammar

Disaster reports deliberately repeat while-he-yet-spoke and I-alone-escaped. Removing that repetition would destroy the scene's accumulating pressure. Seven sons and three daughters remain separate counts. Seven thousand sheep,three thousand camels,five hundred yokes of oxen and five hundred female asses remain their supplied units;five hundred yokes are not rewritten as an invented ox count. Three raiding bands,four house corners and seven days plus seven nights retain their distinct roles. The six/seven assurance in5:19is an ascending poetic pair,not a fabricated list or divine-person count.

Lamenting optatives and the inherited exclamatory suffix carry6:2,6:8,6:25and7:20without an unsupported exclamation glyph. Direct questions and counterfactuals retain their modality. Intransitive perish keeps its subject unmarked;negative scope in7:21extends to both pardon and take-away. These are grammatical repairs in this new unsealed draft,not changes to prior historical editions.

## 8. Bodily protest remains unsoftened and unexpanded

Worms,dust-clods,broken skin,spittle,strangling and longing for the grave remain in the supplied fictional speeches. Strangling-lament is a breath-bind nominal for7:15,not a procedural description. No medical diagnosis or prescribed treatment is added. The text is intentionally uncomfortable;the translator does not replace it with reassurance,psychological diagnosis or a modern answer. The final personal-I that will not be in7:21is allowed to end the checkpoint unanswered.

Each verse has its exact English source,authored analysis,script text,Roman reading,construction gloss and a source-specific note. All160verses have source-critical anchors,with separate quantity and pronoun audits. Native layout uses the approved four-column spread,Maxima,leading and Unta folios;no typography is reduced to hide an awkward or long word. The continuation remains an incomplete working section of one intended complete Bible.
