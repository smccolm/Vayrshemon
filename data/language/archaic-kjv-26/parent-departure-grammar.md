# Historical KJV 0.10-kjv.25 · violence, refuge and a people's boundaries

Numbers 31–36 completes all 1,288 verses of Numbers, adding 228 individually authored verses. Combined coverage is 4,941/31,102; 26,161 remain. This is an incomplete Bible checkpoint, not completion of the user's goal. Earlier targets, modern v0.10 and frozen Jonah are unchanged. New forms are historical-branch inventions, not ancient attestations. Commentary is translator self-review, not independent certification.

## Military whole, tribal coalition and nested divine self

The commanders' not one man of us and we have brought in 31:49–50 use singular-we for the returning military whole. Its our-one charge and our-one souls preserve a collective custodian with plural individual souls and separately acquired goods. This corporate number does not include the captives within the protected no-loss claim. That contrast is left stark.

Gad and Reuben speak as one negotiating coalition in 32:5,16–19,31–32. Singular-we does not turn two named tribes into one tribal identity, and the fighting members remain distinct from wives, children and livestock left in fortified cities. Their my lord in 32:25,27 remains a representative person's singular courtesy. Moses' I sent in 32:8 is personal-I; the quotation of the deity's I swore and followed me in 32:11 changes to plural-I. The whole does not mechanically replace every first-person form.

## War testimony without moral smoothing

All-males-slain in 31:7, captive women/children in 31:9, and the further killing command in 31:17 all remain as supplied; the translator does not resolve their tension by changing all to some. Moses' accusation about Balaam's counsel stays attributed to him. The source's sexual-knowledge euphemism is retained without added explicit description, and for yourselves in 31:18 is not replaced with a rescue claim or an invented future scene. The deity's command and the commanders' zero-loss report are translated, not endorsed as modern conduct. Human captives remain within the source's property-accounting lists rather than being silently replaced with livestock or money.

## Two halves and tenfold tribute difference

675,000 sheep, 72,000 bovines, 61,000 asses and 32,000 people split into equal halves. Combatants' half yields one in 500 to the priest: 675,72,61,32. The congregation's half yields one in 50 to Levites: calculated 6,750,720,610,320. The latter computed values are validation commentary, not words inserted into verses that give only a rate. Effective fractions of the whole are 1/1000 and 1/100. Gold 16,750 shekels is a separate weight tally. Thousands/hundreds in command ranks are group-size titles, not counts of captains.

Beeves uses the already-existing bovine sense, neither castrated ox specifically nor all livestock. Lead is metal, not leadership. Tin is a material loan. Tablets among gold ornaments are a flat-jewel category, not writing boards. Keep-charge takes a contextual custodial-duty sense, distinct from give-a-charge as solemn instruction.

## A conditional eastern settlement

The coalition's revised offer promises armed service until the others inherit. Its initial refusal to cross and later commitment are both retained. Moses' stated alternative if they do not cross—possessions among the others in Canaan—remains, even though a translator might expect a harsher penalty. Half-Manasseh enters the grant in 32:33 without being retroactively added to the earlier two-tribe petition. Jazer/Jaazer, Shebam/Shibmah, Ataroth/Atroth and the supplied separate Atroth, Shophan are not silently harmonized. Renamed cities receive only the names the source actually supplies.

## Itinerary and boundary memory

Every march leg is translated in order. Depart, move, journey and go retain mild source variation; camp replaces the unrelated resin sense of pitch. Sin and Zin remain different names; Ijeabarim and Iim retain supplied forms. The extent of the final Jordan camp is not rewritten as another march. Aaron's fortieth year, fifth month, first day and age 123 are explicit. Neither twelve fountains nor seventy palms become invented divine-person counts.

Northern-boundary Hor has a separate contextual entry from Aaron's southern Hor. Ephod the father is not the priestly garment; Kemuel of Ephraim, Elizaphan of Zebulun and the tribal Ammihud fathers are distinguished from earlier name bearers. The border sequence is not converted into an unsupported modern map. Nine-and-a-half western plus two-and-a-half eastern tribal allotments total twelve. Expulsion, destruction of cult pictures/cast images and the warning I will do to you what I intended for them retain their coercive source character and plural-I reversal of privilege.

## Open city-land and unresolved measurements

Suburbs means assigned surrounding open land, not a modern commuter settlement. The cities for Levites coexist with the earlier denial of a territorial tribal inheritance. Six refuge cities plus forty-two others total forty-eight; three refuge cities lie on each side of Jordan. The thousand cubits outward from the wall in 35:4 and two thousand on each of four sides in 35:5 are both retained. No speculative diagram is used to conceal that the measurement frames require interpretation.

## Killing, judgment, refuge and blood

Slayer is a neutral kill-person category before classification; murderer is distinct. Unawares in this legal context is without purposeful killing, clarified by no enmity, no ambush, not seeing and not seeking harm. It is not a deliberate surprise attack. The source's iron, stone and wood examples remain beside the later intent conditions rather than being rewritten into a modern statute. A blood-avenger is a stated role, not a present-day authorization.

Refuge prevents death before congregation judgment. The congregation judges and rescues the protected slayer, who must remain until the anointed high priest dies. Killing outside the refuge boundary is assigned no blood-guilt to the avenger in that stated case; the text does not thereby label the slayer a murderer. A lone witness cannot bring a death sentence; the translation does not add an exact two-witness number absent here. Satisfaction is ransom/payment, not pleasure. No ransom buys a murderer's life or the slayer's early return. Land blood-pollution and cleaning only by shedder blood remain intentionally alien, followed by plural-I dwelling among Israel.

## Ownership retained, marriage constrained

The Gilead clan fathers speak of our-one brother and ancestral inheritance as one property-holding lineage, with representative personal my lord. Their stated jubile consequence is preserved rather than forced into a universal all-property-returns rule. The daughters' earlier inheritance right remains, but their choice of husband is constrained to their father's tribal family. Both whom they think best and only within that family are translated. The later list order—Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, Noah—differs from the earlier list and is kept. Father's brothers' sons is explicit cousin relationship, not vague relatives. Every tribe keeping himself is corporate singular self-reference without an invented first-person speech.
