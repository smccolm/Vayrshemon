# KJV 0.10-kjv.248 · Galatians4–6 · custody, freedom and mutual burdens

75 new verses complete the149 Galatians verses in the authored sequence. Seven common entries and one contextual name-form are added. No productive paradigm, earlier target or seal changes. This frozen KJV edition remains outside modernization; translator self-review is not independent literary certification and the whole Bible remains incomplete.

## Shared household, personal travail and the Spirit cry
The community continues singular-whole `wesg` with plural children, communal freedom, expectation, conduct and perseverance. The author remains personal-I in anxiety, bodily weakness, maternal birth-labour and confrontation; the shared living subject marked plural-I in2:20 is not generalized to every spiritual metaphor. In4:6 the supplied YOUR hearts remains plural, followed by the Spirit of the Son crying Abba, Father. No my, our-hearts variant, psychological label or numerical Trinity equation is inserted. Singular thou in4:7 and singular thyself amid plural helpers in6:1 retain shifts of address. Your spirit in6:18 remains singular with plural addressees.

## Guardianship, elements and solicited attachment
Tutors are child-guardians; governors here are household stewards, not provincial rulers. The father-appointed endpoint and repeated made-of-woman/made-under-law keep their awkward source shapes. World-rudiments leaves elements open between elementary ordering and powers rather than deciding atoms or a named spirit roster. Knowing God corrected to being known by God remains an explicit reversal. Zealously affect is courting attachment, evaluated separately by purpose; the new lexical expression is an authored shortening, not a productive rule imposed on other words. Unknown bodily infirmity and eye-giving remain without a diagnosis. First proclamation is ordinal, and temptation-in-flesh stays the printed wording.

## The author's allegory stays an allegory
Two sons and two covenants keep their counts. Agar is the printed name-form beside earlier Hagar, and gendereth means begets. Answereth-to is allegorical correspondence, distinct from a spoken reply or geographic identity. Sinai in Arabia, present Jerusalem in bondage, Jerusalem above as communal mother, barrenness/non-travail and the exclusion quotation all remain in the argument. The text explicitly calls these things an allegory; the translation does not retroactively rewrite Genesis or turn the source's charged mapping into a claim about modern peoples. Flesh-born, promise-born and Spirit-born are not silently normalized to one term.

## Freedom, desire and contrasting lists
Stand fast means firmly, not abstain from food. Neither circumcision nor its absence is made the sole positive good; faith working by love remains. Cut-off keeps the source's harsh bodily/exclusion resonance without an added specific instruction. One word summarizes the neighbour command without counting surface tokens. Reciprocal biting/devouring/consuming stays predatory imagery. Variance is discord; emulations jealous rivalries; seditions divisions; heresies parties; revels excessive festive conduct. Adultery and murders remain in the supplied KJV list. Spirit fruit remains singular, faith is not changed to faithfulness, and temperance means broad self-restraint. No first-person pronoun is added to the flesh/Spirit opposition.

## Mutual aid, own responsibility and the closing hand
Carry each other's burdens and each carries his own burden are both retained. Prove is test; communicate good things is sharing rather than only speech. Sowing/reaping keeps its conditional due-season patience; flesh corruption is decay rather than merely dishonesty. All people remains the wider scope of aid despite the especially-faith-household priority. The singular large letter and own hand remain as printed, with size ambiguity documented rather than replaced by plural letters. The conduct rule gets a normative sense distinct from a measuring instrument or assigned territory. Israel-of-God retains its added clause without an imposed identity equation. Bodily marks stay unspecific signs, not an invented account of their production.
