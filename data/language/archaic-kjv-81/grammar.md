# KJV historical continuation81 — entrusted service, death and a body speaking

## One body speaking and different speakers within quotation
In11:1 assembled Israel says we are thy bone and thy flesh. Singular-we presents the gathered kin as one body in its address to David; it does not erase the individual elders or make them one biological organism. The nested divine address in11:2 has a different speaker. Its two my-people possessors use the authored plural-self form mypl, an intentional feature of this alien historical register. The verse does not itself state a Trinity count, and none is inserted. Saul's injured me in10:4 and David's thirst and moral refusal in11:17/19 remain personal-I. David's my God is likewise a personal possessor, distinct from the deity's quoted my-plural-self people.

## Human one, counted membership and exclamatory wish
One in David's wish for water is an indefinite human giver and keeps the human-one category. One of the Levites, one of three mighty men and one time are explicit counted expressions and use one_num. These distinctions prevent both a blanket cardinal substitution and a blanket human substitution. The water request combines optative wish and exclamatory morphology. Its force is carried in the language, with no unsupported exclamation mark in Thimfora. Wish is not permission or an already fulfilled promise.

## Ancestral repetition without retrospective normalization
The resident lists distinguish individual ancestry chains from initial enumerations. Ibneiah and Ibnijah are distinct supplied forms. The local Azariah-Hilkiah-Meshullam-Zadok-Meraioth-Ahitub chain is retained without rewriting it to chapter six. Shilonites in this resident list receive a local affiliation record without forcing the earlier Shiloh-prophet interpretation. Jehiel is expressly named as father of Gibeon here; the earlier unnamed statement remains untouched. Zechariah/Zacher, Shimeam/Shimeah, Tahrea/Tarea, Jarah/Jehoadah and Rephaiah/Rapha remain in their supplied passages. This is preservation of differing textual formulations, not a claim that all variants must represent unrelated biological persons.

## Entrusted work, watches and accountability
Porters are gatekeepers. Waited means served at the gate, and charge is entrusted duty. Wards become assigned guarding watches through the guard-time compound kjv_duty_watch, distinct from a dependent under guardianship. Seven-day attendance is retained without inventing a complete rota. The four chief porters differ from the212chosen gatekeepers. By tale means counting vessels in and out; the tale-story noun is excluded. The new verb kjv_oversee shares the inherited oversight form and explicitly denotes supervision, a documented noun-to-verb use rather than a new unrelated phonological root. Free singers are exempt in their service context, not idle or exempt from every possible obligation.

## Sanctuary materials and recurring preparation
Fine flour, wine, oil, frankincense and spices stay distinct alongside vessels and instruments. Ointment is prepared anointing material, without a modern medical assumption. Mattithiah's pans use the cooking-pan sense rather than the fire or ash-pan sense. Shewbread remains the inherited deity-presentation bread category, prepared each sabbath. Chambers, treasuries, daily opening and day-and-night singing service preserve their different functions. Sacred administration is deliberately rendered as concrete and slightly alien work rather than polished ecclesiastical English.

## Death narration and attributed causation coexist
Saul's bodily wound, request, attendant's refusal and both sword deaths remain sequentially explicit. Refusal is unwillingness, not inability. The source all his house died is not silently narrowed to harmonize later descendants. This account sends the head to Dagon's temple and buries bones under an oak; other details from Samuel are not imported. News directed to idols remains an awkward source attribution. The narrator's final claim that the LORD slew Saul remains alongside the narrated bodily death. The translation neither erases this theological causation nor invents a second physical killing event. Familiar spirit retains the source divination category and is not reduced to an ordinary acquaintance.

## Parallel warrior accounts remain their own accounts
Jashobeam the Hachmonite and300 slain remain here, without replacing them by Adino, the Tachmonite or800 from another account. Pasdammim, barley and plural they in the defended plot remain. Joab's first ascent and repair of the remainder are explicit. Three of thirty, Abishai's three-versus-two comparison and exclusion from the first three preserve the source's potentially difficult roster structure. Benaiah kills two men likened to lions and separately an actual lion on a snowy day. The Egyptian's five-cubit stature is retained here without retroactively adding it to the earlier account. Supplied warrior names, affiliations, brothers and paired sons retain their local forms and relations.

## Reputation, risk and refusal
A name among the mighty denotes reputation while retaining the source name metaphor. David's comparison of the water to the men's blood remains a moral image tied to their risked lives; it does not become literal blood in the vessel. He pours it to the LORD rather than merely spilling it. Firstborn and first to ascend are rank or sequence, whereas troop counts and explicit membership are numerical.
