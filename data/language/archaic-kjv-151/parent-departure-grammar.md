## Personal purchase inside a plural-self oracle

The king's quotation32:3/5contains divine plural-I;the king is not himself adopting that identity.32:6–16and32:25use personal-I for Jeremiah's transaction and prayer.32:8also quotes Hanameel's personal I-pray-thee. The later divine response resumes plural-I.32:31,37,40and41keep my-plural anger,fury,face,fear,whole heart and whole soul. Those bodily and interior possessions remain alongside the plural-self speaker rather than being abstracted away.

## A signed deed is not a subscription

Purchase-deed is buy-write,the written title evidence32:10–16and32:44. Subscribe is expressed as writing one's name on the deed. Witnesses,sealing,open evidence,law,custom,weighed silver and balances are preserved. The earthen vessel stores both forms for many days. No modern paper format,duplicate-document procedure,mass conversion or purchase subscription is invented. Hanameel son of Shallum is the prophet's cousin;Baruch son of Neriah son of Maaseiah retains the explicit ancestry.

## Legal entitlement and fitting conduct are different

Right32:7/8is the existing entitlement noun for redemption and inheritance. Right34:15is fitting conduct in the divine sight,not the same entitlement and not anatomical right-side. Redemption is the redeem-event nominal. Court is the physical prison enclosure,not royal social life;mounts are siege mounds.33:13telleth enumerates animals,not stories.33:15execute means do judgment,not necessarily execution by killing. Meat offering33:18retains the established food-offering sense.

## Shared names need not share grammatical referents

33:16names Jerusalem with she and LORD our-one righteousness.23:6used he in its naming clause. The shared title is retained without forcing both clauses into one grammatical referent.33:17/18promise continuity both for David's throne and priestly Levites;the ritual offerings are not spiritualized away.33:20–26tie those promises to hypothetical day/night and cosmic conditions. The quoted claim that two families were rejected33:24remains an attributed objection rather than the narrator's conclusion.

## One heart is explicit unity,not a divine count

32:39gives one heart and one way to the people:both source cardinals remain. They do not count divine persons.32:1keeps tenth/eighteenth years;32:9seventeen shekels;32:18thousands as a plural magnitude.33:1second,33:7/11first and33:24two families retain their distinct roles.34:14keeps seven-year endpoint and six years of service without normalizing them.34:18twain is two portions of the calf,not a dual person pronoun.

## Return can restore or coerce

The return-causative is not intrinsically benevolent.32:37/44and33:7/11/26restore captives,while34:11/16force released servants back into subjection and34:22brings the besieging army back. Their explicit subjects and objects control the reading.34:9's release is limited to the source's Hebrew/Jewish kin category;the verse is not rewritten as an unprinted universal abolition law.34:16keeps at-their-pleasure with the released people.

## Liberty to catastrophe preserves the bitter wordplay

The same free-state nominal marks proclaimed liberty34:8/15/17and the deity's ironic liberty to sword,pestilence and famine34:17. Initial compliance34:10and subsequent reversal34:11both remain.32:18's fathers' iniquity in children's bosom stays beside the earlier sour-grape argument without harmonization.34:4/5keeps no sword-death and peace-death for Zedekiah beside the siege;funeral odours do not imply an invented cremation procedure. The calf-parts rite and corpse-as-food consequence remain unembellished source narration.
