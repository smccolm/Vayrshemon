## One household,one council,and a shared soul

35:6–11uses singular-we and our-one for the Rechabite household's inherited commitment. Wife,son and daughter nouns remain plural;singular-we does not reduce their membership.36:15–17uses the same communal resources for the deliberating officials,37:3for the petitioning people,37:9for their quoted false reassurance,and38:4/25for the officials' coercive demands.38:16retains the particularly awkward who-made-for-us-this-soul with singular-we and a singular soul. This does not assert that the text enumerates divine persons. The king's oath itself stays personal-I.

## Personal testimony and divine speech stay separate

35:3–5is the prophet's personal account.35:13–19switches to divine plural-I and my-plural possessions.36:2/3and31also use divine plural-I;36:5is Jeremiah's personal confinement,36:18Baruch's personal description of dictation and ink.37:7belongs to the deity being consulted,while37:14/18/20are the prophet's denial and petitions.38:14/16/19/25are the king's personal voice;38:15/20/21/26the prophet's. Nested quotations retain say-markers so that a king quoting officials or an oracle quoting a king does not flatten into one speaking self.

## A household rule under emergency displacement

35preserves the bans on wine,houses,sowing,vineyards and possession alongside tent residence and stranger status.35:11reports residence in Jerusalem because of both Chaldean and Syrian armies. No unprinted repeal or hypocrisy judgment is added to resolve the tension. The contrast with Judah concerns obedience to an ancestral command versus refusal to hear divine speech.35:19want means lack;the promise of a man standing before the deity is not assigned an invented priestly office.

## Writing objects and the destroyed scroll

Three explicitly derived senses enter the dictionary:ink is write-water,scroll-leaf is scroll-part,and penknife is pen-knife. The source leaves36:23are written portions of a roll,not plant leaves,a junior household page,or a claim about bound pagination. Roll uses the inherited written-scroll key rather than the rotation verb.36:18keeps oral dictation and ink;36:23keeps the unlabelled he who cuts,with later verses explicitly assigning the burning to the king.36:32restores all earlier words and adds many similar words. Neither addition nor restoration is erased.

## Named pairs,distributive fear,and exact quantities

36:19addresses Baruch and Jeremiah with a final dual-you;36:26uses dual-them for the same named fugitives hidden by the LORD. Other groups remain plural or communal-singular as their role requires.36:16both-one-and-other distributes fear over the officials;it does not claim they feared one another or identify exactly two.35:2and38:7retain one chamber and one eunuch;36:1/9/22retain fourth/fifth years and ninth month;36:23keeps three or four roll portions,not seven;36:28first is ordinal.38:10retains thirty rescuers as printed;38:14third names an entrance. No alternative numeral is imported.

## Prison,material care,and the accusation of defection

37:12keeps the opaque separate-himself-thence phrase rather than importing an inheritance transaction.37:13/14fall-away-to marks the accuser's claim of defection and the prophet's explicit denial;it is not narrator confirmation.37:15states that Jonathan's house was made a prison;37:16cabins are small chambers.37:21commit is put-in-custody,not the unrelated perform-an-act sense.38:11cast clouts are discarded cloth;38:12places cloth and rotten rags under arm-hollows beneath cords. Rescue out of mire leaves Jeremiah in the prison court.38:22echoes that mire in the women's accusation against the king without making his predicament literal mud.

## Uncomfortable conditions and concealed counsel

37:10retains the extreme hypothetical of a defeated army's wounded survivors rising to burn the city.38:15keeps both negative questions,including the awkward will-you-not-hear.38:17/18retain the contrasting surrender conditions;38:23keeps the king as cause of the city's burning.38:19's they/their remain open rather than naming an unprinted betrayer.38:26reports the prepared petition about avoiding Jonathan's house;38:27reports that the private matter was not perceived. No narrator approval or condemnation of the concealment is invented. Repeated capture wording38:28is retained.
