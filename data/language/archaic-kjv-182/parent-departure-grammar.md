## Complaint,reply and the community that refuses death

The prophet's complaint1:2–3uses personal-I and directly challenges divine inaction. The response1:5–6uses divine plural-I for the work and raising of the invading Chaldeans. No first-person form is inferred for every third-person divine mention. The address1:12retains my God/my Holy One and then communal singular-we for we shall not die. The source's we is not replaced by an alternative thou reading. The collective pronoun expresses the speaking community without specifying a population count or number of divine persons.

## Oppression and comparative righteousness

The law's slackness1:4means weakened force,not a detached rope. Judgment never going forth and wrong judgment proceeding remain deliberately opposed. The Chaldeans receive the divine commission but their judgment/dignity proceed from themselves1:7. The faces supping-up like east wind1:9retain an awkward image rather than a different marching-direction reading. The source changes from they to his mind/he/his god1:11;the grammar does not erase that shift. The prophet's challenge1:13retains both cannot look on iniquity and why look on treachery. The devoured man is more righteous than his oppressor,not declared absolutely sinless.

## Fishing implements as gods

Humans become sea-fish and creeping creatures1:14. Angle1:15uses the existing fish-hook entry rather than geometric angle. Drag receives a new pull-net compound,distinguishing it from the unqualified net while avoiding an invented net design. The invaders offer sacrifice to their net and incense to their dragnet1:16. Food remains food broadly;the text does not explicitly make the human captives their meat. The final question1:17retains net-emptying and continuing slaughter without answering it prematurely.

## The watching witness and the speaking vision

The watchtower witness2:1uses personal-I and retains when I am reproved. Writing tables2:2are tablets,not meal tables. The running reader is not assigned a courier identity. The vision's tarrying/not-tarrying2:3and future speaking remain the printed tension. The righteous living by his faith2:4retains his rather than an inserted later doctrinal paraphrase. The proud figure's wine,home and deathward appetite2:5are retained. Hell is the inherited underworld-depth image here,not automatically a final fiery punishment.

Knowledge of glory2:14does not collapse into glory alone.

## Questions to the deity and the celestial pair

The three questions3:8move from LORD to thy and retain rivers twice before sea. The bow3:9is a weapon and the difficult oaths of the tribes remain instead of alternative arrow or rod readings. The deep3:10has his voice and hands without becoming a newly named deity. The sun/moon dwelling3:11uses a genuine dual reference because those two bodies are explicitly coordinated. The later they went remains ordinary plural to avoid asserting a uniquely certain poetic antecedent. This dual is not a count of divine persons.

The witness's belly,lips,bones and inward trembling3:16are bodily vulnerability,not divine plural-I. The final he/people/them invasion clause remains awkward and unnamed.

## Joy without repaired circumstances

The personal-I resolve3:18follows these losses without a new prosperity promise. Hinds3:19are female deer. The final chief-singer direction and my stringed instruments remain part of the supplied text. The personal possessors and high-place wording are not silently transformed into a communal voice or an idolatrous shrine reference.
