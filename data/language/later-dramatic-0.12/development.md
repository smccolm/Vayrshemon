# Later dramatic Vayrshemon 0.12 — inherited clause flow

This is a later-language development from the pinned dramatic 0.11, informed by
the author-approved, definitive historical KJV 0.10-kjv.281. The historical text
stays sealed. A shared construction does not make the plays adopt the Bible's
deliberately foreign, archaic register. These are authored language decisions,
not claims of independently attested linguistic history.

## Additive clauses and recoverable continuation

The inherited independent conjunction remains available, particularly for
antiphonal replies, cumulative oaths, lists, rhetorical emphasis and performance.
An additive clause can instead place **’ã** (Roman **’oi**, analysis `:add`)
after its first complete phonological host. It preserves addition: it does not
mean “therefore”, “then”, or “but”. A speaker label or punctuation is not a host.
The host keeps case, number, possession, tense and force. The clause clitic is
outside these word endings. It can mark a licensed additive answer fragment.

Unmarked juxtaposition (`:cont`, no spoken segment) is licensed for a locally
recoverable same-actor sequence, with every original argument and verb retained.
It is not random deletion of “and”, not automatic subject omission, and not
permission to merge speakers. In this edition two explicitly reviewed narrative
sequences in Oberon's speech use it. Other cases retain an audible link.

## Relations and dependent roles

Inherited bound relational chains `:relpair` **’yë**, `:reltriple` **’yër**,
and `:relquad` **’yël** preserve two, three, or four analytical relation levels.
They follow the first host of the governed phrase and expand back to the same
left-edge relation stack. They are an available literary construction, not a
command to replace ordinary possessives. The two 0.11 play ledgers contain no
adjacent `of of` stacks; this edition therefore invents none to demonstrate them.
Distinct nested heads can retain ordinary genitive marking. A human-authored
scope analysis is required: shorter print alone does not validate a relation.

The dependent personal-pronoun allomorph `:dep` **’em** can distinguish an
oblique pronoun beside a free pronoun. It retains BOTH pronouns, their referents
and all number distinctions, including I(pl.) and we(sg.). It cannot attach to a
possessive chain or arbitrary noun. No such collision was found in either play.
The finite dependent-boundary clitic `:subend` **’eth** makes a clause boundary
audible where dependent and matrix predicates happen to be identical. It does
not erase either predicate. An author must establish that dependency first.

## Dramatic repetition is evidence

All 19 adjacent repeated-word/predicate cases found in the two prior ledgers are
retained. “No, no”, “write, write”, “pray, pray, pray”, “ever, ever”, “a truth's a
truth”, and “which is which” have semantic or dramatic jobs. The fairies' three
“And I” replies, Titania's “for her sake” parallelism, Hermia's cumulative oath,
Quince's stilted instructions, Bottom's doggerel and Robin's closing appeal keep
their deliberate patterns. Twenty explicit source-unit decisions replace only
clause linkage. There is no random synonym cycling and no global deduplication.

## Vocabulary, identity and typography

Six inherited bound grammatical forms are added to the versioned dictionary;
there are no new lexical roots and no changes to existing lexical forms. The
zero continuation is a construction, not a seventh dictionary word. All 0.11
scope labels, native vocabulary, literary exceptions, four first-person voices,
pronoun choices, wordplay decisions and exclamatory grammar remain in force.
Maxima follows actual sentence/speaker boundaries, never page breaks. The two
script/reading columns retain byte-identical legacy-slot text. Source English,
source-unit order, line ranges, continuation metadata and quantities are exact.

## Validation limits

Both plays are complete; every source unit is checked and newly laid out with
the approved sizes, geometry, paint, leading and paragraph alignment. The delta
lists the exact changed windows and reasons. Structural checks and translator
self-review do not constitute independent literary certification or recovery of
all Shakespearean metre and wordplay.
