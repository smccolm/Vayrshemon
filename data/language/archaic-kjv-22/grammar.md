# Historical KJV 0.10-kjv.22 · disputed voices and burdens of a whole

Numbers 11–16 adds 220 authored verses to the pinned v21 register. The whole Bible remains incomplete. Earlier targets and frozen Jonah stay unchanged. This is a deliberately awkward historical register, permanently excluded from modernization; translator self-review is not independent certification.

## Plural-self, whole community, pair and several witnesses

The deity claims plural-I, my-plural and plural-self without supplying a numerical count of divine persons. Moses's private exhaustion in 11:11–15 is singular-I, while the embedded hungry community says singular-we. In 11:21 Moses quotes the deity: the speaker changes inside the sentence, and the quoted I is plural-self. LORD's third-person hand followed by my-plural word in 11:23 preserves the source's shift.

Aaron and Miriam are a named pair: their challenge and shared fault use the inherited productive dual in 12:2,11. Aaron's I beseech is his individual singular-I. The three called in 12:4 are Moses, Aaron and Miriam, not a Trinity count. The spies' several witnesses use plural-we in 13:27–29,31–33; Caleb recruits the community as singular-we in 13:30. Joshua/Caleb's shared observation is dual in 14:7, then the beneficiary nation is singular-we in 14:8–9. Dathan/Abiram refuse as a pair in 16:12–14, whereas the fleeing assembly fears for singular-we in 16:34.

These are intentional readings of discourse roles, not claims of pronoun rows absent from the original chart. They do not introduce a psychiatric diagnosis, extra gods or a fixed count of selves.

## Distributed spirit does not erase individual agents

Seventy elders share the burden after the deity takes of the spirit upon Moses. The translation does not say all of Moses's spirit is removed. Eldad and Medad are among those written/enrolled, so seventy is not silently increased to seventy-two. The supplied KJV did not cease is retained rather than replaced by a different textual tradition's did not do so again. Prophecy here means inspired utterance, not exclusively future prediction. Moses's emphatic wish that all the people were prophets uses the inherited exclamatory/optative endings; it is not a completed fact and requires no unsupported exclamation-mark glyph.

Moses retains conceive, beget and nursing-father imagery. Carrying the people's burden uses carry, while enduring the murmuring in 14:27 uses endure; the birth verb is not used for either. The severe kill-me request remains the quoted character's anguish.

## Appetite, bodily images and asymmetric consequences

The complaint's freely eaten food in Egypt is the speakers' remembered assertion, not a denial of prior slavery. The whole speaks of one soul and plural eyes. Food lust is a contextual appetite sense. Manna is ground in grinding vessels, not building mortar. Quails extend about a day's journey on either side and two cubits high; the source does not resolve pile depth versus flying height, so neither is invented. Least gathering is ten homers, not omers and not the group total. Plague begins before the flesh is chewed; no medical mechanism is added.

Miriam's Ethiopian designation remains without identifying her with a named woman or modern nationality. Only she is described as leprous; Aaron's matching punishment is not invented. The stillbirth/half-consumed image and paternal-spitting analogy remain. Seven-day exclusion is followed by readmission, and the whole people wait for her return. Mouth-to-mouth divine speech and beholding the LORD's likeness remain beside other source visibility claims without harmonization.

## Witnesses can disagree and exaggerate

Each spy and father retains the local pairing. Oshea precedes the explicit naming as Jehoshua; the latter is not normalized to Joshua everywhere. Zin and Sin remain distinct. Eshcol the brook is distinguished from the earlier person. The source's southward wording, Hebron/Zoan seven-year comparison and one cluster between two carriers remain. Fat/lean land is agricultural bodily metaphor. Enemy size, land eating inhabitants and the claim about how enemies see the witnesses are attributed testimony, including its epistemic leaps. Caleb's confidence and the others' fear remain conflicting claims.

## Pardon can coexist with exclusion and inherited burden

The community's two counterfactual death wishes take exclamatory optative past-perfect forms. Moses's argument imagines what Egyptians and other nations would say if the deity killed all as one man; the allegation that LORD could not bring them in is nested hypothetical speech, not narrator fact. The divine plural-I declares pardon, then excludes the counted murmuring generation. Caleb and Joshua are named exceptions; children feared to become prey will enter, yet carry their parents' burden for forty years.

Forty reconnaissance days correspond to forty years. Third/fourth generations and ten temptations retain the source counts, without invented event lists. My breach of promise in 14:34 is preserved as the deity's uncomfortable first-person claim; it is not silently replaced with an alternative rendering about opposition. Whoredoms retains sexual-unfaithfulness metaphor without adding a literal sexual event. The later ascent violates the new direction; neither Moses nor ark joins it.

## Proportions belong to each kind of gift

Numbers 15 distinguishes lamb, ram and bullock accompaniments. Flour: one tenth, two tenths and three tenths respectively; oil/wine: one quarter, one third and one half hin respectively. No unstated metric conversion or flour unit is inserted. The per-animal instruction scales these schedules without combining fractions as counts of animals. Meat offering is grain food here, not flesh. First dough is presented as a lifted gift, with the threshing-floor comparison retained.

The same ordinance explicitly includes the sojourner in its stated domain. This does not erase every social distinction elsewhere in the source. The implied promise of future settlement remains after the preceding generation's exclusion.

## Ignorance differs from defiance, custody from execution

The collective unknown offence uses a bullock and kid with their stated accompaniments; the individual ignorant offence uses a first-year female goat. These are retained beside earlier Leviticus rules without silently substituting a harmonized schedule. Presumptuous wrongdoing has another consequence. Ought means anything, not should.

The Sabbath wood-gatherer is held in custody because the action is not yet declared. Ward is not a legal dependent, and sticks are pieces of wood, not adhering. The subsequent stoning and death are explicit narrated acts. No emergency motive or excuse is invented. Garment fringes and blue ribbon function as visible remembrance; no knot count or later practice is imported. The instruction distrusts heart and eyes using the source's sexualized disloyalty metaphor. The final divine self-identification is repeated.

## An accusation can reverse the promised-land rhetoric

Korah the Levite and Eliab the Reubenite are distinguished from earlier homonymous bearers; On is a person here, not the city. The rebels accuse Moses/Aaron of taking too much, and Moses returns their phrase. Levitical service does not already equal priesthood. Dathan/Abiram describe Egypt as milk-and-honey land and ask whether Moses will put out men's eyes: the inversion and bodily question remain, without a forced paraphrase. Their dual refusal is not the entire nation's consent.

Moses's no-ass/no-injury claim is his personal defence. He sets an explicit negative test: ordinary death would mean LORD had not sent him. The source's extraordinary alternative remains attributed and narrated, not presented as a modern empirical claim by the translator.

## One offender, assembly liability and material remembrance

The mediators ask whether one man's sin warrants wrath against the whole assembly. The deity's plural-I threatens consumption, while the human pair intercedes. Families and little children remain in the scene, not deleted to soften the account. Earth opens her mouth and swallows people alive; quick means alive, not rapidly. Earth deaths, the 250 incense-presenters' fire deaths and 14,700 later plague deaths are separate groups. The last total explicitly excludes the Korah affair; no invented casualty total for swallowed households is added.

The censers remain hallowed despite their owners' condemnation and become broad altar-cover plates. Stranger in the memorial is specifically non-Aaronic descent, not automatically foreign nationality. A later complaint accuses Moses/Aaron of killing LORD's people. Aaron runs into the already-started plague and stands between living and dead; stayed means halted, not resided. The repeated ending is not a second plague.
