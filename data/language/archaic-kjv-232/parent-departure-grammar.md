# Acts23–25: competing corporate voices and the limits of a hearing

## Several singular communities, not one universal we

The Pharisee scribes' we-find-no-evil and let-us-not-fight-God in23:9 use singular-we for the defending party. The spirit-or-angel clause remains conditional. The conspirators' we-bound-ourselves and we-ready-to-kill in23:14–15 use the same number class for a different pledged faction. Shared grammar does not imply shared beliefs,innocence or membership.

Tertullus's we in24:2–8 speaks for the accusing delegation. His personal I-beg in24:4 asks that the governor hear us,the singular-whole party. Our-law and our-hands are that delegation's possession. Festus's us in25:24 instead groups the people present in the hearing. Source frames and speech boundaries identify the referent;the language does not need a separate inflection for every assembly.

Agrippa and Bernice are a concrete two-person visiting pair in25:14,so they-dual is appropriate. The procession in25:23 also includes chief captains and principal city men,so it is not indiscriminately marked dual. Two centurions in23:23 is an explicit object count,not a theological analogy.

## Personal witnesses and the Lord's plural-self

Paul's I-lived-in-good-conscience,I-did-not-know,I-am-a-Pharisee and I-am-questioned remain personal claims. His not-knowing that Ananias is the high priest is not explained away by an invented eyesight problem,irony or hidden motive. Ananias the priest is contextually distinct from the earlier property seller and Damascus disciple.

In23:11 the Lord's about-me is plural-self within the encouragement to Paul. The Lord's night appearance and the command to witness also at Rome remain. There is no other printed divine first person in this batch. The centurion's me in23:18,the captain's me in23:19/22 and I clauses in his letter,the governor's I-hear,and each official's later I retain personal number.

The official letter in23:27 claims rescue after understanding that the prisoner was Roman. Earlier22:29 retains the captain's later realization unchanged. The translator does not rewrite either scene to harmonize the captain's self-account with the narrative. Festus's25:20 claim of uncertainty likewise remains beside25:9's narrator description of favour-seeking. A speaker's account is not automatically the narrator's certified truth.

## Denials, allegations and the hearing's limits

The Sadducee denial names resurrection,angel and spirit,while the supplied Pharisee response says both. Both remains the source's grouping expression;no extra cardinal two or rewritten doctrine list resolves the seam. The full23:9 fight-not-against-God clause remains.

The conspirators'23:12/21 oath forbids both eating and drinking until the killing. More than forty is a threshold,not exactly forty. Their desired further inquiry is marked as pretence. Paul's nephew is identified only by sister-son relation;neither his nor his mother's name is invented. The secrecy instruction remains tell-no-man.

Tertullus's pestilent and sect-of-Nazarenes accusations remain attributed speech. His claims of attempted temple profanity and intended judgment according to their law do not retroactively make the earlier temple-entry supposition true. The complete24:6–8 passage about Lysias's violence and summoning of accusers is retained. Its relative whom is not expanded into a named referent where the supplied sentence leaves that linkage less explicit.

Paul's24:12–13 denies the alleged disputing/stirring at the temple,synagogues and city,and denies that the present accusations can be proved. His24:18 account says purified,without multitude and without tumult. Resurrection in24:15 covers righteous and unrighteous;no class is removed. His conscience discipline toward God and people remains effort,not a claim that his mere assertion decides the hearing.

## Judicial appeal and imperial address

The new judgment-ask verb marks a formal judicial appeal in25:11–12/21/25. It is distinct from a request for pity or attractiveness. Paul's if-I-am-death-worthy-I-do-not-refuse-to-die and if-the-charges-are-absent-no-one-may-deliver-me remain two conditions,followed by the appeal. Festus's consultation and announced destination do not invent an acquittal or conviction.

The25:16 official statement retains the supplied to-die wording and face-to-face opportunity to answer accusers. It is presented as the Roman practice Festus claims,not a modern legal advisory. His lack of a certain report in25:26 and reluctance to send a prisoner without specifying allegations in25:27 are kept distinct from finding nothing worthy of death.

Augustus is recorded as the imperial designation used here,without identifying that title-holder with Luke2's named Augustus. My-lord in25:26 addresses a human imperial superior and therefore uses personal my,not divine my-plural. Caesar's earlier established ruler designation remains available without an inserted personal imperial name.

## New vocabulary and contextual names

Four common entries are added. Judicial appeal is judgment+ask. Temperance is self+restrain,a broad self-control term rather than an alcohol-only movement or bodily temperature. Civic providence is before+care,Tertullus's flattering account of an official's foresight rather than divine predestination. Superstition is god+fear,the religious-concern noun in Festus's distant account. It relates to the earlier adjective but does not itself insert an unstated too or turn the translator into the speaker making the judgment.

Fourteen contextual proper entries distinguish priest Ananias,Felix,Claudius Lysias and his shortened Lysias,Antipatris,Tertullus,the Nazarene party,Drusilla,Porcius Festus and his shortened Festus,Agrippa,Bernice,the Augustus designation,and the Herod attribution of the judgment hall. Claudius Lysias is not the emperor Claudius. Bernice receives no unprinted queen or sister title. The hall's Herod does not force a merger with an earlier namesake. Full and shortened official names are explicitly linked as source forms,not counted as new persons.

Several existing words require careful sense selection. Noble in24:3 is the rank honorific,not the newly recorded Berean disposition. Prove in24:13/25:7 is the established demonstrative verb. Both in23:8 is the source grouping word. Exercise in24:16 is disciplined striving rather than physical drill. Felix's room in24:27 is succession in office rather than a chamber. The body imprisonment bands use the restraint noun,while the army and escort are groups. Grievous allegations remain serious complaints,not evidence of guilt.
