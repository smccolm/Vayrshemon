# Ezekiel29–32: river-maker claims and underworld lament

## 1. Divine and royal plural-self without collapsed attribution
Personal-I belongs to the prophet's word-reception dates and narration. Divine speech uses plural-I and my-plural. Pharaoh's quoted river-maker boast in29:3 and29:9 also adopts the exalted civic plural-self;29:3's explicit myself uses the plural-self reflexive. The shared morphology does not certify the king's claim or merge him with the deity opposing him. The formula and quotation boundaries carry the attribution. No singular-we or dual is inserted where the source gives none;future Trinitarian passages remain untranslated.

## 2. Dragon,reed and unpaid service
29:3's dragon follows the historical source label,while32:2 distinctly says whale. Neither is silently modernized to crocodile. Body scales differ from balance scales.29:7 retains by-thy-hand and loins-at-a-stand rather than substituting a different reading. Meat in29:5 is food. Base/basest kingdom is low political standing. Forty years repeats in29:11–13.29:18's no wages for the Tyrus service remains beside earlier siege predictions;29:19–20 explicitly makes Egypt the compensating wage and says they worked for the divine plural-self.

## 3. Egypt's cities and injured arms
Tower of Syene is retained as printed. Libya,Lydia,Chub,Sin,Aven,Pibeseth and Tehaphnehes receive contextual source-name entries,without modern identifications or name harmonization. No and Sin remain place-names rather than a negation and moral fault. Woe-worth-the-day becomes woe-to-the-day using exclamatory morphology. Careless Ethiopians are without apprehension,not newly accused of negligence. Roller is a binding bandage;the new cloth-bind noun distinguishes it from a wheel or machine. The strong arm and already-broken arm remain distinct without an added cardinal or body-part dual pronoun. The human king extends the sword supplied by the divine speaker.

## 4. Assyrian cedar and shifting person
Chapter31 begins with the Assyrian comparator and ends by explicitly identifying Pharaoh and his multitude. Both remain. Shroud is a shading canopy,not a burial wrapper;shot-forth is plant growth,not a projectile. Chestnut follows the supplied name.31:10 switches thou to he without correction. The mighty one in31:11 is a person rather than a counted one.31:6's bear is actual birth;the same verb is excluded from shame-bearing in32. The repeated branch,water and shade clauses carry the image's accumulation without artificial synonym rotation.

## 5. Lower earth and consolation among the dead
Grave in31:15 is burial language. The hell passages use the inherited deep-underworld semantic entry,not an added doctrine of fiery final punishment. The trees' comfort below in31:16 and Pharaoh's comfort among the dead in32:31 remain deliberately unsettling.31:14 keeps its awkward all-that-drink-water attachment and negative tree-exaltation clauses.31:17's his-arm image is not expanded into a named alliance. The translation preserves the source's deathward geography without importing a later cosmology.

## 6. Cosmic waters,weapon-display and difficult agency
32:5 retains thy height filling valleys rather than replacing it with bodies.32:7 retains put-thee-out and the moon's feminine possessive.32:10 gains weapon-shake for brandish,a threatening display rather than an actual strike. Disturbed waters are stirred,not psychologically distressed;32:14 makes them deep,not silently clear,and rivers run like oil. The final32:32 explicitly gives the terror to divine plural-self,my-plural terror;the translation does not transfer responsibility to Pharaoh. Repeated recognition formulas remain after their stated acts.

## 7. Civic gender and the uncorrected negative
The underworld catalogue keeps its her/his/him shifts for Asshur,Elam,Meshech and Tubal. The two named peoples do not license an inserted dual where the source instead has civic singular reference.32:27 says they shall NOT lie with the fallen mighty;that negative is not reversed to match an alternate reading. Weapons under heads and iniquities on bones remain. Borne shame is endure,not birth. Every-man fear in32:10 is distributive and does not become a communal singular-we.

## 8. Exact scope and evidence
These97verses add two common semantic compounds and eight contextual proper-name entries,with no new inflectional endings. Dates remain29:1[10,10,12],29:17[27,1,1],30:20[11,1,7],31:1[11,3,1],32:1[12,12,1],32:17[12,15]. No month number is supplied in32:17. Forty-year clauses are separately checked. Every verse has source anchors,back-reading and a note;all inherited targets remain unchanged. This is translator self-review and structural validation,not independent literary certification. Modern0.10 and frozen Jonah remain untouched. The entire Bible is still incomplete.
