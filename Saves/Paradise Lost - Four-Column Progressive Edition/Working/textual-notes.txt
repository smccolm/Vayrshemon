The supplied English remains the source column, including long s, historical
spelling and its apparent transcription errors. Original verse line breaks are
native soft line separators (U+2028), so a group of verse lines remains one
aligned passage rather than becoming a different number of paragraphs in each
column. The ledger also keeps each original substring and its source offsets.
The target rendition follows the thought and imagery; it does not claim to
reproduce Milton's English metre. No missing or unattempted passage is replaced
by a summary or an ellipsis. The ledger distinguishes completed work from the
remaining source. A status of incomplete means that later books are pending.

Names and senses

Biblical people, places and divine names inherit the existing language entries
by referent. Beelzebub, Molech, Chemosh, Ashtoreth, Tammuz, Dagon, the covenant
ark, and the common noun for one's allotted portion are distinguished explicitly.
Adonis the river is separate from Adonis the person. Titan in the genealogy is
not Titan as a poetic name for the sun. The Dorian musical mode is separate from
Doric architecture. Mammon here is a personified spirit. The catalogue's claims,
the narrator's judgement of the Mulciber myth, and Satan's claims remain those
of their speakers. The Gibeah passage retains rape explicitly.

Unta

The book heading's Roman numeral uses a native numeral. Spelled quantities,
ordinals, fractions and multiples use Unta words; plural millions remain plural
millions, without inventing an exact total. The number ledger records each
authored numeric construction. Firstborn, head-first and demigod use the same
ordinal or fractional roots in their derived expressions.

Build

.venv/Scripts/python -X utf8 -m tools.paradise_lost_working --compose

This command compiles authored analyses only. It does not translate the pending
books automatically. It verifies an uninterrupted source prefix, all four
saved columns, save/reopen identity, aligned paragraph starts, glyph coverage,
page bounds, and folio placement in both reading modes. A manually edited
publication cannot be overwritten while its hash differs from the layout receipt.

childbirth and rape, the hounds' feeding, and the threatening exchanges remain
explicit. Death, Sin, Night and the other personified abstractions retain native
roots; established classical and biblical names reuse existing readings.

The poem's Alchymie denotes the sounding trumpet metal; the goal in the games
is a chariot turning mark; the Furies are the named avenging beings rather than
ordinary anger. The twice-mentioned ten-thousand scale keeps fathoms in the
fall and miles in the rebound; the source does not give the same unit twice.
Numeral II in the heading remains a numeral. Written quantities and multiples,
including ninefold, three times threefold, tenfold, thirds and halves, use Unta
words. Source ranges and authored number readings remain in the ledgers.

The clear drop and suffusion retain Milton's historical descriptions of
blindness. Foreknowledge is distinguished from predestination; satisfaction
means meeting justice's demand rather than pleasure. The Son's offered death,
the forfeiture, punishment, resurrection, judgment and destruction remain
explicit. The poem's religious satire is retained without added qualification.
Indulgences here are ecclesiastical remissions of punishment; dispensations
are exemptions from obligations, and bulls are sealed papal letters. Habits
in this inventory are clothes, not repeated behaviors. The painter's pencil
is a paintbrush. The alchemical Hermes and Proteus, volatile substance, alembic,
elixir and potable gold retain their concrete imagery. Trepidation denotes the
older cosmology's supposed oscillation; quintessence is the heavenly substance
distinguished from the four lower elements.

Biblical references reuse the inherited identities and vocabulary, including
Adam, Peter the disciple, John the Revelation witness, Aaron, Jacob, Esau,
Shinar, Padanaram, Luz, Beersheba and Golgotha. Uriel the angel is separately
keyed from human biblical namesakes. Maeonides remains the poem's epithet for
Homer. The source's alternate geographic spellings retain their referents.

Unta III remains a numeral in the book heading. Firstborn, second root, seven
planets and spirits, twelve breastplate stones, thrice-happy isles, ten thousand
leagues and the three-form lunar face use native word constructions. "At once"
meaning together and "once" meaning formerly are distinguished from counted
occurrences. Neither the lunar hemisphere nor quintessence adds a separate
numeral absent from the source wording.

These readings restore the words for translation without adding explanatory

identity in the Pandora passage is separately keyed from the biblical person.
The native spelling inherits Japheth's established biblical form. Uzziel and
Zephon likewise have separate angel identities with inherited name forms.
Seleucia the royal city is distinct from the biblical port. Sabaean denotes
Sheba's country. May is the month, and the flower-de-luce is the iris flower.

Animal spirits retain the historical physiological meaning, life-bearing
vapours. Tempering the sun's heat is moderation, not the hardening of metal.
Lot in Eve's condition and Gabriel's final speech means an allotted fate;
Uriel's assignment by lot retains the distinct lot-casting sense. Both use
the inherited native vocabulary. The naked bodies, marital intimacy,
criticism of abstinence, physiological dream imagery and explosive-powder
comparison are translated without omissions. Satan's justifications remain
his speech, with the narrator's description of a tyrant's plea separately
preserved. The alternative motions of sun and earth remain alternatives.

IV is an Unta numeral in the heading. Written quantities, including the
watch's halves, sevenfold wrath, doubled strength and the two balance weights,
use Unta word constructions. The final balance distinguishes departure from
battle; Satan's side rises because it is lighter. Long passages were divided
at authored boundaries for the facing spread without removing target words.

Eve's dream is fully related, including the tasting and flight, then explicitly
distinguished from a waking transgression. Adam's account of fancy and reason
retains that distinction. The central speech distinguishes bodily nourishment,
vital and animal spirits, the intellect, discursive reasoning and intuition.
Animal spirits retain the historical physiological sense used in Book IV.
The angels' eating is actual eating in the narration, not merely an appearance;
digestion, transubstantiation and exhalation all remain explicit. Nice means
fastidious about food; ingrateful food means unpleasing, not morally ungrateful.
Eve serves naked, and the narrator's comment about love and jealousy is intact.

The Virtues and Powers remain distinct named orders. Regency here is an angelic
realm, not temporary government. Longitude in the unfolded earth comparison
means extension in length. Cream is made from pressed kernels, and must is
fresh grape juice. The berry drink is not silently given an added recipe.
The royal grooms are attendants, and the lower wings skirt the body as clothing.
The son of Maia remains that relational description; no extra name is inserted.
Raphael, Abdiel, Tobias, Galileo and the mythic names have separately recorded
identities. Abdiel's native name inherits the biblical spelling without merging
him with the human namesake. Mary is the mother of Yeshūa; the source does not
name him in this passage, so the translation does not add his personal name.

The Father's irony about defending the heavenly throne, Satan's claims to
freedom and self-creation, and Abdiel's rejection of those claims remain their
respective speeches. The threats, punishment and uncreation are not softened.
V is a numeral in the heading. Written halves, thirds, doubles, six wings,
the five wandering fires, the four-element grouping, seven marriages and
the ten-thousand-thousand ensigns use Unta words. Indefinite myriads retain
their indefinite sense. All stages of the revolt and Abdiel's departure remain.


Book VI
-------
All 912 verse lines, the full Argument, repeated title and colophon are
translated. English preserves the supplied wording and physical verse breaks.
The 1674 text at https://www.jacklynch.net/Texts/pl6.html was used to check
obvious damaged readings, not to replace the witness in the English column:
4745 sight = fight; 4753 alded = aided; 4877 ro assert = to assert;
4919 feat = seat; 5021 add rest = addressed/prepared;
5091 Adraenelec = Adramelec; 5234 Some o•• = Some one;
5275 gripe first = gripe fast, grasp firmly; 5276 poor = pour.
Alt (5263) means halt. Realtie (4832) means sincerity/honesty, not real estate;
the period reading is retained in English and rendered honesty in the target.
Reins in the anatomical list are kidneys; the later reins of war are bridle
reins. Grateful truce is welcome/pleasing. Unobnoxious means not liable to pain.

Every phase of the three-day battle remains. The first sword wound, the red
nectarous fluid, the body-organ list, angels' self-formed limbs and the rebels'
other wounds are explicit. Spiritual survival except by annihilation is not
confused with merely surviving a particular injury. The night council,
artillery materials and manufacture, firing, and counterattack with uprooted
mountains are translated as narrated, with no gaps or substituted summaries.
The supplied speaker's claims of freedom, invulnerability and divine
fallibility remain that speaker's claims. No modern recipe or commentary is
inserted. The source's historical substance names retain their source senses.

The heavy terms and hard contents retain the diplomacy/artillery wordplay.
Belial's understand/stand-under pun is rendered by a transparent native
under-stand compound, keyed separately from ordinary understanding. His
upright is physical standing as well as a moral taunt. The proposed overture
remains an opening offer; the opened ranks and gun mouths retain the scene's
second meaning. The double sense of discharge is preserved in context.

Adramelec inherits the sealed biblical Adrammelech spelling, separately keyed
as the rebel angel. Ariel and Arioc likewise inherit biblical name spellings
without merging their identities with human namesakes. Uriel, Raphael, Abdiel,
Moloch and Belial retain the previous readings; Zophiel and Ramiel are separately
localized. Asmadai has an adapted form related to the earlier Asmodeus name.
The fiery chariot's Urim, beryl, sapphire, amber appearance, firmament and four
cherubic shapes use established biblical vocabulary.

Ten paces and the tenth, one third, halves, two battle days and the third day,
four faces on each of four shapes, three-bolted thunder, ten thousand thunders,
ten million saints, twenty thousand chariots and the nine-day fall use Unta
words because the source writes words. VI alone is a heading numeral. The
Messiah holds back more than half his strength, expels rather than annihilates
the rebels, and the final warning to Adam and his weaker companion is complete.


Book VII — complete source reading and vocabulary review
Every source word, the Argument, all 640 verse lines, repeated title and colophon are retained. The original English column is verbatim, including defects. The following readings affect the target only: physical line 5781 thought -> though; 5841 ray self -> myself; 5960 Warers -> Waters; 5990 bate -> bare; 6095 spouts our -> spouts out; 6109 Crane• -> Crane; 6130 Martin -> Mattin (morning song); 6131 Foul living -> Soul living; 6269 be also went -> he also went. The latter living-soul phrase follows the creation context and Genesis 1:24. Primary comparison: Milton, 1674 second-edition text, https://www.jacklynch.net/Texts/pl7.html.

The supplied smelling Gourd at 5998 is retained as smelling, not silently modernized to swelling; it is also the reading in the consulted 1674 transcription. The gourd uses the prior poem’s edible-plant term, not Jonah’s shade-plant. A conservative number reading keeps the thousand and thousand thousand as number words, alongside all six ordinal days and the seventh-day rest.

Urania is the heavenly inspiration explicitly distinguished from the classical nine Muses. Lucifer shares the sealed biblical name but has a separate poetic identity. Rhodope the mountain is not Rhodophe the woman. Pleiades uses the sealed celestial name. The compasses are the two-legged drawing instrument, not a navigational compass. The Son’s filial Godhead and the Father’s omnipresence remain distinct expressions.

Genesis phrases use established scriptural roots and, where applicable, their saved constructions: light, the firmament, gathered waters, grass and fruit trees, luminaries, fishes and birds, animals, human formation and the prohibition. Milton’s additions and changes are translated in full, not replaced by the biblical passage. Raphael says the Father addresses his Son, so the explicit plural let us/our is retained.

The animal catalogue retains seals, dolphins, Leviathan’s historical gills and spouting trunk, full bird plumage, formation and migration, nightingale and swan, cock and the unnamed rainbow-tailed bird, lion, ounce, leopard, tiger, mole, stag, Behemoth, river-horse, crocodile, insects, serpents, ant, female bee feeding her husband drone, and the serpent’s brazen eyes and hairy mane. No modern zoological correction replaces the poem’s claims. Behemoth and the river-horse remain distinct labels. The later serpent is obedient and harmless to Adam at this point.

The six-day work, seventh-day rest with unceasing music, possible inhabited stars, and thrice-happy conditional praise all remain. The closing invitation allows further questions within human measure. Physical verse line breaks are retained; translated syntax can continue across a physical line boundary.
