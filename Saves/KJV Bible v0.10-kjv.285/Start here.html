The two native Bibles contain the same Vayrshemon for all 31,102 verses. The four-column edition has 22,130 pages. The compact edition has 1,914 pages on 957 duplex sheets.

In Revelation 1:8, 1:11, 21:6 and 22:13, Alpha and Omega read neksus’t īvden and hüŧh’na īvden, the first and last written signs of the source alphabet. The adjective hüŧh’na comes from the chart root for “end.” The KJV’s separate “beginning and end” remains a separate expression.
