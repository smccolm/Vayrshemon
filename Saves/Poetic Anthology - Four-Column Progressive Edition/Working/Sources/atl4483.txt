She does not sorrow
over the dirty parquet floor,
as I would,
or the piles of laundry
to put away
or the chaos in her kitchen,
as I would,
for she is not pinioned there,
as I am (if i don't struggle).
The hub of her universe
is located
not in her clean house
but in ideas about being-in-the-world,
about God and man,
Marxism and socialism,
society and herself,
how to see these things lined up
somehow
or served on a paperback platter,
i shall go on an eating spree.
