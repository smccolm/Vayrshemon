Bismi l-Lāhi al-Raḥmāni al-Raḥīmi
Ñiŋ mu peki-pekiro suukuwo le ti n-te Baafode Jaabi ye miŋ ke fanna feŋoolu bee ye.
Bisim ilāhi wo niŋ al-Raḥmāni barakoola
wo niŋ al-Raḥīmi ŋa ñiŋ dookuwoo foloo wo le la
Al-ḥamdu li l-Lāhi tentoo ñanta Mansa ye miŋ
tentoo te baŋ noo la neemoo kaŋ ye miŋ fuu la
Ŋee daani Maariyo salaatu la miŋ se loo kuri ke
wo niŋ salaamu Muhammadu naa la aaloola
Wo tambi koo la n-be Mandinkoolu bee kili la
dookuwoola miŋ ñanta niŋ n-bee lafita kayiroo la
Kuŋ loo le laata n-na moofiŋ duu ye kene baa to
miŋ ye n-tu kooma n-na koyi mansoolu feeroo la
Ŋa a je le ko moo koyi kafoo loo tee la kaŋo la biriŋ
kunuŋ ka naa bii wo niŋ jankoo ye la aadoo la
Wo le ye n-te peki-peki wo niŋ ñoŋ doolu mi nu yaa je
tentoo be jeerindilaa Mansoo ye ñiŋ kuwo la
Niŋ n-kafuta ñooma ŋa saferoo ke n-na kaŋo la biriŋ
londoo la yiriwa wo niŋ fahamoo si taa ñoo la
Wo le yaa ke keeñaa le ka n-sewandi miŋ fasaroo
ke ta n-ñe Mandinka la n-ka a yeeti hakiloo la
Feŋ maa ke fo kuma-koyindoo niŋ daliilu si naa
mantoolu kaŋ ñoŋ moyoo niŋ batoo si taa ñoo la
Duŋ ñoo to niŋ jakaloo dunta n-na kuwoolu kono
ñiŋ wandi kaŋ nooma naa aadoolu saaboo la
Diŋ maŋ malu a faa la ka a ñaa bee kuya ma waraŋ
kaa faŋ ye moo-moo te ñiŋ peroo la kuwoo noo la
Wo ye kaa la halakoo wo naa faama la nimisa kesoo
hanaa si arakaaŋoolu wili-wili ke a te boo noo la
Balaŋo la tooroo wo niŋ baaloo la junube siyaa
wo niŋ barada la dana tamboo te foo noo la
Tubaabu maanoo wo naa tulu kaba ka taa sukuroo
wolu ye n-ñinandi n-na seloo niŋ daramboo la
Luŋ miŋ siloo sukita ñiŋ bori feŋoolu ñaato i nii
kuŋ feŋoolu muumee wo luŋ domoro te soto noo la
Amunta miŋ jikita kami kililaa ka nimisa ñamiŋ
saŋ miŋ wuloo janita naa sii taa be kumboo la
Londoo sotoo maŋ diyaa sako wandi kaŋ na aduŋ
moo naa la kaŋo naa la aadoo wolu te fatanoo la
“N-suusuuta kanna” wo niŋ n-ŋaa moyi te kaañaanna
hani n-si jamanoolu wili-wili ke a te keenoo la
Mooyaa te ñiiñaa la niŋ londoo ti jee to aduŋ
lonna fanaŋ filita naa mee jankuu diinoo la
Ñiŋ kaŋ safe benta hani mansoolu maa taa bii
bari n-si diinoo wo niŋ sooloo loŋ noo la
Naa mee ta fo ninsiree Maariyoo si baraka ke jee
n-sin janku minna buray foroyaalu faano la
Moo doolu saa jiki ko ñiŋ baaroo mu filiti waraŋ
diina la tiñaa mu ye miŋ safee naŋ Araboo la
Kee danku kaatu n-ka Mariyoo daani miŋ yeŋ daa
fo n-kana ke yelemandiroo moo ti waraŋ fuu la
Fo i maa jee kiilaalu faŋ kiita i la moolu la kaŋo
damaa la kaa moyita ñamiŋ Alkuraanoo la
Neemoo wo niŋ kiisoo be kiilaalu be ye wo niŋ
mantoolu mumee ka taa bula yawma loo luŋo la
Muna n-te kaŋ-kaŋoolu niŋ seeloolu topotoo la
fo n-si mbaluu n-faŋ ye dookuwoo niŋ halaaloo la
Aniŋ ka kisi-kisiri ke fo mbee si kese bondi
mooyaalu feeto ka taa bula dinaa topotoo la
Moo bee ye nimisa ka tuuboo kalisa Mariyoo yeŋ
kaa daani suutoo wo niŋ tilitoo ala yanfoo la
Faa si n-so kambeŋo wo niŋ tee-siti la bankoo kaŋ
moo bee si naa a tentu miŋ kayiroo la yiriwoo la
N-ŋee daani Mariyoo salaatu la miŋ si lookuri ke
wo niŋ salaamu Muhammad naa la aaloola
