Unas! You did not go away dead. You went away alive to sit upon the throne of Osiris.
Your staff is in your hand to command the living. The handle of your lotus staff is in your hand. Command the people of the hidden places!
Your arm is Atum’s arm, your shoulders are Atum’s shoulders, your belly is Atum’s belly, your back is Atum’s back.
Your buttocks are Atum’s buttocks, your two legs are Atum’s legs, your face is Anubis’s face.
The places of Horus serve you. The places of Seth serve you.
