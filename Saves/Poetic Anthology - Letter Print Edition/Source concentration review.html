Source concentration review

The revised anthology retains 347 selections and introduces 102 complete works. Its 449 works span 253 source groups and occupy 1,000 print pages. The largest repeated source supplies 8 works. Both editions preserve the complete Hawaiian creation chant He Kumulipo and the Argentine El gaucho Martín Fierro.

The comparison covered all 31,102 KJB verses, the 42 works in the collected Shakespeare, and the six other literary and philosophical books on the shelf. It checked for complete short readings and substantial sequences of shared wording. A second comparison checked the Vayrshemon wording of all 449 selections against 2,022,839 native words on the same shelf. Neither comparison found a duplicate work.
