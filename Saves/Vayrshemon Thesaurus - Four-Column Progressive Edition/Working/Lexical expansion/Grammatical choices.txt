# Base key | short label | English meaning | native definition | native example | English rendering. The native exemplar includes @ as the actual inflected representative.
at | At the point of contact | A locative that places something precisely where two surfaces, paths, or working arrangements meet. Either side of the junction is too imprecise. | at place where two part:pl meet:pr | mark @ two stone:pl join:pr put:imp. on either stone alone mark lose:fu. | Put the mark right where the two stones meet. On either stone alone it will be lost.
in | Inside what is left | Inside the portion that remains after the surroundings or the original container have partly disappeared. | in part which after loss remain:pr | roof go:perf. child yet @ last wall:pl play:pr. | The roof is gone. The child still plays inside the surviving walls.
at | At a former position recoverable by a trace | Locates a thing at its earlier position by a mark left there. This form lets a speaker point to an empty spot and still say where the thing stood. | at former:m place which by mark yet know:psv:pr | boat go:perf. by wet:m mark i @ stone it lie:pa show:pa. | The boat is gone. By the wet mark I can show where it lay against the stone.
from | From a source one saw | Marks an origin witnessed at the point of departure. Knowing the destination does not authorize this choice of preposition. | from place where speaker deed of depart:ev see:pa | @ other shore i her/boat push:psv:pa see:pa. where she then go:pa i know:neg:pr. | I saw the boat push off from the far bank. Where she went next I cannot say.
from | From the last hand in the chain | Marks an immediate giver, carrier, or teller while leaving the first source open. | from last person through whose hand thing pass:pa | fish @ child i receive:pa. who it catch:pa i yet know:neg:pr. | I got the fish from the child. I still do not know who caught it.
from | Out of, with the origin carried along | Marks a source whose influence remains discernible in what has left it. | from source whose mark yet in thing remain:pr | he @ other shore come:pa. in his/knot its/way yet see:can:psv:pr. | He came over from the other shore. You can still see that shore's practice in his knots.
with | Alongside by choice | Marks a companion who could have taken a different course and deliberately joined this one. | with other person who other way go:can:pa but together come:pa | road:pl separate:pa. she @ i continue:pa although her/home other way be:pa. | The roads divided. She came on with me though her home was the other way.
with | By means of what one had to hand | Marks a temporarily adopted instrument. The choice belongs to the occasion and makes no claim about its usual use. | with thing which for this work at hand be:pa | knife lose:pa. @ stone i knot open:pa. | I had lost the knife. I opened the knot with a stone.
with | With, bearing a share of the outcome | Marks participation that brings a stake in the result. A spectator standing beside the participants does not qualify. | with other person so gain or loss both share:pr | many on shore stand:pa. only those who their/food put:pa @ wepl fish:pa. | Many stood on the shore. Only those who put in their provisions fished with us.
before | Before the last chance closes | Marks the last usable opening for an act that another event will soon make impossible. | before deed after which work do:can:neg:psv:pr | @ tide turn:pr boat move:imp. after it turn:pr shore too far:m be:fu. | Move the boat before the tide turns. Once it has turned the shore will be too far off.
before | Before, with room to spare | Before an event by enough time for another ordinary action to intervene. The interval is part of the assertion. | before deed with time enough:m for other work | she @ bell arrive:pa. enough time for wet:m coat dry:inf have:pa. | She arrived well before the bell, with time even to dry her coat.
before | Before a witness capable of answering | Marks the person in whose accountable presence an undertaking takes place. Mere visibility is insufficient. | before person who hear:pa and later answer:can:pr | @ keeper wepl share divide:pa. today she each share name:can:pr. | We divided the shares before the keeper. Today she can still account for each one.
after | After in the wake of | Signals an aftermath that still shapes the later event. A calendar date alone does not establish this relation. | after deed whose effect yet remain:pr | @ flood house dry:m be:pa but road still soft:m be:pa. | After the flood the house was dry, but the road still gave underfoot.
after | After the first safe interval | Later than an event by the interval needed for a new act to become feasible. | after time which for safe:m work need:psv:pr | @ wind cease:pr wepl wait:pa until branch:pl still:m become:pa. then roof ascend:pa. | Once the wind had dropped, we waited for the branches to settle before going up.
after | Taking one's order from | Marks performance after an example that supplies the order of actions. Two unrelated acts merely occurring in succession do not qualify. | after other deed which order of work show:pr | child @ her/mother knot make:pa. first turn and last turn same:m be:pa. | The child tied the knot after her mother, taking the first turn and the last in the same order.
to | As far as a handover | Marks the point where one person's responsibility ends because another takes it up. | to place where other person burden take:pr | i fish @ gate carry:pa. there buyer it weigh:pa and take:pa. | I carried the fish as far as the gate. There the buyer weighed it and took it over.
to | Toward an intended recipient | Marks where something is directed while leaving delivery unconfirmed. | to person for whom thing send:psv:pr though receive:ev know:neg:psv:pr | letter @ my/sister send:pa. her/answer yet come:neg:perf. | I sent the letter to my sister. We have yet to hear whether it reached her.
to | Into someone's keeping | Marks a completed transfer of custody. The receiving hand has accepted the thing. | to person who thing in their/care already take:perf | key @ child give:pa. she it in her/bag put:pa and gate watch:pa. | I handed the key over to the child. She put it away and took charge of the gate.
of | Of by origin | Marks the source from which a thing or practice came even where it is now held elsewhere. | of place or person from whom thing first come:pa | this knot @ west shore be:pr. here now all it use:pr. | This knot is of the west shore. Everyone here uses it now.
of | Of by present custody | Marks whose keeping something is in at the time of speaking without settling who made or owns it. | of person in whose hand thing now keep:psv:pr | box @ keeper be:pr. goods in it other person own:pr. | It is the keeper's box to look after. The goods inside belong to someone else.
of | Of in remembered association | Marks a bond of association that survives separation or loss. The speaker deliberately brings the earlier tie into the present description. | of former:m bond which in memory yet remain:pr | empty:m chair @ my/father wepl yet call:pr though long:m time other person there sit:pr. | We still call it my father's chair though someone else has sat there for years.
near | Near enough to intervene | Within reach in time to help or interfere if the need arises. Distance is reckoned against an action. | near enough:m so before loss help:can:pr | she @ boat stay:pa. first knot slip:pa and her/hand it catch:pa. | She stayed close enough to the boat to catch the first knot that slipped.
near | Near by sight alone | Near enough to recognize while access may still be blocked. This preposition is natural for opposite banks. | near enough:m to see:inf though pass:can:neg:pr | village @ across water show:pa. by road two day:pl need:pa. | The village stood visibly near across the water. By road it was two days away.
near | Near by familiar route | Near as reckoned by someone who knows a reliable approach. A visitor may reasonably judge the same distance differently. | near by way which traveller know:pr | from here my/home @ be:pr child say:pa and way between wall:pl show:pa. | “Home is near from here,” the child said, and showed us the passage between the walls.
must | Must because a thing will cease to be possible | Necessity imposed by a closing physical opportunity, without an order from anyone. | do:must:pr because way soon close:fu | tide already turn:pr. wepl @ now go:inf. nobody command:neg:pr. | The tide is turning. We must go now. Nobody has given an order.
must | Must because one has undertaken it | A necessity the speaker accepts through a previous undertaking even when they could physically walk away. | do:must:pr because before promise give:pa | i @ return:inf. child there for my/word wait:pr. | I must go back. The child is waiting on my word.
must | Must on the evidence | Marks the sole reading left after the relevant alternatives have been tested. It creates no duty to do anything. | after other reading:pl test:psv:pa only one_num true:m be:can:pr | both gate:pl closed:m be:pa. mark:pl inside fresh:m be:pa. she @ here yet be:inf. | Both gates were shut and the marks inside were fresh. She must still be here.
may | May by another's leave | Permission explicitly given by someone able to grant it. The permitted act can remain physically difficult. | person by right other person deed do:inf allow:pr | keeper key give:pa. now i @ enter:inf but door yet heavy:m be:pr. | The keeper gave me the key. I may go in now, though the door is still heavy.
may | May as an open possibility | A live possibility compatible with what is known. The speaker makes no claim about anyone's permission. | deed which with what know:psv:pr yet possible:m be:pr | boat see:neg:psv:pr. it @ behind rock wait:inf. | The boat is out of sight. It may be waiting behind the rock.
may | May, with the choice returned | Signals to another person that the speaker gives up a claim on their decision and leaves the choice genuinely open. | other person choose:caus:inf after own:m claim release:ev | i ask:perf. now you @ stay:inf or go:inf. both answer:pl i receive:fu. | I have asked. You may stay or go. I will accept either answer.
who | Which accountable person | Asks who can answer for an act or decision. A name alone fails to answer if the named person had no charge of it. | who for deed answer:can:pr ask:inf | @ gate open:pa ask:pa. child witness name:pa. i keeper name ask:pa. | “Who opened the gate?” The child named a witness. I wanted the keeper who had charge of it.
who | Whom could we recognize again | Asks for an identifying feature that would let the hearer recognize a person on another occasion. A personal name may be unavailable. | who by mark again recognize:can:psv:pr ask:inf | @ she ask:pa. i name know:neg:pa but her/voice among many recognize:can:pa. | “Who was she?” I had no name to give, but I could have picked her voice out of a crowd.
who | Who was there to know | Asks for the witness whose presence makes a claim knowable. It leaves the identity of the person performing the act open. | who there be:pa and therefore truth know:can:pr ask:inf | fire start:pa. @ there see:pa i ask:pa. who fire make:pa yet ask:neg:pa. | The fire had started. “Who was there to see it?” I was not yet asking who lit it.
