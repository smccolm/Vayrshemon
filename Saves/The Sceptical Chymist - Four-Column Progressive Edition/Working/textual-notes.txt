The supplied English file is the copy text. Its original spelling of the title is The Sceptical Chymist. The English reading text expands the long s and the printer's doubled capital V for W and regularizes whitespace. It retains the speakers, historical terminology, quoted languages and the publisher’s announcement.

The 1661 printer’s directions have been applied to the text. The loose sheet supplied near the end now follows “is not to imitate Nature” in Part III and precedes “But, pursues Carneades”. The paragraph split after “Structure or Texture” has also been repaired. The separate printer’s directions and errata list are omitted from the reading edition. The printer reports that other loose papers were lost, including material at the end of the first dialogue. The supplied file contains no replacement for that loss.

The four-column edition covers all 300 source paragraphs, from the title through the publisher’s notice and final “END.” The English in the 4,401 aligned passages reconstructs the corrected reading text in order. Latin, Greek and Hebrew are retained in the source column and rendered in the Vayrshemon columns. The vocabulary records distinguish the principles proposed by the speakers from materials handled in the laboratory, including the Mercury principle and metallic quicksilver.

Offset-indexed readings are in corrections.json. Offsets refer to the supplied physical line after conversion of long s, before whitespace normalization. The comparison transcription of the 1661 printing is Project Gutenberg ebook 22914, https://www.gutenberg.org/files/22914/22914-h/22914-h.htm . Greek stigma ligatures are expanded where used for στ. The Hebrew in the Creation passage uses the ordinary initial mem.
