## What makes it historical and awkward

The historical translator favors repeated complete subjects, serial finite clauses, carried-word/carried-prayer constructions, heavy resumptions and explicit nominal objects. Old `tren` for three survives beside the modern language's `venik`. Its imagined antiquity is authored; the real evidence for the form is earlier project versions.

Religious concepts receive uneasy calques: above-owner, name-lord, cut-gift, gift-good, harm-back, life-self, apart-house and intention-back-turn. These choices retain the source's assertions and tensions. Divine relenting is not confused with confession of moral guilt; threatened calamity is not collapsed into human wickedness. Source questions, uncertainty, numbers, agency and deliberate repetition remain.

The edition records difficult readings instead of hiding them: Jonah 2:8's “their own mercy”; the city's three-day measure; the source's unspecified gourd species; and people in 4:11 who are not explicitly called children. The supplied 1:15 reads “look up Jonah”; the English remains verbatim while the target's contextual lift/take-up reading is documented. The back-readings are authored explanations, not an independent equivalence test.
