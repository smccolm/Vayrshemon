# Sources for headwords and concepts

Use the historical OED for changes of meaning, Roget for conceptual neighbours, and the language-specific resources for distinctions English may leave implicit. The links below distinguish downloadable data from places to consult an individual entry. The [expansion plan](Expansion%20plan.md) explains how a candidate enters the books. The [pilot queue](Pilot%20queue.md) puts these sources to work.

## English

### Oxford English Dictionary, first edition

**Free online text is available for the historical OED**, originally titled *A New English Dictionary on Historical Principles*. Its 128 fascicles appeared from 1884 to 1928 and formed ten volumes. The companion [A–Z download manifest](oed-first-edition.json) selects 16 plain-text OCR files across those volumes. Each record includes a direct text URL, a metadata URL, byte size, the Archive’s SHA-1 and the byte range sampled. The proposal and 1933 supplement are separate works outside this baseline. [Oxford edition history](https://oed.hertford.ox.ac.uk/bibliography/) · [Penn’s volume catalog](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/metabook?id=newenglishdictionary) · [Read A–B as text](https://archive.org/download/ANewEnglishDictionaryOnHistoricalPrinciples.10VolumesWithSupplement/01.NEDHP.AB.Oxford.Murray.1888._djvu.txt)

Two substitutions matter. The bundled D–E OCR ends among entries around *dissimilar*. Use the separate **D and E** texts. Their terminal samples reach *Dz-* and *Ez-* respectively. The bundled **O–P** text is only 305,394 bytes. The Toronto copy is 32,624,966 bytes, with *ol-* in an interior sample and *py-* in the final sample. All 16 selected files returned the requested final 2,200 bytes. This tests accessibility and catches some truncated files. The scans still need a page-by-page coverage check before claiming a complete headword harvest. [D text](https://archive.org/download/oxforddictionaryv3p1unse_a5h6/oxforddictionaryv3p1unse_a5h6_djvu.txt) · [E text](https://archive.org/download/oxforddictionary0003unse/oxforddictionary0003unse_djvu.txt) · [O–P text](https://archive.org/download/newenglishdic07murruoft/newenglishdic07murruoft_djvu.txt) · [Bundle inventory](https://archive.org/metadata/ANewEnglishDictionaryOnHistoricalPrinciples.10VolumesWithSupplement)

Extract lemma, homograph number, part of speech, numbered sense, usage label and scan page. Retain obsolete and regional senses that explain the project’s books. Keep illustrative quotations, spelling variants, running heads and cross-references distinguishable from headwords. When the plain text interleaves columns, consult the page image and coordinate-bearing OCR offered with that scan. File sizes and alphabetic endpoints alone cannot detect an omitted leaf in the middle. [O–P scan and alternate formats](https://archive.org/details/newenglishdic07murruoft)

Penn catalogs the historical work as public domain in the United States. Keep its edition and transcription provenance. **Current OED Online** is a different, continuously revised resource. Eligible public-library users can have free reading access through a subscription. I found no publisher-authorized free bulk textual edition of the current OED. Use the historical files for harvesting and an available library subscription for individual modern lookups. [Penn catalog](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/metabook?id=newenglishdictionary) · [Oxford bibliography](https://oed.hertford.ox.ac.uk/bibliography/) · [Example of library access](https://www.sheffield.gov.uk/libraries-archives/online-reference-tools)

### Roget’s Thesaurus

Gutenberg eBook 22 has a free plain-text copy derived from the 1911 edition, with later electronic additions by MICRA. The text mirror opened successfully and contains numbered heads, including head 1, *Existence*. Record the head number, part-of-speech subsection, word or phrase, and any directional cross-reference. Oppositions, topical associates and synonyms can sit close together. A list under the same head is a reading list for an editor. Gutenberg reports US public-domain status. Retain the edition notice to distinguish electronic additions from 1911 usage. [Gutenberg catalog](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/22) · [Read or download the text](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GITenberg/Roget-s-Thesaurus_22/master/22.txt)

### Open English Wordnet

Use the **2025 base edition** for machine-readable modern sense groups. The release provides WN-LMF XML and JSON. The GitHub JSON ZIP responded to a download check while the main XML host returned HTTP 503. Keep lexical sense IDs, synset IDs, part of speech and typed links such as antonym or hypernym. The base vocabulary omits the optional names collection. Credit Open English Wordnet under CC BY 4.0 and retain the notices shipped with the files. Older multilingual WordNets frequently use Princeton 3.0 offsets. Match those through a documented mapping, or retain them in their original namespace. [Download the verified base JSON ZIP](https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet/releases/download/2025-edition/english-wordnet-2025-json.zip) · [Versioned release](https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet/releases/tag/2025-edition) · [Project and license](https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet)

### An independent concept index

Concepticon links elicitation glosses from published concept lists to numbered concept sets. Use it to find domains the English sources sample poorly and to record a common question across languages. A concept-set ID can connect research notes even when the native answer is a phrase. Preserve list ID and source citation alongside the concept-set ID. The project publishes its data under CC BY 4.0. [Concepticon and citation](https://concepticon.clld.org/) · [Downloadable data](https://concepticon.clld.org/download)

## What a future harvest should retain

Pin the exact release, commit or dated snapshot and compute a local checksum at acquisition. Save the publisher’s notices, credits and download locator beside that source. Keep imported text and derived tables attributable to their specific source. This matters when sharing a Perseus or OpenThesaurus extract under share-alike terms. Author the Vayrshemon definitions and examples from the actual semantic evidence and keep a pointer back to it. Track **independent witnesses** separately from the number of datasets that repeat a Princeton or DanNet analysis.
