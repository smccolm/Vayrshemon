## Develop variation without erasing the fuller reading

Language **0.6.0** retains M11's Tempest source, English, eight sentence-initial Maxima, dictionary and all 256 earlier assertions exactly. Its new continuity proposal has separate grammar, wiki, assertion and page IDs. Two coherent readings compare:

> Our actors are spirits. They have become only breath.

The full version repeats the actors expression; the optional literary continuation leaves that established subject implicit. Predicate, adverb scope and finite tense remain. This option requires an explicit immediately preceding subject with the same author-annotated lexical identity, established discourse status, subject topic, neutral focus and literary register. Topic shifts, contrast, missing context, absent reference, a different identity and an invalid antecedent clause reject it. Full forms and prior orders remain available.

The compiler checks the annotated identity and the antecedent's own assertions. Bound pages additionally require that antecedent immediately before the continuation in the same paragraph. It cannot infer real-world reference, rhetorical quality or semantic equivalence. The proposal is recorded as **draft**, not attested language or a mandate to omit repeated subjects. The wiki explains these limits and retains the fuller construction for comparison.
